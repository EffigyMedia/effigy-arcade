### RLG-157 — Police only ever come from behind, and the roadblock cop is set dressing

Owner, 2026-09-07: "I don't think they should spawn in front of you and come back
at you. They should only come from behind. The exception to this is when you pass a speed
trap or the cop sitting at a roadblock. And the cop at a roadblock never chases you -
that's just set dressing."

Three parts, and the third is the easiest to miss. Police arrive from BEHIND only. A speed
trap is parked ahead by its nature and is the exception, as is the cruiser standing at a
roadblock. And that roadblock cruiser must never join a pursuit at all - it is scenery
with a light bar on it.

WHAT THIS TOUCHES: `spawnCop` already spawns behind. `spawnSuper` also spawns behind.
Patrols in traffic spawn AHEAD by design, but they only engage after you pass them, which
is the owner's own exception written from the other side. The roadblock cruiser is a
`parts` entry with `cop:true` and is not in the `cops` array at all - it has never chased
anybody, so that half may already hold and needs proving rather than building.
