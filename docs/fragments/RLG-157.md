### RLG-157 — Police only ever come from behind, and the roadblock cop is set dressing

Owner, 2026-09-07: "I don't think they should spawn in front of you and come back
at you. They should only come from behind. The exception to this is when you pass a speed
trap or the cop sitting at a roadblock. And the cop at a roadblock never chases you -
that's just set dressing."

Three parts, and the third is the easiest to miss. Police arrive from BEHIND only. A speed
trap is parked ahead by its nature and is the exception, as is the cruiser standing at a
roadblock. And that roadblock cruiser must never join a pursuit at all - it is scenery
with a light bar on it.

WHAT THIS TOUCHES: `spawnCop` already spawns behind. `spawnSuper` also spawns behind.
Patrols in traffic spawn AHEAD by design, but they only engage after you pass them, which
is the owner's own exception written from the other side. The roadblock cruiser is a
`parts` entry with `cop:true` and is not in the `cops` array at all - it has never chased
anybody, so that half may already hold and needs proving rather than building.
- **RULED 2026-09-08T13:13:04-04:00** — REOPENED AND FIXED 2026-09-08, v0.13.39. Owner: "there are still police coming from up ahead."

IT WAS TWO RULINGS MEETING, not either of them being wrong. A speed trap is parked 35,000 to 52,000
units AHEAD by its nature. [[RLG-046]] rules that the police engage any car breaking the limit and
not only the player. So a trap far up the road that spotted a speeding NPC left its post and became
a MOVING police car in front of the player - chasing somebody else, and never once having come from
behind.

MEASURED BEFORE THE FIX: every cruiser was tagged the frame it first existed, and three of fourteen
in one run were trap-born and first seen about 36,000 units ahead, already out of trap state. Zero
cruisers that arrived behind ever drove in front, so this morning's box-at-speed was NOT the cause -
which is worth recording, because it was the obvious suspect and it was innocent.

THIS RULING'S EXCEPTION IS A TRAP THAT ENGAGES YOU AS YOU PASS IT. It does not stretch to one that
sets off after somebody else while still up the road, and this ruling is both later than RLG-046 and
the live complaint.

THE GATE IS THE ONE THE PLAYER'S OWN CASE ALREADY USES: a trap acts when it is behind the player,
which is exactly when the player has passed it. RLG-046 stays whole - a speeding driver still gets
pulled - and the arrival stays behind, because a trap you have passed IS behind you. It names no
vehicle class and adds no second rule. WHAT IT COSTS is the sight of somebody else being pulled over
up ahead, which is a real loss to the living road RLG-046 was for.

AND THE HARNESS IS THE OTHER HALF OF THIS. This ruling was proved once by hand and had NO CHECK, so
it could go wrong silently - and did. `patrol-test.py` now tags every cruiser at first sight and
keeps two facts about it, because the two ways "police up ahead" happens need different fixes: born
ahead, or born behind and driven in front. A cop still in trap state is not counted, and the
threshold is one car length, because a patrol is promoted the frame you draw level with it and reads
as +16 to +37 units.

A SECOND DEFECT FELL OUT OF THE SAME RUN, and it was mine from this morning. The box station is
built from the PLAYER'S speed, which was harmless while the box only formed at a standstill and
became a way round the car's own ceiling once it formed at any speed: 16,393 against a cruiser's
10,886, a patrol car keeping station with a supercar it cannot catch. It was already pushed. Both
police cars are clamped to `copTop` now and both read exactly at their ceilings.
