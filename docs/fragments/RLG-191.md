### RLG-191 — The formula cars get headlights, and they are wired
- **RULED 2026-09-09T19:01:28-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"although the formula has a headlight lamp, it doesn't show illuminated in
any of the sheet frames, I wanna make sure it's actually wired."*

**IT HAD NO LAMP AT ALL, so there was nothing to wire.** `paintFormulaFace` declared nothing - no
`head` and no `turn` - and the blank headlight frame on the fleet sheet was therefore accurate rather
than a fault. This is not a regression from [[RLG-185]]: the formula face has never carried a lamp,
in either the drawing that shipped before it or the one that replaced it.

**IT MATTERED BEYOND THE SHEET.** `head` is read at one place in the engine: the rear-view pass lights
the headlights of the cars behind you once the light level drops. A formula rival in your mirror at
night therefore showed nothing at all.

**THE QUESTION WAS THE OWNER'S TO ANSWER AND IT WAS PUT TO THEM.** A real single-seater has no
headlights, and the 2026-08-29 ruling already says the formula car has no indicators BECAUSE THE LAMP
DOES NOT EXIST - a headlight is the same question, not a bug. Three options were offered: leave them
unlit, give them a marker light that is not a headlight, or give them real headlights. **The owner
ruled for real headlights.**

**A PAIR ON THE TUB, EITHER SIDE OF THE NOSE CONE.** They are inside the outline both ends share, so
they change no silhouette - a lamp is a detail, and details are exactly what the two ends of a vehicle
are allowed to differ about. They are declared under `head`, which is the name the mirror already
asks for, so they light with every other car on the road and needed no wiring of their own.
