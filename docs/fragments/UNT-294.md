### UNT-294 - A finished tournament is retired, whichever way you leave the trophy

- Routing class: `defect` -> **claude-opus-5**, effort **high**. Bias 1. Finding the cause is the work, and a wrong guess costs a second pass. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-13T00:03:26-04:00** - v0.14.8. The owner reported that completing a tournament and leaving by MAIN MENU rather than NEW TOURNAMENT put the next tournament at race four with them in first place, one race from a gold. THE TROPHY SCREEN HAD THREE WAYS OUT AND ONLY ONE RESET ANYTHING: NEW TOURNAMENT called tourReset, while MAIN MENU and the two buttons behind SEE YOUR NEW CAR set tourOn = false. tourOn IS NOT THE STATE - it is recomputed from raceTour by enforceModeRules every time the garage opens, so clearing it clears nothing. tourRound, tourPts and tourField stayed exactly as the finished tournament left them. THE FIX IS NOT AT THE BUTTONS. It cannot reset when the trophy opens because that screen reads tourPts and tourField for the standings, and it must not be three fixes at three buttons - naming every exit is the shape that left hasBar and copLivery wrong when a second police car arrived, and it leaves the next button somebody adds carrying the defect back in. So a flag says the tournament is SPENT and enforceModeRules acts on it: that is the single door back, because showGarage calls it through enforceCarRules and a player has to pass the garage to drive. It sits above every branch in that function, because a police car takes an early return and a player may walk into one from the trophy. NEW TOURNAMENT no longer calls tourReset itself, so there is exactly one place that ends a tournament. A PART-RUN TOURNAMENT IS UNTOUCHED, which is RLG-115's deliberate rule - only one that has PAID OUT is spent, which is what the flag distinguishes and what tourOn never could. tools/tour-exit-test.py is new and PLAYS THE WHOLE TOURNAMENT, using API.parkFinish to end each round, so the four rounds and the trophy are real. --falsify reproduces the owner's report in their own words: the garage still holds round 3 and it started at round 3, which is the fourth race. TWO HARNESS LESSONS WORTH KEEPING: data-act names repeat across screens, so an unscoped selector found a drive button on a veil that was not on screen and waited thirty seconds for it; and a round ends when a SCREEN OPENS rather than after a fixed wait, because the finish is handled 700ms after the line with an animation in front of the trophy. NOT COVERED BY MEASUREMENT: the harness finishes last so it never reaches SEE YOUR NEW CAR, and those two buttons are covered by construction rather than by having been watched.
