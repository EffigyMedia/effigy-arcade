### RLG-284 — The mountain cliff edge is jagged, not a straight line
- **RULED 2026-09-16T23:56:33-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-16: "The cliff edge in the mountain biome should be jagged a non-uniform."

WHAT IT IS ABOUT. The mountain biome draws a cliff, and its edge is a single clean line. A cliff is rock that has broken away, so a straight edge reads as a wall rather than as stone - the eye takes uniformity as manufactured.

WHAT TO LOOK AT FIRST: the mountain entry in `SCENERY` in `road.js` and whatever paints the cliff face. The shape of the fix is the same one the coast already uses for its shoreline - a per-segment offset that is deterministic from the segment index rather than random per frame, because a cliff that reshuffles as you drive is worse than a straight one. `coast-test.py` measured the shoreline's largest step against its mean and is the model for measuring this.

AND IT IS A LOOK, SO THE OWNER JUDGES IT ON THE DEVICE. A harness can say the edge is not a straight line and cannot say whether it reads as rock.
