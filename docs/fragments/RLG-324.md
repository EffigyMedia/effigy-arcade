### RLG-324 — bridge-test's haze check fails: the water under the deck reads only 0.171 of the draw away
- **RULED 2026-09-21T23:07:26-04:00** — FOUND, NOT FIXED. tools/bridge-test.py fails one check on every run: 'and far enough off to be hazed, which is what says it is below you', reading 'it reads only 0.171 of the draw away'. Run on 97973ab0, before this session's two units, it fails the same way with the same number, so neither the tree wall nor the glass survey caused it. RLG-278 cites this check as the proof that a floor far below reads as depth, so while it fails the bridge's water is unproven on that point.

A SECOND CHECK IN THE SAME HARNESS IS UNSTABLE, not failing: 'the row is still mostly water' read 217, 328 and 345 water pixels of 480 on three runs of one build, against a threshold of 264. The row sits just under the skyline, where boats and ironwork come and go, so a count there measures the shipping as well as the water.

FIRST STEP: read what 0.171 is measured from. The draw distance was doubled to 300 in RLG-295, and a fraction of the draw that was once far enough may be half as far now.
