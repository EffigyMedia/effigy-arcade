### RLG-305 — Traffic stops dead when a race ends, instead of driving on
- **RULED 2026-09-21T00:15:28-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21, from the device: 'When the race is over all the traffic comes to a dead stop they should just keep moving just like the player.'

THE PLAYER IS ALREADY HANDLED AND THE WORLD IS NOT. Crossing the line sets `finished` and `coasting`, and the note on that block says why the car is handed to the AI: it lifts, holds its lane and coasts down, so you cannot crash after winning. The road around it was never given the same treatment.

FIRST SUSPECT, AND IT IS ONE LINE. `step` runs `if(mode === 'race' && !finished){ ... if(!held) stepRacers(dt); }`, so every RIVAL stops being stepped the instant the line is crossed. Whether the civilian traffic freezes by the same gate or a different one is not established and must be read rather than assumed - the owner's report says 'all the traffic', which is the wider claim.

AND THERE IS A RECORDED LESSON ABOUT EXACTLY THIS SHAPE. The early `return` in `step` was removed once before for the same reason, and its own comment records it: 'THIS USED TO return... the world weather, the traffic, the police, and stepRacers' all stopped for two seconds. The fix then was to let everything below run and hold only the CAR. This is that fix, not yet applied to the finish.

WHAT IT MUST NOT DO: the rivals must not keep RACING. A race is over; the cars on the road are traffic now. Whether a rival that crosses the line becomes an ordinary driver or simply coasts in its lane is a question this has to answer, and the owner's sentence - 'just like the player' - points at coasting rather than at racing on.

Not started.
