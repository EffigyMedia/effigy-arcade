### RLG-274 — A tournament round grids you by your championship position
- **RULED 2026-09-16T03:22:36-04:00** — TRACKED AND DECIDED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-16: "I hadn't thought about it, but when you finish the first race, you start the second race in the position that you earned in, the third race starts in your position, and the fourth race, the same. Is this how it works?"

IT IS NOT HOW IT WORKS, and the owner assumed it did. `buildField()` strings all eleven rivals out AHEAD of the player every time - `z: pos + 700 + rank*520 + rnd(-120,120)` - and `reset()` sets `place = 12`. Neither reads `tourField` or `tourPts`. So rounds two, three and four start exactly like round one, with the player twelfth. The championship is SCORED like a championship and GRIDDED like four unrelated races, and the standings screen between rounds is a scoreboard the next round ignores.

DECIDED: GRID BY THE STANDINGS. From round two on, the player lines up where the points table puts them - leading the championship starts at the front, last starts at the back. The owner chose this against a reversed grid (leader at the back) and against leaving it alone. The reason it is worth doing is that it makes the standings screen decide something instead of being read and forgotten.

WHAT THE WORK TOUCHES. `buildField()` owns the grid and is called from `reset()` for any `mode === 'race'`, so a single race must be unaffected - it has no standings to grid from, and its own eleven cars must go on being strung out ahead. `tourStanding()` already answers where the player sits overall. `place = 12` in `reset()` is the starting readout and has to agree with where the car actually is, or the HUD opens on a lie.

AND IT IS A BIGGER CHANGE THAN IT LOOKS. Today the player is behind the whole field, so every rival is drawn ahead and the rearview is empty at the line. Gridding the player mid-pack means cars BEHIND the player at the start, which is a road state the first frames of a race have never been in. The rubber band, the pileup guarantee and the rolling-start hold all read positions at the line.

NOT STARTED, and it is deliberately NOT part of [[RLG-268]] - the retry pool and the per-class save are their own unit, and this is its own feature.
