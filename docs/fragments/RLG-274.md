### RLG-274 — A tournament round grids you by your championship position
- **RULED 2026-09-16T03:22:36-04:00** — TRACKED AND DECIDED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-16: "I hadn't thought about it, but when you finish the first race, you start the second race in the position that you earned in, the third race starts in your position, and the fourth race, the same. Is this how it works?"

IT IS NOT HOW IT WORKS, and the owner assumed it did. `buildField()` strings all eleven rivals out AHEAD of the player every time - `z: pos + 700 + rank*520 + rnd(-120,120)` - and `reset()` sets `place = 12`. Neither reads `tourField` or `tourPts`. So rounds two, three and four start exactly like round one, with the player twelfth. The championship is SCORED like a championship and GRIDDED like four unrelated races, and the standings screen between rounds is a scoreboard the next round ignores.

DECIDED: GRID BY THE STANDINGS. From round two on, the player lines up where the points table puts them - leading the championship starts at the front, last starts at the back. The owner chose this against a reversed grid (leader at the back) and against leaving it alone. The reason it is worth doing is that it makes the standings screen decide something instead of being read and forgotten.

WHAT THE WORK TOUCHES. `buildField()` owns the grid and is called from `reset()` for any `mode === 'race'`, so a single race must be unaffected - it has no standings to grid from, and its own eleven cars must go on being strung out ahead. `tourStanding()` already answers where the player sits overall. `place = 12` in `reset()` is the starting readout and has to agree with where the car actually is, or the HUD opens on a lie.

AND IT IS A BIGGER CHANGE THAN IT LOOKS. Today the player is behind the whole field, so every rival is drawn ahead and the rearview is empty at the line. Gridding the player mid-pack means cars BEHIND the player at the start, which is a road state the first frames of a race have never been in. The rubber band, the pileup guarantee and the rolling-start hold all read positions at the line.

NOT STARTED, and it is deliberately NOT part of [[RLG-268]] - the retry pool and the per-class save are their own unit, and this is its own feature.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T04:06:53-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.51.

`gridSlot()` IS THE WHOLE RULE and it is three lines: outside a tournament, or in round one, the player is last exactly as before; otherwise the slot is `tourStanding() - 1`, clamped. It reads the SAME function the between-rounds screen prints, so the grid and the standings screen cannot disagree.

THE LINE IS BUILT AROUND THE PLAYER. The grid is twelve slots and the player holds one, so a rival's place in it is its own index stepped past the player's slot - `gslot = i < slot ? i : i + 1`. Its distance is `slot - gslot`, signed, and its z is `pos + sign * (700 + (steps-1) * 520)`, which reproduces the old line EXACTLY when the player is last: 700 for the adjacent car and 520 a slot after that.

TWO THINGS KEYED OFF THE LOOP INDEX AND HAD TO MOVE TO THE SLOT. The number painted on the car was `FIELD - rank`, which put #1 at the front only because the player was always behind everyone; it is `gslot + 1` now, which keeps #1 at the front in both cases. And the pace spread `0.99 - i*0.008` is `0.99 - r.rank*0.008`, where `rank` is how many slots away from the player the car lined up - so the tuned pattern survives being mirrored: the cars nearest you either side are the quickest and the far ends of the line are the slowest, which is what makes a field you can work through. With the player last the two forms are arithmetically identical.

AND THE HUD OPENS ON THE TRUTH. `place` is recomputed every frame from the cars actually ahead, so a wrong value corrects itself within a frame - but that frame is the one the player looks at while the countdown runs, and a grid that says P2 under a HUD that says P12 is the kind of disagreement this codebase has been caught by before. It is `slot + 1`.

MEASURED. `tools/grid-order-test.py` counts the cars BEHIND the player at the line - the thing that could not happen before and cannot be faked by a slot number - reading each rival's `dz` off `API.grid` rather than the variable that arranged them ([[RLG-065]]). Round one: 11 ahead, 0 behind, HUD P12. Single race: the same. Seeded second in the championship: 1 ahead, 10 behind, HUD P2. The numbers on the cars still run 1 at the front to 12 at the back with one of the twelve missing, and they run in order along the road. `--selftest` pins the grid to last through `API.gridPin` - which IS the old build, because the slot is the only thing the line is laid out around - and the file reports it on two checks.

THE STANDING IS SEEDED, NOT DRIVEN, and that is stated rather than hidden: `API.seedTour` writes the round and the points a finished round would have left. Earning one means driving a whole round to the finish, which measures the race rather than the grid, and `race-test` already drives a finish.

IT COULD NOT BE MEASURED UNTIL [[RLG-275]] WAS FIXED FIRST. Two `API.grid` definitions shared the name and the later one won, so the harness asked for the starting line-up and got a class question with three keys. That is fixed in the same commit because this unit's proof cannot run without it, and it is recorded as its own ruling.

AND IT MADE A THIRD THING VISIBLE WHICH IS NOT FIXED: [[RLG-276]], a rival gaining more speed in the single frame the count releases than in the whole second of racing after it. `launch-test` has been unable to reach that check for as long as the duplicate lasted.

WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE: whether starting mid-pack reads as a reward or removes the pleasure of coming through the field, and whether cars appearing in the mirror at the line is a good surprise. Both want the owner on a device. Nothing else in the race was retuned for it - the rubber band, the pileup guarantee and the rolling-start hold are untouched, and whether any of them wants revisiting now that the player can start ahead of cars is an open question rather than a finding.

launch-test's other rolling-start checks, resort-test, race-test, tour-save-test, class-test, league-test and smoke-test all pass on this build.
