### RLG-303 — A mountain biome should be arrived at by the land going up, not by a wall at a line
- **RULED 2026-09-20T18:29:14-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20, shown a capture of driving at a mountain biome from a desert: 'If we're judging it based on that screenshot, then that is ugly and not what we're going to want. Notice how it's disconnected. It doesn't read as a mountain. It's supposed to read as if you're driving into a mountain biome with a mountain on one side and a valley on the other.'

WHAT THE PICTURE ACTUALLY SHOWED, read back off it:
1. A FLAT-BOTTOMED CURTAIN OF ROCK standing on the desert. The face is one quad per column at ONE z - the boundary segment - so it is a billboard with no depth, and its base is a straight horizontal cut at that segment's road line. Desert sand meets mountain rock along a hard line that belongs to neither.
2. NOTHING LEADS UP TO IT. The wall beside the road, the drop beside the road and the ground colour all switch on at the boundary, so the place does not approach - it is stamped on.
3. THE SLOT IS A CANYON'S IDEA. A notch through rock is what a slot canyon IS. A mountain road does not go through a hole in a mountain; it runs along the side of one, and the first build put a mountain pass behind a doorway.
4. THE TWO OBJECTS DO NOT MEET. The wall running alongside and the face at the boundary are separate drawings at different angles and the eye reads them as two things, which is the 'disconnected' the owner named.

WHAT IS ALREADY DONE (v0.14.112): the face is gated on a place declaring `face`, and only the CANYON does. The mountain has no boundary face at all, which is where it was before and is not ugly. This ruling is the replacement, not the revert.

THE SHAPE OF THE FIX, and it is one idea rather than four. A MOUNTAIN SHOULD GROW OUT OF THE GROUND. `bioMix(seg)` already runs 0 to 1 across a boundary and everything that changes colour reads it; nothing that changes SHAPE does. Scale the mountain's own two shapes by it:
- `wallRiseAt` multiplied by `bioMix`, so the hillside rises from nothing over the blend band instead of appearing at full height at a line;
- `DROP_DEPTH` multiplied by `bioMix` on the hazard side, so the valley deepens from nothing over the same band.
Then driving into a mountain biome IS the land going up on one side and falling away on the other, which is the owner's sentence, and there is no second object to disconnect from the first. It also removes the hard base line: there is no base, because the rise starts at zero.

WHAT TO WATCH:
- `bioMix` is read per SLICE, so the ramp is in world space and fixed to the road - it will not slide past the camera. That is the same property the ridge noise needs and gets.
- The RANGE across the valley (RLG-297) is planted on the floor at `RANGE_AT` of the draw; with the floor rising from zero it must not end up above the road. Gate it on the drop having depth.
- The SKYLINE's height cap is derived from the place's scenery and hands over on its own schedule (RLG-022, RLG-104); a hillside that grows over the same band should agree with it rather than fight it.
- `hazard-test`, `cliff-test`, `wall-plane-test` and `biome-test` all assert things about a mountain's drop and wall AT FULL SIZE. They settle on a pinned pair where `bioMix` is 1, so they should be unaffected - but that is a prediction, not a measurement.
- AND THE CANYON MUST NOT BE CHANGED BY THIS. The owner accepted its walls and its face. If the rise is applied to every walled place, a canyon's walls would grow too, and its face would then stand in front of walls that are not yet full height. Make the rise the MOUNTAIN's, declared the way `face` is.

Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-20T21:54:07-04:00** — BUILT in v0.14.114, and UNSIGNED - the land is the owner's on a device (RLG-116).

ONE IDEA, AS THE RULING ASKED. `bioGrow(seg, B)` is how far a place's landform has grown at a segment, and it is read by the two things that give a mountain its shape: `wallRiseAt` multiplies the plain height by it, and `DROP_DEPTH` is multiplied by it on the hazard side. Driving into the place IS the land going up on one side and falling away on the other. There is no second object to disconnect from the first, and no hard base line, because the rise starts at zero.

IT IS THE MOUNTAIN'S OWN, DECLARED THE WAY `face` IS, which is the trap the ruling names. `grow:1` sits in the MOUNTAIN's record and nowhere else. A canyon's walls do not grow, so its boundary face never stands in front of walls that are not yet full height. The proof runs the canyon through the identical measurement and it has to come out unchanged - and with `grow:1` added to the canyon by hand, two of its checks go red.

THE BAND IS ITS OWN AND IT IS NOT `bioMix`'S, and that is a departure from the ruling's letter for the ruling's own reason. The COLOUR band is 18 segments, which the car covers in well under a second: ground is allowed to change at something close to a line because that is what ground does, and land that reaches full height in under a second has not risen, it has appeared. The WEATHER already has its own crossing at four times the colour band for exactly this argument, so the landform takes the same width. `GROW_BAND` is 72 segments, 14,400 units, and it is a tunable with a committed default that `API.wallModel` reads and writes. WHETHER 72 IS RIGHT IS THE OWNER'S and it is one constant.

MEASURED FROM WHERE THE PLACE TAKES OVER, NOT FROM THE MIDDLE OF THE COLOUR BAND. `bioAt` switches at `biomeEdge` and nothing of the place is drawn before it, so a ramp anchored on the band's start would begin at half height - the line again with a slope painted on it. It falls back to nothing over the same run as the place is LEFT, so a mountain sinks into the ground behind you rather than ending.

`growFrom` IS A SECOND ANCHOR AND `leftAt` COULD NOT BE REUSED. `leftAt` answers the same question and `skyGone` reads it, and a check pinning ONE place everywhere wants the skyline's handover finished while the land is at full height. So the ramp has its own anchor, set wherever `leftAt` is, and cleared by `setBiomePair` when the pair collapses to one place. Nothing about the horizon moves when it does, and the four harnesses that settle on a pinned pair are unaffected - measured, not predicted.

FOUR SMALLER THINGS FOLLOW THE SAME RAMP, because a half-built landform with a full-strength detail on it is the disconnection in miniature. The LIP - a lit band on ground that does not drop is a bright line across the boundary, which is the hard line this ruling exists to remove. The RANGE across the valley, whose height is scaled and whose width is not, because land rising is not an object getting smaller; the ruling's own warning was that it must not end up above the road, and it is planted on a floor that has not fallen. And BOTH of the mirror's, or a mountain sinks into the ground ahead of you while standing at full height behind.

THE WALL IS NOT DRAWN AT ALL AT ZERO. A band of zero height is not nothing: it reaches half a road width below its own top edge, which paints rock over the ground beside the road.

PROVED BY `tools/land-grow-proof.py`, WHICH IS NEW, AND THE NUMBERS ARE THE PICTURE. With the boundary 60 segments off, twelve segments past it: the wall's painted top edge is at y 320.9 grown against 83.9 switched, and the valley floor is 52.3 px under its rim against 316. One band in - 72 segments - the two are identical at 172.4 px and the same wall top, which is what tells a RAMP from a permanent shortening. The canyon is identical at both settings on its own crossing. Both halves read what the passes WROTE AS THEY PAINTED: `wallTrace` for the wall, and `dropFloorAt` with one number added - `deep`, how far below its own rim a slice's floor landed, because the existing `floorN` answers whether the plane is below the road at all and cannot say how far.

AND THE PROOF WAS WATCHED FAILING, TWICE AND IN THE TWO WAYS IT HAS TO. With the growth taken off the wall and the drop it reports the top edge moving 0.0 px and the valley at 327.4 px under the rim against a full 327.4. With `grow:1` added to the CANYON it reports the canyon at 0.167 of its height and its painted wall moving 131.4 px. ONE OF ITS OWN LINES WAS REMOVED FOR PASSING WITH THE DEFECT IN: a check that the canyon's ramp reads 1 at every distance reads 1 whatever the canyon declares, because the crossing being measured is a mountain's and a place that is neither end of the live pair has no boundary to grow out of.

WHAT IS STILL THE OWNER'S, and it is the whole of it: whether the land now reads as land, and whether 72 segments is a rise or a wait. `GROW_BAND` is the constant. Also unjudged - there is one segment at the boundary where the guard rail stands over a valley of no depth, because the rail is not on this ramp.

SUITE: smoke-test 7/7. drive-test 36/37, the one being the nitrous settle line that is recorded as environmental. wall-plane-test all pass. hazard-test 4 fail and canyon-test 3 fail, both identically at HEAD and for reasons this does not touch.
