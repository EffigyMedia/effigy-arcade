### RLG-171 — A cruiser behind the player passes through traffic with no consequence

FOUND WHILE MEASURING [[RLG-158]], NOT REPORTED. It is written up rather than fixed, because
changing it alters how hard a pursuit is to survive and that is the owner's call.

The cruiser-versus-traffic collision runs only under this guard:

    if(k.grace<=0 && k.z > pz - 900) for(const c of traffic){ ... hurtCop(k, 45, 'traffic') ... }

`pz` is the player's own position, so a cruiser more than 900 units BEHIND the player is
tested against nothing. Since [[RLG-157]] established that police only ever arrive from
behind, that is most of them for most of a pursuit: they drive through the traffic they are
weaving past and take no damage for it.

**IT IS WHY THE OWNER'S REPORT COULD NOT BE REPRODUCED.** [[RLG-158]] says the police "are
constantly crashing into traffic and destroying themselves", and no harness run on either
engine ever put a single cruiser out that way - ninety seconds of pursuit at four stars,
and five conditions from one star at half speed to five at nine tenths. The device sees the
crashes because a real player's cruisers get level and ahead, where the guard opens. A
harness driving in a straight line at a fixed speed leaves them behind it, where the guard
is shut.

**THE GUARD IS PROBABLY A CULLING SHORTCUT** - there is no point testing a car far up the
road behind you - but 900 units is about a fifth of a second at chase speed, which is far
tighter than the distance at which a cruiser is still visible in the mirror and still part
of the chase.

**WHAT DECIDES IT IS A JUDGMENT, NOT A MEASUREMENT.** Opening the guard to the whole
mirrored road would make pursuits materially easier - cruisers behind you would start
destroying themselves on the traffic you are both driving through, which is what the owner
reported as a fault when it happens in front of them. Whether that reads as the police being
stupid or as the player's best weapon working properly is a taste question. Ask before
building.

**IT WAS REMOVED RATHER THAN MOVED BECAUSE IT WAS THE ONLY ONE OF ITS KIND.** A rival racer is
tested against traffic with no reference to the player at all - `road.js:14650`, `hurtRival(r, 26)`
- and so is the player's own car. The cruiser was the single body on the road carrying a positional
exemption from traffic damage. The owner has ruled that everyone takes damage including the player,
which makes the exemption the odd thing rather than the opening of it.

**AND IT COSTS NOTHING.** The traffic array is culled at 34,000 units behind, so a cruiser further
back than that has nothing to be tested against and the loop finds none - which is all the guard was
buying.

## THE PROOF, AND THE FIRST VERSION OF IT WAS WRONG

`tools/coptraffic-test.py`. It drives the way that used to prove nothing - straight, at a held
speed, which is exactly what leaves a cruiser behind the guard - and asserts what the guard forbade.

**IT COUNTS HITS AND NOT KILLS, WHICH IS THE CORRECTION THAT MADE IT WORK.** The first version asked
whether a cruiser was WRECKED by traffic while behind the guard. It read zero on the fixed build and
zero on the broken one, because a cruiser needs several hits to go out and the one that finally
kills it usually lands after it has closed back onto the player. **A check that reads the same on
both builds is measuring the wrong thing, and it took a baseline run to see it** - the fixed build
reported 4 traffic kills against the broken build's 2, which is noise on a road generated fresh per
load.

    WITH THE GUARD REMOVED   6 traffic hits in the band, over 90 seconds
    WITH THE GUARD RESTORED  0 traffic hits in the band, over 90 seconds

Both runs had the opportunity: 11,491 and 10,779 cruiser-frames were spent more than 900 units
behind the player. **The precondition is its own check** - if no cruiser is ever in the band the run
is BLKD rather than passed, because that is the shape a check passes in while the defect is present.

**AND IT SEPARATES TRAFFIC FROM EVERYTHING ELSE THAT HURTS A CRUISER.** Damage is only counted while
the car is inside the road, which excludes the barrier, and only when the rise is exactly 45, which
is the traffic value - a 60 appeared in the band on the first run and it was a ROADBLOCK, which also
hurts from inside the road and which the barrier test alone could not separate.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE

**THE OWNER SAID THE POLICE WERE "CONSTANTLY CRASHING INTO TRAFFIC AND DESTROYING THEMSELVES" AND
SIX HITS IN NINETY SECONDS IS NOT CONSTANT.** The guard is why no harness could reproduce it, and
opening it is what the ruling asked for - but whether the rate now reads as the police being stupid,
as the player's best weapon working properly, or as still too rare is a judgment on a device and
nobody has made it. `hurtCop(k, 45)` against a cruiser's health is the single tunable if it is
wrong.
