### RLG-242 — At four stars a roadblock is a slalom of two or three, with openings on alternating sides
- **RULED 2026-09-13T15:13:09-04:00** — **REQUESTED AND BUILT 2026-09-13, v0.14.16. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]). Owner: *"I also want the roadblocks to become more problematic at 4 stars. What I mean is I want roadblocks to then be 2-3 roadblocks that need to be 'slalomed'."*

## WHAT CHANGED

`spawnRoadblock` is now the event and `placeRoadblock` builds one block, unchanged from the old code. Below `SLALOM.stars` (4) an event is one block, exactly as before. At four stars or more it is a chain of `min` to `max` (2 to 3) blocks, `gapZ` (9,000 units) apart, with each opening put `side` (0.58) off the centre line and the next one on the other side. So a car that threads the first block is lined up wrong for the second.

- One `ROADBLOCK AHEAD` warning per event, not one per block.
- The straightness check now also covers where the last block of a chain would stand (`roadblockReach`), because a block round a bend is a wall you cannot see.
- Each block still pays and costs what one block did: running one adds `HEAT_BLOCK`, threading one repairs 25 per cent and adds 25 per cent NOS, and striking one does 28 damage and cuts speed to 30 per cent. **A three-block chain threaded clean therefore pays three times.** That was not ruled on.

## THE PROOF

`tools/slalom-test.py`, new. It reads the openings off the panels (`API.roadblockOpenings`) rather than off `gapX`, and the game's own count of blocks threaded and struck (`API.slalom`). EVD-766:

    ok    below four stars a roadblock is still one block   sizes seen [1]
    ok    at four stars it is a chain of two or three, both sizes seen   sizes seen [2, 3]
    ok    the openings alternate sides, measured off the panels
    ok    the blocks are 9000 units apart
    ok    a driver who weaves threads a three-block chain at 150mph   threaded 3, struck 0
    ok    a driver who holds the first opening strikes a later block   threaded 2, struck 1

`--falsify` moves the chain to 99 stars, and exactly the chain, alternation and spacing checks fail (EVD-767). roadblock-test (EVD-768) and smoke (EVD-769) pass.

## WHAT THE MEASUREMENT SAYS ABOUT DIFFICULTY

The weaving driver also threaded all three blocks at 200 mph and at 250 mph. **The harness steers instantly and perfectly**, so this says the slalom is possible at any speed. It does not say it is hard for a thumb. `gapZ` is the one tunable that sets the difficulty, and only the owner on the phone can judge it.
