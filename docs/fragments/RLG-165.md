### RLG-165 — A bridge is a sharp ramp up, a flat deck, and a sharp ramp down

Owner, 2026-09-07: "the shape of getting onto a bridge is still wrong. It should be a
sharp ramp up for maybe a quarter of a mile, then absolutely flat for the duration of the
bridge, and then for the quarter mile before transitioning into the next biome, the sharp
ramp down. I haven't seen the tunnel so I will keep testing, but I imagine it has the same
problem. It needs the same shape, but inverted."

THREE SEGMENTS, NOT A CURVE: a sharp ramp of about a quarter of a mile, a dead flat deck
for the length of the crossing, and a sharp ramp down over the quarter mile before the
next place. The tunnel is the same shape upside down - down, flat, up.

AND THE TUNNEL IS EXPENDABLE. Owner, same message and not the first time: "if we can't get
the tunnel looking right, I have no problem just removing it from the game." The bridge is
not - "the bridge looks pretty good apart from the shape issue".
