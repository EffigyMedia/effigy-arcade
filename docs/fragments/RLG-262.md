### RLG-262 — An Interceptor is earned by three stars and a pass over 170 past a trap or a patrol, with no speed held
- **RULED 2026-09-15T21:06:31-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15: "150 held shouldn't be a condition. Simply having at least three stars and passing a patrol or a speed trap over 170 is the trigger."

WHAT IS BUILT TODAY (code read, 0.14.36). `superWatch` sends an Interceptor when `heat >= 3 && supersEarned && fastFor > SUPER_HOLD`, where `fastFor` counts the seconds above SUPER_MPH (150) and SUPER_HOLD is 4. `supersEarned` is set in ONE place: the player branch of `trapWatch`, when a trap catches the player over 170 (RLG-030). A patrol pass sets nothing.

WHAT THE RULING CHANGES, in the next unit:
1. The held-speed condition goes: `fastFor`, SUPER_MPH and SUPER_HOLD leave the dispatch test. `API.pursuit` reports `fastFor`, `superMph` and `superHold`; whatever stays must still report truthfully.
2. `patrolWatch` sets `supersEarned` when the player passes a patrol over 170, as a trap does. It must be set on the pass itself, not on the engagement, because a patrol with no open slot does not engage (RLG-257) and the owner said "passing a patrol".
3. The dispatches were staggered by setting `fastFor = 2.2` after each one. With `fastFor` out of the test, the stagger needs its own timer, or two Interceptors arrive in one frame. SUPER_SLOTS (2) still caps them per car (RLG-256).
4. tools/super-gate-proof.py and heat-test read this gate and will need the same change.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T21:31:19-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.37.
1. THE HELD SPEED IS OUT OF THE TEST. `superWatch` asked for `fastFor > SUPER_HOLD` - four unbroken seconds above SUPER_MPH (150) - on top of the stars and the banked pass. It now asks for `heat >= 3 && supersEarned` only. `fastFor`, SUPER_MPH and SUPER_HOLD are kept because `API.pursuit` reports them and harnesses read them; nothing decides on them.
2. THE STAGGER HAS ITS OWN TIMER. It rode on `fastFor = 2.2` after each dispatch, which did nothing once `fastFor` left the test. `SUPER_STAGGER` (2.2 s) and `nextSuperT` replace it, so the two Interceptors do not arrive in one frame.
3. A PATROL EARNS ONE TOO. `patrolWatch` sets `supersEarned` when the player passes it over 170. It is set on the PASS, before the slot check, because a patrol with no open slot does not engage (RLG-257) and the owner named the pass.

PROOF. tools/super-trigger-test.py replaces tools/super-gate-proof.py, which measured the condition this ruling removes. At three stars it drives past a trap at 176 mph, drops to 100 mph - under the old gate - and an Interceptor still arrives in half a second; the same with a patrol, which banked nothing before. At two stars with the pass banked, none arrives. The control holds 168 mph at three stars, over the limit and under the 170, and none arrives. Against 95658b69 it fails exactly three checks: the arrival without the held speed, and both patrol checks.

THE CONTROL IS DRIVEN UNDER THE THRESHOLD RATHER THAN WITH THE BANK HELD EMPTY. The road lays traps of its own, and a pass at 190 mph banks the pass and dispatches in the SAME frame - `trapWatch` runs immediately before `superWatch` - so a harness clearing `supersEarned` every frame cannot hold it clean. Measured: an Interceptor at 6.1 s on a run doing exactly that.

heat-test's precondition changed with the rule. It asserted that the car had banked four seconds above 150, which was the flaky check in this file; it now asserts five stars with nothing banked, which is what the rule reads. interceptor-seen, wanted-stars and smoke pass.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T21:36:36-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, after 0.14.37 shipped: "One modification to the interceptor: three stars allows one interceptor on you, four stars allows two. Apart from that the triggers are the same."

NOT BUILT - the clear fell due as this was starting, and the two-line change was rolled back rather than left uncommitted. What it is: `superWatch` computes `want = Math.min(SUPER_SLOTS, Math.ceil(heat / 1.5))`, and `Math.ceil(3 / 1.5)` is 2, so three stars already allows two. It becomes one below four stars and SUPER_SLOTS (2) at four and above. The trigger itself does not change. tools/super-trigger-test.py needs an arm for each: at three stars one arrives and a second does not, and at four stars two do.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T21:49:39-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.38. `superWatch` asked for `Math.min(SUPER_SLOTS, Math.ceil(heat / 1.5))`, and `Math.ceil(3 / 1.5)` is 2, so three stars already allowed two. It now asks `heat >= SUPER_TWO_AT ? SUPER_SLOTS : 1`, with SUPER_TWO_AT 4 and SUPER_SLOTS 2. The trigger itself is unchanged.

PROOF. tools/super-trigger-test.py gains a level for each: at three stars ONE Interceptor is out after twelve seconds, at four stars two, sent 2.2 seconds apart. Measured on 0.14.38: three stars sent one at 0.1 s and no second in twelve; four stars sent them at 0.1 and 2.3. Against 558ac7fb three stars sent two, at 0.1 and 2.3, and the check fails there. heat-test and smoke pass.

TWO TEST FAULTS FOUND WITH IT, both in how the arms were read rather than in the engine.
1. THE COUNT. The arms counted Interceptors that arrived AFTER the pass, and one is always sent on the frame the 170 is banked, during the pass. So three stars read as "none arrived" for a build that had correctly sent one. The arms now count what is on the road.
2. THE CLOCK. The stagger was timed from the end of the pass, which put the second Interceptor 0.5 s after a zero that was 1.7 s late. One clock now runs from the start of the arm, and the stagger measures 2.2 s.
