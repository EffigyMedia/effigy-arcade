### RLG-257 — Remove the police radio; only speed traps and patrol cars add cruisers, and both obey the slots
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:14:38-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, the full answer this fragment comes from: "I want to get rid of the radio only patrols and speed traps can add to your engaged cruisers, they all obey the same slot allotment. It still seems like police cruisers, and possibly also interceptors do not collide with each other. They need to have their own navigation behavior so that they avoid collisions with traffic and each each other intelligently. We are also missing the proper functionality for them to slow you down if a cruiser or interceptor is directly in front of you. It should force your car slowly to a stop and it's up to the player or other racers NPC's cause they should all function the same to quickly swing to the side to get out from behind their slow down triggering zone so you can speed up and go around them. Another thing I noticed was that the roadblocks disappeared in the mirror as you passed them, which tells me they are despond too quickly or stop being rendered or whatever."

THIS FRAGMENT IS THE FIRST SENTENCE. The radio (`spawnCop` with from 'radio', dispatched every 12-22 s while a cruiser is on the player, capped by `dispatchCap`) goes. A woken patrol engages only into an open slot, as a trap does since RLG-256. Asked before this: the owner asked what the radio is; it was explained from the code. The shift wingmen (RLG-203) also use `spawnCop`; they are not the radio and are not named in the ruling.
