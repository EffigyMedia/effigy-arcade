### RLG-257 — Remove the police radio; only speed traps and patrol cars add cruisers, and both obey the slots
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:14:38-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, the full answer this fragment comes from: "I want to get rid of the radio only patrols and speed traps can add to your engaged cruisers, they all obey the same slot allotment. It still seems like police cruisers, and possibly also interceptors do not collide with each other. They need to have their own navigation behavior so that they avoid collisions with traffic and each each other intelligently. We are also missing the proper functionality for them to slow you down if a cruiser or interceptor is directly in front of you. It should force your car slowly to a stop and it's up to the player or other racers NPC's cause they should all function the same to quickly swing to the side to get out from behind their slow down triggering zone so you can speed up and go around them. Another thing I noticed was that the roadblocks disappeared in the mirror as you passed them, which tells me they are despond too quickly or stop being rendered or whatever."

THIS FRAGMENT IS THE FIRST SENTENCE. The radio (`spawnCop` with from 'radio', dispatched every 12-22 s while a cruiser is on the player, capped by `dispatchCap`) goes. A woken patrol engages only into an open slot, as a trap does since RLG-256. Asked before this: the owner asked what the radio is; it was explained from the code. The shift wingmen (RLG-203) also use `spawnCop`; they are not the radio and are not named in the ruling.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T19:58:04-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.35.
1. THE RADIO IS GONE. The Hot Pursuit branch that called `spawnCop` every 12-22 seconds while a cruiser was on the player, capped by `dispatchCap`, is removed. A wanted level now buys police through the slots (RLG-256).
2. A PATROL OBEYS THE SLOTS. `patrolWatch` reads `slotOpen(null)` before it wakes a patrol. With no open slot it adds the sighting heat and the patrol stays in the traffic; `passed` fires once per car, so it cannot report the player twice. A woken patrol is now COMMITTED to the player (`tgt`, `onPlayer`, `engaged`), as a trap is since RLG-256, so it fills the player's slot and no other car's.
3. INTERCEPT IS UNCHANGED. The shift wingmen use `spawnCop` through their own floor rule (RLG-203) and are not the radio. The owner confirmed on 2026-09-15: "The radio functionality might still be required for the intercept mode where the player is the cops."
4. THE INTERCEPTORS DO NOT USE THE RADIO EITHER. `superWatch` dispatches them on heat 3, a pass over 170, and 150 mph held, and it is untouched. The owner asked about this: "the only reason to keep radio would be to deploy interceptors when their condition conditions are met."

PROOF. tools/ladder-test.py is rewritten: it used to prove the radio's ladder climbed, and now proves the radio is gone. At heat 3 and heat 5, with a cruiser committed to the player 9,000 back and the player held at 72 % - faster than a cruiser, so the chase cannot end the run - it watches 25 seconds a level. `copCensus().sent` stays 0 and no car from 'radio' appears, while traps and patrols still put five cruisers on the player in the same window. Against a73f61ce the radio sent 1 at heat 3 and 2 at heat 5, so the check fails there. tools/trap-slots-test.py gains the patrol arms: with a slot open a passed patrol engages; with it full the patrol stays in the traffic and still adds one sighting.

A TEST FAULT FOUND AND FIXED WITH IT. trap-slots-test read the trap's state at the END of its window, and a cruiser that wrecks the NPC it caught loses it, gives up and parks again, which read as "did not engage". A probe of ten runs showed the trap engaging on the first frame every time. The arms now record the first frame the trap leaves its post. Three runs are stable.

heat, shift-stop, lawless, bust, wanted-stars, heatpoints, coptraffic, pursuit-stop and smoke pass. duty-test fails as it did before this session. commit-test and patrol-test failed while three browsers ran at once and pass alone.
