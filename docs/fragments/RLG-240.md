### RLG-240 — The run clock is shown as a fuel gauge, in two designs for the owner to choose between
- **RULED 2026-09-13T13:41:17-04:00** — **REQUESTED AND RULED 2026-09-13.** Owner: *"I like time to be represented by a fuel gauge (in addition to the actual UI numbers countdown)."* Asked what full means, the owner chose **full at the start allowance**, pinned at full while the clock holds more. Asked where it goes: *"I would prefer it to be inside the speedometer, but if it's too cluttered, then I would rather it be its own third dial so let's design both and then pick the best."*

**BOTH DESIGNS ARE BUILT, v0.14.13, and the owner has NOT chosen. NOT PUSHED.** `FUEL_GAUGE.style` switches between them and defaults to `'speedo'`, the owner's stated preference. Whichever is not chosen is to be deleted, with the switch.

| Design | What it is |
|---|---|
| `'speedo'` (A) | An arc in the empty sector at the bottom of the speedometer face, between 7 and 5 o'clock, filling from E toward F in the same groove radius as the red zone. |
| `'dial'` (B) | A third, smaller dial left of the tachometer. A half turn, E at 9 o'clock to F at 3 o'clock, so it reads differently from the two sweeping dials beside it, with a pump symbol under the pivot. The gauge canvas and its `#dials` box widen from 115 to 160 to the left, so the other two dials do not move. |

Both read `clock / CLOCK_START` clamped to 0..1. Both carry a red reserve section and turn red inside it. `FUEL_GAUGE.reserve` is 20 seconds, a starting value the owner has not ruled on. Both appear only while `clockRuns()`: on a test drive with the timer off, time is not a currency, which is RLG-125's reasoning about the crate.

**FOUND WHILE PHOTOGRAPHING IT:** the first capture of design B pushed the speedometer off the right edge of the screen. `#dials` is 115 px wide in the stylesheet, so a wider canvas overflowed to the right. The box is resized with the canvas now.

**THE PROOF IS PHOTOGRAPHS, NOT A HARNESS, for now.** The owner is choosing between pictures, so both designs were captured in the engine at 140, 60 and 12 seconds on a 414-by-896 phone viewport and sent as one sheet. smoke-test passed with the gauge drawn. A harness that reads the gauge's pixels belongs to the unit that ships the chosen design.

**RELATED:** [[RLG-239]], the same day's request to split the crate into three pickups, calls the time pickup a fuel jerry can. If that is built, the jerry can refills this gauge.
- **RULED 2026-09-13T14:11:21-04:00** — **THE OWNER CHOSE, 2026-09-13, and asked for a different placement than either design.** *"I want the actual fuel gauge, but placed above and in between the other two gauges so they form a triangle of gauges. How does that interact with the other collapsible HUD elements? I want the fuel gauge pump indicator to turn YELLOW when under 1/8 tank."*

**BUILT, v0.14.15. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]). Design A, the arc in the speedometer, is deleted with `FUEL_GAUGE.style`. This unit was opened after its first edit to road.js; the rule is to open it before, and it was broken.

## THE TRIANGLE

The fuel dial (radius 19) sits centred between the two faces (radius 26, 55 apart) and 39 above their centres, which clears both by 2 px. The dial canvas grows from 59 to 87 tall, `FUEL_H` 28, and the `#dials` box grows with it.

## HOW IT MEETS THE COLLAPSIBLE HUD - the owner's question, answered from the stylesheet

The right-hand cluster stacks bottom-up: pedals, NOS bottle, shifter, dials. Every offset is measured from below. `--bottle` is 0 for a car without NOS and closes its gap ([[RLG-028]]), `body.manual` lifts the dials over the gate, and `body.pedals-left` mirrors the stack. **The dials are the top of the stack, so the triangle grows upward and nothing else moves.** Photographed in five layouts on a 414-by-896 viewport: automatic with NOS, manual with NOS, manual without NOS, automatic without NOS, and pedals left. The dial nests in every one.

## THE LAMP

Below `FUEL_GAUGE.low`, an eighth of a tank (15 seconds of 120), the pump symbol lights yellow (`#ffd23c`) with a small glow. Yellow is what a low-fuel lamp is in a car. The needle does not change colour, so there is one signal in one place. The last eighth of the groove is painted red.

## WHEN IT IS SHOWN

`fuelShown()` is `clockRuns() && !CFG.circuitOnly`. **Never on the circuit:** Motorsport has real fuel, burned by the throttle and refilled in the pit, so a dial reading its race clock would be a fuel gauge that is not about fuel. When the circuit wants one it can feed this dial its own fraction.

## THE PROOF

`tools/fuel-gauge-test.py`, new, reads the dial canvas's pixels and the box's position (EVD-762):

    ok    the pump is dim at a fifth of a tank   lamp pixel [114, 121, 137, 249]
    ok    the pump lights yellow below an eighth of a tank   lamp pixel [255, 210, 60, 255]
    ok    the fuel dial is painted while the clock counts   face alpha 246
    ok    and not with the timer off   face alpha 0
    ok    the cluster grows upward and its bottom edge stays put   height 63 to 92, bottom 785.6 to 785.6

`--falsify lamp` fails only the yellow check (EVD-763). `--falsify grow` fails only the grow check: the canvas hangs 29.7 px out of the bottom of its box (EVD-764). The harness's first sample point for "not with the timer off" sat inside the speedometer and failed on every build; it was moved before any step was recorded. smoke 6/6 (EVD-765).

**WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE:** whether a 19-radius dial is readable at a glance on the phone, and whether the yellow lamp is noticed at speed.
