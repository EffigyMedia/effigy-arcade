### RLG-240 — The run clock is shown as a fuel gauge, in two designs for the owner to choose between
- **RULED 2026-09-13T13:41:17-04:00** — **REQUESTED AND RULED 2026-09-13.** Owner: *"I like time to be represented by a fuel gauge (in addition to the actual UI numbers countdown)."* Asked what full means, the owner chose **full at the start allowance**, pinned at full while the clock holds more. Asked where it goes: *"I would prefer it to be inside the speedometer, but if it's too cluttered, then I would rather it be its own third dial so let's design both and then pick the best."*

**BOTH DESIGNS ARE BUILT, v0.14.13, and the owner has NOT chosen. NOT PUSHED.** `FUEL_GAUGE.style` switches between them and defaults to `'speedo'`, the owner's stated preference. Whichever is not chosen is to be deleted, with the switch.

| Design | What it is |
|---|---|
| `'speedo'` (A) | An arc in the empty sector at the bottom of the speedometer face, between 7 and 5 o'clock, filling from E toward F in the same groove radius as the red zone. |
| `'dial'` (B) | A third, smaller dial left of the tachometer. A half turn, E at 9 o'clock to F at 3 o'clock, so it reads differently from the two sweeping dials beside it, with a pump symbol under the pivot. The gauge canvas and its `#dials` box widen from 115 to 160 to the left, so the other two dials do not move. |

Both read `clock / CLOCK_START` clamped to 0..1. Both carry a red reserve section and turn red inside it. `FUEL_GAUGE.reserve` is 20 seconds, a starting value the owner has not ruled on. Both appear only while `clockRuns()`: on a test drive with the timer off, time is not a currency, which is RLG-125's reasoning about the crate.

**FOUND WHILE PHOTOGRAPHING IT:** the first capture of design B pushed the speedometer off the right edge of the screen. `#dials` is 115 px wide in the stylesheet, so a wider canvas overflowed to the right. The box is resized with the canvas now.

**THE PROOF IS PHOTOGRAPHS, NOT A HARNESS, for now.** The owner is choosing between pictures, so both designs were captured in the engine at 140, 60 and 12 seconds on a 414-by-896 phone viewport and sent as one sheet. smoke-test passed with the gauge drawn. A harness that reads the gauge's pixels belongs to the unit that ships the chosen design.

**RELATED:** [[RLG-239]], the same day's request to split the crate into three pickups, calls the time pickup a fuel jerry can. If that is built, the jerry can refills this gauge.
