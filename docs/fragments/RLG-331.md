### RLG-331 — Every mountain surface must share one colour source
- **RULED 2026-09-23T20:47:55-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-23, from the device: "The ground the mountain color and the scenery mountain colors the valley mountain colors all need to match so they they look like the same mountain valley we can differ the colors but they all need to match at the same time."

THE RULE IS NOT "ONE COLOUR", IT IS "ONE SOURCE". The owner is explicit that the colours may differ - what they may not do is differ INDEPENDENTLY. The ground beside the road, the scenery's mountains, the wall or face, and the range across the valley are four surfaces of one landscape, and a player reads them as one place only if they move together. Today they are picked in separate places, so a change to one leaves the others behind and the valley reads as several different rocks.

SO THE FIX IS A SEAM, NOT A PALETTE. One place decides the mountain's rock, and every surface asks it - shading, hazing and distance applied on top as each already does.

NOT STARTED.
- **RULED 2026-09-23T21:16:42-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-23.

A MOUNTAIN HAD THREE UNRELATED PALETTES AND THE WORST OF THEM WAS A DEFAULT NOBODY CHOSE.

  the ground and the wall   `grassLo`/`grassHi`         a green-grey
  the scenery's rock faces  `ROCK_TONE.rock`            a neutral grey
  the range across the valley  `skyBase`, UNDECLARED    '#150c22', A VIOLET

MEASURED OFF THE SCREEN BEFORE THE CHANGE: the range read rgb(69,66,85) against a ground of rgb(122,135,128). Not a shade apart - a different hue family, blue-dominant against green-dominant. ONLY FOUR PLACES DECLARE A `skyBase` AND MOUNTAIN IS NOT ONE OF THEM, so its valley silhouette came from a fallback that belongs to nothing in the scene.

THE PATTERN TO FOLLOW ALREADY EXISTED IN THE FILE. `SANDSTONE` carries its own note saying what it is for - "wall art, the ground the walls stand in, and the silhouette on the horizon" - and a CANYON reads all three from it, which is why a canyon has never had this fault. `GRANITE` is the same table for a mountain and the same three readers.

THE GROUND IS UNTOUCHED BY IT, which is the part worth keeping. `GRANITE.dark` and `GRANITE.mid` ARE the two colours MOUNTAIN already had, so the verge is the colour it has always been and the scenery and the range came to meet it rather than the other way about. After: the range reads rgb(77,86,90) against a wall of rgb(76,95,87) and a ground of rgb(112,121,116).

AND IT IS ONE SOURCE, NOT ONE COLOUR, which is what the owner asked for: "we can differ the colors but they all need to match at the same time". They still differ by distance, by light and by hour - the skyline still washes toward the sky's own haze with depth. They can no longer differ by accident.

NOTHING ELSE READS THE TABLE. `ROCK_TONE.rock` has exactly one caller, MOUNTAIN's scenery; TUNDRA takes 'snow' and DESERT takes 'sand' and neither moved.

smoke-test 7/7, tree-wall-test and skyline-test with no failures. biome-test failed once and passed twice on the same build - it is a timing-sensitive check and the failure did not reproduce.

WHAT IS STILL OPEN FROM THE SAME REPORT, and both want the owner's eye on the new build first: [[RLG-329]] the sheer cliff face, and [[RLG-330]] the range's base. THE BASE ALREADY READS BETTER FOR THIS CHANGE ALONE - the "hard cut-off mask" is a straight edge that was made obvious by the colour changing across it, and the colour no longer changes across it. Whether it still reads as a mask is a question for the device, not for a harness.
