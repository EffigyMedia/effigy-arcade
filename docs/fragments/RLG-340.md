### RLG-340 — Which vehicles appear by time of day and place, and how dense the traffic is
- **RULED 2026-09-24T12:09:07-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-24, THINKING ALOUD RATHER THAN RULING: "I'm also considering what vehicles are available at what time of day and what biome, this would include traffic density, but I don't want to affect the gameplay that much."

TRACKED, NOT STARTED, AND DELIBERATELY SO. "Considering" is not a ruling, and the second half - "I don't want to affect the gameplay that much" - is a constraint with no number in it yet. Building to a guess here would be expensive to undo, because traffic density is the single strongest lever on how the Interstate feels.

WHAT IS ALREADY TRUE, so the scope can be judged against it rather than imagined. Traffic already knows the place: `bioAt` decides what stands beside the road, and the fleet has work vehicles, civilians and police classes. What it does NOT do is vary WHICH vehicles appear by place or by hour, or how MANY.

THE THREE QUESTIONS THAT HAVE TO BE ANSWERED BEFORE A LINE IS WRITTEN:
1. WHICH AXIS MATTERS MOST - the mix of kinds, or the density? They are separate features with separate risks. A farm truck in a FARMLAND is scenery; twice as many cars at rush hour is a difficulty change.
2. HOW FAR MAY DENSITY MOVE? "Not that much" needs a band. Today's count is the baseline and a range around it - say plus or minus a quarter - is a different product from half to double.
3. IS THE HOUR A REAL FACTOR OR A LOOK? Fewer cars at midnight is a rule a player will feel and plan around; the same cars with their lamps lit is a look. The first changes the game, the second does not.

AND ONE CONSTRAINT THAT IS ALREADY SETTLED AND MUST NOT BE BROKEN HERE: ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR ([[RLG-042]], [[RLG-054]]). A place or an hour may decide WHICH vehicles are on the road. It may not make them faster, slower or grippier - the car is capable and the driver decides, and every attempt to branch on a vehicle class for behaviour has been ruled out three times.

DO NOT START THIS WITHOUT THE OWNER ANSWERING AT LEAST QUESTION 2.
