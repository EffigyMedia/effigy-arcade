### RLG-202 — Selecting a cruiser or a super cruiser
- **RULED 2026-09-10T03:22:13-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED, AND THE SCOPE IS NOT AGREED. Owner, 2026-09-10, listing it between
the crate rule and the police mode: "Selecting a cruiser or super cruiser."

**IT HAS AT LEAST THREE READINGS AND THEY ARE DIFFERENT SIZES.** It was said in the same breath as
the police mode below it, which is the reason to think it belongs to that mode rather than to the
garage:

    (1) THE GARAGE, AND THEN IT IS LARGELY DONE. Both are garage cars as of [[RLG-181]]: the CRUISER
    is paid by a sports gold under pursuit and the SUPERCRUISER by a supercar gold, each shows as a
    silhouette with its condition written under it until it is won, and each takes the force's white
    or black. If this is what was meant, what remains is whatever the owner finds missing when they
    stand in front of the two cards on a device.

    (2) THE POLICE MODE'S OWN CHOICE. Starting a shift as police means choosing which car you take
    out, and that is a different screen from the garage - the two force cars are the whole list, the
    interceptor is the faster one, and the choice is a difficulty setting as much as a car.

    (3) WITHOUT WINNING THEM. That the two police cars should be selectable in the police mode
    whether or not the tournament ladder has paid them out yet, because a mode about being police
    that will not let you be police is a locked door. This is a rule about unlocks and it contradicts
    (1) rather than extending it.

**ASK BEFORE BUILDING.** The wrong reading costs a screen. [[RLG-027]] is the precedent - a menu
polish ruling that has sat open because its scope was never agreed, and it is still open.
- **RULED 2026-09-10T03:29:58-04:00** — **THE OWNER ANSWERED, 2026-09-10, AND IT IS NONE OF THE THREE READINGS - IT IS TWO RULES.**
Owner: "if you have them unlocked, and you have them selected, one of the game mode choices would be
a mode where you play as a cop taking out racers before they finish a race. Unlike production and
utility vehicles, the cruiser and supercruiser are eligible for their class of race. You can use a
cruiser to do sports races and tournaments, and you can use a supercruiser to do super races and
tournaments. But in addition, they also get a police duty mode."

**RULE ONE - EACH POLICE CAR RACES ITS OWN CLASS.** The CRUISER runs the SPORTS ladder and the
SUPERCRUISER runs the SUPER ladder. They are not novelty vehicles: they are ordinary entrants that
happen to have a light bar, which is the opposite of the production and utility rule in
[[RLG-115]].

**RULE TWO - AND THEY GET A MODE THE OTHER CARS DO NOT.** Selecting one adds a fourth entry to the
mode control, alongside TEST DRIVE, SINGLE RACE and TOURNAMENT: the police duty mode of
[[RLG-203]]. The mode is not a separate machine and it is not chosen from a menu of its own - it is
a mode a CAR unlocks, exactly as the class gate in [[RLG-115]] REMOVES modes a car cannot enter.
The same control, and the same shape, working in the other direction.

**AND RULE ONE IS NOT WHAT THE CODE DOES TODAY, which nothing had noticed because nobody could
select these cars until [[RLG-181]] shipped this morning.** There are TWO class systems in this
file and they do not agree:

    `BODY_CLASS` is the UNLOCK class - `cruiser`, `supercruiser`, `super`, `formula`, `production`,
    `utility`. It decides what a gold pays and what a silhouette says.

    `classOf` is the RACE class, and it has three answers - `sports`, `super`, `formula`. It reads
    `SPORTS_BODIES` and `FORMULA_BODIES` as lists and FALLS THROUGH TO `super` for everything else.

So `classOf('CRUISER')` returns **super** today. A player in a patrol car is put on a grid of
STALLIONs, MATADORs and CRESTs, and runs the supercar ladder - and the car's own record says the
opposite in as many words: the SUPERCRUISER note reads *"It is the floor of the supercar class,
exactly as the CRUISER is the floor of the sports class."* The comment is right and the function is
wrong.

**WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THE WORK.** Rule one is a small, well-defined change to `classOf` - the
CRUISER answers `sports` and the SUPERCRUISER answers `super`. It is not a one-line change, because
two things downstream read the race class:

    `racerBodies` builds the grid from it, so a cruiser would face ROADSTERs, TUNERs and MUSCLEs.
    That is the intent - a police car in the sports league - and it is worth watching, because a
    CRUISER at 142mph against MUSCLE at 184mph is a different race from the one the sports ladder
    was tuned for. The SUPERCRUISER is the easier half: at 190mph it is the floor of a class whose
    ceiling is 194.

    `tourScore`'s finish branch reads `cls` to decide which police car a gold pays -
    `sports ? 'CRUISER' : 'SUPERCRUISER'`. Win the sports ladder IN a cruiser and it would offer
    the cruiser you are already driving. The screen already handles a prize the player owns, and
    that path has never been reachable before, so it needs a read rather than an assumption.

**IT IS BUILDABLE ON ITS OWN**, without the mode and without the mode's name.
- **RULED 2026-09-10T03:30:35-04:00** — **AND THE PARAGRAPH ABOVE IS A CODE READ, NOT A MEASUREMENT.** `classOf` and `racerBodies` were read in the source; no harness was run against a cruiser on a grid, because nothing in the engine's API reports which BODIES the rivals were built from - `rivalState` gives their distance and speed only. The read is not in doubt (`classOf` returns 'sports' only for the three names in `SPORTS_BODIES` and falls through to 'super' for everything that is not formula), but this project has been wrong about a code read before - [[RLG-042]]'s vmax column was true while three comments around it were false. Whoever builds rule one should watch the grid change rather than trust this.
