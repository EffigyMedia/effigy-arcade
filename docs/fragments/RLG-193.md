### RLG-193 — The pickup's cab is shaded at the end it is furthest from
- **RULED 2026-09-09T19:05:20-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"the pickup's cab should be shaded slightly dark from the back since it's
further from the camera than from the front perspective."*

**IT IS THE RULE THE FORMULA WINGS ALREADY TAKE** ([[RLG-185]]), applied to the one other vehicle in
the fleet whose two ends are made of DIFFERENT PARTS rather than of the same parts seen from opposite
sides. A pickup is a cab at one end and a bed at the other. From behind you are looking at the bed
with the cab beyond it; from the front it is the cab you are standing at. Both ends drew the cab in
the body's own gradient, so neither view had a far end.

**THE BED IS DELIBERATELY UNTOUCHED, and that is the half that makes it work.** The bed is the near
end at the rear view and it should stay the brightest thing there. A cab darkened against an equally
bright bed reads as depth; darkening both would only be a darker truck.

`shade(..., 0.74)` on all three stops of the cab's gradient, so the cab keeps its own form and its own
highlight and simply sits further away. The face is unchanged.

**AND IT IS THE SECOND HALF OF A PRINCIPLE WORTH STATING PLAINLY.** Two ends of a vehicle share one
outline ([[RLG-184]]); what tells the player which end they are looking at is the DETAIL inside it,
and depth is one of those details. Any vehicle whose two ends are different parts - so far the
formula cars and the pickup - should say which part is nearer.
