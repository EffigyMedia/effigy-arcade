### RLG-246 — Every car in the garage is drawn at its true relative size, not normalized
- **RULED 2026-09-14T21:09:57-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED.** Owner, 2026-09-14: *"All the cars shown in the garage need to be the same relative size. Do not normalize the sizes."*

The garage card fits each car to the card, so a small car and a large one are drawn at about the same size. The owner wants one scale for the whole fleet, so that a car's size on the card shows its real size against the others.

**Read first:** [[RLG-210]] (the card shows one end, larger and centred) and [[RLG-182]] (the fit by width and height). The fit in `drawGarageCar` and `carEnds` is what normalizes. A capture on 2026-09-14 shows the locked STALLION drawn wider than the owned COUPE, which is what a per-car fit does.

**Open, for whoever builds it:** which car sets the scale. The largest body the garage lists has to fit the card at that one scale, so the smallest will be drawn small.
