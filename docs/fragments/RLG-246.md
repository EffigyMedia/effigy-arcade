### RLG-246 — Every car in the garage is drawn at its true relative size, not normalized
- **RULED 2026-09-14T21:09:57-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED.** Owner, 2026-09-14: *"All the cars shown in the garage need to be the same relative size. Do not normalize the sizes."*

The garage card fits each car to the card, so a small car and a large one are drawn at about the same size. The owner wants one scale for the whole fleet, so that a car's size on the card shows its real size against the others.

**Read first:** [[RLG-210]] (the card shows one end, larger and centred) and [[RLG-182]] (the fit by width and height). The fit in `drawGarageCar` and `carEnds` is what normalizes. A capture on 2026-09-14 shows the locked STALLION drawn wider than the owned COUPE, which is what a per-car fit does.

**Open, for whoever builds it:** which car sets the scale. The largest body the garage lists has to fit the card at that one scale, so the smallest will be drawn small.
- **RULED 2026-09-14T21:41:05-04:00** — **BUILT 2026-09-14, v0.14.23. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]).

## WHAT "TRUE RELATIVE SIZE" MEANS HERE

The road. Every player car's sprite canvas is drawn one car wide (`PLAYER_W`) on the road, whatever the body, and the mirror draws a face across the same width. So a sprite pixel is worth `1 / img.width` of a car width at both ends. The card now uses that scale: `garageUnit()` is one car width in card pixels, the same for every car, and each end is drawn at `garageUnit() / img.width`. The per-car fit in `garageFit` stays as the fallback if the unit cannot be measured, and `sc` is still reported for the harnesses that read it.

- **The scale is the largest that fits the fleet.** Every car's widest end fits the card's width, and every ordinary car's taller end fits the ordinary card's depth. Oversized bodies (`big`, [[RLG-087]]) are left out of the depth bound, because they already get the taller card.
- **The floor is the card's.** A shorter car now stands on the same line as the tallest, at `CARD_H - 6`, instead of hanging from the top. `carEnds` reports the same floor.
- **Flipping does not resize a car.** Both ends use the same car width. A winged car is taller from behind, which is true.

## THE COST, FOR THE OWNER TO JUDGE

**The PICKUP sets the scale** (ink 158 px tall in its 200 px canvas), so one car width is about 188 card pixels, and ordinary cars are drawn 176 to 188 px wide. Under the old per-car fit they were drawn up to about 276 px. **The card height did not change.** A taller card would make every car larger and push the menu down. That is the owner's call, and it is not built.

Measured across the garage at the shared scale: ROADSTER, TUNER, MUSCLE, CRUISER, COUPE, HATCH and SALOON are 176 px wide on the card; VECTOR, APEX and COMET 184; STALLION, MATADOR, SUPERCRUISER and CREST 188. By height, SALOON is 129 px and the formula cars 85.

## THE PROOF

`tools/garage-scale-test.py`, new. For every ordinary garage car it reads the card's painted width and computes `card width x spriteW / inkW`, the card width of one whole canvas. `carEnds` now reports `spriteW` for this.
