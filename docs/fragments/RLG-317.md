### RLG-317 — A skyline hands over by masking one place against the next, and should move its props aside instead
- **RULED 2026-09-21T19:22:30-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21: 'I finally figured out what's been bothering me about when the biomes change and the skyline does the thing that it does to transition from one biome to another. What you do is simply mask away, one with the other, and it reads completely artificial. What we need is for the skyline elements to be standalone props that move off the side and grow in from the center not just a mask. You know what I'm saying?'

THAT IS AN ACCURATE READING OF [[RLG-252]]. The horizon is three regions measured out from the vanishing point - the place ahead in the centre, the place you are in either side, the place you left on the outer edges - and each place's band is PAINTED INTO ITS OWN REGION THROUGH A CLIP. The band itself does not move or change; the clip decides how much of it you see. So a boundary between two places on the horizon is a vertical cut through two skylines, sliding sideways. A cut is a mask, and it reads as one.

WHAT IS ASKED FOR INSTEAD: the skyline as INDIVIDUAL PROPS - a peak, a tower, a tree - each an object that belongs to a place. The place ahead's props GROW IN from the centre; the place you are leaving's props MOVE OFF to the sides. Nothing is cut; things arrive and depart.

WHAT THIS TOUCHES, read once. The skyline is built as ONE SPRITE PER PLACE - a tiled band with its plan fixed per place (RLG-094) and its colour rebuilt per hour bucket (RLG-080). The continuous forms (the canyon's ridge, the jungle's canopy) are random walks across the band on purpose (RLG-104, RLG-113), so they are not made of separable objects at all. A prop model is a different construction: a list of placed shapes per place, each drawn with its own position and scale. The free-standing forms - towers, peaks, trees, mesas - are already planned as individual objects inside the sprite builder; the continuous ones would need an answer of their own, and 'the jungle skyline is actually perfect the way it is' (owner, same day) is a constraint on that answer.

This is a redesign of the horizon, not a fix, and it is the owner's to schedule.
