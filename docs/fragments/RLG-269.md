### RLG-269 — A trap is silent and dark until it engages, and then pulls out under its own physics
- **RULED 2026-09-16T00:58:13-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "Speed traps have their sirens on. They should be silent with no emergency lights unless they engage somebody. When they do engage it looks like they just pop onto the middle of the road. They need to accelerate and pull onto the road like normal. It also seems like they immediately accelerate up to the player. They need to use proper physics like any other car once they decide to engage and leave their parked position."

THREE FAULTS IN ONE REPORT.
1. THE SIREN. 0.14.28 made a parked trap DARK (RLG-253) and did nothing about the sound. The siren is mixed from `copNear`, which counts cruisers by distance and does not ask whether the nearest one is parked, so a trap the player drives past wails.
2. THE PULL-OUT. `trapEngage` clears `trap` and `armed` and leaves the car where it is - on the verge at x = side * 1.16 - and the ordinary chase steering then walks it onto the road. The owner sees it appear in the middle of the road rather than pulling out of a lay-by.
3. THE PHYSICS. The trap is handed `spd = spd * 0.95 + 1800` at the moment it engages (RLG-247 chose that figure so a cruiser entering from behind is not instantly lost). That is a standing start replaced by a speed, which is what "immediately accelerate up to the player" describes. A cruiser leaving a parked position should accelerate through `aiAccel` like every other car, from zero.

THE THIRD ONE FIGHTS AN EARLIER RULING and that has to be settled when it is built: RLG-247 gave the trap that figure because a cruiser starting honestly from rest cannot catch a player at speed and is culled at 34,000 behind. Either the trap keeps a realistic launch and accepts that a fast player is gone, or something else closes the gap - a dispatch further up the road, or the cruiser catching up only while the player is slowed.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T01:00:28-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.45, part one of three. The siren's loudness came from one loop over `cops` that asked only how far the nearest car was. It now skips any car that is not lit, which is the same test the painter uses (`copBarLit`), so a parked trap is silent as well as dark.

PROOF. tools/trap-silent-test.py reads the live held siren voice through `API.sirenNow` - the oscillator, filter and gain themselves, not the code that set them (RLG-065). A trap held 1,200 ahead, inside the 7,000 the siren listens over: gain 0 across 166 frames. The same car with `trap` cleared: 0.0399. Against 4bf77fe8 the parked arm fails.

OWNER, 2026-09-16, immediately after: "It might also be for nonemergency patrol cruisers too."

CHECKED, AND THEY WERE ALREADY SILENT. The traffic siren loop counts only `c.emergency`, which is an ambulance on a call; a patrol car in the traffic carries `patrol` and never sets `emergency`, so it has never sounded. Its light bar is not drawn lit either - the traffic painter lights a bar only for `emergency`.

WHAT A PATROL DOES STILL SHOW is the bar PAINTED INTO ITS SPRITE: solid blue on one side and solid red on the other, which is the unlit colour pair and still reads as lit on a phone at distance. That was measured on 2026-09-15 while the rogue cruiser was being hunted (the capture bar_compare.png put an unlit patrol beside a lit cruiser). Not changed here: darkening the unlit pair is a change to every police sprite in the game and wants the owner's eye on a capture, not a harness.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T01:36:16-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.47, parts two and three, and the owner settled the conflict with RLG-247 first: "Well, then it needs to get rebalanced, because when I passed, it was instantly passing me, and then in front of me."

1. NO LAUNCH SPEED. `trapEngage` handed the car `spd * 0.95 + 1800` - above the player's own speed - on the frame it left the verge. It now leaves at rest and `aiAccel` does the rest, which is the standing rule that one physics serves every car (RLG-042). The cost, which is the owner's to weigh on the device: a trap you pass at 190 will not catch you, and the pressure has to come from the patrols and the Interceptors.

2. AND THE BOX CANNOT TELEPORT IT EITHER. Removing the launch speed was not enough - measured, a trap that left the verge at rest was at 0.79 of the player's pace on the NEXT frame, because the box station ASSIGNS a speed built from the player's. `TRAP_LAUNCH` (2.5 s) is set when a trap leaves its post; while it runs the car accelerates through its own gearbox, and after it the station works exactly as before.

PUTTING THE WHOLE STATION THROUGH `aiAccel` WAS TRIED AND REVERTED, and the measurement is why. It is the tidier rule, and it broke the stop: the cruiser in front could no longer shed speed at PURSUIT_STOP.decel, so a player dragged down by RLG-259's zone drove into the back of it - pursuit-stop-test read 'none' and then 'WRECKED' where it expects BUSTED, on two runs. The gate is on the launch only.

PROOF. tools/trap-pullout-test.py drives past a trap at 90 %: it engages at 0.147 of the player's speed - one frame of acceleration from rest - still 1.141 from the centre, then reaches 4,724 of speed and comes to 0.009 from the centre, and never gets past the player. Against da24006d it engages at 0.789 and that arm fails. Two arms had to be corrected while it was written: the rest check samples the frame AFTER the engagement, so a fifth rather than nothing is the bar; and the pull-out check reads the CLOSEST the car came to the centre, because a cruiser that gives up parks on the verge again and the last reading is 1.15 for a car that crossed the whole road.

bust, pursuit-stop, cop-cop, trap-slots, trap-silent and smoke pass. stop-zone's INTERCEPT rival arm failed once and passed on the rerun; its staging is noisy, as recorded when it was written.
