### RLG-256 — A speed trap engages a car only if that car has an open engagement slot for its heat level
- **RULED 2026-09-15T09:34:05-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15: "We want speed traps to only actively pull out and engage if the car in question has open engagement slots per its heat level, otherwise it just adds to its heat level and waits for another car with open slots. This means racers in the lead don't pull ALL the traps."

WHAT EXISTS TODAY (code read, 0.14.32):
1. Only the player has a heat level (`heat`, `heatPts`). Racers and speeding NPCs have none. On shift, `fieldHeat` keeps one level for the whole field, not one per car.
2. The only slot table is `dispatchCap()`: 0 cruisers below 2 stars, then heat - 1, capped at 3. It limits the radio's cruisers on the player and does not count trap or patrol cruisers.
3. `trapWatch` engages the player when the player passes over the limit, and engages the first speeding NPC within 2,200 units of a trap the NPC has passed. Nothing counts how many cruisers are already on that car.
4. A cruiser records its target in `k.tgt` (null for the player) and `k.onPlayer`.

OPEN QUESTIONS PUT TO THE OWNER before building: how many slots each heat level gives, and whether racers and NPCs get a heat level of their own.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T10:09:05-04:00** — OWNER'S ANSWERS, 2026-09-15: slots are "Stars+1, no max, and 2 slots for interceptors"; the Interceptors' 2 are separate from the cruisers' slots; every racer and NPC keeps its own heat.

BUILT IN 0.14.33.
1. HEAT PER CAR. A racer or traffic car carries `heatPts`. A trap sighting adds HEAT_SEEN (20), capped at HEAT_MAX. `coolCarHeat` cools it by the player's rule: nothing while a cruiser is committed to the car, then after COOL_GRACE (3 s) a star every HEAT_COOL (30 s). The player keeps `heatPts`. Only the player's stars are drawn.
2. SLOTS. `carSlots` = TRAP_SLOTS_BASE (1) + TRAP_SLOTS_PER_STAR (1) x stars, no ceiling beyond the 5-star heat cap. `cruisersOn` counts live, non-trap, non-Interceptor cruisers: for the player, any not working somebody else (`onPlayer !== false`); for another car, those with `tgt` that car.
3. THE TRAP. It clocks each car once (`k.clocked` for the player, `k.clockedCars` for others). It reads the slot BEFORE it adds the heat, so the pass cannot open the slot it asks about. With an open slot it engages and COMMITS to that car (`trapEngage`: `tgt`, `onPlayer`, `engaged`); before, it left uncommitted and its first search could take a different car. Without one it adds the heat, stays parked and waits for the next car. A pass over 170 still earns the Interceptors. Racers are now clocked: the loop read traffic only. The RLG-247 and RLG-157 gates are unchanged. A trap that gives up and parks clears both clocked records.
4. INTERCEPTORS. `superWatch` caps them at SUPER_SLOTS (2), down from 4.
5. `dbgTrapsOff` closes every slot the player has.

NOT CHANGED: radio dispatch (`dispatchCap`) and woken patrols do not read slots. The owner's rule named traps.

PROOF. tools/trap-slots-test.py stages each case with the road's own trap: player at 0 stars with 0 and 1 cruisers on, at 2 stars with 2 and 3 on; an NPC Speeder at 0 heat with 0 and 1 on and at 250 points with 2 on; and an NPC cooling from 150 to 140 in 6 s. Every engagement is checked for the car it committed to, and every refusal for the heat it still added (20). It passes, and fails seven checks against 658a9a62, where a trap engages the player with the slot full. heat, trap-switch, trap-post, pursuit-stop, bust, smoke, interceptor-seen, wanted-stars, heatpoints, ladder, radar, commit, coptraffic, race and lawless pass. interceptor-test's "DRIVE refuses" check fails the same way on 94017b4f. duty-test fails as recorded before this session. drive-test's top-speed settle check failed at 98.2 %, as before. Racers take the same `consider` path as NPCs, but no race was staged.
