### RLG-194 — A garage toggle puts the novelty vehicles away
- **RULED 2026-09-09T19:11:06-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"there needs to be a toggle that hides the unlocked production and utility
vehicles from the garage to prevent clutter since they are novelty vehicles."*

**THEY ARE A REWARD THAT MAKES THE GARAGE WORSE THE MOMENT IT IS COLLECTED.** Production and utility
are the game's two SECRET unlocks - twenty-five and fifty miles of test driving, absent from the
garage rather than greyed until they are earned ([[RLG-114]]). Winning them adds SEVEN cars the
player will rarely pick to a list of ten, and that cost is paid on every garage visit for the rest of
the save. Measured on a save with both won: ten cars listed before, seventeen after.

**IT HIDES, IT DOES NOT UN-WIN.** `optNovelty` is read in one place, at the end of `garageBodies`,
and it filters the LIST. The unlock flags are untouched, `carLocked` still says the cars are owned,
and turning it back on returns exactly the same seventeen. The harness checks the save flags after
the toggle for that reason.

**THE CONTROL IS ABSENT UNTIL THERE IS SOMETHING TO HIDE.** `noveltyOwned` asks whether any work
vehicle is actually listed, and the button is only rendered when one is. A switch for cars the player
has never seen would advertise a thing the game deliberately refuses to explain - which is the same
reasoning that keeps the cars themselves absent rather than greyed.

**IT NAMES WHAT IT SHOWS.** `WORK VEHICLES - SHOWN / HIDDEN`, so ON is the fuller garage, matching the
stripes control beside it, which also names what it adds.

**AND IT EXPOSED A REAL FAULT IN THE CAR-SWAP RULE, which the harness caught rather than a person.**
`enforceCarRules` moves the player off a car the garage will not list, and it moved them to `ks[0]` -
the first car in the list, which can be a LOCKED SILHOUETTE, because the garage lists cars still to be
won. A player who put the work vehicles away while sitting in the van landed on a card reading `???`
with a DRIVE button that refuses. It takes the first PLAYABLE car now and falls back to `ks[0]` only
if there is none. The toggle is what made that path reachable: before it, the only thing that removed
your car from the list was a circuit garage refusing a bus, which happens before the card is ever
drawn.

**PROVED BY `tools/novelty-test.py`, AND THE PROOF WAS FALSIFIED FIRST.** The easy check is that the
list gets shorter, and a toggle that emptied the garage would pass it - so every check is about WHICH
cars moved: exactly the work vehicles leave, every other car stays, and the order of what is left is
unchanged. With the one filter line removed the harness fails three of those checks; restored, it
passes twelve.
