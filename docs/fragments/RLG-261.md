### RLG-261 — Speed traps are laid far less often, on a random interval
- **RULED 2026-09-15T20:00:09-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15: "I don't remember if I said so, but we need to make the interval between speed traps much longer. They have way too frequently. It would never allow you to cool down. So let's make the interval way longer but also a random range so it's not consistently a specific interval."

ANSWERS THE SAME DAY: the interval is a random 30 to 60 seconds, and a higher wanted level still shortens it "a little" - each star by 10 %, to half the range at five stars.

WHAT IT WAS. `nextCopT = Math.max(2.0, rnd(5, 10) - heat*0.6)`: one trap every 5 to 10 seconds, down to 2 seconds at heat 5, while fewer than `TRAPS_BASE + heat/2` were parked. Measured on 0.14.27: eighteen traps came up the road in 90 seconds. A star sheds in 30 seconds of clean driving (HEAT_COOL), so a trap every five seconds is what the owner means by never being allowed to cool down.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T20:27:16-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.36. `TRAP_GAP` is [30, 60] seconds, `TRAP_GAP_HEAT` takes 0.10 of it per star and `TRAP_GAP_MIN` floors that share at 0.50, so five stars halve the range to 15-30 seconds. The old line was `Math.max(2.0, rnd(5, 10) - heat*0.6)`. The trap population cap (`TRAPS_BASE` + heat/2, capped at `TRAPS_MAX`) is unchanged; at this interval the road rarely reaches it.

PROOF. tools/trap-gap-test.py watches the road lay traps and times the gaps: 150 s clean, then 100 s at five stars. Measured on this build: 33.8, 58.5 and 34.4 seconds clean, and 20.2, 16.6 and 22.4 at five stars. It also asserts the gaps are not all the same, which a fixed interval would fail. Against 50fd1be2 it laid twenty traps in 150 seconds at gaps of 5.1 to 9.3, and failed both arms.

THE TEST CLEARS THE POLICE EVERY FRAME, and that is what makes it the TIMER's measurement. Two other things read as a trap being laid: a cruiser that gives up parks and becomes a trap again, and a tick is skipped once the cap is parked, which doubles a gap. The first version of this file measured 0.1 and 10.6 second "gaps" from those two.
