### RLG-174 — The wanted ladder - one dedicated effect per star, and a helicopter at five

TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-08: "I think we should introduce a new concept for every
star. During one star you only ever have one cruiser engaging you. At two stars two more
cruisers engage you and try to box you in. At three stars roadblocks are introduced. At four
stars super cruisers are deployed. At five stars a helicopter is deployed. This last thing is
new. We will have to design it and implement it but effectively keeps five stars on you
forever unless you enter a tunnel?"

THE VALUE OF THIS IS THAT A STAR STOPS BEING A NUMBER AND BECOMES A THING THAT HAPPENS. The
police system already escalates with heat, but it does so through several separate gates that
overlap, so no single star has a meaning a player could name. The ladder gives each one an
answer to "what is new about this level".

FOUR OF THE FIVE RUNGS ARE RE-WIRING RATHER THAN NEW WORK, and it is worth saying which,
because it changes what this costs:

    ONE CRUISER AT ONE STAR is a cap that does not exist. `dispatchCap` returns
    `heat < 2 ? 0 : min(3, heat - 1)`, so at one star the radio sends NOTHING and every
    cruiser on you arrived from a trap or a patrol - which is why one star can already
    have four cars on it. The ladder needs a cap over the TOTAL, not over the radio.

    TWO MORE AT TWO STARS, BOXING, is [[RLG-158]] and is built. The box forms at any
    speed and takes stations either side and across the front.

    ROADBLOCKS AT THREE is a one-number change: they are gated on `heat >= 2` today.

    SUPER CRUISERS AT FOUR is a two-number change, and it collides with a live ruling.
    They are gated on `heat >= 3` AND on having gone 170mph past a trap ([[RLG-030]],
    the owner's own condition). The ladder must say whether the earned event survives
    as a second condition or is replaced by the star.

THE HELICOPTER IS THE ONLY NEW MACHINE and the owner has already named its hardest problem
with a question mark. A unit that holds you at five stars for as long as it can see you needs
a stated way out, or five stars is a dead end rather than a threat. A tunnel is a good answer
because the road already has them, it is legible without being taught, and it makes a piece of
scenery into a tactic.

WHAT THE OWNER MUST STILL DECIDE, and none of it should be guessed:

    Does the helicopter DO anything besides watch - a spotlight, calling cruisers to your
    position, or blocking the cool-down - or is holding the star its whole function?

    Is the tunnel an escape or a pause? Losing it in a tunnel and being re-acquired on the
    far side is a different feeling from losing it for good.

    What happens with no tunnel on the road? Every biome must be able to offer one, or five
    stars is unwinnable in the desert and trivial in the city.

    Does it replace the cruisers on you or add to them?

RELATED, AND FROM THE SAME CONVERSATION: [[RLG-175]] on super cruisers being earned by rivals
as well as by the player, which changes what "at four stars" means when the four stars are
somebody else's.
