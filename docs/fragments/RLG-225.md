### RLG-225 — The ambulance puts down the supercar's steering wheel

**REPORTED 2026-09-12, from the device.** Owner: the ambulance is being driven with a sports car's
steering wheel.

---

## WHAT WAS WRONG

`PLAIN_WHEEL` in `drawWheel` is the list of bodies that get a working vehicle's wheel rather than a
sports one. It named SALOON, COUPE, HATCH, CAB, PICKUP, VAN and SEMI, and the lowercase traffic
kinds beside them. **AMBULANCE was not in it**, so `plain` was false for that one vehicle and it
took the sporty treatment in full: carbon weave across the top of the rim, chrome inserts down the
spokes, a silver bezel round the switchgear, a racing tick at twelve o'clock, and a rim at TH 9.5
against a working wheel's 8.6.

An ambulance is a van underneath - `rig:'van'`, and the front and rear painters already say so. Its
wheel should have been the van's from the day the body was added.

## WHY IT SURVIVED, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING

**The RIM was already right.** `roundRim` names MEDICAL, and the comment above that line says why:
the list is keyed on the MARQUE, and giving the ambulance its own marque for the boss badge would
otherwise have flipped its wheel from round to flat-bottomed as a side effect. Somebody saw the
marque problem and fixed the marque problem.

So the defect was not a flat-bottomed wheel in an ambulance, which anybody would have caught. It was
**the right shape wearing the wrong jewellery** - a plain round rim with a supercar's decoration on
it - and that is a harder thing to see in a mirror at speed than in a picture side by side. The
owner saw it on the device.

**THE LESSON: A BODY JOINS MORE THAN ONE LIST, AND FIXING ONE OF THEM LOOKS LIKE FIXING THE FAULT.**
Two lists decide what a vehicle is driven with, they are keyed on different things - one on the
marque, one on the body - and only one of them was ever taught about the ambulance.

## WHAT CHANGED

AMBULANCE was added to `PLAIN_WHEEL`, and the lowercase `ambulance` with it, because that kind is
real traffic and the list carries the lowercase kinds for exactly that reason.

Every other body was checked against the list in the same pass. The nineteen bodies are STALLION,
MATADOR, VECTOR, APEX, COMET, ROADSTER, TUNER, MUSCLE, SUPERCRUISER, CRUISER, COUPE, HATCH, SALOON,
CAB, PICKUP, AMBULANCE, VAN, SEMI and CREST. The sports, super and formula cars are meant to be out
of the list; the two patrol cars are deliberately out of it, because the owner named production and
utility and a cruiser is a pursuit vehicle. **AMBULANCE was the only one missing.**

## THE PROOF

`tools/wheel-test.py`, new. It measures the DRAWN WHEEL rather than the list that drew it - reading
`PLAIN_WHEEL` back and asserting AMBULANCE is in it would be testing the fix against a copy of
itself. It asks the engine for finished wheels through `API.wheelOf` and compares them pixel by
pixel:

1. **Outside the boss, the ambulance's wheel and the van's are the same drawing.** Not one pixel may
   differ. It reports 0.
2. **Inside the boss they differ** - 419 pixels, which is the red cross against the van's GENERIC
   marque. Without this question the first one passes by comparing two blank canvases.
3. **A sports wheel is still tellable from a working one** - the MATADOR against the VAN differs in
   4,047 pixels outside the boss. Without this the first question passes on a build where every car
   got the plain wheel, or on one where `wheelOf` stopped drawing.

`--falsify` serves the engine with AMBULANCE struck back out of `PLAIN_WHEEL`. Question 1 then fails
with 3,177 differing pixels, which is the size of the defect the owner was looking at. **The check
was watched failing with the fault present before this was written down.**

The wheels row of `docs/fleet/traffic.png` was regenerated and shows the ambulance's wheel beside
the van's: the same rim, the same arms, the same horn pad, and a red cross where the van has its
own badge.

## WHAT IS NOT FIXED HERE

**`WORK_BODIES` has the same hole and it is a different object.** That list decides which cars get
the black gear knob with white text, and it reads COUPE, SALOON, CAB, PICKUP, VAN, SEMI - so the
AMBULANCE and the HATCH are both driving with a polished ball. The owner's 2026-08-30 ruling that
made the list said *production and utility vehicles*, and both of those are one. It is left alone
here because it is a second vehicle detail rather than the one that was reported, and widening a
reported fix without being asked is how a unit stops being one thing. Put to the owner.
