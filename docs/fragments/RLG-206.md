### RLG-206 — Four personalities: Commuter, Speeder, Outlaw, Racer
- **RULED 2026-09-10T11:55:45-04:00** — **THE OWNER SPECIFIED THE PERSONALITY SYSTEM, 2026-09-10, and it is GLOBAL.** Asked whether these
were INTERCEPT's rules: *"These aren't just intercept behaviors, but global."* So this is the
ordinary road, every mode, and [[RLG-203]] inherits it rather than owning it.

**FOUR PERSONALITIES, BY NAME AND BY RULE.** The owner's words:

    COMMUTER  "obey the traffic laws. They have high obedience to horns and sirens."
    SPEEDER   "drive as fast as they can (within reason) but WILL pull over when cops
               engage them. They have moderate obedience to horns and sirens."
    OUTLAW    "drive as fast as they can (recklessly) and WILL NOT pull over when cops
               engage them. They have low obedience to horns and sirens."
    RACER     "behave the same as Outlaws, but they have NO obedience to horns and sirens."

**THE FOURTH NAME WAS DRIVER FIRST, AND THE OWNER CHANGED IT FOR A REASON WORTH KEEPING.** *"Lets
Change driver to commuter... Yeah. They are all technically drivers. So that's why I want the Name
change."* A name that describes all four cannot label one of them. It is the same fault [[RLG-195]]
fixed at the other end of the scale, where "racer" named the lawbreaker and left the competitor
without a word.

---

**BUILT IN UNT-255, v0.13.77 - THE NAMING AND THE OBEDIENCE. The pull-over half is NOT built** and
belongs to [[RLG-047]], which is about the stop itself: who pulls over, and what the police do when
they have one stopped.

**`CIVILIAN` BECAME `COMMUTER` AND A FOURTH CONSTANT JOINED THE SCALE.** `COMMUTER 0, SPEEDER 1,
OUTLAW 2, RACER 3` - and the ORDER is load-bearing rather than cosmetic, because two police tests
already read this as an ordinal: `mind >= SPEEDER` is how the force decides who is worth looking at.
A RACER has to sit above an OUTLAW for that to keep meaning what it says.

**WHICH TURNED UP A LATENT FAULT THAT ADDING A PERSONALITY WOULD HAVE MADE REAL.** `CROSSING`, the
deer's mind, was **3** - inside the run of driving personalities - and a deer is pushed into the
`traffic` array like everything else. So `mind >= SPEEDER` was already TRUE of a deer. It never
showed, because the next line asks how fast the thing is going and a deer carries no `spd` or
`cruise` at all. It is now 9: a crossing animal is not more lawless than an outlaw, it is not on the
scale. Only `isCrossing` reads it, and it reads it by equality.

**`heed` BECAME `obedience`, at the owner's word, and it now STARTS AT THE PERSONALITY'S VALUE**
rather than at 1 for everybody. `OBEY` is the table - 1.00, 0.60, 0.25, 0 - and it multiplies the
odds the request already carries, so a siren asking at 0.90 reaches a commuter at 0.90 and an outlaw
at 0.225. The values are committed defaults and the owner tunes them.

**TWO THINGS ABOUT THE ZERO, because zero is not a small number.** A RACER is never asked at all -
it is counted as `deaf` and skipped - and it is never worn down and never recovers, because there is
nothing to recover from. Both mattered: the old fatigue floor was a flat `0.12`, which would have
LIFTED a racer's obedience to 0.12 the first time one was asked, and the old recovery climbed back
toward **1**, which would have quietly turned every worn-down outlaw into a commuter a few seconds
after the player passed it. The floor is now a share of what the car started with, and recovery
climbs to the car's own ceiling.

**AND THE RIVALS CARRY THE PERSONALITY THEY WERE NAMED FOR.** `buildField` sets `mind: RACER`.
Nothing reads it today - `scatter` only ever walks `traffic`, so a rival could not be asked to move
over even before this - but "a racer answers nothing" being true only because of which array a car
happens to live in is one refactor away from being false.

**PROVED BY `tools/obedience-test.py`, EVD-624.** It stages a car of each personality in front of
the player rather than waiting for the road to deal one - an OUTLAW is about one car in twenty, so a
harness that waited would be measuring the spawn odds `mind-test` already measures. Measured at 40
trials, one ask each:

    COMMUTER  88%      declared 0.90
    SPEEDER   52%      declared 0.54
    OUTLAW    28%      declared 0.225
    RACER      0%      declared 0, and it moved in no trial at any point

**THE TRIAL LENGTH IS PART OF THE MEASUREMENT.** At 1.4 seconds every staged car was asked TWICE and
the tiers blurred - a commuter and a speeder both read 95 per cent, because 0.90 and 0.54 are hard to
tell apart once you have had two goes at each. One pass of the 0.55s cooldown separates them. The
check asserts the ORDER rather than the rates, because the rates are the thing the owner is meant to
tune.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER.** The pull-over behaviour ([[RLG-047]]), and whether the spawn mix should
change now that obedience varies - a road where one car in twenty ignores you entirely is a different
road, and `mind-test` measures the mix but has no opinion about it.
