### RLG-247 — Hot Pursuit police come only from behind, try to pass, and box the player to a stop from in front
- **RULED 2026-09-14T21:09:58-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED.** Owner, 2026-09-14: *"In hot pursuit, there is def cop cars still spawning ahead and waiting on me to catch up to them already engaged. They need to only come from behind and they need to try and get around you. If they get in front of you they force you to a stop unless you can get around them (get enough X distance from them). It's the inverse of how it works in intercept."*

## WHAT THE OWNER ASKS FOR

1. **Police in Hot Pursuit spawn only BEHIND the player.** None appears ahead, already engaged, waiting for the player to reach it.
2. **They try to get around the player** and ahead.
3. **A cruiser in front forces the player to a stop**, unless the player gets around it: enough lateral (X) distance from it.
4. **It is the inverse of Intercept**, where the player is the police car and the stop is made on others. Read Intercept's stop before designing this one: [[RLG-047]] (a non-racer pulls over for the police), [[RLG-203]] (Intercept).

## READ FIRST

[[RLG-158]] (the police lift for traffic and box at speed), [[RLG-169]] (the heat economy), [[RLG-174]] (the wanted ladder), and roadblocks ([[RLG-242]]). A roadblock is placed AHEAD by design, so it is not the ahead-spawn the owner means; confirm that with the owner if a harness finds only roadblocks ahead.

**Open:** how much lateral distance counts as getting around, and what "forced to a stop" does to the run (a bust, a penalty, or a stop the player can drive out of).
