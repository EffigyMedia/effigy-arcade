### RLG-081 — Turbo and supercharger upgrades, with the mechanics and the gauges that go with them
- **RULED 2026-08-30T08:55:05-04:00** — REQUESTED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-08-30, mid-session and explicitly for later: "turbo and
supercharger upgrades that add the appropriate mechanical functionality and gauges/dials."

WHAT THE SENTENCE ASKS FOR IS THREE THINGS, and they are not the same size. An upgrade the player
buys, a change to how the engine makes power, and an instrument that shows it. The third is the one
that is easy to get wrong: a boost gauge that is a dial painted on the HUD and not fed by anything is
decoration, and this project has already ruled once that a lamp is the unlit bulb drawn again lit
rather than a light bolted on ([[RLG-053]]).

WHAT IS ALREADY TRUE AND CONSTRAINS IT. ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR (owner, 2026-08-28) - the rubber
band is the only named exception, and an upgrade the player buys is not a second physics, it is the
same physics with a different number in it. THE CAR IS CAPABLE, THE DRIVER DECIDES - so forced
induction belongs to the CAR, and nothing about it may read a vehicle class to decide how it
behaves. The fleet already has a supercharged muscle car with a blower drawn on its bonnet, and
`c.overWipers` exists because that blower sits in front of things - so the art already assumes some
of this.

WHAT IS NOT DECIDED AND NEEDS THE OWNER. Whether the two are alternatives or can be stacked; whether
they are per-car purchases or a garage-wide upgrade; whether turbo lag is wanted, which is the whole
character of a turbo and is also the thing that makes a car harder to drive; and what a
supercharger's whine does to the audio mix. None of that should be guessed.

DO NOT START THIS. It is recorded because the owner said to record it.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T17:43:05-04:00** — THE OWNER HAS RULED: THEY ARE ALTERNATIVES, AND THE WHOLE THING IS PARKED. 2026-08-30: "I don't think
you should have a turbo and a supercharger they are alternatives, but there is no system currently for
upgrading a car so we will just keep that whole thing in our back pocket."

That settles the first of the three open questions this fragment recorded - the two do not stack, a car
has one or the other or neither - and it names the blocker that makes the rest moot. There is no
upgrade system. Until a car can OWN a modification, forced induction has nowhere to live except as a
permanent property of a body, which is a different feature and not the one asked for.

TURBO LAG AND THE SUPERCHARGER'S WHINE REMAIN UNANSWERED, and they should stay unanswered until the
work is picked up: lag is the whole character of a turbo and it is also what makes a car harder to
drive, and neither is a question worth deciding a year before it is built.

[[RLG-084]] - a radar detector - lands on exactly the same blocker on the same day, which is the
signal that the upgrade system is the real piece of work behind both.
