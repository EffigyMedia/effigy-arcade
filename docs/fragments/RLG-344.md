### RLG-344 — The massif stops dead in the distance instead of running out
- **RULED 2026-09-26T23:31:44-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-26, with the surface flags on: "the second one shows how weird it looks when the mountain stops surrendering sharply in the distance." The shot is `docs/reference/shots/RLG-344-massif-cut.png`.

WHAT IT SHOWS. The massif - blue under the flags - runs down the right of the frame and ENDS on a hard straight diagonal, with the cliff face in red and the valley floor in cyan beyond it. There is no taper, no ridge running out and no haze: one slice has a mountain on it and the next has none, and the join is a clean line across the picture. Read by hue, the massif occupies x 222 to 1317 and rows 300 to 2424 of a 1320 x 2868 frame, and its lower-left boundary is that edge.

IT IS A SEPARATE REPORT FROM [[RLG-337]] even though it was photographed in the same session with the same debug row on. That one is about a surface that does not paint; this is about a surface that paints correctly and STOPS in a way that reads wrong. Neither fix is the other's.

NOT STARTED, AND THE FIRST QUESTION IS WHAT A MASSIF SHOULD DO AT ITS END. A range that runs out needs either height that falls away over some distance, or haze that takes it into the sky before the geometry ends, or both. That is a look decision and it is the owner's. `MASS_H`, `bioGrow` and `WALL_HAZE` are the three dials that already exist near it - `bioGrow` is how much of a place there is at a segment and it is what makes a mountain GROW at the near boundary ([[RLG-303]]), so the far end may simply be the same idea unapplied.
