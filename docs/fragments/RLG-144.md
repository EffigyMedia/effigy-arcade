### RLG-144 — The tunnel is transparent on the approach, because its opacity IS the crossing ramp
- **RULED 2026-09-01T15:20:05-04:00** — RULED 2026-09-01 - Owner, looking at a capture of the tunnel mouth: "why is that tunnel
transparent?"

BECAUSE ITS OPACITY IS THE TRANSITION, AND THAT WAS A DELIBERATE CHOICE THAT HAS NOT AGED
WELL. [[RLG-105]] built the bore as a sheet laid over the ordinary world with the place's own
darkness as its alpha - `ctx.globalAlpha = placeDark()` - and the note there records the
reasoning: it made the entrance and the exit cost nothing, because `placeDark` already rides
the biome crossing, so driving at a mouth fades the world out and driving from one fades it
back in with no second mechanism to keep in step. It also gave the photosensitivity ramp
[[RLG-060]] wanted for free.

THE COST IS WHAT THE OWNER IS LOOKING AT. Halfway through the crossing the entire bore - the
walls, the vault, the far cap - is drawn at about half opacity, so the farmland behind it
shows straight through. It is not a tunnel being approached; it is a tunnel being
cross-faded, and at any moment other than fully inside it reads as a ghost.

THE TWO THINGS WERE FUSED AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME THING. How DARK it is inside a bore is a
property of the place and should ramp with the crossing, exactly as it does. Whether the WALLS
EXIST is not a matter of degree - a tunnel is either there or it is not, and the part of it
that is there should be opaque. This is the same shape as [[RLG-094]], where what a city looks
like and what colour it is at this hour were one value and had to be separated.

WHAT THE FIX PROBABLY IS, AND IT SHOULD BE MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED. The bore should be
drawn SOLID, from the mouth inward, with the mouth's world position deciding how much of it
exists rather than how visible all of it is. `drawPortal` already draws the mouth as a hole in
a hillside and already knows where the boundary is, so the geometry to clip against is
present. The `placeDark` ramp then keeps its real job - the light level and the comfort ramp -
and stops doubling as an existence check.

AND IT INTERACTS WITH [[RLG-143]]. The owner has asked for water at land level around a
tunnel, drawn behind the entrance, so the bore reads as going under a body of water. A
translucent bore would show that water straight through its walls, which is worse than not
drawing it. This should be fixed before that is built.

NOT DIAGNOSED FURTHER. The alpha is the obvious cause and the code says so plainly, but
whether the whole picture is explained by it - the far cap, the outward wall fill, the portal
- has not been checked, and RLG-041's lesson is that the obvious mechanism is often one of
several and not the one being seen.
- **RULED 2026-09-01T15:41:22-04:00** — FIXED 2026-09-01, v0.11.15, and the diagnosis in this fragment held.

THE EXTENT IS WHAT THE CROSSING DECIDES NOW, NOT THE ALPHA. The bore is built from its own
MOUTH inward and drawn solid. `eventZ0` already holds where that mouth is to the world unit -
armed when the place is placed - so nothing new had to be tracked. Approaching, the tube is a
short one starting ahead of you that grows as you close on it; inside, it starts at the
camera; and it stops at the far portal rather than running on past it.

MEASURED ACROSS AN APPROACH: the mouth stands 27,564 units in front of the camera, closes to
9,741, then reaches it - while the darkness ramps 0 to 1 over the same stretch. Those are two
numbers moving independently, which is the whole of the fix stated as a measurement.

AND THE DARKNESS KEPT ITS REAL JOB, which is the half that could have been broken while
fixing the other. It still rides the biome crossing, so the mouth is still a ramp rather than
a cut and [[RLG-060]]'s comfort argument still holds. The check asserts BOTH - a build that
made the walls solid by freezing the ramp would have passed a check that only looked at the
walls.

THE DEBUG SETTER WAS PART OF THE FAULT AND IS WORTH RECORDING SEPARATELY.
`API.startBiomeChange` placed a tunnel without arming its event, so a harness driving into one
measured a bore beginning at the camera - a state the real game never produces. The first run
of the new check failed on exactly that and read as a fault in the engine. The setter already
carried a note saying a hook that takes a different path from the thing it stands in for
proves less than it looks like it proves; it rolls the side and the climate for that reason
and now arms the event too.

AND THE FIRST VERSION OF THE CHECK MEASURED ITS OWN PATIENCE. Fourteen samples at 140ms never
got the car near the mouth, so every reading was zero and the check reported that the tube
never started ahead of the camera. The ramp measurement further up this same harness carries a
note about precisely this trap - the boundary is placed DRAW segments away and the car has
about 37,000 units to cover - which is why it was recognised in one run rather than chased.

WHAT IS NOT MEASURED HERE. Whether the mouth READS as a solid hillside with a hole in it at
speed, and whether the exit is still comfortable now that the walls do not fade. The capture
shows a solid portal with farmland running up to it; the owner judges the rest.

AND [[RLG-143]]'S NEXT PIECE IS UNBLOCKED. Water at land level around a tunnel, drawn behind
the entrance, was the thing a translucent bore would have shown straight through.
