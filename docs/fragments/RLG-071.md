### RLG-071 — What silver and bronze should pay, now that the cars are gone
- **RULED 2026-08-29T09:53:18-04:00** — Silver and bronze paid the TUNER and the MUSCLE car. Both are in the starting class since the ladder
was built (RLG-070), so those places were handing over cars the player already owned. Removed on the
owner's instruction, 2026-08-29. What they should pay instead is to be explored; nothing is invented
in the meantime, and second and third place currently pay only the standing itself.

The constraint that makes this hard is worth stating, because it is what killed the old prizes. A
reward must not be a car from a class the player can already reach, or it is either an insult at
second place or a shortcut past the gold at first. The ladder is now the whole of the car
progression, so a second car cannot be the prize for anything except a gold.

Candidates, none of them decided:

  A PAINT OR A FINISH.  The iridescent set is already the formula gold. A matte or a metallic set,
  or one colour per tournament, would sit at silver and bronze without touching the ladder.

  A LIVERY.  Stripes are on or off today, and only for the supercars and sports cars. A silver could
  pay a second stripe pattern, a bronze a number roundel. That is a real reward that costs nothing in
  speed - which is the shape a consolation prize should have.

  A WHEEL OR A BADGE.  Every marque has its own steering wheel and badge. A prize that lets a player
  put another marque's wheel in their car is cosmetic, visible every second of a drive, and cannot
  unbalance a race.

  A TRACK, A TIME OR A WEATHER.  Not built. Once RLG-057's skies exist, a storm start or a night
  start is a thing to be earned.

  RETRY CREDITS, OR A LONGER LADDER.  A silver could bank a restart for the next tournament. This is
  the only candidate that touches difficulty, which is why it is listed last.

The owner asked for this to be recorded and left; it is not started.
