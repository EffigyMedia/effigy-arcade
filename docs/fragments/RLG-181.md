### RLG-181 — The supercruiser unlock is awarded and unlocks nothing

TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Found while building [[RLG-180]] on 2026-09-09, not reported by the owner.

**A SUPERCAR GOLD WON UNDER PURSUIT AWARDS A PRIZE THAT UNLOCKS NOTHING.** Four things in the
code agree that the super cruiser is a thing you can win, and the fifth makes it impossible:

    `tourScore`'s finish branch writes `{ supercruiser: true }` into the save when the player
    takes first overall in a SUPER tournament with HOT PURSUIT on;

    `garageBodies` carries a debug branch for it - `if(need === 'cruiser' || need ===
    'supercruiser') return !!dbgPolice` - which is code written to open a car for testing;

    the reward screen counts `g0.supercruiser` among the things you have won;

    `BODY_CLASS` has no entry for it at all, and **`BODY.SUPERCRUISER` carries `npc:true`**,
    which is what `garageBodies` filters on first. So it is not a garage car and never reaches
    the list, locked or otherwise.

**AND `cycleBody` CARRIES A COMMENT SAYING WHY**, which is how this was almost certainly
introduced: *"SUPERCRUISER is an NPC vehicle, not a garage car - listing it here made the garage
try to build a body that does not exist in BODY and the whole screen threw on a non-finite
gradient."* Something was removed to stop a crash and the award was left behind.

**THE OWNER HAS TO CHOOSE BETWEEN TWO ANSWERS AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME SIZE:**

    MAKE IT DRIVABLE. The interceptor becomes a garage car like the cruiser, with a
    `BODY_CLASS` entry and the `npc` flag dropped. That is a FEATURE - it needs a garage body
    that does not throw, which the comment above says was the original problem, and it needs
    the paint, the stats card and the mode rules the cruiser already has. It is also the
    answer that makes the hardest prize in the game mean something.

    OR STOP AWARDING IT. Remove the merge in `tourScore` and the mention on the reward screen,
    and the supercar gold under pursuit pays the formula class and nothing else. That is one
    line and a caption, and it makes the pursuit switch worth less on the top rung than on the
    one below it - which is strange enough to be worth saying out loud before choosing it.

**DO NOT SIMPLY ADD IT TO THE SILHOUETTES.** A locked card for a car that can never be unlocked
is worse than the current silence: [[RLG-180]] exists to tell the player what is winnable, and a
silhouette that stays locked forever teaches the opposite of what that ruling is for.

**A SAVE MAY ALREADY HOLD THE FLAG.** Anyone who has won a supercar gold under pursuit has
`supercruiser: true` written down and has never seen anything for it. Whichever answer is taken,
that save must not break - the cruiser's own precedent is the retired `traffic` flag, which is
still honoured for anyone who earned it under the old rule.
- **RULED 2026-09-10T03:17:39-04:00** — **THE OWNER CHOSE THE FIRST ANSWER, 2026-09-10: make it drivable.** Built in UNT-248.

**IT WAS THREE LINES AND ONE REAL DEFECT.** Everything the prize needed was already written and
waiting for the flag to come off: `openBy` already answered for the class name `supercruiser`,
`UNLOCK_HOW` already carried its condition, `paintChoices` already gave it the force's two colours,
`hasBar` had already been widened from one body to any `force` record, and the garage card already
named the gold that pays it. What was missing was `BODY_CLASS['SUPERCRUISER'] = 'supercruiser'` and
the removal of `npc:true`.

**THE HISTORICAL CRASH IS GONE AND THE COMMENT THAT RECORDS IT IS NOT WRONG.** `cycleBody` says
listing this car made the garage throw on a non-finite gradient. At that time it was a sprite with
no `BODY` record, so the painter had nothing to build from; it has had its own record since
[[RLG-055]], and the boot-time sprite builder has been assembling it from that record all along.
The garage now paints it with no page error, which `tools/interceptor-test.py` asserts directly
because a silent throw is what the flag was hiding.

**AND MAKING IT A GARAGE CAR PUT IT UNDER AN INVARIANT IT HAD NEVER FACED.** `carEnds` skips NPC
bodies, so [[RLG-184]]'s rule - two ends of one car are the same outline - had never been asked of
this one. It failed the moment it was listed: the face was four pixels shorter than the tail, and
`car-ends-test` went from seventeen cars green to eighteen cars with one failure.

**THE CAUSE WAS THE LIGHT BAR DECLARED TWICE.** The face computed its top as `roofT - h*0.030` and
the tail computed it from the cabin span. A bar is the highest thing on a police car, so it is
OUTLINE and not furniture, and outline is declared once. `barTopY` now holds the tail's expression -
the one derived from the metal the bar stands on - and both painters trace it. The interceptor's
silhouette error fell from 1.2 per cent mean and 2.2 per cent worst to 0.6 and 0.8, which is the
MATADOR's own figure: it is a MATADOR underneath, so agreeing with it exactly is the right answer
rather than a coincidence.

**WHY NO OTHER FORCE CAR WAS AFFECTED.** The CRUISER draws its bar through `paintRig('cop')`, one
piece of code serving both of its ends, so it could not disagree with itself and measures 0.0 per
cent. This painter's force branch is reached by the interceptor alone today.

**A SAVE THAT ALREADY HOLDS THE FLAG NEEDS NOTHING**, which was the last requirement in the
original ruling. `unlocked('supercruiser')` reads the same key `tourScore` has always written, so
anyone who took a supercar gold under pursuit and saw nothing for it now finds the car in the
garage. The harness proves both states: locked with the condition written under it before the flag,
open and drivable after it.

**WHAT NO HARNESS HERE CAN SAY.** Whether the interceptor is any good to drive, whether 190mph with
a cage in it reads as the floor of the supercar class, and whether the silhouette says "police car"
before it is won are the owner's verdict on a device.
