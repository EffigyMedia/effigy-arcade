### UNT-272 - The flip control is a rotate glyph, and a locked car does not get one

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-210]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-12T10:09:32-04:00** - RLG-210 amended, v0.13.87. The flip control is a rotate glyph instead of the word REAR, and a locked car no longer gets one. The glyph is drawn here rather than fetched: the owner's reference is somebody else's artwork on somebody else's server, this product makes no network call at launch, and nothing licensed enters this repository - so the concept was the brief and the two arcs are ours. It is rotationally symmetric so it needs no mirroring on a flip, and the aria-label still says which end it will show. It went on at 26 pixels, the owner said it was not legible, and it is 34 with a 21-pixel mark now - below about eighteen the arrowheads stop reading and the ring becomes a plain circle. The locked-car rule reverses what shipped an hour earlier, and the owner's reason is better than mine: both ends of a silhouette are the same flat grey. The check is paired so that no button cannot pass on a card that never rendered - the button is gone, the silhouette still paints, and an owned car gets the button back - and it was watched failing with the carLocked term removed.
