### RLG-065 — Looping sounds stop when the app is backgrounded and do not come back
- **RULED 2026-08-29T04:42:44-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-29, from the device:**

> "If I put the app in the background (switch from it to this app) some of the sound effects stop
> working, namely looping ones."

**FOUND AND FIXED in v0.9.18. The split in the report is the whole diagnosis:** one-shots keep
working and loops do not, because a one-shot builds fresh nodes every time it plays and a held voice
is created once and kept.

**What happens.** Four seconds in the background calls `teardown`, which CLOSES the audio context.
That is deliberate and it should stay: a merely suspended session on iOS can wake up after a
force-quit, which is how a backgrounded page once carried on blipping through one. But every held
voice - the engine note, the wind, the siren, the horn, Hardpoint's four layers, Quietus's drones -
belongs to that context and dies with it.

**The rebuild path existed and could never run.** `audio.js` had exactly one place that fired
`resetSubs`: the watchdog's `ctx.state === 'closed'` branch. `teardown` clears the watchdog's own
interval and sets `ctx` to null, and the branch sits behind `if (!ctx) return`. So the subscribers
were only ever fired by a path that is unreachable after the one thing that closes a context.

**Fix, first half:** `A.audio.init()` fires the subscribers itself for every engine after the first.
That is where the rebuild actually happens, whatever triggered it. The first build is left alone,
because a game builds its own voices when it starts and firing them there as well would create two
of everything.

**Fix, second half, and the harness found it rather than a person:** **Hardpoint never subscribed.**
Quietus and `road.js` both call `AR.audio.onReset`; Hardpoint's `snd` had no `arm` at all, so its
four held layers could not come back even once the shell started asking. It subscribes now, and the
null-out matters as much as the rebuild - `open` only creates a layer it does not already have, and
the ones it was holding pointed at a closed context.

**THE CHECK TOOK THREE ATTEMPTS AND THE FIRST TWO WERE WORTHLESS. This is the useful part of the
record.**

1. **Count calls to `audio.onReset`.** Read ZERO on a build that was fixed - the game subscribes
   while the page parses, and a harness can only wrap the hook afterwards. It was measuring the
   harness's own timing.
2. **Read the layer's gain.** Read a healthy **0.1300 on the broken build** and 0.1232 on the fixed
   one - the wrong way round. A `GainNode` on a CLOSED context reports its value quite happily. It
   is silent and it does not know it.
3. **Ask which context the layer belongs to.** A node carries the context it was made on, so if
   `layer.gain.context` is not `Arcade.audio.ctx`, the sound is gone whatever its gain says.

**Measured, both ways round:** with the fix, `live`. With `audio.js` and `hardpoint.html` reverted,
`orphaned on a closed context` - while the gain check went on reporting 0.13 in both. That is the
project's standing warning about vacuous checks, met twice in one afternoon on one defect.

**The general note is the project's own, in a new place:** a sound test cannot listen, but it can
read the graph. Reading a VALUE off the graph is not reading the graph - the shape of it is,
and the shape here is which engine a node is plugged into.
