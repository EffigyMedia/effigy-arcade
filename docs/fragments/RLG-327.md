### RLG-327 — The tyre marks are drawn off the bottom of the screen, and the smoke is dead code
- **RULED 2026-09-22T21:06:51-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-22: "We also want to doctor and remove any dead code. I think once upon a time we implemented tire marks but I haven't seen them ever. Tire smoke too."

BOTH HALVES ARE TRUE AND THEY FAIL FOR DIFFERENT REASONS. Proved by `tools/rubber-proof.py`, recorded as EVD-1296, five assertions passing.

THE SMOKE IS DEAD CODE, PLAINLY. `tyreSmoke` is declared, aged every frame by `stepRubber`, drawn every frame by `drawRubber`, and capped by a constant of its own - and NOTHING HAS EVER PUSHED INTO IT. `layRubber` carries the comment that removed it: the tyre smoke fought with the damage smoke coming off the bonnet for the same patch of screen and neither read clearly. The decision was right and the code was left behind. After the hardest scrubbing the harness can produce, the array holds nothing.

THE MARKS ARE LAID, AGED, PROJECTED, FILLED - AND MISS THE FRAME. They are laid at `pos + PLAYER_Z - 340`, which is 340 units BEHIND the player's own car, and the road behind your own wheels is below the bottom edge of a forward-facing view. Measured on a 480x862 canvas, sampled on the frame where the most marks were drawn: 144 marks on the road, 114 thrown away as behind the car, 30 projected and filled, and they land between y=1085 and y=12036. THE FRAME ENDS AT 862. NOT ONE MARK HAS EVER BEEN ON SCREEN.

A FIRST HYPOTHESIS WAS WRONG AND IS RECORDED BECAUSE IT WAS PLAUSIBLE AND BECAUSE IT WOULD HAVE COST A WASTED FIX. It looked like a draw-order fault: `drawRubber` is the first thing `drawWorld` paints, `drawRoad` paints the tarmac afterwards, so the marks appeared to be buried under the road surface. That is TRUE of the draw order and IRRELEVANT to the bug - the marks are off the bottom of the frame before the road ever covers them. Reordering the passes would have changed nothing and would have looked like a fix. A screenshot with the tarmac lifted showed no marks either, and that is what sent this back to the geometry.

SO A FORWARD VIEW IS THE WRONG PLACE FOR A TYRE MARK. What is behind the car is what the MIRROR shows, and `drawRubber` is called only from `drawWorld`, which is the front pass. The glass has no rubber pass at all. THIS IS THE SAME SHAPE AS [[RLG-326]]: the things that belong behind you - your own smoke, your own rubber - are drawn only where they cannot be seen, and are absent from the one view that would show them.

AND IT COSTS A FRAME SOMETHING FOR NOTHING. Thirty marks a frame is thirty projections and ninety fill calls, every one of them off-screen. The cap is 180 marks, so a long slide pays three times that. It was never measured by the layer sweep in [[RLG-299]] because it had no switch; it has one now, `layerOff('rubber')`, added for this proof.

WHAT THE OWNER HAS NOT BEEN ASKED YET, and it is one question rather than three: whether the rubber is DELETED with the smoke, or MOVED to the glass where it would be seen. The second is more work and would put marks in the one view a player checks when the back of the car is loose. The first is free and honest. Nothing is built either way.
