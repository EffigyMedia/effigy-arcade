### RLG-322 — A tournament won on the last of the clock shows three end screens over each other, and save-and-quit loses the win
- **RULED 2026-09-21T22:37:02-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21, from the device on 0.14.118: 'I just finished a tournament while coasting out of time. I got the initials entry screen, which was quickly covered up by the victory screen, which was quickly covered up by the you lost out of time screen. Looks like I did unlock the sports cars but the tournament is not considered finished because I chose save and quit.'

THREE FAULTS IN ONE REPORT, AND THEY ARE PROBABLY ONE CAUSE.
1. The finish and the run clock both end the run. The car crossed the line with the clock nearly out and kept coasting, so the clock expired AFTER the finish and ran its own ending over the win. A finished race must stop the clock. RLG-305 is the same moment seen from the traffic side: the world keeps being stepped after the line, and so, it appears, does the clock.
2. The end screens stack. The initials entry, the victory card and the out-of-time card each appeared and was covered by the next. Only one ending may run, and the initials entry must hold until it is answered.
3. The unlock was banked and the tournament's completion was not. The sports cars unlocked, so the win reached the ladder; but after save and quit the tournament does not count as finished. Either the completion is written only by a screen the player never reached, or the out-of-time ending overwrote it.

FIRST SUSPECT, UNREAD: the out-of-time check does not look at 'finished'. Read the clock's expiry path and the finish path together before changing either. The bust card in RLG-314 is a third ending on the same seam.
- **RULED 2026-09-22T09:26:34-04:00** — BUILT in 0.14.122 (UNT-415). ONE CAUSE, TWO FAULTS, AS SUSPECTED. RLG-305 (0.14.118) stopped the finish from setting state = 'wrecked', so the world keeps moving after the line - and state now stays 'driving' after a finish. The out-of-time ending tested clock <= 0, a stopped car and state === 'driving', and never asked whether the race was over. So a car that crossed the line on an empty clock coasted to a stop and ran OUT OF TIME. In a tournament that ending goes to showTourEnd, the failed-round card, whose QUIT & SAVE wrote the finished ladder back to disk as round 4 of 4, unfinished. That is the owner's report exactly, and the harness's falsify arm reproduces it: the initials entry, then 'OUT OF TIME ROUND 4 OF 4' over it.

THE FIX. (1) The out-of-time ending requires !finished. (2) wreck() returns at once when finished, so a crash after the line cannot end a decided race either - the bust already checked finished. (3) The last round retires its ladder AT THE LINE (tourDone and tourClear), beside the prize writes that were already there, instead of only on the trophy screen; the police shift's last round does the same. So even with no second ending, a player who leaves from the initials entry no longer keeps an unfinished ladder.

THE 'VICTORY SCREEN' in the report was not reproduced as a separate screen: in the harness the initials entry is followed by the trophy only after ENTER. It is taken to be the trophy flashing between the two veils, which one ending removes; if it is still seen on the device it is a separate fault.

PROVED by tools/tour-line-test.py, which plays three rounds, saves and quits, resumes, and crosses the last line on an empty clock with the car rolling. Two falsify arms: 'clock' fails only the one-ending check, 'save' fails only the two save checks.
