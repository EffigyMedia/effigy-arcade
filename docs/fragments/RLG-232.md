### RLG-232 — A finished tournament followed you into the next one

**REPORTED 2026-09-12, from the device.** Owner: *"When I complete a tournament if I don't hit the
new tournament button and I just quit to the menu any tournament I go into starts at race four with
a position of one, and I can immediately place first and win the new tournament."*

**BUILT, v0.14.8.** It is an exploit rather than a blemish: one race paid a whole ladder rung.

---

## THE MECHANISM

The trophy screen has three ways out and **only one of them reset anything**:

| button | what it did |
|---|---|
| NEW TOURNAMENT | `tourReset()` — correct |
| MAIN MENU | `tourOn = false` |
| SEE YOUR NEW CAR → DRIVE IT / KEEP | `tourOn = false` |

**`tourOn` IS NOT THE STATE.** It is recomputed from `raceTour` by `enforceModeRules` every single
time the garage opens, so clearing it clears nothing at all. The state is `tourRound`, `tourPts` and
`tourField`, and all three stayed exactly as the finished tournament left them — **round four, with
a winning point total behind it.** The next tournament therefore opened on its last leg with the
player top of the standings.

## WHY THE FIX IS NOT AT THE BUTTONS

**It cannot be reset when the trophy opens**, because that screen READS `tourPts` and `tourField` to
show the final standings and the points total.

**And it must not be three fixes at three buttons.** Naming every exit is the exact shape this
codebase has paid for three times — `hasBar` and `copLivery` were both wrong the moment a second
police car arrived ([[RLG-202]]) — and it leaves the next button somebody adds to this screen
carrying the defect back in.

So a flag says the tournament is **spent**, and **one place acts on it**. `enforceModeRules` is the
single door back: `showGarage` calls it through `enforceCarRules`, and a player has to pass the
garage to drive. Every way out of the trophy is covered by construction, including one added
tomorrow. The retirement sits **above every branch** in that function, because a police car takes an
early return out of it and a player may well walk into one from the trophy screen.

`NEW TOURNAMENT` no longer calls `tourReset` itself. It goes to the garage like everything else and
is retired by the same line, so there is exactly one place that ends a tournament.

## A PART-RUN TOURNAMENT IS NOT TOUCHED

[[RLG-115]] deliberately keeps `tourRound` alive when a car change drops the mode, so a
half-finished ladder waits for you rather than being erased because you took a van out. **Only a
tournament that has PAID OUT is spent**, which is what the flag distinguishes and what `tourOn`
never could.

## THE PROOF

`tools/tour-exit-test.py`, new. **It plays the whole tournament**, which is the only way to reach
the screen the owner was looking at: `API.parkFinish` puts the finish line behind the player so a
round ends when the engine next looks, and the four rounds are real rounds with a real trophy at the
end of them rather than a state poked into the engine and read straight back.

    ok  the garage is set to TOURNAMENT
    ok  the tournament ran its four rounds and reached a trophy
    ok  leaving by MAIN MENU retires the tournament
    ok  the next tournament starts at round one

**`--falsify` reproduces the owner's report in the owner's words**: *the garage still holds round 3*
and *it started at round 3 — one race from a gold*. Round 3 is zero-indexed; it is the fourth race.

**TWO THINGS THE HARNESS LEARNED THE HARD WAY, both worth keeping.** `data-act` names repeat across
screens — the unlock card has a `drive` of its own — so an unscoped selector found a button on a
veil that was not on screen and waited thirty seconds for it to become visible. Every control is
taken from `#veil:not(.hidden)` now. And a round ends when a SCREEN OPENS rather than after a fixed
wait: the finish is handled 700ms after the line with an animation in front of the trophy, and a
flat sleep read "no trophy" on a build that was about to show one.

## WHAT THE PROOF DOES NOT COVER

The harness finishes its rounds in last place, so it never reaches **SEE YOUR NEW CAR** — that needs
a gold. Those two buttons are covered **by construction** rather than by measurement, because the
retirement is at the single door and not at the buttons. That is the reason the fix was built this
way, but it is not the same as having watched it.

**[[RLG-026]] is still open and may be the same family** — a stale tournament rewards system still
triggering. It was not read as part of this and should be, before it is assumed to be fixed.
