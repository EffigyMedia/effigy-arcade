### RLG-023 — Interstate: weather needs two layers, one behind the verge and one on the road
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:39-04:00** — - **Owner report:** the snow overlay only covers the verge where the under-horizon lives. There need
  to be two weather layers - one that lives between the verge and the background, and one that just
  paints the road slices.

- **Why one layer cannot do it.** Precipitation drawn in one pass has to pick a depth, and whichever
  it picks is wrong for half the frame: in front of the road it covers the car, behind the verge it
  vanishes over the tarmac. Two layers means the fall can be behind the distant ground and in front
  of the road surface at the same time, which is what it does in life.
