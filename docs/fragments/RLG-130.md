### RLG-130 — The mirror's roadside is far sparser than the windscreen's
- **RULED 2026-08-31T21:03:01-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-08-31, after playing through all the tournaments: "The scenery in the rearview mirror is
much more sparse than what it actually is in the front view. It should be the same or at least
closely comparable."

THE REPORT IS ACCURATE AND THE CAUSE IS THREE SEPARATE DELIBERATE CULLS, each added for a real
reason. That matters, because removing any one of them puts back the fault it was fixing. They are
all in the mirror's scenery loop:

  1. ONE ROW WHERE THE WORLD HAS FIVE. `const mrows = 1`, with a comment saying two rows cost about
     four frames a second in a forest. FOREST declares `rows: 5`, SWAMP 4, MOUNTAIN and TUNDRA 3, so
     in every rows-based place the glass is drawing A FIFTH to A THIRD of the ranks the windscreen
     draws. This is the largest of the three by a wide margin.

  2. A LOWER PER-ROW CHANCE. The loop tests `mSpec.rowDensity || mSpec.density`, and for a rows-based
     place `rowDensity` is the per-rank figure - FOREST is 0.66 against a `density` of 0.92. Correct
     on its own terms and it compounds with the first: one rank, and that rank thinned.

  3. THE NEAREST OBJECTS ARE CULLED OUTRIGHT. `mCap = mw * 0.20` and anything wider is skipped. The
     trees you have JUST driven past are the biggest ones in the glass and they are the ones removed.
     The reason is sound and is recorded in the code: at 44 pixels tall the near trees filled the pane
     and buried the road and the cars on it, which is the one thing a mirror is for.

  There is also a 1.5-pixel minimum at the far end, against the forward painter's 1.2, but that is
  small next to the other three.

SO THE OWNER'S "THE SAME" AND THE PANE'S 44 PIXELS ARE IN GENUINE TENSION, and this is the question
to put back rather than a number to pick. A mirror that draws what the windscreen draws is a mirror
that is mostly tree trunks. "CLOSELY COMPARABLE" is the achievable half of the request and the near
cull is the part that should stay.

THE ORDER TO TRY, cheapest first, and each is measurable before it is felt:

  RAISE `mrows` FROM 1 TOWARD THE PLACE'S OWN COUNT. This is the change that would actually answer
  the report. It is also the one with a measured cost against it, and the cost was measured in the
  worst case: THE FOREST IS THE HEAVIEST BIOME and this project cannot currently say by how much -
  fps-test reads 48 to 60 there across samples on one unchanged build, so the SPREAD is as large as
  the effect. Two rows must be measured across many samples, not one.

  THEN LOOSEN `mCap`. Less than the row count buys, and it directly re-opens the looming fault.

DO NOT TOUCH `rowDensity` TO COMPENSATE. It is shared with the forward view, so raising it thickens
both and answers a comparison by moving the thing being compared to.

AND THE FADE IS NOT THE PROBLEM. [[RLG-128]] fixed the mirror's alpha ramp four hours before this
report - scenery fades out at the draw edge rather than at the car. That is a separate axis from how
MUCH is drawn, and it is working.

NOT STARTED.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T23:04:04-04:00** — BUILT, 0.10.46. The mirror draws the ranks the windscreen draws.

WHAT WAS MEASURED, and it is the owner's complaint turned into a number rather than judged by eye in
a 44-pixel pane. Counting what each view actually DRAWS in a frame, through the engine's own scenery
trace, in a forest:

    one rank (as shipped)      the windscreen drew 5,254 objects, the glass drew 335   -  6%
    every rank (now)           the windscreen drew 5,324 objects, the glass drew 1,440 - 27%

Four times the roadside, and "much more sparse" becomes a difference rather than a gulf.

`MIRROR_ROWS` IS A CEILING RATHER THAN A COUNT. Each place draws its own `rows`, and no place on the
board declares more than five - so the mirror now takes the same rank count as the windscreen instead
of a fixed one of its own.

THE FRAME RATE COST COULD NOT BE MEASURED HERE AND THAT IS THE HONEST FINDING. Nine samples per arm,
in the forest, which is the heaviest place on the board:

    one rank, 1.5px far cutoff     52.4 - 60.4 fps, median 57.2
    two ranks, 1.5px               44.0 - 60.4 fps, median 55.6
    two ranks, 2.2px               49.2 - 60.4 fps, median 54.4
    one rank,  2.2px               47.6 - 60.4 fps, median 59.2
    five ranks, 2.2px              46.4 - 60.8 fps, median 54.8

THE FOURTH ROW IS WHY NONE OF THIS IS CONCLUSIVE. One rank with a TIGHTER cutoff draws strictly fewer
objects than the shipped baseline and scored a LOWER minimum, 47.6 against 52.4. A configuration that
cannot be slower measured slower, so the minimum is noise. The medians span 54.4 to 59.2 across all
five arms and the forest's own spread on an unchanged build is eight frames. Two ranks and five ranks
are indistinguishable from each other.

SO THE DEVICE IS THE JUDGE AND THE THREAD ALREADY SAID SO: if the phone is slow anywhere it will be a
forest. All three levers are live - `API.mirrorRows`, `API.mirrorNear`, `API.mirrorMin` - so a report
from the device is answered without a rebuild.

ONE THING WAS PAID FOR RATHER THAN ADDED. The far-end cutoff went from 1.5 to 2.2 pixels. An object
under two pixels in a pane 44 tall is a smudge that still costs a full `drawImage`, and the far end
of a fifth rank is precisely where the ranks cost their frames and contribute least. It is not a
saving that can be claimed with confidence either, for the same reason as everything else above.

THE NEAR CULL IS UNCHANGED AT 0.20 AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. It is the third of the three culls this
fragment identified, and it is the one with a known fault behind it: the nearest trees filled the
glass and buried the road and the cars on it, which is the one thing the mirror is for. It is a lever
now rather than a constant, so it can be loosened on a device where the consequence is visible.

`tools/scenery-test.py` carries the guard, and it asserts the RATIO rather than a count - a mirror
can never match a windscreen object for object, and what changed is the size of the gap. It also
asserts that the rank count is what closed it, so the check cannot pass while watching something else.

NOT JUDGED ON A DEVICE. Whether four times the roadside reads as right or as clutter in a 44-pixel
pane, and whether a forest still holds 60fps on the phone, are the two questions this cannot answer.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T23:13:22-04:00** — AND THE CHECK THIS UNIT ADDED WAS ITSELF CALIBRATED FROM ONE READING, which is the fault this project has a standing note about and which I committed anyway.

IT FAILED ON ITS OWN EVIDENCE RUN. [[EVD-220]] records 412 mirror objects against 180 at one rank - a ratio of 2.29 against a threshold of 2.5 that had been set from a single earlier reading of 4.3. Three runs afterwards all passed, so it would have been easy to call it noise and move on.

THE RAW COUNTS ARE NOT THE STABLE QUANTITY. A single 120ms trace catches however many frames the machine rendered in that window and whatever the car happened to be driving past, so the counts swing by a factor of three between runs. Summing three windows and comparing the RATIO reads 27, 27 and 28 per cent against 7 per cent on three consecutive runs.

THE THRESHOLD ALSO CAME DOWN, from 2.5 to 2.0 times, for the same reason: it was a number read off one measurement rather than a claim about the change. What the ruling actually says is that the glass carries far more than it did, and doubling is far more.

THE COMMIT THAT SHIPPED IT IS LEFT STANDING RATHER THAN REWRITTEN, because the record of a check being wrong is worth more than a tidy history - and its own evidence record is what caught it.
