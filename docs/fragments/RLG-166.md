### RLG-166 — Traffic behind the car arrives without popping into existence

Owner, 2026-09-07: "we either need to spawn the traffic behind the car at a greater
distance or alpha fade them in or both, because right now if you just stop the car and
look at your rearview mirror you just constantly see traffic just popping into existence."

STOPPING IS WHAT MAKES IT OBVIOUS. `spawnBehind` exists to keep a stopped car from sitting
on an empty road, so the moment you stop is exactly the moment it fires hardest - and it
drops cars 2,600 to 4,200 units back, which is inside what the mirror draws. So the
feature that stops the road emptying is the one you watch working.

TWO ANSWERS AND THE OWNER WILL TAKE EITHER OR BOTH: put them down further back than the
mirror reaches, or fade them in over their first moments. The scenery already fades at the
draw edge and the mirror already fades its own roadside, so the second has precedent here
rather than needing inventing.

Note the far edge is not free: a car dropped further back has further to come before it
passes you, and `spawnBehind` was written because nothing arriving is worse than something
arriving oddly.
