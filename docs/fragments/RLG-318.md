### RLG-318 — The swamp's skyline is triangles and should be swamp trees
- **RULED 2026-09-21T19:22:54-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21: 'the skyline in the swamp needs to look more like swamp trees, not like the triangles you have.'

AND THE JUNGLE IS RIGHT AS IT IS - owner, the same exchange: first 'the jungle can be sort of like the canyon but green as far as the skyline is concerned', then 'Yeah, the jungle skyline is actually perfect. The way it is. I forgot that it was the way I was asking it to be.' So the jungle's continuous canopy ([[RLG-113]]) stands and is NOT to be touched by this or by [[RLG-317]].

THE SWAMP DRAWS THE FOREST'S TREELINE. `buildSkyline` gives SWAMP and FOREST the same `tree` kind, and RLG-113's own note already says so: 'narrow trunks with holes punched through the line'. On a swamp that reads as a row of triangles - conifers - which is the wrong tree for standing water. Swamp trees are cypress and tupelo: tall bare trunks, flared at the base, with flat or ragged crowns and hanging moss. A silhouette with a trunk and a broken, drooping crown is the shape to reach for, and it is a third tree kind rather than an edit to the forest's.

Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-22T11:56:36-04:00** — BUILT in 0.14.127 (UNT-420). The swamp's skyline form 'wetTree' now plans a kind of its own, 'cypress', painted in paintSkylineShape: a narrow trunk flared at the foot, a flat ragged crown of three tiers wider than deep, and moss in the flank shade hanging off the lowest tier; the raggedness comes from the object's own position, so a tree is the same every repaint. The forest keeps its conifers, and the JUNGLE's canopy is not touched, as ruled. skyline-test passes. Its look is the owner's call on a device.
