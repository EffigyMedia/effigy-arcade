### RLG-172 — A clean driver is not a target, and a bust is the end of a pursuit

Owner, 2026-09-08: "There's STILL an overwhelming amount of cops just coming back at me one after
the other engaged to me... And why do I start hot pursuit with heat at all?"

ONE ROOT CAUSE ANSWERED BOTH, AND IT WAS NOT WHERE THE PREVIOUS TWO FIXES LOOKED. The player was the
DEFAULT TARGET of every cruiser's search, unconditionally, and weighted toward at 0.55. That is
right for a cruiser already on you and wrong for every other one - so a trap that left its post for
a speeding NPC, which [[RLG-046]] rules it should, re-targeted within 1.4 seconds and adopted the
player however they were driving. They accumulate, because each arrives for somebody else's offence
and stays for yours.

MEASURED AT 76MPH AGAINST A LIMIT OF 80, with no heat and not one radio dispatch: FOUR cruisers
engaged to a driver who had done nothing, every one of them trap-born. After the fix, the same run
holds at zero engaged for its whole length and the only cruiser on the road is a trap pulling over
an NPC - which is RLG-046 working as ruled.

AND THAT IS WHERE THE HEAT CAME FROM, which is the owner's third question. [[RLG-170]] charges two
points a mile WHILE PURSUED, so a clean driver was being billed for a pursuit that should never have
started. Nothing was wrong with the reset - `heatPts` is zeroed on every run - and the heat was
earned, by a rule that was being fed a pursuit it should not have had.

THE FIX IS ONE CONDITION: you have to be worth chasing to be in the running at all - wanted, or over
the limit - OR the cruiser is already on you. The second half is the part the old comment CLAIMED
and the code did not make: "a cop already on you does not abandon the chase". The rest of the search
is untouched, so a cruiser still picks the nearest speeder and a pursuit can still be taken off you
by somebody else's driving.

WITHOUT THAT SECOND HALF THE BUST BECAME UNREACHABLE. Braking to a stop with the heat cooled away
made every cruiser lose interest, and `bust-test` read "nothing happened in seven seconds". A cop
already on you keeps you.

AND THE BUST CHECK WAS HOLDING THE DEFECT IN PLACE. It staged a cruiser beside a driver with NO HEAT
and expected an arrest - a case that only ever passed because every cruiser adopted the player
whatever they had done. A clean, stopped driver being boxed in and arrested IS the owner's report,
not a feature. The scene now carries what a bust needs, and the complaint is a check of its own: a
driver wanted for nothing cannot be busted.

STILL OPEN, AND IT IS THE OWNER'S: what a trap should do about a speeder that is not you. It waits
at the roadside and acts when one passes, which is the owner's own description of what a trap is.
Whether it should then LEAVE to chase that driver - adding a moving police car to the road - or stay
parked is RLG-046's territory and was not narrowed further without a ruling.
- **RULED 2026-09-08T17:43:00-04:00** — REFINED BY THE OWNER 2026-09-08, v0.13.41. "The only reason a cruiser who is engaged currently to
an NPC should switch its targeting to you is if you pass them at a speed above the car they are
currently targeting by a small threshold. I don't think every cruiser should just inherently switch
its targeting to you just because you exist and you are also speeding half a mile behind them."

THE FIRST FIX STOPPED A CLEAN DRIVER BEING ADOPTED AND LEFT THIS. A speeding player was still a
candidate for every cruiser on the road and still weighted toward at 0.55, so one already running an
NPC down would drop them for you from most of a mile back - the pile-up again with a speed test in
front of it.

SO THE SWITCH IS AN EVENT, NOT A COMPARISON. A cruiser already on somebody keeps them unless you go
PAST it - within 3,000 units, about the moment of going by at speed - and you are doing more than the
car it already has by ten miles an hour. A cruiser with nobody is unaffected, and one already on YOU
keeps you, which is what makes the bust reachable.

MEASURED: half a mile up the road with the player 40mph faster, it keeps the NPC, run after run.
Alongside and clearly faster, it takes the player.

THREE FAULTS CAME OUT OF BUILDING IT AND ALL THREE WERE THE SAME SHAPE - a fallback that quietly
meant "the player":

  A FRUITLESS SEARCH WROTE ITS EMPTY RESULT THROUGH, wiping the cruiser's target and setting the
  speed it was comparing against to ZERO - which makes "faster than the car it already has" true of
  any speed at all. The very next search handed the cruiser to the player.

  THEN KEEPING THE OLD TARGET BROKE THE OPPOSITE CASE. A cruiser that found nothing AND never had
  anything kept an undefined target, and the line that reads it treats undefined as YOU. It busted a
  clean stopped driver in the very check written to forbid that. A cruiser with nobody is given the
  road ahead of itself now.

  AND THE BUST ITSELF COUNTED ANY CRUISER STANDING NEAR A STOPPED CAR. A driver wanted for nothing
  could be boxed in and arrested for pulling up beside a parked police car. A bust is the end of a
  pursuit, so it now needs a cruiser actually on you - and a parked speed trap counts for nothing,
  because it is watching the road rather than you.

THE MARGIN HALF IS IMPLEMENTED AND NOT ASSERTED, and the reason is in the harness rather than left to
be rediscovered. Staging it needs the player held a few miles an hour above a traffic car and both
ends fight it: `setSpd` is overwritten by the player's own acceleration between ticks, and the
traffic AI drives an NPC's speed toward its own cruise whatever is written to it. Asking for a 4mph
gap produced 34. The DISTANCE half is asserted, which is the half the complaint was about.

AND ONE ASSERTION OF MINE WAS WITHDRAWN. "None that arrived behind you gets in front of you"
contradicts [[RLG-158]], where a cruiser that has caught you takes a station AHEAD to slow you into a
bust - the owner's own request. It was failing on the box working as designed.
