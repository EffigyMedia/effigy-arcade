### RLG-236 — A five-speed has no slot below fifth on the thumb, and the knob is drawn over the rails
- **RULED 2026-09-13T13:08:12-04:00** — **REPORTED AND RULED 2026-09-13, from the device.** Owner: *"The manual shifter bugs are still
happening."* Four screenshots showed a five-speed in fifth at 77 mph, then N in the middle of the
third rail, then N in the slot below fifth while it coasted. Asked what was wrong, the owner chose
two answers: the slot below fifth must go on a five-speed, and *"The knob is drawn UNDERNEATH the
rails"*.

**BUILT, v0.14.11. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]).

## THIS REVERSES PART OF THE ANSWER RECORDED IN [[RLG-220]]

On 2026-09-12 the owner said of the neutral slot: *"It's fine if it's there for 5 speed... remove it
on 4 speed!"* On 2026-09-13 they said the slot below fifth must go on a five-speed too. The newer
word stands. The five-speed keeps its third rail, and the rail carries fifth only.

## FAULT 1 - THE THUMB COULD STILL REACH THE SLOT BELOW FIFTH

`shiftStep` has refused the slot since [[RLG-069]]: it checks `gateSlots()` before it moves the knob
down. **The `pointermove` listener did not ask.** It turned the thumb's height into a row and moved
there. This is the RLG-220 fault again on the other axis. RLG-220 found that the drag path picked
its rail without asking the car; the same path also picked its row without asking. The keys and the
pad, which are out of scope, had the check. The thumb, which ships, did not.

The fix is one line in the listener. When the thumb asks for the bottom row and the current rail has
no slot there, the knob stays in the centre row.

## FAULT 2 - THE KNOB WAS DRAWN UNDER THE SHORT THIRD RAIL

A five-speed's third rail is a different height from the other two, so it cannot be a box-shadow
copy. It is `body.gears5 #shifter::after`. **An `::after` paints after the element's children**, so
it was drawn across the knob whenever the knob sat on that rail. A four-speed and a six-speed draw
their rails on `#gate`, an earlier sibling, which is why only the five-speed showed it. `#knob` has
`z-index:1` now.

Motorsport's stylesheet draws the five-speed gate differently, with no `::after` stub, so it does not
have this fault and was not changed. Motorsport is parked.

## THE PROOF

`tools/gate-rails-test.py`, extended. It drives the knob with a real pointer (`page.mouse`), for the
reason RLG-220 recorded: synthesised events with invented ids never arm the drag and read as a pass.
The new gesture walks across the centre row to the last rail, up to the top slot, and hard down past
the plate.

    ok    CRUISER   a pull down from fifth finds no slot below it   knob on rail 2 at y 33, gear 0
    ok    CRUISER   the knob is drawn over the rail   the knob face above its label reads luma 187
    ok    MATADOR   and a six-speed does reach sixth on the same pull   knob on rail 2 at y 62, gear 6

**The six-speed line is what makes the five-speed line evidence.** Without it, "no slot below fifth"
is true of a pull that never happened.

The drawing check reads the screen, not the stylesheet. The stub crosses the knob's centre column,
so the pixel between the knob's top edge and its label is the light face when the knob is on top
and the rail's black when it is not.

`--falsify` now takes three values, and `tools/falsify-expect.py` (new) passes only when exactly the
named check fails:

| Falsifier | Serves back | Failed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rails` | the drag loop on `RAIL_X.length` (RLG-220) | only the four-speed rail check, rail 2 of 2 | EVD-736 |
| `below` | the listener without the slot check | only the five-speed pull, y 62 | EVD-737 |
| `knob` | `#knob` without its z-index | only the drawing check, luma 9 | EVD-738 |

falsify-expect was itself shown to fail. Run against the clean build with an expected failure named,
it exits 1.

knob-test passed (EVD-740). smoke-test 6/6 (EVD-741).

## GATE-TEST IS STALE AND WAS NOT CHANGED

`tools/gate-test.py` fails 6 checks (EVD-739). **It fails the same 6 at HEAD with this change
stashed**, so the failures are not from this unit. One of them now expects behaviour this ruling
removes ("a five-speed has fifth at the top of the third rail and neutral below it"). Another
expects a six-car black-knob list where the code has eight. Several read `[[]]` or `y ?`, which is
the shape of a harness that no longer arms. It was spun off as its own task rather than repaired
here.

**WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE:** whether the knob now feels right under a thumb on the phone.
