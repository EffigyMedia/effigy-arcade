### RLG-252 — The horizon shows the biome ahead as a sliver that grows on entry and shrinks as it recedes
- **RULED 2026-09-15T01:38:10-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED, AND WAITING ON THE OWNER TO CONFIRM THE INTERPRETATION. Owner, 2026-09-15: "background shows biomes as an angle slice of the horizon based on distance. it should remain a small sliver until you are actually entering it then grow to encompass then shrink as it recedes. at any point you should see the biome you are approaching, the biome you are in and the biome you are leaving. Repeat back your interpretation so I can know we are on the same page."

WHAT THE CODE DOES NOW (drawSky, RLG-022): the skyline is ONE band tiled across the whole horizon. During a transition the outgoing band sinks and shrinks and the incoming band rises from behind the horizon (SKY_SWAP 'move'); both span the full width. At most two biomes are on the horizon, and only during a transition.

INTERPRETATION PUT TO THE OWNER: the forward horizon is split side to side into spans. The biome being approached is a narrow span centred on the road's vanishing point, and it stays a sliver while its boundary is far. The biome the player is in fills the horizon either side of it. The biome being left holds the two outer edges. On entering the next biome, its centre span widens until it fills the horizon; the old biome is pushed out to the edges and narrows there as it recedes, until it is gone; and the biome after that appears as a new sliver at the centre. The span widths follow the distance to each boundary. Open questions put with it: whether the mirror shows the same thing reversed, and how narrow "a small sliver" is.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T01:44:40-04:00** — CONFIRMED BY THE OWNER, 2026-09-15, with one change. On the mirror question: "yes" - the mirror shows the same thing reversed, with the biome being left at its centre. On how narrow the sliver is: "it should grow from nothing until it fills the horizon, and the opposite behind you." So there is no fixed minimum sliver. The approaching biome's span starts at zero width and grows continuously until it fills the horizon; the receding biome shrinks continuously to nothing. The rest of the interpretation above stands. Still to be chosen in the build, as a tunable with a committed default: the distance over which the span grows, and where in that distance it fills the horizon.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T21:44:42-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.68, AND ONE HALF OF THE RULING IS BLOCKED ON A DECISION THE ENGINE CANNOT MAKE.

WHAT IS BUILT. The horizon is three concentric regions measured out from the ROAD'S OWN VANISHING POINT - the same `vx` the far road and the coast's shoreline are built on, because that is where the road you are on meets the place it is going. The centre is the place approached, growing from nothing; either side of it is the place you are in; beyond that, out to the screen edges, is the place you have left, shrinking with distance until it is gone. `SKY_SWAP` is `'span'`, and `'move'` and `'fade'` remain selectable because that is how the owner judged the first pair.

THE THIRD PLACE COST ONE VARIABLE. The engine has only ever held `biomeFrom` and `biomeTo` - the ends of the crossing in the picture - so at most two places could be on the horizon and only during a transition. `biomePrev` and `leftAt` are set where the pair is replaced and nowhere else, for the reason `rollSide`'s note gives about a second roller.

THE SPANS ARE MEASURED FROM THE CAR, AND THE FIRST ATTEMPT USED `skyT`. `skyT` is `bioMix` at the far edge of the draw, which reaches 1 while the boundary is still a draw distance up the road - so the centre span went from nothing to the whole horizon during the blend and sat full for the entire approach. The ruling asks for a sliver "until you are actually entering it", which is a distance from the CAR, not a state of the blend.

THE THREE REGIONS ARE EXCLUSIVE, and the first build painted the place you are in across the full width as a base and cut the other two out of it. That LEAKS, because the bands are not the same height: a desert mesa is shorter than a city tower, so the city stood above the desert inside the desert's own span. `bHalf` closes the gap rather than being a second width - at `gone` 0 it sits ON `aHalf`, at 1 it has reached the edge - because writing it as its own width left a hole between the two whenever the sums disagreed, and the region a hole shows is the SKY.

`SKY_NEAR` IS THE DRAW DISTANCE and cannot usefully be more yet. A crossing is placed AT the horizon, so a larger figure makes the place ahead appear already part-grown: at 400 segments it arrived owning 62 per cent of the horizon. At `DRAW` it enters at nothing, at the moment its ground does, and fills the frame as the car reaches the boundary. `SKY_RECEDE` is 220 segments, about a draw distance and a half, so a place is gone from the horizon at roughly the point it is gone from the road. `API.skyNear` moves the first live.

MEASURED AS NUMBERS, NOT LOOKED AT, because which biome drew a span is the whole ruling and a skyline is a silhouette that says nothing about it. Over a staged FARMLAND to CITY to DESERT run: 174 frames with DESERT ahead of CITY, `near` running 0.01 to 0.98, never going backwards, the centre span never wider than the ring around it, and a third place on the edges in all 174.

NOT VACUOUS, AND THE FIRST FALSIFIER DID NOT WORK - which is the useful part. With the growth distance cut to 2 segments, a step from nothing to the whole horizon, every check still passed: the first sample is 0, the last is 1, and a step never goes backwards. What separates a growth from a jump is how much of it is spent IN BETWEEN, so that is counted now - 2 of 176 frames part-grown with the step, against a healthy spread without it.

fps-test is unchanged at about 55 in every place, so up to three tiled bands cost nothing measurable. biome-test, skyline-test and smoke-test pass.

WHAT IS BLOCKED, AND IT IS HALF THE RULING. "At any point you should see the biome you are approaching" - and for most of a run the engine has not CHOSEN one. `planKey` is set when the countdown expires, and the countdown starts above a million units: measured over a sixty-second drive it never fell below 444,000, and `planKey` was null the whole way. So the place ahead appears only once a crossing has been placed at the horizon, which is the last DRAW segments rather than "at any point".

CHOOSING IT EARLIER IS A CHANGE TO THE GENERATOR AND [[RLG-150]] SEPARATED THESE TWO DELIBERATELY - WHERE a place begins from WHEN it is picked - so it is the owner's call rather than one to make while tuning a number. Until then the sliver is correct and appears late.
