### RLG-160 — A destroyed car keeps its smoke and fire, because that is the state of it

Owner, 2026-09-07: "when a car is destroyed - police, traffic, doesn't matter - the
damage VFX go away. They need to keep their smoking fire, cause that is the state of
things."

A wrecked car currently loses its damage effects at the moment it is wrecked, which reads
as the car being repaired by the thing that destroyed it. Smoke and fire belong to the
state, not to the transition into it.
