### RLG-319 — The swamp is brackish water on both sides, railed on both, with trees growing out of it
- **RULED 2026-09-21T19:22:55-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21: 'For some reason, swamp only has the barriers on one side... It would also be nice if the swamp was green brackish water beyond the guard rails with the swamp trees growing out of that water.'

THE ONE-SIDED RAIL IS THE CODE DOING WHAT IT WAS TOLD. The swamp declares `hazard: 'water'`, and a hazard goes on ONE side - the rolled side - with the rail on it (RLG-265). The other side is ordinary ground with ordinary scenery. So the swamp is a road with a pond down one side, and the owner is describing something else: water on BOTH sides, railed on both, with the trees standing IN it rather than beside it.

WHAT THAT IS, AS THE ENGINE ALREADY HAS IT. A place railed on both sides exists - the CITY, 'the one place with a drawn limit on BOTH sides' - so the rail on both is a table entry, not new code. Water filled out to the edge of the screen exists - the bridge's `overWater`, 'the place's GROUND is sea, full width' - but a bridge DECK stands above it with nothing growing, and the swamp is at the water's own level with trees in it. And standing water with a colour of its own exists - the sea's `seaTone`. The new part is the TREES STANDING IN WATER: today the scenery pass skips the wet side altogether ('nothing stands in the sea'), and a swamp wants the opposite rule.

BRACKISH IS A COLOUR AND A HAZE, NOT A SHORELINE. Green-brown, low sheen, and it should take the swamp's own fog rather than the coast's blue.

SEE [[RLG-318]] - the trees in the water and the trees on the skyline are the same tree, and should be drawn from the same shape. And [[RLG-320]], which asks the wider question this raised.

Not started.
