### RLG-238 — The road ahead shows through a tunnel wall
- **RULED 2026-09-13T13:34:14-04:00** — **REPORTED 2026-09-13, from the device, and NOT STARTED.** Owner: *"The future roadway is shown through the tunnel wall."* It arrived while [[RLG-237]] was being built, so it may describe build 0.14.10, before the tube was moved to start at the camera.

**NOT REPRODUCED, AND NOT TO BE DIAGNOSED FROM A GUESS.** A harness drove a tunnel with its bend set to 1.0 and took six screenshots on build 0.14.12. Two things looked wrong at the far end, and neither is certainly what the owner means:
- the dark depth-fade blocks at the far end are rectangles, and on a bend they jut sideways past the tube;
- on a bend, a red kerb wedge is painted over the lower left wall.

A third candidate was not seen but follows from the code: the tube is 16 samples joined by straight lines, spaced by the square of the distance, so the last spans are thousands of units long. On a bend the road between two far samples can curve outside the straight wall between them.

**NEXT:** ask the owner for a screenshot on 0.14.12 or later, then measure against the screenshot. See [[RLG-153]], the open tunnel complaint.
- **RULED 2026-09-13T14:03:25-04:00** — **DIAGNOSED AND BUILT 2026-09-13, v0.14.14. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]).

**THE OWNER CORRECTED ME TWICE, AND WAS RIGHT BOTH TIMES.** I recorded this as not reproduced and asked for a screenshot. Owner: *"Every one of those tunnel pictures has the offending issue clearly shown."* My own six captures of a winding tunnel did show it. After the first fix I called the remaining black blocks a separate artefact. Owner: *"No it's still clearly happening in those pictures. The black showing THROUGH the tunnel walls."* It was the same fault in a second pass.

## TWO WRONG EXPLANATIONS WERE MEASURED AND DROPPED

1. **Sparse samples.** The tube is 16 samples spaced by the square of the distance, so I expected the road to bend outside the straight wall between two far samples. A probe (`boreLeak`, since replaced) measured that across 132 road segments: 1.2 pixels at worst. Not the cause.
2. Before that, in [[RLG-237]], the camera leaving the bore. Also measured wrong.

## THE CAUSE: THE FILL RULE, IN TWO PLACES

**1. THE ROAD.** Each tube surface was ONE polygon: along its lower edge from near to far, then back along its upper edge. On a bend the wall's screen x runs one way and then the other, so the polygon folds over itself. Canvas fills with the nonzero rule, under which the two layers of a fold have opposite winding and cancel. The fold is exactly where a nearer stretch of wall stands between the eye and the road beyond the bend, so that stretch was a hole. **`boreStrip` now builds every surface (vault halves, wall faces, outer surfaces, barrier) span by span, winding every quad the same way before it goes into one path**, so overlaps add. One `ctx.fill` per surface, as before.

**2. THE BLACK.** `drawBoreDepth` paints the far end's darkness AFTER the walls, so nothing hid it. On a bend the darkness round the corner was laid straight over the nearer wall. **Each dark quad is now clipped to the screen less every nearer wall-face span**, added as the loop walks outward, so each span is clipped once a frame.

## THE PROOF

`tools/tunnel-bend-test.py`, new, on a tunnel with its bend forced to 1.0 and traffic cleared on every poll.
- `API.boreOcclusion`: road points that a nearer wall span hides in the geometry are put to `isPointInPath` against the paths the last frame filled, under the browser's real fill rule.
- `API.boreDarkThrough`: points in the last three dark quads that a nearer wall span hides are read back off the canvas pixels. The wall is pale, so near-black is the darkness.
