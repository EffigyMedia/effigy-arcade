### RLG-293 — shift-stop-test: three rivals survive the staged stop two runs in three
- **RULED 2026-09-19T16:03:06-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-19, queued behind RLG-156 drag racing ('finish what you were doing first'). tools/shift-stop-test.py, section 'AND STOPPING THEM ALL IS THE WIN' (about lines 247-290), fails about two runs in three with '8 stopped, 3 running, finished=False'. Measured 2026-09-18: the same rate on 77e4fd36 and on 916810e9, three runs each, so the RLG-279 contact-order change did not cause it. The section stages all eleven rivals into the player's line with API.stageStop(i, 900 + (i % 3) * 700, MAX_SPD*0.03) in up to five passes; the harness's own note says a rival shoved out of the player's line survives. TO DO: find by measurement why three stay running - read API.rivalState per rival (x against playerX, dz, spd, stopT, out) - then fix the staging, or the engine if the stop rule has a real defect. Prove it passes at least five runs running and can still fail with a defect put back. Do NOT change the stop rule's tunables without the owner.
- **RULED 2026-09-19T19:31:23-04:00** — MEASURED AND FIXED IN THE HARNESS, v0.14.96. NO ENGINE DEFECT WAS FOUND, and the stop rule's tunables are untouched.

FIRST, THE RATE TODAY, because the record said two runs in three. On HEAD: 8 clean runs out of 8. On the measured commit 916810e9 in a worktree: 1 failure in 7, and that one read '8 stopped, 3 running' exactly as reported. tools/shift-stop-test.py has not changed since 2026-09-10, so the five-pass staging loop was already there when the two-in-three was measured; the flake is LOAD-SENSITIVE, which is the property a harness must not have.

WHY IT FAILS. The section staged eleven rivals at three distances - 900 + (i % 3) * 700 - so FOUR cars landed on the SAME z in the SAME lane. Measured with a probe reading each rival's lateral position: a clump staged at 900 was at 374, 664 and 1364 one second later, which is the cars shoving each other apart, and a car shoved out of the player's line stops meeting the stop rule (STOP_WIDE is 0.55 of a lane). The harness's own note had named this and had answered it by re-staging survivors, which is a retry rather than a cure.

THE FIX IS THE STAGING: four cars at a time, each at its own distance, 900 to 3000 - all inside STOP_NEAR's 3200 and 700 apart, which is more than a car's length - and the existing loop brings the eleven down in three passes. What the section measures is unchanged.

PROVEN: 5 clean runs at HEAD and 5 on 916810e9, the commit where the old staging failed, plus the recorded run EVD-1113. NOT VACUOUS: with the staging hook's lane move removed - the fault its own comment records - the check failed twice, and the survivors read off 0.73 to 0.77 against the rule's 0.55 with zero hold, which is the same shape as the original flake. API.rivalState now also reports x, the offset from the player, the lane and the wreck timer, which is what made the diagnosis possible. smoke-test passes (EVD-1114).
