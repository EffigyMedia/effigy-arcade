### RLG-163 — Five outline stars, horizontal, filling by heat points

Owner, 2026-09-07: "the wanted level stars are expressed vertically when there's one
star, but then they become horizontal. They should just be horizontal the whole time, and
we should show five stars but as outlines that fill in as the full stars as you increase
the levels. It would be even cooler if we have a gradient fill from left to right of each
star to represent the heat points each star represents, and the gradient fill is your
current amount of heat points."

Three things: the row is always horizontal; all five are always present as outlines; and
each star fills left to right by the heat points inside that level rather than snapping
between empty and full.

Depends on RLG-162 for the points themselves - a gradient needs something continuous to
draw.
