### RLG-062 — occlusion-test measures one road, so it cannot be compared with another
- **RULED 2026-08-29T03:51:47-04:00** — **BUILT the same day it was found.** `occlusion-test` drove one 22-second stint on one road and
reported what the sprite pass did. The road is generated fresh on every load, so the single variable
the numbers are most sensitive to - how hilly the road happened to be - changed completely between
runs and did not appear anywhere in the output.

**Three runs on one unchanged build:**

| run | drawn | clipped | culled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 181 | 5 | 0 |
| 2 | 257 | 59 | 40 |
| 3 | 265 | 101 | 147 |

**Two faults, not one.** It is an unrepeatable measurement, and run 1 also FAILED its own
`drawn > 200` assertion - so it is a flaky gate as well.

**This was found by making the mistake.** [[RLG-041]] v0.9.12 quoted "culled 0 -> 167" from single
runs either side of a change as stark proof, and both numbers turned out to sit inside the ordinary
spread. That claim is withdrawn in RLG-041 and this fragment exists so the harness cannot do it
again.

**The fix: three shorter stints, each from a fresh page and therefore a fresh road, summed.** Hills
are met by at least one of them, so the assertions stop depending on the lottery. The per-stint
figures are printed as well, and the report says out loud that a single run cannot be compared with
another - because the next person to quote this harness will be reading its output, not this
fragment.

**Measured after: two consecutive runs, all assertions comfortable**, with the spread visible in the
output - culled 2, 18, 22 in one and 8, 51, 3 in the next. **The spread did not go away and it was
not supposed to.** What changed is that the total no longer depends on it and the reader can see it.

**The general lesson, and it is narrower than the project's existing one about numbers the driver
can influence.** That note is about a harness whose DRIVER perturbs the thing being measured. This
is a harness whose WORLD does: the terrain, the traffic mix and the weather are all generated per
run, and any of them can swamp the effect being looked for. **A harness that generates its world
needs repeats or a seed before any of its numbers can be compared across builds.** `racer-test` has
a seed and `merge-test` has one; this one has neither and now has repeats instead.
