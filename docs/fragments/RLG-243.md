### RLG-243 — Police cars flash small emergency lamps at both ends, in step with the light bar
- **RULED 2026-09-14T08:50:36-04:00** — **BUILT 2026-09-14, v0.14.18. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]).

Owner: *"add emergency lights within the headlights and tail lights of the police vehicles so a little blue and a little red lamp - matching the side the colors are on the light bar."* The owner was unsure whether the rear lamps belonged beside the brake lights or in the rear glass, and asked for both to be rendered. After the side-by-side capture, the owner placed each car's lamps:

| Car | End | Placement | Owner's words |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRUISER | front | a small lens at the inboard end of each headlight | "I like the front lights" |
| CRUISER | rear | a short bar each side at the foot of the rear window | "prefer the rear lights in the window" |
| SUPER CRUISER | front | a bar the shape of the turn indicator, under it, at the lamp's rake | "a bar like the turn indicator, under the turn indicators" |
| SUPER CRUISER | rear | the innermost chevron of each cluster, taken from the brake lamp | "the first inner chevron instead of the brake one" |

## HOW IT IS BUILT

- **Each small lamp is its own declared lamp**, named after the half of the bar it flashes with: `em.rl` follows `bar.rl`, `em.fl` follows `bar.fl`. `emOf(ids)` adds the matching `em.*` ids, and the three places that light a bar use it: `drawCopLights`, the mirror, and the player's own car. The lamp and its half of the bar cannot fall out of step.
- **They are separate declarations, not more drawing inside the bar's.** A painter bakes the bar BEFORE its lamp clusters, so an unlit lens drawn with the bar would be covered by the cluster housing.
- **The colour is the lens's own side of the bar**, from `BAR_SCHEME`: from behind the car's left is blue, and from the front it is red, the same mirroring the bar has.
- **Police only.** They are keyed on `kind === 'cop'` in the rig painters and on `force` in the car painters. The ambulance keeps its bar and gets no small lamps.
- On the SUPER CRUISER the brake lamp now draws seven blades a side instead of eight. The indicator still owns the outer two.

## A DEFECT FOUND ON THE WAY, AND FIXED

**A patrol cruiser in the mirror never flashed its bar.** `paintRigFront` painted the two lenses straight into the sprite with no declaration, so the mirror asked `FRONT_SP.cop` for `bar.fl` and `bar.fr`, found neither, and lit nothing. `mirror-bar-test` passed, because it checks that nothing is painted ABOVE the roof, not that the bar lights. The lenses are declared now, with the same rectangles and the same unlit colours, so the dark sprite is unchanged.

That is wrong: it passed, exit 0.** The title was written before the run. The failure is a flaky stopping-distance ranking, not this change.

**No harness asserts that the small lamps light.** The fleet sheet shows them lit, and nothing measures them in play. Whether they read at road distance on a phone, and whether the red chevron reads apart from the red brake blades beside it, is the owner's verdict on the device.
