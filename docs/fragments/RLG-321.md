### RLG-321 — Motorsport's nitrous check fails in every drive-test run: the car settles at 98 per cent, not 110
- **RULED 2026-09-21T22:29:39-04:00** — FOUND, NOT FIXED. drive-test has one standing failure, and only in MOTORSPORT: 'so the car settles a tenth above its own top end'. With the bottle open the circuit car settles at 98.0 to 98.4 per cent of its declared top end, where Interstate's car settles at 110.0 per cent in the same run. Every recorded drive-test run from UNT-394 to UNT-407 exits 1 on this line (EVD-1138, 1142, 1146, 1149, 1165, 1169, 1172), and so does UNT-411 (EVD-1195).

IT IS NOT THE ENGINE CLOCK. The first reading was 2.1 seconds on the engine clock, which looked like a slow GPU-off frame rate cutting the test short. The committed engine at 97973ab0, run the same way, settled at 98.2 per cent after 4.2 seconds, which is as long as Interstate's passing run. So the car has the time and does not reach the speed.

FIRST SUSPECT, UNREAD: something on a circuit caps speed below the bottle's ceiling - a corner, a rubber band toward the pack, or a circuit-only limit through CFG. The check's own threshold is the other thing to read, since a circuit may never have been meant to reach it. Read the check before the engine.
