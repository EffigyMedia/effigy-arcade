### RLG-129 — A circuit paints the ground of a place it is not in
- **RULED 2026-08-31T20:37:45-04:00** — FOUND WHILE BUILDING THE CLIMATE REFACTOR AND DELIBERATELY NOT FIXED THERE. It is a separate defect
with a separate cause and it belongs in its own unit.

WHAT IS WRONG. `stepBiome` has a seam for a circuit - `if(CFG.biome)` - and it sets `biome` and
nothing else:

    const b2 = CFG.biome();
    if(b2 !== biome){ biome = b2; ... }

`biome` is where the PLAYER is, and it is what the weather, the skyline and the roadside scenery
read. THE GROUND DOES NOT READ IT. `bioAt` paints each slice from `biomeFrom` and `biomeTo`, the two
ends of a crossing, and neither is ever set on this path - they hold their declared default of
FOREST for the whole race.

SO A CIRCUIT IN A DESERT HAS GREEN VERGES. Motorsport rolls `circuitBiome` per circuit
(`motorsport.html:2305`), so every place but forest is affected, and the band under the horizon is
painted from the same pair and is wrong with it.

WHY IT WAS LEFT. The unit that found it was the biome and climate refactor, which the owner ruled
must be one unit and one commit. Adding a second defect to that commit is exactly what the commit
cadence forbids, and the fix changes what every non-forest circuit LOOKS like - which is an owner
judgment on a device rather than a harness result.

THE FIX IS PROBABLY ONE LINE, `biomeFrom = biomeTo = b2` beside the assignment, and it should not be
believed without a check. A circuit never crosses into another place, so both ends holding the same
key is the correct state rather than a convenience.

WHY NO HARNESS CAUGHT IT, WHICH IS THE MORE USEFUL HALF. biome-test drives Interstate, where
`openBiome` sets the pair correctly. `circuit-test` asserts what a circuit must NOT have. Nothing
compares the ground a circuit paints with the place the circuit says it is in, and that is the check
this defect is asking for.

`road.js` carries a comment at the seam saying the pair is deliberately not set here, pointing at
this fragment, so the next reader does not take it for an oversight and fix it inside another unit.
