### RLG-296 — The horn reaches much too far ahead
- **RULED 2026-09-20T09:12:48-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20, from the device: 'The horn affects much too far forward! It should only really be a few car lengths.' The horn's reach is what decides which traffic hears it and gives way. A car is 390 units long, so a few car lengths is of the order of 1,200 to 2,000 units, against whatever the reach is now. Find the constant the horn scatters on, state what it is today, and put the new figure to the owner as a tunable with a committed default. RLG-037 is the fragment that made the horn load-bearing - the corridor guarantee is not absolute BECAUSE the horn moves cars out of the way - so shortening its reach may move that measurement; corridor-falsify and traffic-test are the checks to watch. Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-20T12:33:12-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-20, v0.14.103. THE HORN AND THE SIREN HAVE PARTED. The reach was `sirenReach(spd)` for both - two seconds of road, floored at 4200 and capped at the drawn road - which at 55 per cent of top speed is 16,866 units, or FORTY-FOUR CAR LENGTHS. RLG-205 gave the siren that window and said in as many words that it widened the horn too, 'which is intended rather than incidental - they are one function'. The owner has now driven it and ruled against it for the horn alone.

THE DIFFERENCE IS WHAT THE TWO THINGS MEAN, and it is why one keeps a time and the other takes a distance. A siren says clear the road I am about to be on: the same request whoever is driving and however fast, which is a TIME. A horn says move, you are in my way: the car you are about to be behind and nobody further up the road, which is a DISTANCE. `HORN_CARS` is 4 and `hornReach()` is that many times `typeLen('sedan')` - 1,520 units - stated in CAR LENGTHS because that is the unit the owner used, and READ from the car's own size rather than typed, so the reach cannot drift away from the thing it counts.

IT IS NOT SCALED BY SPEED, deliberately. At 200mph four car lengths is about a sixth of a second of road, which sounds too short until you remember that a car further off than that is one you will have passed or hit before it could move. If the device says otherwise the fix is a FLOOR IN TIME rather than a bigger number, and it was not built on a guess.

`isHorn` IS AN EXPLICIT ARGUMENT AND THE TEMPTING TESTS ARE BOTH WRONG. `fromSpd === undefined` looks like the horn and is not: the player's own siren in a police car passes no speed either, so the short reach would have gone to that siren as well. `horning` is wrong the other way - it is false while a cruiser's bar is on, so it happens to work today and would stop working the moment anything else sounded a horn. One caller passes the flag.

PROVEN by tools/horn-reach-test.py (EVD-1139). It measures THE LOOP'S OWN GATE rather than whether a car moved, because `scatter` rolls 40 per cent against an `obedience` that falls with every refusal and a car well inside the reach legitimately stays put: with exactly ONE car on the road, `scatterStat.far` is a yes or a no. Measured - asked at 400, 800 and 1,400 units; out of reach at 1,800, 3,000, 8,000 and 16,000; the nearest refusal at 1,800 against a stated 1,520. The siren still asks 16,866 at a MEASURED 8,433 units a second, which is exactly two seconds. --falsify serves the horn back on the siren's reach and BOTH HORN CHECKS FALL while both siren checks hold (EVD-1140), which is the shape that says the two parted rather than both got shorter.

TWO INSTRUMENT FAULTS FIRST, AND THE SECOND IS WORTH KEEPING. (a) The harness parked a car and DROVE AT IT: at 55 per cent of top speed the player covers about 2,400 units in the 280ms before the horn sounds, so a car parked four lengths ahead was behind the bumper by the time the loop looked at it, and every near distance read as out of reach. It holds both still now, which costs nothing because the horn's reach does not depend on speed. The horn's own 0.55s COOLDOWN swallowed every second press in the same version, reported as `seen 0`, which also reads as out of reach. (b) `holdSpd` IS IGNORED WHILE THE CAR IS ON THE LINE and the engine says so where it happens - 'held wins: a car on the line is not going anywhere, whatever a harness asked for'. What is measured instead is `startSpeed()`, A QUARTER OF TOP SPEED, which is a perfectly plausible held speed and is what makes it dangerous: the check read 7,667 against an expected 16,866 and reported that THE SIREN HAD BEEN SHORTENED BY THIS WORK. It had not been touched. Any harness that asks for a speed and then measures something must wait for the count.

THE CORRIDOR, WHICH THIS RULING ASKED TO BE WATCHED. traffic-test over four runs on this build: 0.199 FAIL, 0.410 ok, 0.391 ok, 0.232 FAIL - two in four, against RLG-037's recorded one in three and its measured band of 0.175-0.26 failing and 0.34-0.56 passing. Both numbers here land inside those bands, so nothing has moved that can be told apart from the run-to-run spread. corridor-falsify still proves the line fires on a closed road. BUT THE ARGUMENT BEHIND RLG-037 IS WEAKER NOW AND THE OWNER SHOULD KNOW IT: that ruling says the corridor guarantee is not a contract BECAUSE the horn moves cars out of the way, and the corridor traffic-test measures sits 14,000 to 24,000 units ahead - which the horn no longer reaches. In play the two do not meet, because by the time the car ARRIVES at that corridor it is four lengths away and the horn reaches it. Nothing was tuned either way.
