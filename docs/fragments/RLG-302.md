### RLG-302 — Everything that spawns should fade in rather than pop, and the fade distance may need remeasuring
- **RULED 2026-09-20T15:34:09-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20, from the device: 'We also want to make sure that the gantry and other items fade in as it spawned in as opposed to just popping in, maybe we need to remeasure the fade distance.' RLG-218 already made ONE arrival ramp that everything reads - cars, trees, crops, boats and lamp posts all fade over the last slice of the drawn road, at the mirror's six per cent (RLG-128), written as `1 - n/DRAW`. THE GANTRY IS EVIDENTLY NOT ON IT, and the owner says 'and other items', so the first job is to find every kind of thing that is placed on the road and ask which of them reads the ramp: checkpoint gantries, roadblocks, crates, speed traps, the finish line, road furniture. AND THE DISTANCE ITSELF IS NOW SUSPECT: RLG-295 doubled the draw, and a ramp written as a FRACTION of the draw doubled in world units with it - six per cent of 60,000 is 3,600 units where it used to be 1,800. That is either a longer, gentler fade or a thing that is see-through for twice as long, and nobody has looked. MEASURE BEFORE TUNING. Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-20T17:45:34-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-20, v0.14.111. THREE PAINTERS WERE OFF THE RAMP AND THE OWNER NAMED ONE OF THEM. RLG-218's ramp lives in `drawSprite`, and every kind on the road reaches it - traffic, police, roadblocks, crates, rivals - EXCEPT the three that have painters of their own: the checkpoint GANTRY, the FINISH LINE and a bridge TOWER. That is why the gantry is the one that could be named, and why 'and other items' is exactly two more. The ramp is applied in the item loop rather than inside each painter, so there is still ONE ramp and one place that reads `edgeFade` - a painter that grew its own would be the second copy RLG-218 exists to prevent.

THE REMEASUREMENT THE RULING ASKED FOR. The band is `EDGE_FADE`, 1/16 of the draw, and it has not changed. WHAT IT IS WORTH DOUBLED when RLG-295 doubled the draw: 3,750 units where it was 1,875, which at 120mph is 0.41 seconds where it was 0.20. THAT IS A GENTLER FADE RATHER THAN A WORSE ONE, because the same fraction of a longer road is also a SMALLER object - the note on the band records 34,000 units giving a six-pixel object, so at the far edge of a 60,000 unit draw things now arrive at three or four pixels. Nothing was tuned. `API.arrivalRamp` reports the band, what it is worth in units and in seconds at a stated speed, and what the ramp gave each kind on the last frame.

AND THE HONEST LIMIT OF THE PROOF, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH READING. THAT THE RAMP REACHES THE SCREEN IS NOT PROVED BY A HARNESS. Three instruments were built for it and all three failed:
(a) ASKING THE ENGINE what ramp value it had worked out. It is recorded whether or not any painter uses it, so the whole harness PASSED ITS OWN FALSIFY ARM with the three painters served back off the ramp. A number a program reports about itself is not a picture.
(b) DIFFING TWO RENDERS of the board with the ramp and without. An unpassed gantry COUNTS DOWN, so most of that diff was the digits on it - 135 changed pixels against a signal of 147 - and once the floor was set from that noise the falsify arm passed again. Marking the board passed stops the clock and also stops it drawing most of what makes it visible, which took the signal to 26.
(c) MEASURING THE INK the board lays down against the same scene with no board in it. With `eA` PINNED TO 1, so the switch could not possibly matter, it still reported 31 against 818 - all of it warm-up, because three of one arm then three of the other is not a comparison. Alternated, it reported 179 against 858 in the FALSIFIED arm and 185 against 501 in the live one: the same answer either way.
A board at the far edge of a 60,000 unit draw is a few pixels of a 480x900 frame, and the frame is never still enough for that to be read by counting them. The harness now checks the ramp's ARITHMETIC and its REACH ACROSS KINDS and says so; the three lines that put these painters on it are two words each and are reviewable; whether the arrival LOOKS right is the owner's on a device, which is this project's standing rule for exactly this kind of change.

WHAT IS STILL OPEN: whether 0.41 seconds is the right length now that it is worth twice what it was. It is `EDGE_FADE`.
