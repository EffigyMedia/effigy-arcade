### RLG-188 — A marque is drawn about its own origin, and VECTOR's was not
- **RULED 2026-09-09T16:25:29-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09, from the wheel sheet:** *"the badge for the vector on the steering wheel needs
to be centered like the other two cars' steering wheels."*

**IT WAS DRAWN 4.9 UNITS HIGH.** A marque is drawn in a frame whose ORIGIN is wherever the caller puts
it - the boss of a steering wheel, the middle of a nose, the boot lid between the lamps - so a badge
that is not balanced about (0,0) is off centre in every one of those places at once. VECTOR's two
chevrons ran from y -13.0 to +3.2, so its centre of ink sat almost five units above the point it was
being placed on.

The pair has been dropped onto the origin. Nothing about the chevrons' shape or their spacing changed,
which is the point: this was never a drawing problem.

**AND IT IS WORTH A CHECK RATHER THAN A LOOK.** Every marque in the game is a candidate for the same
fault and nothing measures it. The fleet sheet renders them all, so the evidence is there to be read,
but reading it is a person looking at a picture - which is exactly the kind of verification this
project has ruled against three times. A harness that renders each marque into its own frame and
reports the offset of its ink box from the origin would settle every one of them at once.
