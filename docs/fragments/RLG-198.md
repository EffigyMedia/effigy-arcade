### RLG-198 — An unlit RED lamp is a near-black casing, which the owner already ruled against for amber
- **RULED 2026-09-09T21:07:28-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09, reading the utility sheet:** *"the Lorry is missing the brake lamps on the top of
the trailer in every frame, except for the illuminated brake ones. Is that wrong in the real
painters?"*

**THE PAINTER IS CORRECT AND THE LAMPS ARE THERE IN EVERY FRAME.** `decl` draws a lamp UNLIT into the
sprite the moment it is declared and then registers it, so the semi's five roof markers are baked into
the tail sprite exactly like every other lamp. Nothing is missing and nothing is conditional.

**WHAT THE OWNER IS SEEING IS THE UNLIT COLOUR.** `RED_OFF` is `#280a0d` - so dark it is nearly black -
and a marker is `h*0.016` tall, about four pixels. Four pixels of near-black on a dark cell reads as
nothing. They appear the moment the lamp lights because `RED_DIM` and `RED_ON` are bright.

**AND THAT IS INCONSISTENT WITH A STANDING OWNER RULING.** On 2026-08-29 the owner ruled, of the
indicators: *"the unlit bulb is a DARK amber and the lit one is a BRIGHT amber. Not a near-black casing
and not a white-hot core: it reads as the same lamp in two states, which is what makes a signal legible
at a glance rather than a light appearing out of nowhere."*

That ruling was applied to the ambers and never to the reds. The two constants say so plainly:

| | unlit | lit |
|---|---|---|
| amber | `#7a4f12` - a readable dark amber | `#ffb43c` |
| red | `#280a0d` - a near-black casing | `#ff2a22` |

**SO THE FIX IS ONE CONSTANT, AND IT IS NOT A SMALL CHANGE.** `RED_OFF` and `RED_OFF_HI` are read by
every tail lamp, every brake light and every rear cluster in the fleet - the player's own car, the
traffic, the racers and the police. Lifting them would change the look of the entire road at night and
in the mirror, which is a judgement only the device can make.

**REQUESTED, NOT BUILT.** The owner asked a question rather than giving an instruction, and the answer
is that the painter is right and the palette is the thing that disagrees with an earlier ruling. What
value to lift `RED_OFF` to, and whether the lorry's markers want a size increase as well as a colour
one, are the owner's to decide.
