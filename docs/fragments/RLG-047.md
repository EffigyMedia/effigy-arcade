### RLG-047 — A non-racer pulls over for the police, and the police conduct the stop
- **RULED 2026-08-28T17:34:16-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "maybe we should make behavior where more often than not a non-racer will
pull over for the cop. Which means we need behavior for them, pulling over in the cop, pulling over
with them."

**Recorded as the owner phrased it: "maybe", and "more often than not".** This is a proposal with a
tendency attached, not a settled rule, and the record should not harden it into one. What is
settled: a non-racer USUALLY complies; it is not certain, and the exception is where the interest
is.

**Two behaviours, and the second is the one that does not exist at all:**

1. **The car pulls over.** It leaves its lane, goes to the verge, and stops. Some of the parts are
   there - traffic already yields to a siren, which [[RLG-035]] fixed after finding three separate
   faults in it - but yielding is moving aside, and this is leaving the road and coming to rest.
2. **The cruiser conducts the stop.** It follows the car over, stops behind it, and stays there. The
   police have no notion of a completed stop today: pursuit ends by wrecking, by escaping, or by the
   player being caught.

**A stationary car on the verge is a new object on this road and needs thinking about, not
assuming.** Everything else in the world is moving, and several systems take that for granted - the
spawner keeps things out of the drawn road ([[RLG-031]]), the corridor guarantee measures a lane-open
window ([[RLG-037]], which is already fragile and fails about one run in four), and traffic merges
around slower cars. A car stopped at zero on the verge, with a cruiser behind it, is the slowest
thing the merge logic has ever been asked to handle. **Check RLG-037's corridor measurement before
and after** - this is exactly the kind of change that would quietly make it worse.

**Depends on [[RLG-046]]**, which depends on [[RLG-045]]. There is nothing to pull over FOR until the
police engage cars other than the player, and nothing for them to engage until a speed limit exists.

**A verdict on a real device will be needed.** Whether a stop reads as a piece of world or as
traffic behaving strangely is a feel question, and no harness answers it.
