### RLG-164 — A radar detector warns of speed traps and traffic police ahead

Owner, 2026-09-07: "We could add a radar detector element for upcoming speed traps
and traffic cops, just like it was done in the original Need for Speed games."

A warning that something is ahead, in time to lift before it sees you. It answers a
problem measured under UNT-193: escaping a pursuit needs twelve clear seconds, and at
speed you meet a fresh trap or patrol before that runs out, so the escape the owner made
the main mechanism is hard to complete by running alone. A detector is what makes the
choice - lift now, or risk it - rather than a surprise.

It covers both parked traps and the patrol cars driving in traffic.

OPEN: whether it is always fitted or something earned. Not decided here.
- **RULED 2026-09-08T02:32:40-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-08, v0.13.30. Four bars under the mirror, beside the wanted stars, that fill
toward the nearest thing ahead that can catch you - a parked speed trap or a patrol car still
driving in traffic. Both kinds are swept, because they live in two different lists and a detector
that read one would look finished while being half built.

THE RANGE IS THE SPAWN HORIZON, written as `OUT_OF_SIGHT` rather than as a number of its own.
Police are placed no nearer than that, so every contact enters the detector's cone from the far
edge and closes; a longer reach would let one appear INSIDE the cone with no approach, which is
the same pop this project has fought in the scenery, moved into a gauge. It is 35,000 units, about
0.27 of a mile - six and a half seconds of warning at 150mph and about five at the top end.

STRENGTH IS A DISTANCE, NOT A COUNT. Two traps abreast are no more dangerous than one, and a gauge
that added them would say the road was worse than it is.

THE RATE OF THE BEEP IS THE READING, not an ornament on it. The game is played with both thumbs on
the road, so the interval carries the same number to the ears - nearly a second apart at the far
edge, a chatter on arrival - and the pitch rises with it, which is what makes the last few pips
read as urgent rather than merely frequent. It is a clean sine pip and deliberately NOT the
loud-hailer bark: that bark says something has already happened, and a warning that sounds like a
consequence teaches the wrong thing.

THE OPEN QUESTION IS DECIDED FOR NOW, NOT SETTLED. It is always fitted. A thing you unlock needs a
garage card, a price and a save field, none of which were asked for, and the detector is an aid to
reading the road rather than a performance part. If it should be earned, that is a later ruling.

tools/radar-test.py is the check and it falsifies: with the traffic half of the sweep removed, the
two patrol assertions went red and the two trap assertions stayed green.
