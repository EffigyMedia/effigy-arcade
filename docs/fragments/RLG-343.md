### RLG-343 — The horizon parallax follows the road far ahead instead of the car's own heading
- **RULED 2026-09-24T12:19:59-04:00** — THE OWNER HAS DIAGNOSED IT, 2026-09-24: "I've also kind of figured out why the horizon parallax seems weird. It's because you're moving it as the road twists and turns way down the road not where the player's car is if you get what I'm saying."

THAT IS EXACTLY WHAT THE CODE DOES, and the constant says so in its own name. `SKY_BEND_AT = ROAD_FAR` - the WHOLE draw distance, 300 segments of 200 units, sixty thousand units ahead - and the skyline's target is `-bendPx(pos + SKY_BEND_AT) * 0.55`.

AND `bendPx` MAKES IT WORSE THAN IT LOOKS, because of what it subtracts. `bendPx(z)` is `bendCache(z) - bendCache(pos) - slopeCache(pos) * ((z - pos)/BEND_STEP)`: the road's lateral offset at z, measured from the car AND with the car's own heading taken out. So `bendPx(pos)` is identically zero, and what is left at any distance is CURVATURE - how far the road deviates from the direction the car is already pointing.

SO THE SKYLINE SWINGS WHEN THE ROAD BENDS A MILE AHEAD WHILE THE CAR IS STILL GOING STRAIGHT, and does not swing when the car itself turns. That is the wrong way round, and it is why the parallax reads as the world sliding rather than as the viewer turning.

WHAT A DISTANT THING ACTUALLY PARALLAXES AGAINST IS THE VIEWER'S HEADING. A skyline miles away does not care what the road does ahead of you; it sweeps because YOU have rotated. In this engine the camera's heading is `slopeCache(pos)` - the accumulated direction of the road at the car - which is precisely the term `bendPx` SUBTRACTS.

SO THE FIX IS NOT A SMALLER `SKY_BEND_AT`. Shortening the distance would keep reading curvature and simply read less of it, which is a quieter version of the same wrong quantity. The driver has to change from the road's deviation ahead to the car's own heading.

AND THE COEFFICIENT WILL HAVE TO BE RE-TUNED, because the two quantities are in different units - 0.55 was fitted against a deviation in pixels and a heading is not one. It goes in configuration with a committed default, never an edited-in-place constant, so the owner can ask for more or less of it after seeing it move.

THIS IS A FEEL CHANGE AND ONLY THE DEVICE CAN JUDGE IT. A harness can prove the skyline now answers the car's heading rather than the road ahead - park the car on a straight with a bend far ahead and the skyline must NOT move, then turn the car and it must - but whether the amount looks right is the owner's.
