### RLG-301 — A canyon shows its wall head-on as you drive into it, and in the mirror as you leave
- **RULED 2026-09-20T15:34:09-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20, from the device: 'What we do need is the canyon walls facing the roadway when you're driving towards the canyon and also in the rearview mirror when you exit one.' RLG-297's walls run ALONGSIDE the road and there is nothing at the END of them: a canyon is entered and left through open air. What is missing is the face you see HEAD-ON as the road runs at a canyon mouth, and the same face behind you in the glass once you are through. THE PLACE BOUNDARY IS WHERE IT GOES - `biomeEdge`, the segment at which one place hands over to the next, which the planner already sets a draw distance ahead and which `bioMix` blends across. The wall's own plane and colour are already built and per-slice; an end wall is that plane turned to face the camera at one z rather than running past it. WATCH the skyline hand-over (RLG-022 SKY_SWAP), because a canyon's skyline band is capped to its own wall height and the two must not disagree at the seam. Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-20T16:14:42-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-20, v0.14.110. A CANYON USED TO ARRIVE AS A HAIRLINE, and a capture of the approach is what showed it: from a mile of desert the place was a thin vertical sliver standing at the vanishing point, widening as you reached it. The walls were doing exactly what RLG-297 built them to do - running alongside - and nothing stood at the end of them, so there was nothing to drive AT.

THE FACE IS THE MASSIF. A slot canyon is a cut THROUGH rock, so what you see coming is a wall across the whole view with a NOTCH in it and the road going through the notch. That is what is drawn: the place's own rock from the wall's own height down to the ground at the boundary, filling the frame on both sides of the slot - and THE SLOT IS EXACTLY THE GAP THE TWO WALLS LEAVE, `wallFootX` either side, so the face and the walls cannot disagree about how wide the way through is.

IT IS BUILT IN COLUMNS, BECAUSE A MASSIF HAS A SKYLINE. The first build drew two rectangles and its top was a DEAD STRAIGHT LINE across the whole frame - a mesa, and the same flat-wall complaint the face beside the road had already earned. It is cut into `END_COLS` columns now, each taking its height from the SAME ridge noise the walls use, sampled along the LATERAL axis instead of along the road: one noise, two directions, so the rock that faces you and the rock that runs beside you are the same rock. The area is what the two rectangles cost; only the call count grows.

AND IT IS IN THE GLASS WHEN YOU LEAVE. The drop had to be put in the mirror twice and the wall once, so this went in with the windscreen's rather than after a report. THE PLACE IS READ FROM THE SEGMENT BEYOND THE EDGE rather than from `biomeFrom`, because `biomeFrom` becomes the NEW place the moment the change completes - which is exactly when you are looking back at the one you left.

AND THE BUG THAT CHECK NUMBER THREE EXISTS FOR. `widx >= biomeEdge` is true for EVERY step of the mirror's walk once the edge has fallen past `MIRROR_BACK`, so the first version pinned the massif at the back of the pane and left it there for ever: measured still painting with the boundary 52,000 units behind a glass that looks 34,000 back. The edge has to be INSIDE the glass's reach for there to be anything to look at.

PROVEN by tools/canyon-mouth-test.py (EVD-1153). Painted on 16 and 14 frames with the boundary 120 and 40 segments ahead; on 11 and 12 with it 30 and 120 behind, which is the glass; on ZERO with it 240 behind, which is past the glass. Taking the face away through `API.endWallOff` moves 10,819 pixels. A FOREST boundary paints none either way, which is the control that stops all of it passing on a build that puts a face at every boundary there is. --falsify serves the face never drawn and THE THREE POSITIVE CHECKS FALL WHILE THE TWO ABSENCE CHECKS HOLD (EVD-1154) - an arm that took those two down would have broken the harness rather than removed the feature.

WHAT IS COUNTED IS FRAMES PAINTED, NOT PIXELS, for the reason the valley range's check had to learn: how much of the face shows is the road's slope and how far the boundary is.

skyline-test, biome-test and wall-plane-test pass - the hand-over this ruling warned about is intact. smoke-test 7/7; drive-test 36/37 on the recorded settle flake.

STILL THE OWNER'S: whether the massif reads as rock you are driving at, and whether `END_COLS` at 22 breaks its top enough or reads as a row of pillars.
