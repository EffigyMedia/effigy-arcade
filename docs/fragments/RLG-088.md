### RLG-088 — Three, two, one, GO before the car moves
- **RULED 2026-08-30T20:11:31-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-30: hitting DRIVE should give a three, two, one, GO countdown, made with some flare.

IT IS A START LINE, WHICH IS A THING THE GAME DOES NOT HAVE YET. Today DRIVE tears down the menu and the car is already moving. A countdown is not decoration on that: it is the moment the run begins, and it gives the player somewhere to put their thumb before the road starts arriving.

THE CAR MUST BE HELD, NOT MERELY COVERED. A countdown drawn over a car that is already accelerating is a lie the first frame gives away. The run has to be built, the world drawn, and the car held at a standstill until GO - which also means the throttle has to do nothing until then, or the player will be moving before the word appears.

WHAT THE FLARE SHOULD BE MADE OF, and it is worth writing down before building anything, because flare is where a menu screen goes wrong: the numbers belong to the same type the HUD already uses, they arrive rather than fade, and each one is smaller and calmer than a person expects while GO is the one that is not. Sound carries most of it - three pips and a higher tone on GO - and this game already has a synthesizer with a ui bus to put them on. A screen shake or a colour flash on GO is the kind of thing to try and then take away again.

AND IT IS SKIPPABLE AFTER THE FIRST TIME, or it becomes a toll. A player who has just crashed and wants another go should not sit through three seconds to get one. A tap during the count should shorten it rather than cancel it, so the start is still a start.

THE CIRCUIT ALREADY HAS A GRID. Motorsport starts a race from a standing start, so whatever this becomes has to agree with what a race start already does rather than run alongside it - one countdown, used by both, or a clear reason why the circuit keeps its own.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T20:30:40-04:00** — BUILT, 2026-08-30. Three seconds, held rather than covered, with the world running behind it.

NEITHER MODE HAD A STANDING START, so the question of agreeing with the circuit answered itself: the circuit builds its field and then moves off exactly as the highway does. One count-in serves both, and there was no second start sequence to reconcile.

THE CAR IS HELD BY GATING THE WHOLE OF step(), not by zeroing the throttle. Speed, the run clock, distance and score all sit still because the frame returns before any of them is touched, while everything drawn afterwards keeps running. That is what lets a player look down the road they are about to take.

WHERE THE FLARE ACTUALLY COMES FROM, and it is not a bigger font. It is TIMING. Each number arrives at 1.35 of its size and settles back inside a fifth of a second, and a ring leaves it as it lands and is gone by two fifths - so the eye reads a hit rather than a label. The numbers are deliberately calmer than a person expects, which is the only reason GO feels like anything: it is bigger, green against their orange, it lifts as it goes, and it stays on the glass while the car is already moving, so the word and the launch happen together instead of one after the other.

THE SOUND IS THE OPPOSITE OF THE RUN-OUT TICK, deliberately. That one holds a single note and gets more insistent, because the note beside it correctly says a rising pitch reads as a fanfare and a clock running out should be ominous. A start should be a fanfare, so the pips climb a whole tone each and GO is a major chord with a sweep under it.

THE THROTTLE SHORTENS IT AFTER THE FIRST RUN, to a third of a second, and does not cancel it - a third of a second still reads as a start, and dropping straight to GO would take the launch away. It is the second run onward, so the count is always seen whole once.

THE CHECK HOLDS THE PEDAL DOWN FROM BEFORE THE COUNT BEGINS. That is the whole of what makes it a test: a car nobody is asking to move sits still whether it is held or not, and the first version passed on a build with no countdown in it at all. With the pedal down throughout, speed reads 0 while held and 1001 afterwards. Remove the hold and three checks fail together - the car reaches 4490 with 2.83 seconds still to go, it covers 0.078 miles, and 2.82 seconds come off the run clock.

WHAT IS NOT CHECKED: whether the flare lands. Whether a number reads as a hit rather than a caption is the owner's eye on the device, and no harness can be given that judgement.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T20:48:40-04:00** — TWO REGRESSIONS, BOTH REPORTED BY THE OWNER WITHIN MINUTES OF THE BUILD.

THE WORLD CHANGED AT GO, AND IT WAS TWO CORRECT CHANGES MEETING BADLY. RLG-022 made biomeStarted clear at every reset, so a run chooses its own opening place instead of inheriting the last one's. This fragment then made the count-in return from step() before stepBiome runs, so the car is held. Separately both are right. Together, the opening place was chosen on the FIRST FRAME AFTER THE COUNT - at GO, in front of the player, as a snap. Measured: FOREST for the whole three seconds, then OCEAN the instant the count ended.

THE PICK IS ITS OWN FUNCTION AND THE RESET CALLS IT. The world the player looks at for three seconds is the world they drive into. This is the argument for a reset owning its state outright rather than leaving it to whichever function happens to run first - exactly the shape RLG-090 has just been filed about.

AND A COUNTDOWN DOES NOT CLIMB. The owner: the pitch going up while counting down is wrong. It is, and this file had already said so - the note on the run-out tick argues that a pitch climbing each second reads as a FANFARE and that a countdown holds one note and gets more insistent. That was written about a clock running out and it is just as true of a start light, which repeats and then changes rather than rises. The pips are one 392 Hz note now; what changes is how hard it is struck, how long it rings and how much low weight sits under it, which leaves GO as the only different note in the sequence and therefore the only one that reads as a release. I had the right note in front of me and wrote the opposite of it.

THE CHECK SAMPLES THE PLACE ACROSS THE COUNT AND PAST IT, and compares the first sample against every later one. Asking merely whether a biome exists would have passed; the question is whether it is the SAME one. Leaving the pick to the first frame fails it with the exact transition the owner described.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T20:59:00-04:00** — AND THE HORIZON STILL SNAPPED. Owner, 2026-08-30, on the build that stopped the whole world changing at GO: the ground holds still now, but the far end instantly changes to the next biome, so there is still a massive jump.

biomeNext WAS LEFT AT ZERO, WHICH MEANS A CHANGE IS DUE. Choosing the opening place at the reset fixed the ground under the car and left the sky doing exactly what it had been doing, one step further out: the first stepBiome after the count found the countdown already expired and placed a new place at the horizon on that frame. A place you have just arrived in is not also a place you are leaving, so the distance to the next one is armed when the opening place is chosen, with the same range every other change uses.

THE CHECK NOW WATCHES THE HORIZON AS WELL AS THE GROUND. It read biomeSweep().player alone, which is the place at the CAR - so a ground that held still while the far end changed read as one unchanged biome and passed. With the arming removed it reads DESERT then SWAMP, which is the owner's report in two words.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T14:49:39-04:00** — THE THROTTLE SHORTCUT WAS REMOVED IN v0.10.27, by [[RLG-110]], and this records why so nobody puts it back without knowing.

`hurryStart()` shortened a repeat count-in to 0.34 seconds on a press of the gas pedal, and the reason was good: three seconds of waiting is a toll on exactly the player who is enjoying the game most. IT WAS RIGHT WHILE THE COUNT WAS WAITING. It is not waiting any more. The car is held in neutral and the throttle is how the player sets the revs for the launch, so the one button that skipped the count is now the button the count exists for - and a third of a second is not long enough to catch the rev window in.

The shortcut and the feature cannot share the throttle, and the feature is the reason the count is there at all. It was removed rather than moved to the brake, because a player resting a thumb on the brake at the lights would skip a start they meant to make.

THE TOLL IS NOW PAID BY THE FEATURE INSTEAD: the three seconds are something to DO rather than something to sit through. Whether that is enough is the owner's, on the device. If a skip is still wanted it is one line, and the control it goes on is the open question.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE: `seenStart` is still set when the first count of a session completes, and `tools/start-test.py` still asserts that the first start of a session is seen whole and that the game remembers it. Nothing in that harness asserted the hurry itself, so its checks still pass unchanged.
