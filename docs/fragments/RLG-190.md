### RLG-190 — The production cars' door mirrors were drawn at one end only
- **RULED 2026-09-09T16:40:29-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"you haven't done any re-work for production cars regarding matching
silhouettes front and back?"*

**THEY WERE REWORKED AND THEY WERE PASSING, AND THE QUESTION WAS STILL A GOOD ONE.** COUPE, SALOON,
CAB, CRUISER, MUSCLE, TUNER and ROADSTER all came down from 10-35 per cent to between 2.8 and 3.8 in
[[RLG-184]] - under the 4 per cent limit, so the check went quiet, and every one of those seven sat
ONE EDIT away from failing a thing it was never actually satisfying. Thirteen other bodies measured
0.0. A family clustered just under a threshold while the rest of the fleet is exact is a finding, not
a pass.

**IT WAS THE SAME FAULT AS EVERYWHERE ELSE: A DOOR MIRROR ON THE FACE AND NONE ON THE TAIL.** The
profile said so exactly - columns 3-4 and 19-20, the shoulders, where the front's ink began about 3
per cent of the box higher than the back's. That is the mirror standing proud of the greenhouse just
outside its edge. It is now in `rigFurniture` under `saloon` and both painters call it.

**MEASURED AFTER: all seven read 0.0.** Thirteen of the seventeen bodies are now exact and the
remaining four - CREST, CRUISER, MATADOR, STALLION - sit between 0.7 and 0.9 per cent, which is the
antialiased end of the ground shadow and is as close as pixel rounding allows.

**AND ONE PIECE OF DEAD DUPLICATION WENT WITH IT.** The roadster's twin humps were written twice in
`paintRig`, one block immediately after the other, drawing the same two opaque shapes in the same
place. Removing the second copy changes no pixel.
