### UNT-200 - Heat points are worth less, and cooling is slower to match

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-08T01:48:38-04:00** - The owner ruled that incidents should be worth fewer points so the stars climb more slowly and a hot pursuit lasts longer. The three earning rates fall to roughly two fifths - a sighting from 50 to 20, a roadblock 70 to 30, a takedown 80 to 35 - so a star takes five incidents instead of two. The decay moved with them, because at the old rate a 20-point sighting was undone in under three seconds and the level would never have climbed; the lever is HEAT_COOL, which is stated in seconds to shed a star, and it goes from 12 to 30. That holds what an incident is worth in seconds of running almost unchanged while more than doubling the incidents a star costs. Both pursuit harnesses turned out to be killed by the run clock rather than by the retune, which is written up in RLG-169.
