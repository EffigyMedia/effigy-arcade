### RLG-251 — Returning to the garage from any mode shows the car last driven
- **RULED 2026-09-15T01:38:09-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-15: "When you return to the garage from any mode, it should return you to the car you last used."

WHAT "ANY MODE" MUST COVER: TEST DRIVE, SINGLE RACE, TOURNAMENT and INTERCEPT, each left by finishing, by wrecking or being busted, and by quitting from pause; and a reward screen that opens between the run and the garage (`showUnlock` loads the won car into `optBody` to draw it). The car is saved by key in `interstate-opts.body`.

NOT YET MEASURED: which of those paths lands on a different car today. Candidates: `showUnlock` not restoring on every exit, `enforceCarRules` moving the player when a mode and a car disagree, and a reboot reading a stale save. Measure each path with a harness before changing anything, and make the harness the proof.

INTERACTION WITH RLG-250: a fresh save opens on SALOON; a player who has driven opens on the car they last drove, even if that is mid-list.
