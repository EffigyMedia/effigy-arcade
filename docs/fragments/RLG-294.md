### RLG-294 — A manual gearbox is worth a little acceleration and top speed
- **RULED 2026-09-20T00:13:13-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20, while specifying the leaderboard: 'I think we should slightly bump the acceleration and top speed of every car when manual transmission is being used.' EVERY CAR, and the bonus belongs to the CHOICE rather than to the car - it is the reward for working the gate, so it is one pair of numbers applied to whatever car the player is in, not a row per body. HOW MUCH is 'slightly' and is the owner's to tune; it will be a tunable with a committed default, and the numbers must be stated to the owner when built. QUESTIONS THIS RAISES, to be settled before or during the build: whether a rival - which always shifts, and holds its own gear (RLG-156) - counts as manual and gets the same bonus, which ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR would say it does; and whether a leaderboard entry should record which gearbox set it, since a manual run is faster by the bonus. Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-20T01:00:13-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.97. The bonus is FOUR per cent of pull and TWO per cent of top end, as MANUAL_ACCEL and MANUAL_TOP - tunables with committed defaults, and 'slightly' is the owner's to move. It is one pair of numbers over whatever car the player is in, never a row in BODY, because it is what working the gate is worth. Two helpers carry it - playerPull() and playerTop() - and EVERY place the player's car is driven by reads them: the launch's kick and its shove, the gear ceiling, the acceleration term, the rev-limiter's overrun and the target it pulls back to, the gear band's own speed ceiling, and the stat the probe reports.

THE OVERRUN WAS THE TRAP. The limiter compares the speed with the car's declared top end, so a manual car reached the bonus and was immediately read as over-revving and pulled straight back to the declared figure - the top-end half of the bonus would have done nothing at all. Both sides of that comparison read playerTop() now.

A RIVAL IS NOT GIVEN IT, and the owner has the question. Every rival holds a gear and changes it (RLG-156), so one reading of 'manual' would hand the bonus to the whole field, which is a change to how every race runs rather than to what the player's choice is worth.

PROVEN by tools/manual-bonus-test.py (EVD-1119), which measures the SPEED and not the constants: the same HATCH driven with the bonus set to 1.0 and 1.0, then to the committed defaults, on both gearboxes. Manual settles at 1.0200 of its top end against 1.0000, and reaches four fifths of its top end in 10.85 s against 11.40 s; the automatic is unmoved, 11.44 s against 11.48 s, and still settles at its declared figure. NOT VACUOUS: with the engine ignoring the bonus the lift check failed at 1.0000 against 1.02; the sooner check passed on 0.06 s of noise in that run and now requires a quarter-second margin against a measured gain of 0.55 s. A run spoiled by the road - one in several settles a few per cent low - is driven again rather than reported.

COMPARING THE TWO GEARBOXES DIRECTLY MEASURES THE GEAR TABLE, NOT THE BONUS, which is why the check does not: in manual the speed is capped by the gear the car is in. smoke-test and gate-test pass (EVD-1118, EVD-1116); drive-test fails only the known flaky nitrous settle line (EVD-1115). Whether four and two per cent FEEL like enough is the owner's, on the device.
