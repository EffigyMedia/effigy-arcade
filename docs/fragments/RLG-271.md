### RLG-271 — A cop in front slows you more than you can escape
- **RULED 2026-09-16T02:21:36-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-16: "When a cop gets in front of you the amount of deceleration that's forced upon you is way too much. You can't get out of it."

THIS IS THE BOX AT SPEED ([[RLG-158]]). A cruiser that gets in front deliberately runs at nine tenths of the player's speed to slow them down, and the thread already carried the warning that nobody had judged whether it makes a pursuit unwinnable. The owner has now judged it: it does.

WHAT THE REPAIR MUST NOT DO. The box is the police having an answer to a faster car, and removing it hands every pursuit back to top speed. What the owner reports is that the deceleration has no way OUT of it, which is a different complaint from the box existing - so the question is what the player's counter is, not whether the cruiser may sit there.

MEASURE FIRST, and the recorded reason is that this is a number the driver can influence. Read what the box actually does to the player's speed over time, with a driver that tries to escape rather than one holding the throttle straight - swerving, braking to drop back, the horn. A cruiser's own ceiling is `copTop` and it was breached by this same formula once already, so read the cruiser's speed at the same time.

THE LIKELY TUNABLES are the box station's speed fraction, how long a cruiser holds the station, and how close it sits. All three are single constants.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T02:37:12-04:00** — MEASURED 2026-09-16, AND THE OWNER'S REPORT IS EXACTLY WHAT THE CODE DOES. `tools/stop-zone-test.py` pins the player at 120mph with `holdSpd` - a stronger throttle than a thumb can hold, because it writes the speed every frame - and a cruiser in the player's line still takes the car to ZERO. Started 120, lowest 0, ended 0. The swing-aside arm is clean in both directions: a player already out of the line keeps all 120, and one who swings out mid-drag goes 120 to 0 to 120 again. So the ESCAPE works and the DRAG is what is wrong.

THE MECHANISM IS `stopZoneCap`, AND IT HAS NO FLOOR. It ramps a cap down from the speed the car had, at `PURSUIT_STOP.decel` of MAX_SPD per second, toward zero, and the car is clamped to it after the throttle, the brake, NOS and `spdHold` - deliberately, so that the cap beats all of them. `decel` is 0.35, which is 70mph of speed taken away every second: 200mph to a dead stop in under three seconds, and it is the hardest braking in the game.

THE RULING'S OWN WORD IS "SLOWLY" AND THE NUMBER IS NOT. [[RLG-259]] quotes the owner: "It should force your car slowly to a stop and it's up to the player... to quickly swing to the side to get out from behind their slow down triggering zone." 0.35 is not slowly. The number was set beside Intercept's three and was never measured against the word.

AND TWO EFFECTS ARE STACKED WHERE ONE WAS ASKED FOR. The cruiser in front ALSO brakes itself, at the same `PURSUIT_STOP.decel`, through `forceSpd` in the box station ([[RLG-247]]) - so the blocker is running away downward while the zone drags the player down independently. The player's cap is seeded from the PLAYER'S speed, which is the higher of the two, so the two ramps do not cancel: the player is dragged through the blocker's speed and on to zero rather than settling behind it.

THE BOX IS THE THIRD TERM AND IT IS WHY "YOU CAN'T GET OUT OF IT". Escape needs `PURSUIT_STOP.wide` of lateral distance, which is 0.55. The flanking stations sit at plus and minus 0.42 of the player's own x. With a full box of three there is no 0.55 of clear road to move into that is not occupied by a flanker, so the escape the owner specified is reachable against ONE cruiser and unreachable against three.

NOT CHANGED YET. Which of the three terms moves is the owner's call, and every one of them is a single constant: `PURSUIT_STOP.decel`, a floor on the cap at the blocker's own speed, and `PURSUIT_STOP.wide` against the 0.42 flanking offset.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T03:04:08-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.49, ALL THREE TERMS, ON THE OWNER'S DECISION OF 2026-09-16. They were offered the three separately and chose all three.

`decel` 0.35 to 0.18. [[RLG-259]]'s own word is that the zone should force the car "slowly to a stop", and 0.35 took 70mph away every second - 200mph to a dead stop in under three seconds, the hardest braking anywhere in this game. 0.18 is about five and a half seconds over the same range.

A FLOOR AT THE BLOCKER'S OWN SPEED, which is new. `stopZoneCap` ramped to zero and kept going, so the car behind was dragged THROUGH the blocker and on to a standstill - two brakes stacked on one car, because the cruiser in front is separately braking itself at this same rate through `forceSpd`, and the cap here is seeded from the speed of the car BEHIND, the higher of the two. Now you settle behind the car in front. Staying there still stops you, because the blocker goes on braking itself: the stop is something the police car does rather than something the zone does.

`wide` 0.55 to 0.32, AND IT GOES DOWN RATHER THAN UP. `wide` is how far off the line a car may be and still be blocked, so a WIDER zone is a zone that is HARDER to leave - the first attempt at this raised it and had the sign backwards. At 0.55 the escape did not exist against a full box at all: the flanking stations sit at 0.42 either side of the player, so clearing 0.55 from the car in front meant steering into a car that was already there. 0.32 is just over the width of the cars themselves and is reachable before the flanker is.

MEASURED, WITH THE HARNESS REPAIRED FIRST. Before: a player pinned at 120mph by `holdSpd` went to 0. After: 120 to 65, settling at 66, which is the blocker's own 66mph exactly. Swinging aside keeps all 120; swinging aside mid-drag goes 120 to 65 to 120. On shift the same rule reversed reads 13,033 down to 9,200 - the player's police car's speed - and back to 13,033 when the rival gets out of the line. The floor was watched failing: with it taken back out the arm reads 0mph behind a cruiser doing 66.

THREE DEFECTS IN `stop-zone-test` HAD TO BE FIXED BEFORE ANY OF THAT COULD BE READ, and all three were invisible while the zone ramped to zero, because a rule that takes every blocked car to zero passes whatever the staging does. (1) The cruiser was RE-PLACED EVERY FRAME AT THE PLAYER'S OWN SPEED, so once the floor existed it sat exactly on top of the car it was supposed to be slowing - it read 108 of a 120mph pin on the same build measured at 59. It is given a speed of its own now, below the player's, which is what a car you are stuck behind is. (2) THE LINED ARM NEVER HELD ITS LANE: `setLane(0)` was called once before the loop and the car wandered to x 1.12, the barrier, so the blocker kept dropping out of the zone and the drag kept being released and re-seeded. That is what made the arm swing between 66 and 100 on one unchanged build. It re-issues the lane every frame now, as the aside arm always did, and reads 331 of 331 samples in the zone. (3) THE INTERCEPT ARM PINNED THE RIVAL AND THE PLAYER AT THE SAME SPEED, so the rival started on its own floor and the arm read 9200 of 9200 - a drag of nothing on a build that drags. The rival is held above the player now.

AND THE ARM NOW SAYS WHAT THE BLOCKER WAS DOING. "No drag" and "no blocker" look identical in a column of mph and are completely different failures - one is a broken rule, the other a broken arm - so every arm prints the blocker's dz, its lateral position, the player's own, and how many samples had it inside the zone. Two of the three defects above were found by that line and by nothing else.

PLACING THE CRUISER ONCE AND LETTING THE ENGINE DRIVE IT WAS TRIED AND IS WORSE. It brakes itself out of its own station, the pinned player goes straight past, and it was measured 27,000 units BEHIND the player having spent 0 of 331 samples inside the zone. Setting `cool` to 0 to make it aggressive did not save it. The re-place stays.

WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE, and it is the owner's on a device: whether five and a half seconds reads as pressure or as a nuisance, and whether settling behind the blocker rather than being stopped by it still feels like being caught. Both are single numbers - `PURSUIT_STOP.decel` and the floor itself.

pursuit-stop-test and shift-stop-test both pass unchanged on this build, which is what says the two neighbouring rules did not move under it.
