### RLG-326 — No car shows its damage in the mirror
- **RULED 2026-09-22T20:34:22-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-22, while the frame cost was being measured: "car damage smoke & fire not shown in mirror".

CONFIRMED IN THE CODE, AND IT IS EVERY CAR RATHER THAN THE PLAYER'S. The damage effects have two callers and both of them are the FRONT view. `damageFx` is called once for the player at the end of `drawPlayer`, and `damageFxOn` is called for traffic, rivals and police inside `paintBucket`, which is the front pass's sprite painter. The glass paints its own cars with a bare `drawImage` and calls neither. So a burning rival two lengths behind you is a clean sprite in the mirror, and the player's own smoke - which trails BEHIND the car and is therefore mostly a mirror object - is not drawn at all.

IT MATTERS MORE THAN A MISSING DECORATION. The mirror is where a pursuit is read, and damage is the thing the player is tracking when they look back at all. A rival that is about to drop out of the race looks exactly like one that is not.

AND IT IS ENTANGLED WITH [[RLG-299]], WHICH IS WHY IT IS RECORDED HERE RATHER THAN BUILT. The same measurement session found that the player's damage effects are the most expensive thing in the frame after the ground: the player car costs nothing at all undamaged and 6.8 fps at 95 damage, measured at four damage levels with 5 of 5 pairs agreeing at 60 and above. Adding the same effects to the glass adds that cost a second time, to the frame that is already the slowest. So the two have to be decided together: the effects want to be CHEAPER before they are drawn twice.

NOT STARTED. The owner has not been asked yet which way this goes.
