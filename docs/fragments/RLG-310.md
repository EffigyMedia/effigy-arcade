### RLG-310 — A run started on a white-floored city
- **RULED 2026-09-21T00:16:47-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21, from the device: 'I had a run start with a white floored city. Maybe hold over data from improperly quitting a previous run? Or finishing one? I dunno.'

A WHITE CITY MAY BE CORRECT, AND THAT HAS TO BE RULED OUT FIRST. [[RLG-109]] made a city's climate ROLLED rather than stated, with `vary` 0.45 - the widest on the board - precisely so that 'drive into one city and it is under snow, into the next and it is warm rain'. The ground's whiteness is derived: `groundSnow()` is the greater of what has SETTLED this run and the place's own permanent cover, and the cover comes from the instance's snow floor. A cold-rolled city at the start of a run is white ground with no snow falling, by design, and it is indistinguishable at a glance from the leak the owner suspects.

SO THE MEASUREMENT IS THE ROLLED TEMPERATURE, NOT THE WHITENESS. `API.climateNow` and `API.snowFloor` answer it directly: a cold instance is the feature, a warm instance with white ground is the defect. Nothing should be changed until that one number is read.

IF IT IS A LEAK, THE SHAPE IS KNOWN AND HAS BEEN FOUND HERE TWICE. [[RLG-111]] is 'standing water and a scheduled thunderclap survived a reset'. The reset sets `settle = 0` and `settleMelt = 0`, but the derived cover is not run state at all - it is read live from the climate pair, so a `reset()` that leaves `climFrom` or `climTo` holding the last run's instance produces exactly this picture, and zeroing `settle` would not touch it. That is the first place to look and it is a different variable from the one the owner's phrasing points at.

THE REPORT ALSO NAMES TWO EXITS - quitting badly, and finishing - and they are not the same path. [[RLG-090]] already established that a retry starts a new run rather than continuing the last, so there is precedent for the two exits leaving different state behind.

Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-21T09:19:50-04:00** — MEASURED 2026-09-21, AND IT IS NOT A LEAK. No product code changed. What changed is the harness that was reporting one.

A WHITE CITY AT THE START OF A RUN IS A COLD-ROLLED CITY, which is [[RLG-109]]'s design: a city rolls its own climate with `vary` 0.45, the widest on the board, so 'drive into one city and it is under snow, into the next and it is warm rain'. Over 4,000 real run openings, 407 opened in a city and 77 of them - 19 per cent, about one in five - had white ground. THE WARMEST WHITE CITY WAS ROLLED AT 7 DEGREES F, and none of the 77 was above 40.

AND THE TWO EXITS THE OWNER NAMED CARRY NOTHING. A run was made white on purpose, then left, then a new run started - 31 times by quitting mid-run and 29 by finishing a race. Settled snow carried over zero times on both, and no city opened both white and warm. Every new run goes `start` -> `reset` -> `freshWorld` -> `openBiome`, which zeroes `settle` and rolls a fresh climate.

BUT `retry-test` WAS REPORTING A LEAK, AND THAT HARNESS IS THE GUARD FOR EXACTLY THIS QUESTION. It dirties a run with settled snow, retries, waits 500ms, and asserts nothing has settled. It failed today with settle at 0.057 on a run that opened in a TUNDRA - and a mountain or a tundra has a snow floor of its own that fills at 0.12 a second, so half a second in, a clean reset reads 0.057 from the NEW place settling. Measured over 24 resets: settle read 0.000 at the instant of every one, and was non-zero at 500ms only where the opening place had a floor - 0.058, 0.057, 0.055 in mountains at floors of 0.183 to 0.222, and 0.059 in a tundra at 0.272. About one run in six opens somewhere cold, so the harness cried leak about one run in six.

IT READS AT THE INSTANT OF THE RESET NOW, which is the only moment the question can be asked, and standing water the same way for the same reason - a run can open in rain. Run until it opened cold: a MOUNTAIN at a floor of 0.193 and a TUNDRA at 0.297, both passing. And with `settle = 0` taken out of `freshWorld`, it reads 0.911 carried and fails - which is also exactly the white city the owner saw, so the guard is guarding the right thing. Its `storm` precondition was loosened from exactly 1 to above 0.9, because the sky is eased and read 0.926 400ms after being set.

ONE THING IT IS WORTH ASKING THE OWNER, AND IT IS TASTE: one city opening in five is white. That follows from how often a city rolls cold, not from anything broken, and it is `vary` on the CITY row if it is too often.
