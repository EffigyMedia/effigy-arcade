### RLG-234 — verify-097-100 passes three cars that never moved

**MEASURED 2026-09-13**, running it after [[RLG-233]] changed the very arithmetic it exists to
check. **DIAGNOSED ONLY — nothing is fixed.**

---

## WHAT IT PRINTED

    TUNER     clock  60.0 ->  60.0  (lowest  60.0)   0.00 miles, 0 crate(s)
              reached 0 mph of the 146 this car declares
    ok    TUNER does not run the clock out at full throttle
    ROADSTER  clock  60.0 ->  60.0  (lowest  60.0)   0.00 miles, 0 crate(s)
              reached 0 mph of the 153 this car declares
    ok    ROADSTER does not run the clock out at full throttle
    MUSCLE    clock  60.0 ->  60.0  (lowest  60.0)   0.00 miles, 0 crate(s)
              reached 0 mph of the 160 this car declares
    ok    MUSCLE does not run the clock out at full throttle

    both claims survive the checks a harness can make

**No car moved. No clock ran. Three green marks.** The check is "does the clock reach zero", and a
clock that never starts never reaches zero — so a car that fails to launch passes by doing nothing
at all.

## AND IT PRINTS ITS OWN NUMBERS, WHICH ARE NOW WRONG

    checkpoints are 2 miles apart and pay 20s

That was true until [[RLG-233]]. It is a line of text rather than a reading, so it will keep saying
twenty whatever the table holds.

## WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND ONE TOOL

**[[RLG-100]] IS STILL OPEN AND THIS IS THE SECOND TIME ITS INSTRUMENT HAS FAILED IT.** The owner
ruled on 2026-08-31 that the crate's ten seconds had made the ruling unnecessary and asked for a
verification; this tool was written for that and **was not run until 2026-09-08**, when it reported
TUNER 3.46 miles, ROADSTER 1.89 and MUSCLE 3.94. Now it reports 0.00 for all three and calls it a
pass.

**IT IS THE THIRD GUARD FOUND BROKEN IN A DAY**, and the shape is the same every time: a harness
that was right when it was written, left alone while the thing under it moved.

|---|---|
| [[RLG-182]] | `garage-card-test` walked three cars that a fresh save cannot own |
| [[RLG-226]] | `face-test` named its own traffic kinds and had never heard of the ambulance |
| this | `verify-097-100` passes cars that never leave the line |

## WHAT A FIX NEEDS

1. **A precondition that the car actually drove.** Miles covered and top speed reached are already
   printed — they must be asserted. A run of 0.00 miles is BLOCKED, which is not a pass. That one
   change turns every future silent failure into a loud one.
2. **Find out why the car does not launch.** It drove on 2026-09-08. Something between then and now
   stopped it, and the reason matters more than the check — the same wedge may be under other
   harnesses. Do not assume it is this tool's own fault.
3. **Read the award from the engine**, not from a sentence. `API` can answer what a checkpoint pays
   now that it varies by class.

**DO NOT TREAT [[RLG-100]] AS ANSWERED UNTIL THIS RUNS AGAIN.** The 2026-09-08 numbers are the last
real readings anyone has, and they predate [[RLG-233]] entirely.
