### RLG-218 — One arrival band for everything at the draw edge, because a translucent tree is a hole

**RULED 2026-09-12.** Owner, from the device: *"Cars (and pickup crates) can be seen through the
scenery again!"* And, correcting where I went looking: *"When I play and the road bends around the
corner I can see vehicles through the scenery. That's all the problem is, the trees were fine. That
semi is fine."* Then: *"So the vehicles need the same Alpha ramp or else that just looks funky."*

**I SPENT THE FIRST HALF OF THIS LOOKING AT THE WRONG THINGS** — a trailer parked on the verge and
the shape of the trees in the mirror — and the owner had to say so before it stopped. Both were
fine. The report named the condition precisely: **a bend**, and **vehicles through scenery**.

---

## THE MECHANISM, MEASURED

`drawScenery` set `ctx.globalAlpha = Math.min(1, fade * 4)`, and `fade` is the distance ramp that
reaches 1 only right in front of the car. **So everything in the far QUARTER of the draw was drawn
translucent.** Counted on one forest frame through `API.sceneryFrame`:

    12,733 scenery objects in the frame
     4,306 of them drawn at an alpha below 0.99 — a third of the roadside
         0 is the lowest alpha reached

**AND THE TREES DID LOOK FINE, WHICH IS WHY IT SURVIVED.** A translucent tree standing in front of
more trees of the same colour is invisibly translucent. It only becomes a hole when something
light-coloured is behind it — and that is a car.

**WHY A BEND.** On a straight the far traffic sits at the vanishing point, where the road is, and
there is no tree between you and it. On a bend the road swings sideways and puts the traffic *behind
the far treeline* — which is exactly the quarter of the roadside that was see-through. The condition
in the report is the condition in the mechanism.

## AND THE CARS WERE ON A DIFFERENT SCHEDULE

The owner's third message is the other half and it was right. `drawSprite` carried its own
`FADE_IN = 0.16` — the last sixth of the road — while the roadside used a quarter and the lamp posts
used a quarter under a comment saying they should only fade *"at the far edge"*. **Three copies of
one idea, none of them agreeing.**

That is what makes a matched ramp matter rather than being tidiness: a car at the draw edge fading on
a sixth, against trees fading on a quarter, arrives crisp in front of a treeline that is still
half-there. With one band, a car that far away is exactly as faint as the tree in front of it, so it
cannot resolve through it.

## WHAT IT IS NOW

`EDGE_FADE` is declared once, beside `DRAW`, and `edgeFade(t)` is the one function. The cars, the
trees, the crops, the boats, the lamp posts and the lamps' own light all read it.

**The value is the mirror's.** [[RLG-128]] settled a band of six per cent through the glass for this
exact complaint, and the reasoning is written there: long enough that an object arrives, short enough
that it is never the see-through object the owner saw. There was never a reason for the windscreen to
answer differently.

Re-measured on the same frame: **1,095 see-through objects against 4,306** — a quarter of what it
was, and all of them in the last 6% of the draw where the cars behind them are now equally faint.

**THE SAME MISTAKE IS RECORDED TWICE ALREADY IN THIS FILE** and neither note was enough to stop the
third instance. The lamp posts: *"`globalAlpha = fade` made the whole post see-through for most of
its life... a steel column is not translucent at any distance."* The mirror, RLG-128, where the owner
reported it through the glass. Both fixed the site and left the pattern, and the lamp post's own note
still had `fade * 4` under a sentence describing a much shorter band — **the note was right about the
principle and the number beside it did not match**.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER

**Whether it is gone is the owner's verdict on the device.** The mechanism is measured and the count
is a quarter of what it was; that the remaining band is short enough, and that nothing now pops in at
the draw edge, are judgements about motion that no still frame and no counter makes. [[RLG-041]]
could not be closed from a desktop harness for the same reason.

**The arrival is now sharper for everything, and that is the trade.** `drawSprite`'s note records the
measurement behind its old sixth: a car at the draw edge is **6.7 pixels** on a 480-pixel screen,
measured with `spriteWidthAt` after I twice wrote "about a pixel" in the record without measuring it.
Six per cent of the draw is a shorter runway for that arrival than a sixth was. If the pop comes back,
the lever is `EDGE_FADE` and it is one number in one place — which it was not before this.

**`occlusion-test` swings, and it swung here.** Three runs after the change reported scenery crest
clips of 0, 134,130 and 8,001, and the zero run failed all three clip assertions. That is the road
being generated fresh per load, which [[RLG-062]] records and which the thread carries as a standing
warning never to quote a single run of this harness from. Nothing in this change goes near
`crestGate`; the alpha is applied after the clip is decided.
