### RLG-217 — Three orderings for the production class, and the hatchback is the short-geared one

**RULED 2026-09-12.** Owner: *"The saloon is slow, acceleration, max top speed least handling. The
hatchback is the quickest with the slowest top speed, but the best handling and the coupe is in the
middle balanced."* Then, correcting my reading of it in three lines: *"Hatch coupe saloon"*,
*"Acceleration is the opposite"*, *"Handling follows top speed"*.

**THE LAST OF THOSE WAS AMBIGUOUS AND IT WAS PUT BACK TO THE OWNER RATHER THAN GUESSED.** Read one
way it reverses the opening sentence and gives the saloon the best handling; the owner chose the
other, so handling runs the same way acceleration does. The three orderings are:

| | worst | middle | best |
|---|---|---|---|
| **top speed** | HATCH | COUPE | SALOON |
| **acceleration** | SALOON | COUPE | HATCH |
| **handling** | SALOON | COUPE | HATCH |

---

## WHAT WAS WRONG

The hatchback was given its numbers when it was built, and they put it **between** the other two on
all three axes. That is a reasonable-sounding place for a third car and it is not a design: it gave
the class three cars in a row rather than three cars with characters.

**The measurement is what makes the case.** Its acceleration lead over the coupe — the one thing its
own record claimed for it, *"the best of the three off the line"* — measured **9.5 seconds against
9.6**. A tenth of a second is nothing a player can feel, so the car's stated character did not exist
outside the comment describing it.

## WHAT IT IS NOW

The hatchback is the **short-geared** one. It has the quickest launch and the most grip in the class,
and it gives up the top end to have them. That is one coherent car rather than three adjustments:
short gears are why it leaps, and short gears are why it runs out.

    body      hp   mass   grip   vmax   launch   mech      -> accel   brake
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
    HATCH    180   1290   0.78   0.55     1.03   1.04         0.60    0.81
    COUPE    210   1340   0.73   0.60     0.87   1.03         0.54    0.75
    SALOON   160   1480   0.66   0.62     0.88   1.03         0.45    0.68

`accel` and `brake` are not typed in. They are `1.56 * sqrt(hp/mass) * launch` and `grip * mech`,
the model [[RLG-055]] established and the engine's own `accelOf` and `brakeOf` compute.

**THE COUPE IS UNTOUCHED.** It is the middle car, its table was already the middle, and the other two
were arranged around it. **The saloon had to move** because these orderings are relative and nobody
can be given a place without taking one: its top end went past the coupe to 0.62, which is what makes
it the long-legged one, and its launch came down to 0.88 because long gearing is what a high top
speed and a slow launch both are. Its grip did not move — it was already the lowest.

## WHAT THE CARS ACTUALLY DO NOW

Measured, not asserted. `stat-test.py --only cars` drives each body from a standstill in cleared air;
`brake-test.py --group production` holds a fixed speed and reads the push, the steering rate and the
stopping distance.

    body      0-60 (real)   top speed   answers the wheel   pushed wide   stops from 100
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    HATCH          8.9s       110 mph      3.39 lanes/s        0.2015        3397 units
    COUPE          9.6s       120 mph      3.28 lanes/s        0.2154        3689 units
    SALOON        11.3s       124 mph      3.11 lanes/s        0.2390        4060 units

All three orderings hold on every column. The hatchback's acceleration lead over the coupe is now
**0.7 seconds** where it was a tenth.

**ONE THING DID NOT MOVE AND IT IS SAID RATHER THAN HIDDEN.** The saloon's measured 0-60 is 11.3
seconds before the change and 11.3 after, so lowering its `launch` bought nothing on the road. The
two edits cancelled: `zeroSixty` reads speed as a fraction of the car's own top end, so raising
`vmax` keeps the car in its strong pull for longer and offset the shorter launch exactly. The
**ordering** was never in doubt — 11.3 against 9.6 and 8.9 — and the card's `accel` figure does move,
0.47 to 0.45. The lower launch stays because it is the physically true number for a car that was just
given the longest gearing in its class, not because it changed a measurement.

## AND THE TRAFFIC TABLE DISAGREED WITH THE GARAGE ALREADY

`TYPE_VMAX` is what a traffic car can do and `BODY[k].vmax` is what the one in your garage can do.
The note above the ambulance in that same table says a vehicle that disagrees with itself about its
own top end is the fault [[RLG-042]] was written to stop, and that the ambulance's two numbers *"went
up together"*.

**These three had drifted apart and nothing caught it, because nothing reads both tables at once.**
The traffic saloon and the traffic hatchback were both 0.58 against garage bodies of 0.56 and 0.58,
and the traffic coupe was 0.66 against a garage 0.60 — a coupe in traffic was six points quicker than
the one you can buy. They carry the body's own numbers now. `sedan2` is a second saloon shell with no
garage car of its own, so it takes the saloon's.

Raising what these cars CAN do changes nothing about what they DO: a Civilian cruises at the limit
whatever it is sitting in, which is the whole of *the car is capable, the driver decides*.

## THE HARNESSES HAD TO CHANGE BEFORE ANY OF THIS COULD BE MEASURED

**`brake-test.py` could not see a production car at all.** Its body list was eleven cars under a
comment claiming it was *"every driveable body"*, and it held the sports, super, formula and police
cars only. The production cars a new save starts in were never in it. The hatchback made that
visible, because it was added to a class the harness could not reach.

**They cannot share one entry speed**, and that file already knew why: its own note records that
entering at 150mph put the TUNER above its 146mph top end, aero over-run helped it stop, and it came
out braking better than two cars that brake better than it. A production car tops out at 110 to
124mph, so entering at 120 would have done the same thing to all three at once.

So the fleet is measured in **groups**, each carrying the speed it is measured at, every printed line
naming that speed, and **numbers from two groups are not comparable**. The racing group keeps 120mph
untouched: re-run after the change it reproduces [[RLG-055]]'s recorded stopping distances to within
about two per cent on all eleven cars, which is what says the refactor moved nothing.

**AND ONE OF ITS CHECKS WAS ASKING THE WRONG QUESTION OF A GROUP.** *"The difference between the best
and worst is worth feeling"* asserts a handling spread of at least 1.35 to 1. The production group
failed it at 1.05 to 1 — and the check was right about the number and wrong about what it means. A
class whose cars felt 1.35 to 1 apart would not be a class. The claim is about the range of the
**fleet**, so a group now declares whether it spans several classes; the racing group asserts it, and
a single class has its spread printed as a measurement. The minimum body counts moved with it: 6 and
4 were written for one group of eleven, and a group of three tripped them while every car in it was
measured perfectly well.

**`reach-test.py` carried a hand-written copy of seven top speeds**, under a docstring admitting it
was *"copied from BODY and will go stale if a car is retuned"*. It went stale the first time a car was
retuned. The numbers are print-only and fail nothing, which is exactly why nobody would have caught
it — it would have gone on reporting a shortfall against a ceiling that no longer existed. It asks
the engine now.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER

**Whether the class feels right is the owner's verdict on the device.** Every number here is a
headless desktop browser. The orderings are proved; that a 0.7-second gap and a 14mph gap are the
right SIZE of gap is a judgement no harness makes.

**The work vehicles are still unmeasured for brake and grip.** CAB, PICKUP, VAN and SEMI are
driveable after a hundred miles in TEST DRIVE and top out from 100mph down to 80, so they need a
third group at a third speed. Nobody has asked for their figures and none are quoted anywhere.

**Three notes changed and they are authored prose.** The coupe's read *"THE QUICKEST THING THAT IS
NOT A RACER"*, which the saloon's 124mph made false. The saloon's read *"ENTIRELY UNREMARKABLE"*,
which stopped being true the moment it became the fastest thing in the class.
