### RLG-028 — Interstate: cars without NOS do not collapse the gauges to the pedals
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:42-04:00** — - **Owner report:** cars without NOS did not have the gauges collapse down to the pedals.

- **What that means.** The HUD reserves room for a nitrous gauge whether or not the chosen car has
  one, so a car without it drives with a hole in its instrument layout instead of the remaining
  gauges closing up.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T11:21:26-04:00** — THE CLUSTER CLOSES UP OVER A BOTTLE THE CAR DOES NOT HAVE. The wiring for "this car has no nitrous"
already existed - `nonos` on the body, set in `syncBoxClass` - and it hid the bottle and the button.
What it did not do was reclaim the SPACE, because every element above the bottle measured its height
from the bottom of the stage with the bottle's room baked into the number.

ONE VARIABLE RATHER THAN FOUR NUMBERS. The cluster stacks bottom-up - pedals, bottle, shifter, dials
- and `--bottle` is how much room the bottle takes in that stack. Every offset above it is written as
a distance from the pedals PLUS that space, so setting it to zero closes the gap and nothing else has
to know why. Four offsets read it: the shifter, the paddles, the dials, and the dials again when the
manual box is on.

THE NUMBERS WERE CHOSEN SO THE CAR WITH A BOTTLE DOES NOT MOVE. 94 plus 38 is the 132 that was there
before, so a car with nitrous has exactly the layout it had; without one, the dials land at 115.7,
which is where the bottle's own floor was. Nothing new was designed - the hole was simply removed.

AND THE FIRST TWO VERSIONS OF THE CHECK WERE WRONG IN DIFFERENT WAYS.

It measured 200ms after the car changed and read "the stack did not close up" - the class was already
on and the dials were still travelling, because they transition over 0.18 seconds. A measurement
taken during an animation is a measurement of the animation.

Then it asserted the dials must stop above `#pedals`, and failed on a layout the capture shows to be
correct: `#pedals` is a CONTAINER, and the bottle itself sat inside its box. The pads are the
controls, so the pads are what must stay clear - and against those it passes with the dials landing 2
pixels above the gas pedal rather than 2 pixels into it.

Falsified: remove the one line that zeroes the variable and the dials move 0.0 px.
