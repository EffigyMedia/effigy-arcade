### RLG-205 — A siren only reaches three quarters of a second of road
- **RULED 2026-09-10T10:24:34-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Found by measuring [[RLG-203]]'s central tool on 2026-09-10, EVD-623.

**A SIREN REACHES 4200 UNITS AND THE OWNER EXPECTS IT TO REACH FURTHER.** `scatter()` only asks cars
between 120 and 4200 units ahead of the sounding vehicle. Measured with the instrument that counts
the loop's own gates, **96 per cent of the cars examined are rejected by that window** - the 90 per
cent odds and the `heed` fatigue reject almost nobody. At 124mph, 4200 units is about three quarters
of a second of road: the siren clears the car you are already behind rather than opening the road in
front of you.

**THE OWNER'S PICTURE OF IT IS A ROAD THAT PARTS.** *"They have a secret weapon and that is their
siren because that will move cars out of the way."* INTERCEPT is built on that, and the mode asks the
player to get IN FRONT of a racer - which means driving through traffic faster than the racer can,
which is exactly what a longer reach buys.

**WHAT TO DO WITH IT, IN ORDER.** The number belongs in configuration with a committed default rather
than inline - that is this project's standing rule and it is currently a literal in the loop. Then the
value itself is a feel judgement on a device, and it should be tried at several settings rather than
argued about: a second of road, two seconds, four. The 0.55s cooldown is the second lever and is
probably the one to leave alone - it is what stops a latched siren asking the same car sixty times a
second.

**AND THE HORN SHARES THIS CODE.** A horn press calls the same function with 40 per cent odds and no
latch, so widening the window widens the horn too. That may be right - `traffic-test` records that
over 40 presses only 6 were ever in range - but it is a second change riding on the first and must be
measured, not assumed. [[RLG-035]] is the ruling that made the horn move traffic at all.
- **RULED 2026-09-10T14:33:24-04:00** — **BUILT, v0.13.78. The owner chose a window made of TIME over a bigger fixed number, 2026-09-10:
two seconds of road, speed-relative.**

`pos += spd*dt`, so two seconds of road is exactly `spd * 2` in world units - about 19,000 at 124mph
and about 4,000 at 25mph, which is the old flat number arriving naturally at the speed it was almost
right for. Floored at the old 4200 so a crawling or stopped vehicle still clears its own nose, and
capped at the drawn road, because a car you cannot see is not a car you can ask.

**AND THE SOUNDING VEHICLE'S OWN SPEED DECIDES ITS REACH.** `scatter` takes the speed now as well as
the position, so an NPC cruiser and an ambulance each ask two seconds of THEIR road rather than two
seconds of the player's.

**MEASURED, EVD-625, on the same 22-second arm as before:**

    before   0, 4, 11, 5, 2 cars moved      96% of cars beyond the window, 6 ever asked
    after    31 cars moved                  80% beyond it, 70 asked

**Seven times the median, and the shape of the change is in the counters rather than in the total.**
Cars reaching the odds check went from about six an arm to seventy, and the refusals that were
invisible before are now visible: 22 cars said no and 16 had nowhere to go. That is the tool
working - the obedience tiers of [[RLG-206]] had almost nothing to act on while the window was
rejecting 96 per cent of the road.

**WHAT NO HARNESS CAN SAY, and it is the whole of what is left.** Whether 31 cars an arm reads as a
road parting for a siren or as traffic fleeing in panic. It is one constant, `SIREN_LOOK`, and the
owner has the device.

**AND IT WIDENED THE HORN, which is intended rather than incidental** - they are one function.
`traffic-test` records that over 40 horn presses only 6 were ever in range; the horn now asks the
same two seconds at 40 per cent odds instead of 90. That has not been measured separately.
