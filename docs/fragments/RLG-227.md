### RLG-227 — The fleet needs one official classification, and today it has four

**REQUESTED 2026-09-12.** Owner: *"Yes fix the production and utility gear knobs, but we need a
better classification of cars officially."* **Nothing here is decided and nothing is started.**

---

## WHY THIS CAME UP

Four defects in one day, all the same shape. A vehicle is added; some hand-written list of vehicles
is not updated; the vehicle silently takes the wrong treatment and there is no error.

| Found | The list | What the ambulance got instead |
|---|---|---|
| [[RLG-225]] | `PLAIN_WHEEL` | a supercar's steering wheel |
| [[RLG-226]] | `WORK_BODIES` | a sports car's gear knob |
| [[RLG-228]] | `FRONT_SP` | a grey blob in the mirror |
| [[RLG-228]] | `face-test.py`'s own `TRAFFIC` list | **the guard against exactly this has the same hole** |

That last row is the one that settles it. `face-test.py` exists *because* three vehicles had already
been found with no face in the mirror, each one by eye, on a phone, weeks after it shipped. Its own
list of vehicles is hand-written, and the ambulance is not in it either. **A guard written against a
class of defect, carrying the defect.**

## WHAT THE FLEET IS CLASSIFIED BY TODAY

There is no single answer to "what class is this car". There are at least four tables that each
answer a version of it, plus six more lists that name vehicles directly.

| # | Where | Keyed on | Answers | Values |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `BODY_CLASS` | body key | what a car COSTS to unlock | super, formula, cruiser, supercruiser, sports, production, traffic |
| 2 | `classOf` over `PRODUCTION_BODIES` / `SPORTS_BODIES` / `SUPER_BODIES` / `FORMULA_BODIES`, plus `raceClass` on the two police cars | body key | which GRID a car enters | formula, production, sports, super |
| 3 | `CLS` inside `API.fleet` | body key **and** traffic kind | how the fleet sheet GROUPS a car | super, formula, **sport**, police, production, traffic |
| 4 | `CLASSES` in `tools/fleet-sheet.py` | - | which sheets to write | formula, super, **sport**, police, production, traffic |

**Note `sport` against `sports`.** Tables 1 and 2 say `sports`; tables 3 and 4 say `sport`. They are
different vocabularies that look like one, which is the failure mode this whole ruling is about.

And then the lists that name vehicles outright, each maintained by hand:

`PLAIN_WHEEL` (the wheel), `WORK_BODIES` (the knob), `roundRim` (the rim — **keyed on the MARQUE**,
a fifth vocabulary), the `FRONT_SP` build loop (the mirror face), `SUPER_TRAFFIC`, `RACER_BODIES`,
`TYPE_VMAX`, `RACE_BANNED`, `OPEN_FROM_THE_START`, `SECRET_UNLOCK`, and `face-test.py`'s `TRAFFIC`.

## THE STRUCTURAL FAULT UNDER ALL OF IT

**There are two vocabularies for one fleet and nothing declares the link between them.** A garage
body is an uppercase key in `BODY` — `AMBULANCE`, `VAN`, `SALOON`. The same vehicle in traffic is a
lowercase kind — `ambulance`, `van`, `sedan`. The engine knows they are related only where somebody
wrote both names into the same list by hand.

So **every list that must cover "all vehicles" has to be written twice**, and the second half is the
half that gets forgotten. `PLAIN_WHEEL` carries both and was still missing one. `WORK_BODIES` covers
only the uppercase half. `FRONT_SP` covers only the lowercase half.

**And one class is being asked to answer three unrelated questions.** [[RLG-213]] already hit this
and said so in its own comment: *"A class is what a car IS; an unlock is a separate question about
it, and this is the first time the two have had to be asked apart."* It solved that one case with
`OPEN_FROM_THE_START` rather than by splitting the question.

## A PROPOSAL, FOR THE OWNER TO RULE ON

**Not a decision. This is what a session would build if told to.**

**1. THE RECORD DECLARES IT.** This codebase already has the right pattern and states it in three
places: `force`, `bar`, `npc`, `rig` and `raceClass` are all declared on the vehicle in `BODY`, and
[[RLG-202]]'s note says naming bodies in a function *"is the exact shape that left `hasBar` and
`copLivery` wrong when a second police car arrived."* The classification should live there too. A
vehicle added tomorrow declares what it is, once, and every list follows.

**2. THREE AXES, NOT ONE CLASS.** They are independent today and pretending otherwise is what forces
the lists apart:

| Axis | Answers | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| `role` | what the vehicle IS | the wheel, the knob, whether it races at all |
| `league` | which grid it enters when it does race | the field, the ladder, the rewards |
| `unlock` | what it costs | the garage gate, the gold that pays it |

Today `BODY_CLASS` is doing all three and `raceClass` is a patch over the second.

**3. ONE FLEET, ONE VOCABULARY.** Every traffic kind declares which body it is. `ambulance` is
`AMBULANCE`, `sedan` and `sedan2` are `SALOON`. Then a list that means "every working vehicle" is
written once and covers both halves, and `rigBody`'s existing `sedan2` → saloon mapping stops being
a special case and becomes the general rule.

**4. THE LISTS BECOME DERIVED, AND THE HARNESS STOPS CARRYING ITS OWN COPY.** `PLAIN_WHEEL`,
`WORK_BODIES`, the `FRONT_SP` loop and `face-test.py`'s `TRAFFIC` all ask the record instead of
restating it. [[RLG-226]] deliberately did NOT derive `WORK_BODIES` from `BODY_CLASS`, because
binding a steering column to an unlock class means a decision about what a car costs silently
changes what it is made of — **that is the coupling this ruling exists to remove**, and it should be
removed properly rather than baked in first.

## THE OPEN QUESTIONS, WHICH ONLY THE OWNER CAN ANSWER

> **TWO OF THESE ARE ANSWERED (owner, 2026-09-12).** The merged class is named **UTILITY**, built as
> [[RLG-229]] - question 4 below is about a different pair of names and is still open. And
> **production keeps the working wheel and knob**, which settles that the wheel/knob treatment is the
> *what kind of vehicle is this* axis rather than the class: it covers production AND utility, so a
> class cannot be what decides it. That is the three-axis split below, confirmed by a case rather
> than agreed in principle.
>
> **AND THE OWNER HAS CORRECTED THE SHAPE OF THIS PROPOSAL.** *"You are treating interstate and
> motorsport too identically. They share a car, physics engine world, rendering engine, etc. but the
> little car classes do not translate between the two games."* Interstate holds production, sports
> and super, with formula as a novelty, plus the utility vehicles. **Motorsport holds sports, super
> and formula, and no production, no utility and no traffic.** So a class is PER GAME, and the
> `league` axis below is not one global ladder. **This is unbuilt and it makes the circuit garage a
> defect on `main`**: `garageBodies()` in Motorsport returns COUPE, HATCH and SALOON, because
> `raceOnlyGarage` filters on the global `raceLegal` and [[RLG-213]] made production race-legal. The
> fix is a `CFG` seam naming the classes a cabinet holds - the engine is not allowed to ask which
> game it is.

1. ~~**Is the AMBULANCE a working vehicle or its own thing?**~~ **ANSWERED: it is UTILITY**, with the
   other four ([[RLG-229]]). It was asked because the ambulance has a light bar, a siren, a scatter
   and a call, and no `force` - so a `service` role beside the police was the other reading. The
   owner did not take it: one class, five vehicles.
2. **Do the police get the working wheel and knob?** They are excluded today and [[RLG-069]] called
   that a decision rather than an oversight. A cruiser is a saloon underneath.
3. ~~**Does production race on the circuit?**~~ **ANSWERED: no.** Production races on the
   interstate; the circuit is sports, super and formula. Unbuilt - see the note above. The owner's
   words were a RULING and not a device report: the deployed build was measured at v0.14.1, not the
   v0.13.89 that was claimed at the time ([[RLG-231]]).
4. **`sport` or `sports`?** One of them has to go. A class name reaches the save through the unlock
   keys, so this is a rename with a migration rather than a find-and-replace - but [[RLG-229]] has
   now shown what that costs, and the answer is **one entry in `OLD_UNLOCK_NAMES`**. The machinery
   exists and the fear was misplaced; the naming decision is the only real part left.

## WHAT THIS COSTS IF IT IS NOT DONE

Nothing dramatic, and that is the trap. Every instance is one line, invisible until somebody looks
at the right car from the right angle on a real phone, and **the record so far is four found in one
day by one person driving an ambulance.** There are nineteen bodies and a dozen lists.
