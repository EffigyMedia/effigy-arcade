### RLG-235 — The checkpoint clock pays per group: utility 30, production 20, sports 15, super 15, from a 120-second start
- **RULED 2026-09-13T11:34:44-04:00** — **RULED 2026-09-13, after four rounds.** Owner: *"let's go with yours. 30 20 15 15"*. **Those four
numbers are the ruling:** utility 30 seconds a gantry, production 20, sports 15, super 15. The run
starts with 120 seconds. Crates are unchanged at 10 seconds every 20 to 34 seconds.

**BUILT, v0.14.10. NOT SIGNED** - the owner signs a ruling built ([[RLG-116]]).

It replaces the numbers of [[RLG-233]] (production 30, sports 20, super 10). The mechanism of
RLG-233 stays: a table keyed on class, the police paid through the class they declare, and the
award and the flash read once together.

## THE DECISIONS INSIDE THE RULING

1. **One number per group, not one per car.** Owner: *"unique rewards per car will read as
   confusing to players"*.
2. **Round numbers.** Seventy per cent of each group's slowest car gave 29/21/16/12. The owner chose
   round numbers instead.
3. **The design intent, ruled the same day.** A timed run is designed to END, and distance is the
   score. It must punish bad driving, and a good driver can keep it going. A race is scored by
   placement and ends early when the clock runs out.

## TWO SETS WERE REJECTED. DO NOT PROPOSE THEM AGAIN

| Set | Why it was rejected |
|---|---|
| 30/20/15/10 | The four supercars lost up to 1.1 seconds at each gantry even when driven perfectly. |
| 40/30/20/15 | Nine of the nineteen cars could run forever and take no crates, which breaks the design. |

## INHERITANCE (ruled 2026-09-12)

CRUISER takes the sports reward and SUPERCRUISER the super reward, through `raceClass`. **All three
FORMULA cars take the super reward.** Formula has no league ([[RLG-213]]), so `bodyClass` answers
`formula` and there is no row for it. `CP_INHERITS = { formula: 'super' }` maps it. Before this
change a formula car fell through to `CLOCK_BONUS`, the default of 20.

## WHAT CHANGED IN CODE

- `CLOCK_START` 60 to 120. At 60 a slow car cannot reach the first gantry at top speed without a
  crate.
- `CP_SECONDS = { utility: 30, production: 20, sports: 15, super: 15 }`.
- `CP_INHERITS = { formula: 'super' }`, read by `cpSeconds()` before the table.
- `CLOCK_BONUS` stays as the default. No class reaches it now. It stays so that a new class is not
  paid nothing.

## WHAT IT DOES, MODELLED AT TOP SPEED

The world moves 2.14 times faster than the mph label. With every crate taken, break-even is 63 per
cent of a car's two-mile leg.

| Group | Gain at each gantry, every crate taken |
|---|---|
| utility | +3.6 to +8.9 seconds |
| production | +0.8 to +3.0 seconds |
| sports | +0.1 to +1.8 seconds - CRUISER at a bare +0.1 |
| super | +3.9 to +4.7 seconds |

**With no crates, every car's run ends EXCEPT the three FORMULA cars**, which inherit a reward sized
for slower cars. The owner was told this.

**The model says nothing about real play.** It assumes top speed, no traffic and no wreck penalty.
[[RLG-234]], the verify-097-100 harness that passes cars that never moved, must be fixed before
anyone claims a real measurement of the clock.

## THE PROOF

`tools/checkpoint-pay-test.py` measures the seconds that land on the run clock across a real gantry,
and the clock just after a run starts.

    ok    a run starts with 120 sec
    ok    SALOON        pays 20 sec   (production)
    ok    COUPE         pays 20 sec   (production)
    ok    TUNER         pays 15 sec   (sports)
    ok    STALLION      pays 15 sec   (super)
    ok    CRUISER       pays 15 sec   (a sports saloon underneath - declared, not listed)
    ok    SUPERCRUISER  pays 15 sec   (a MATADOR underneath - declared, not listed)
    ok    VECTOR        pays 15 sec   (formula: no league, paid as a super)
    ok    VAN           pays 30 sec   (utility)

**The single falsifier became three, because one could no longer tell the parts apart.** Emptying
the table now leaves every car on the default 20, and production's ruled number IS 20. Each
falsifier puts one part of the change back and passes only when EXACTLY its expected rows fail:

| Falsifier | Puts back | Must fail | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `table` | an empty table | TUNER, STALLION, CRUISER, SUPERCRUISER, VECTOR, VAN | exactly those, each paid 19.8-19.9 |
| `inherit` | no formula mapping | VECTOR | exactly VECTOR, paid 19.8 |
| `start` | a 60-second start | the start check | exactly that, read 60.0 |

**WHAT THE PROOF CANNOT SEE.** No falsifier can show that SALOON and COUPE are paid by the table
rather than by the default, because both are 20. HATCH, the other formula cars, the other supercars
and the other utility vehicles are not driven; they reach the same class through the same table.

smoke-test passed 6/6. drive-test passed 36/37 twice. The one failure both times was the nitrous
settle line, at 98.1% and 97.9%. That line is a recorded environmental flake that fails at HEAD with
road.js stashed (EVD-638), and this change does not touch nitrous.

**WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE:** whether a run now feels too long or too short on a device.
