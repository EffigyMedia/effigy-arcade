### RLG-175 — Super cruisers should be earned by rivals too, not only by the player

TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-08: "I would imagine there would be a fair bit of super
cruisers deployed during a supercar race or tournament or a formula car race or tournament
because all of those cars would also trigger the same required conditions."

THE OBSERVATION IS CORRECT AND THE ENGINE DOES NOT DO IT. `superWatch` reads two things and
both are the PLAYER'S: `heat`, which only the player has, and `supersEarned`, a single
module-level flag set when the PLAYER goes past a trap above 170mph. A rival doing 190 past a
parked trap all race long earns nothing, because there is nothing on a rival for it to be
earned onto.

IT ALSO CUTS AGAINST A JUDGEMENT MADE THE SAME DAY, which is why it is recorded here rather
than folded quietly into something else. [[RLG-173]] made a dispatched car - radio or
interceptor - take the player without searching, on the reasoning that a super cruiser exists
for one driver by definition. In a supercar field that reasoning is simply wrong: the
conditions are met by twelve cars, and the interceptor that arrives has no more business with
the player than with any of them.

WHAT IT WOULD TAKE, and the shape matters more than the size:

    HEAT WOULD HAVE TO BELONG TO A CAR rather than to the game. Today `heat` and
    `supersEarned` are module state. A per-car wanted level is the honest version and is
    a larger change than it looks, because every read of `heat` in the police system
    currently means "the player's heat" and would have to say whose.

    A CHEAPER VERSION EXISTS and should be considered first: leave heat as the player's,
    and give a RIVAL a plain per-car flag for having gone past a trap at speed - enough
    to have interceptors dispatched onto rivals in a supercar or formula event without
    building a second wanted system.

AND IT IS A SPECTACLE FEATURE BEFORE IT IS A DIFFICULTY ONE. The value is a supercar race
where the field is being chased as hard as the player is, which is a thing to look at rather
than a thing to survive. That argues for the cheap version.

SEE ALSO [[RLG-174]], the wanted ladder, which puts super cruisers at four stars - a rung that
means something different once a rival can be at four stars too.
- **RULED 2026-09-08T20:35:37-04:00** — ### 2026-09-08 — the owner set the conditions, and one number does not match the code

**THE TRIGGER IS THE SAME FOR EVERYBODY.** Owner: *"The player or the rivals earning super
cruisers still need to have the same trigger conditions. We're just moving the heat level to
four, but you still need to pass a speed trap over 180 miles an hour."*

That settles the open question in [[RLG-174]] about whether the star replaces the earned event.
It does not. **Both conditions stand, and they apply to whoever meets them** — the rung on the
ladder moves the heat gate from three to four, and the speed past a trap is unchanged in kind.

**ONE NUMBER IS NOT WHAT THE OWNER THINKS IT IS, AND IT MATTERS BECAUSE THEY SAID "STILL".**
The condition in the code is **170**, not 180: `if(spd > MAX_SPD * (170/200)) supersEarned =
true` at `road.js:8979`, and [[RLG-030]] records the owner's own words on 2026-08-30 as *"gone
170 miles an hour past a speed trap"*. So "you still need to pass a speed trap over 180" is a
**change from 170 to 180**, not a restatement. It is recorded as 180 because that is what the
owner said today and today is later; **but it should be confirmed rather than built on**, since
the word "still" suggests they believe it is already the number.

**WHAT THIS COSTS IN A SUPERCAR FIELD, and it is the reason to confirm.** 180 of 200 is nine
tenths of the player's top speed. Several of the six garage cars cannot reach it at all, so at
180 the rule reads "supercars and the formula car only" — which may be exactly the intent in a
supercar or formula event and is a much narrower rule on the Interstate. At 170 it is reachable
by more of the fleet. One ruling, two very different roads.

**AND THE IMPLEMENTATION SHAPE IS NOW FIXED BY THE FIRST SENTENCE.** "The same trigger
conditions" for rivals means the earned flag cannot stay where it is: `supersEarned` is a single
module-level boolean (`road.js:321`), reset per run. Same conditions for twelve cars means the
flag belongs **on the car**, and so does the heat gate — which is the larger of the two changes
sketched above, not the cheap one. The cheap version in this record's first draft, a plain
per-rival flag with the player's heat left global, no longer satisfies the ruling as stated,
because a rival would then be earning an interceptor against the PLAYER's wanted level.

**SO THE ORDER OF WORK IS SETTLED TOO:** heat has to become a property of a car before this can
be built honestly. That makes [[RLG-174]]'s ladder the prerequisite rather than a sibling, since
the ladder is where "what does a star mean" gets its answer, and per-car heat is where "whose
star" gets one.
