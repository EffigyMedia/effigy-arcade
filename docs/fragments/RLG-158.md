### RLG-158 — Police AI navigates traffic instead of destroying itself on it

Owner, 2026-09-07: "The police also need much better AI behavior because they are
constantly crashing into traffic and destroying themselves. They need to be just as
interested in navigating the traffic as they are stopping and arresting you - that being
said their main method of attack should be to surround you and slow you down so that you
get arrested/busted."

TWO CLAIMS AND THEY PULL THE SAME WAY. A cruiser that destroys itself on the first lorry
it meets is not a threat, and a pursuit that ends because the police crashed is not a
pursuit. The dodge already exists - `skill` rises with heat and the chase reads traffic
and roadblocks ahead of it - so this is a question of whether that dodge is strong enough
and whether it is being overridden by the chase line.

AND THE INTENT IS NAMED: surround and slow, so the bust does the work. The boxing
behaviour exists and stations cars around a stopped car; what the owner is describing is
that it should be the goal rather than the endgame.
- **RULED 2026-09-08T03:38:55-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-08, v0.13.32. Both halves of the ruling, and one of them is measured while the
other is only reasoned about.

FIRST HALF - THE DODGE STEERED AND NEVER LIFTED. Everything in the cruiser's road-reading code
picked a LINE around what was ahead; its SPEED came from the chase and from nothing else. So a
lorry with cars either side - a gap no line can find - was driven into at full chase speed. A
cruiser now eases for what it cannot go round, following closer than a civilian does because a
police car in a pursuit tailgates and one keeping a civilian's margin would drop out of every
chase that met a slow car.

THE REACH IS A TIME, NOT A DISTANCE, and that is the difference between this working and this
merely existing. The first version looked 2,400 units ahead, which sounds a long way and is under
half a second at 150mph; a cruiser closing on a car 40mph slower has to begin lifting seconds out.
The window is now a bit over a second of the cruiser's own road and the trigger is the time to
contact.

SECOND HALF - THE BOX DID NOT WAIT FOR A STOP ANY MORE. Owner: "their main method of attack should
be to surround you and slow you down so that you get arrested/busted." The boxing code asked
whether the player was under a tenth of top speed, because it was written to make the BUSTED rule
reachable - which makes being surrounded the CONSEQUENCE of stopping rather than the method of
causing it. A cruiser that has caught up now takes its station at any speed, and the one that gets
in front runs at nine tenths of the player's speed rather than matching it, so staying on the
throttle means going through it. Only the ability to REVERSE is still kept for the standstill: a
car backing up at 150mph is not a manoeuvre.

THE STATISTIC COULD NOT SETTLE THE FIRST HALF AND THE RECORD SHOULD SAY SO. Damage events taken
from traffic over ninety seconds of pursuit ran 6.0, 5.3 and 8.7 a minute before the change and
2.7, 7.3 and 5.3 after. The means move the right way and the ranges overlap, so three runs an arm
prove nothing - the same trap [[RLG-056]] records. What proves it is a CONTROLLED FORCED LIFT:
three cars abreast, so there is no line round them and the throttle is the only answer left. The
cruiser opened at 7,000 and slowed to 5,037 against a wall running at 4,622, taking no damage; on
the engine before the change it held 10,886 the whole way and went through. The box has the same
shape of proof - three cruisers on a car at 12,773 units a second, three on station, against zero
on the old engine.

AND THE OWNER'S ORIGINAL REPORT STILL DOES NOT REPRODUCE. No cruiser was ever put out by traffic
in any harness run, on either engine. The reason is now understood and it is worth writing down: a
cruiser only takes traffic damage `if(k.grace<=0 && k.z > pz - 900)` - that is, only when it is
level with the player or ahead. Every cruiser chasing from behind passes through traffic with no
consequence at all. The device sees the crashes because a real player's cruisers get ahead; a
harness driving in a straight line at a fixed speed leaves them behind. THAT ASYMMETRY IS ITSELF
WORTH A RULING and is not fixed here.
