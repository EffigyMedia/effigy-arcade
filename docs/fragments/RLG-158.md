### RLG-158 — Police AI navigates traffic instead of destroying itself on it

Owner, 2026-09-07: "The police also need much better AI behavior because they are
constantly crashing into traffic and destroying themselves. They need to be just as
interested in navigating the traffic as they are stopping and arresting you - that being
said their main method of attack should be to surround you and slow you down so that you
get arrested/busted."

TWO CLAIMS AND THEY PULL THE SAME WAY. A cruiser that destroys itself on the first lorry
it meets is not a threat, and a pursuit that ends because the police crashed is not a
pursuit. The dodge already exists - `skill` rises with heat and the chase reads traffic
and roadblocks ahead of it - so this is a question of whether that dodge is strong enough
and whether it is being overridden by the chase line.

AND THE INTENT IS NAMED: surround and slow, so the bust does the work. The boxing
behaviour exists and stations cars around a stopped car; what the owner is describing is
that it should be the goal rather than the endgame.
