### RLG-312 — A wooded place wants a wall of trees on its approaching and leaving faces
- **RULED 2026-09-21T00:17:21-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21: 'The biomes with trees should have a wall of trees running along side the approaching face and leaving face (mirror). We need to verify everything can be seen in the mirror.'

IT IS THE SAME SEAM AS THE ROCK, ASKED FOR A DIFFERENT MATERIAL. A walled place gets a face at its boundary: a canyon has one (RLG-301) and a mountain wants a mountainside (RLG-306). A wooded place has neither, so driving into a forest is driving at nothing while the trees arrive one rank at a time from the side. A wall of trees across the approach is the forest's version of the massif.

AND IT SHOULD FALL OUT OF THE TABLE RATHER THAN NAME A PLACE. `face` is already a declared property that only the canyon carries. A place that states a face and a place that states trees are two rows in the same table, and what gets DRAWN at the boundary should be the place's own material. Writing a branch on FOREST here would be the mistake this codebase names repeatedly - 'never write a branch that names a vehicle class', and the scenery's own note: 'It is not a branch on the name. Any place that declares a rolled hazard gets an empty hazard side, so the rule arrives with the table.'

THE SECOND SENTENCE IS THE BIGGER ONE AND IT IS NOT A DETAIL. 'We need to verify everything can be seen in the mirror.' The glass has been found short FOUR separate times, each after a device report rather than before one: the rail, the drop (twice - once for the rail standing on open ground and again when a mountain still showed rock on both sides), the wall, the weather, the checkpoint boards and the finish line. [[RLG-304]] found a fifth that nobody had reported, where the mirror had been drawing the wall at the wrong height for as long as it had existed. THAT IS A SURVEY, not a fix: one pass that lists everything the windscreen draws beside the road and asserts the glass draws it too, so the next omission is caught by a harness rather than by the owner.

SEE [[RLG-313]]: the same pass should ask whether each of them FADES in the glass rather than appearing.

Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-21T22:30:19-04:00** — BUILT, THE FIRST SENTENCE, in 0.14.119 (UNT-411). A wooded place's first segment carries a rank of its own scenery sprites across the view, from the kerb out past the edge of the frame at a spacing of TREE_WALL_GAP (0.55) of one tree's width, at most TREE_WALL_MAX (64) a side. The glass draws the same rank at the place's LAST segment, projected at that segment's own distance, with the glass's own fade. One function, drawTreeWall, serves both views.

IT FALLS OUT OF THE TABLE. treeWallOf(B) is true when B.trees >= TREE_WALL_MIN (0.60) and the place has no face and no mass - that is FOREST, JUNGLE and SWAMP today, and MOUNTAIN (0.55, and a mass) and CANYON (a face) are not. The biome table's trees field was read by nothing before this; it is read now. The sides are the scenery's own rules: nothing in the sea, on the drop, in front of a sheer face, or on a crop side.

PROVED by tools/tree-wall-test.py (EVD-1193) and its falsify arm (EVD-1194), which fails exactly the presence checks and keeps the absence checks. The glass pixel check first passed with the wall removed - traffic moving between screenshots - and now takes the smallest difference over three alternated on/off pairs. Whether the rank reads as the edge of a wood is the owner's call on a device.

THE SECOND SENTENCE IS NOT DONE: the survey of everything the windscreen draws against the glass. A first static pass found four layers the glass never draws - the city street lamps, the valley range, the kerb on a deck and on every circuit, and the deck's expansion joints. That is the next unit.
- **RULED 2026-09-21T23:20:51-04:00** — BUILT, THE SECOND SENTENCE, in 0.14.120 (UNT-412). THE SURVEY IS A HARNESS NOW, not a pass done once. Each layer counts itself where it paints, per view, only while watchDraw is on (API.viewLayers), and tools/glass-survey-test.py tours all twelve places standing in them, the six places with something at their boundary both ways, rain, and a Motorsport circuit, and fails on any layer the windscreen painted that the glass did not. It sees 22 layers in each view: boats, bore, drop, edges, face, ground, joint, kerb, lamp, lanes, mass, portal, rail, range, rim, road, scenery, sea, skyline, treeWall, truss, weather.

FIVE LAYERS WERE MISSING FROM THE GLASS and now paint there, each by the windscreen's own painter: the city's street lamps (paintStreetLamp), the valley range (the place's skyline art on the glass's floor at RANGE_AT of MIRROR_BACK), the kerb on a deck and on every circuit, the deck's expansion joints, and the tunnel's portal (paintPortal). The falsify arm cuts all five and fails exactly the six scenes that should fail (EVD-1197); the survey passes with them in (EVD-1196).

FOR THE OWNER TO RULE, NOT BUILT: the sky's own objects - clouds, stars, the sun and the moon - are drawn out of the windscreen and not in the glass. They are not beside the road, so the survey leaves them out and says so. And the valley range in the glass copies the windscreen's motion model, which the owner has just rejected (RLG-309); when that model changes, the glass's copy has to change with it.

WHAT IS LEFT OF THIS FRAGMENT: nothing but the device verdicts on the tree wall and on the five new glass layers. RLG-313, the fade, is the next unit.
