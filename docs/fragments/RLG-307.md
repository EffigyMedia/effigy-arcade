### RLG-307 — A canyon's boundary face does not connect to the walls beside it
- **RULED 2026-09-21T00:16:00-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21, from the device: 'The approaching (and leaving faces) of a canyons look odd because they dont connect with the side faces.'

THIS IS THE DISCONNECTION AGAIN, ON THE PLACE THAT KEPT ITS FACE. [[RLG-303]] recorded the same reading of a MOUNTAIN: 'the wall running alongside and the face at the boundary are separate drawings at different angles and the eye reads them as two things'. The mountain's face was withdrawn; the canyon's was kept because the owner accepted it. The fault was never in which place had one.

WHY THEY CANNOT MEET AS BUILT. `drawEndWall` paints columns across the frame at ONE z - the boundary segment - each a rectangle whose top comes from ridge noise sampled along the LATERAL axis. The wall beside the road is a plane at a height above the road, tiled per slice, whose top edge runs toward the vanishing point. One is a flat card facing the camera and the other is a surface running away from it, so their top edges meet at an angle and their surfaces meet at a corner that nothing draws.

THE SLOT IS ALREADY SHARED AND THE REST IS NOT. RLG-301 made the face's gap `wallFootX` either side, 'so the face and the walls cannot disagree about how wide the way through is'. That is the only quantity the two drawings share. Their HEIGHT at the join, and the corner between them, are computed independently.

FIRST READING, NOT A PLAN: the face's outermost column and the wall's band at the boundary slice are the two edges that have to agree, in height and in shade. RLG-304's method is the one that works on this class of fault - write the expression once and have both read it.

AND IT IS THE LEAVING FACE TOO, which is the mirror's copy of the same drawing. See [[RLG-306]] and [[RLG-312]]: three reports about the same seam.

Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-21T20:04:45-04:00** — IN PROGRESS 2026-09-21 under UNT-410, NOT COMMITTED. OWNER, same day, on seeing the low-poly mountain: 'You should probably do the same thing for the canyon walls.' So the canyon is on the mass too, with its own profile - `massH` [0, 1.0, 1.04, 1.0, 0.96, 0.92], `massJag` 0.10, a cliff up to a nearly flat rim, because near-vertical and near-uniform is what was accepted of its walls.

THE FACE AND THE WALLS NOW MEET BY CONSTRUCTION. A canyon does not grow, so its front is a vertical cross-section at the boundary that the top surface never draws. It is drawn at the nearest slice of the place - the one whose nearer neighbour belongs somewhere else - FROM THE SAME HEIGHTS as the sides, so there is no second set of numbers to disagree. `drawEndWall` stands down for any place with a mass. Photographed: a low mesa front at 136 segments out, the mouth with its slot at 66, and the walls running straight out of the front at 20.
- **RULED 2026-09-21T21:37:57-04:00** — BUILT in v0.14.118, and UNSIGNED (RLG-116). The canyon is the same mass with its own profile, and its FRONT is drawn from the same heights as its walls, so the two are one surface and cannot disagree at the corner.

THE FRONT WAS FRAGILE ONCE AND IT IS RECORDED. Drawn at the nearest slice of the place, it vanished for the frame whenever that slice was skipped - the proof caught 0 front quads on one road where another had 10. `drawMassFront` now fires from the TRUE boundary's own projection the moment the walk reaches a drawn slice that is somewhere else, before that slice's ground. And the glass closes the canyon both ways: the mouth you came through, from inside (RLG-301), and the end you drove out of, once you have left.

ONLY A PLACE WITH A FACE HAS ONE. Every cross-section - the front and both of the mirror's - is gated on `face`, which only the canyon declares; `canyon-mouth-test` caught the glass standing a sliver of face at a mountain's entrance where its 900-unit step lands a few per cent up the ramp.

THE OLD FACE AND WALL STILL EXIST behind `massOff`, and the mass reports through their own trace, counter and switch - `wallTrace`, `endWallN`, `endWallOff` - so the harnesses written for them measure it unchanged. `wall-plane-test` and `canyon-mouth-test` pass.
