### RLG-298 — The wheel follows the input, not the forces on the car
- **RULED 2026-09-20T09:20:34-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20: 'the steering wheel rotates based on the movement of the car e.g. all the forces acting upon it, but it should only follow the inputs to the front wheels' - and 'there would be some force feedback based on the friction of the road, coming back up through the tires through the steering column.' So the wheel in the cockpit is driven by the STEERING INPUT, not by the car's lateral movement, with a small feedback term from the road's grip on top - a shiver over a rough or wet surface, a kick when a tyre lets go - rather than the wheel following the slide. Find what the wheel's angle reads today (it is expected to be the car's lateral velocity or camX) and separate the two terms: input, then feedback, each a tunable with a committed default. Not started.
