### RLG-338 — The place mix is badly skewed and some places are all but unreachable
- **RULED 2026-09-24T11:57:45-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-24: "Maybe we could normalize the chances of getting any biome so that some aren't so rare." Said after trying and failing to reach a MOUNTAIN to test three open reports.

MEASURED THROUGH THE GAME'S OWN PICKER before the ruling, with `API.walkPlaces` over 9,600 place changes from every starting place in turn:

| place | share |
|---|---|
| CITY | 15.51% |
| COASTAL | 15.19% |
| FARMLAND | 15.11% |
| BRIDGE | 15.01% |
| FOREST | 12.94% |
| TUNNEL | 10.74% |
| CANYON | 4.51% |
| SWAMP | 4.27% |
| JUNGLE | 2.60% |
| MOUNTAIN | 2.11% |
| DESERT | 1.97% |
| TUNDRA | 0.03% |

A MOUNTAIN first appears after 58 places on average - at `PLACE_MILES` of 3.25 to 6, something like 270 miles of driving - and was ABSENT ALTOGETHER from eleven of forty-eight walks of two hundred places. A TUNDRA is three occurrences in 9,600. Six places carry 84.5 per cent of the road between them.

THE CAUSE IS THE TEMPERATURE CHAIN, NOT THE SAMPLE. `pickNext` draws a candidate UNIFORMLY from `BIOME_KEYS` and then accepts it only if `rollClimateNear` can land its temperature within one step of the current one ([[RLG-142]]: no place may be more than `TEMP_STEP_F` from the one before). So a rare place is not rarely TRIED - it is tried one time in twelve like everything else - it is rarely ACCEPTED. Weighting the draw would therefore do almost nothing, and that is the first wrong fix to avoid.

WHAT MAKES IT SELF-REINFORCING: most of the board sits in the temperate middle - FOREST, CITY and TUNNEL at 0.45, BRIDGE 0.55, FARMLAND 0.60 - so the walk has many short steps available near 0.45 and few stepping stones out to the ends. TUNDRA at 0.05 with a vary of 0.04 is the narrowest target at the coldest end of the scale, which is exactly why it is the rarest.

SO THE LEVER IS THE CLIMATE WALK and any fix has to keep what that walk is for. The step rule is not decoration: it is what stops the road going from a desert to a tundra in one place boundary. THE THING TO MEASURE AGAINST is the table above, re-run with `API.walkPlaces`, and the target is that no place is so rare a player never sees it - NOT that every place is equally likely, which would flatten the road's character.

NOT STARTED.
