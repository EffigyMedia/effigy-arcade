### RLG-263 — A checkpoint board is never handed to the mirror
- **RULED 2026-09-15T23:14:00-04:00** — FOUND WHILE FIXING RLG-260, and it is not the same fault. tools/mirror-test.py has failed this check on every run measured today and it fails identically on 94017b4f, where this session started: the windscreen is handed a checkpoint board and the glass is not. The repair crate appears in the same failure on some runs and not others, which is the sampling rather than a defect - a crate has to fall behind the player while the check is watching.

`cpGantries` is culled at `pos - MIRROR_BACK` (road.js near 18061), so the boards are not despawned early the way the roadblocks were. The mirror pass does push them - `for(const cp of cpGantries) if(cp.z < pos) back.push({ o:cp, gantry:true, kind:c })` - so the fault is between that push and what `viewKinds.glass` records, or the board is dropped before it is drawn. Not investigated further.

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND THE MIRROR: RLG-177 rules that the glass is offered everything the windscreen is, and this check is the instrument for that rule. While it fails for a known reason it cannot fail loudly for an unknown one.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T23:30:20-04:00** — DECLINED - THE CLAIM IN THE TITLE IS WRONG, and the measurement that settled it is here so the same wrong conclusion is not drawn twice. The owner asked for a sweep of everything the mirror shows, and this was the first thing it looked at.

MEASURED. A board parked behind the player with `API.parkGantry` IS handed to the glass: at 4,000 units back and at 20,000, `viewKinds().glass` carries 'c' on every frame sampled. `cpGantries` is culled at `MIRROR_BACK` (RLG-133) and the mirror pass pushes every board behind the camera, both correct.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG WAS THE CHECK. mirror-test drives 30 seconds at 55 % of top speed, which is a little under the two miles between checkpoints, so the windscreen was handed a board standing up the road that the car never reached, and the glass was marked "missing" for a board that had never been behind it. The repair crate failed the same way on some runs and not others, for the same reason: it depended on a crate happening to fall behind the car while the sweep watched. The police did too, on about one run in several.

FIXED IN THE CHECK, NOT THE ENGINE (0.14.41): the sweep now parks a board, a crate and a cruiser behind the car, the way it already forced a roadblock. Six kinds are handed to both views on two consecutive runs.
