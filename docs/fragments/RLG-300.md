### RLG-300 — The rock sprites in canyon and mountain are replaced by the sheer faces
- **RULED 2026-09-20T15:34:08-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-20, from the device: 'The original rock pieces and rock faces and cliff faces whatever from the canyon in the mountain don't need to be rendered anymore since we're using sheer faces.' RLG-297 put a continuous plane on the canyon's two sides and the mountain's uphill side, and the loose rock that used to stand for those walls is now drawn IN FRONT OF a solid face - it is doing no work and it is being rasterised. Take it out of the places that have a wall, on the sides that have one, in the windscreen and in the glass. IT IS ALSO PART OF RLG-299: the two places that pay most for the doubled draw are the two with a per-slice surface, and their roadside is the densest on the board - the canyon is 0.98, which the scenery table calls the densest thing on it. Watch scenery-test, occlusion-test and biome-shot: the drop side of a mountain must keep whatever it has, and the strip of sky a canyon leaves between its walls must not change shape. Not started.
