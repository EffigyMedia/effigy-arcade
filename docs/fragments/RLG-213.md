### RLG-213 — The class ladder starts at production, and formula is a novelty with no league of its own
- **RULED 2026-09-11T12:27:18-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED, AND EXPLICITLY NOT DECIDED.** Owner, 2026-09-11: *"I'm also on the fence
about another major thing."* Everything below is the owner's proposal recorded as a proposal. **No
session may build any of it without the owner settling it first**, and the parts are separable, so
"yes" to one part is not "yes" to the rest.

---

## THE PROPOSAL, IN THE OWNER'S OWN SHAPE

*"you start with production cars. So there is production tournament, beating that unlock sports,
beating sports unlock super, beating super unlocks a single formula car that is just a novelty, and
there is no explicit formula race or tournament. As a novelty, you can choose to participate in
single races or tournaments using the formula car in all of the preceding race classes. Production,
sports, and super."*

    START            production
    production gold  ->  SPORTS
    sports gold      ->  SUPER
    super gold       ->  ONE formula car, as a novelty
    formula          ->  no league of its own; it ENTERS production, sports or super

## AND THE FLEET HAS TO BE RESORTED FOR IT

*"One thing this means I have to adjust is I need to have proper production cars worthy of racing. So
the saloon, the coupe, and something else. The pickup truck needs to move back to utility as well as
the cab, or we create another class. So production class simply means the low end race vehicles. Then
we need another class that represents the low end traffic vehicles. Then the utility vehicles stand
as they already do. Or we combine the low end traffic vehicles and the utility vehicles into another
class. Those just get unlocked with a single unlock trigger, which is driving fifty miles in test
drive with any car with the timer on."*

    production   the LOW END RACE cars - SALOON, COUPE, and a third that does not exist yet
    ???          the low end TRAFFIC cars - CAB, PICKUP, and whatever else is scenery
    utility      unchanged - VAN, SEMI, AMBULANCE
                 ... OR the last two merge into one class

    and that non-racing group unlocks on ONE trigger: fifty miles of TEST DRIVE,
    timer on, in ANY car.

---

## WHAT IT COLLIDES WITH, AND EVERY ONE OF THESE IS ALREADY RULED

**IT REVERSES [[RLG-115]], WHICH IS THE BIGGEST THING IN IT.** That ruling is the owner's own:
*"if you have a production or utility vehicle selected, the only mode available is test drive."* The
whole point of it is that the ordinary cars are for driving around in and the events are for the cars
built to enter them - and a PRODUCTION TOURNAMENT makes production cars race cars. The rule does not
break: it moves. What is TEST DRIVE ONLY becomes the traffic-and-utility group rather than
production, and `RACE_BANNED` is re-keyed rather than removed. **But the sentence RLG-115 was written
from stops being true**, and that is a thing to say out loud rather than discover in the diff.

**IT REVERSES [[RLG-114]].** The PICKUP was moved TO production on 2026-08-31 on the owner's word
("we move pickup to production"), and this moves it back. That ruling also records that the move was
a balance change because the classes unlock at different distances - which stops applying if the
whole group unlocks on one trigger.

**IT ENDS THE SECRET UNLOCKS AS THEY STAND.** `SECRET_UNLOCK` holds production and utility, and the
reasoning recorded for it is that they are distance milestones on a mode nobody drives for distance,
so finding out is the whole pleasure. Production cannot be secret if the player STARTS in one. The
utility-and-traffic group can stay secret, and the single trigger is a better fit for that than two
were.

**IT CHANGES WHAT `classOf` MEANS FOR A FORMULA CAR.** `classOf` returns `formula` and `racerBodies`
draws the grid from `FORMULA_BODIES`, so a formula car races formula cars today. Under the proposal
there IS no formula league, so a formula car needs to be told WHICH class it is entering - which is
a choice the player makes, and therefore a control that does not exist. It is the only part of this
proposal that needs a new piece of interface.

**AND IT NEEDS A CAR THAT DOES NOT EXIST.** "The saloon, the coupe, and something else." Three is
the size of every other class in this game. The third production car has to be designed, painted,
given a record and given sprites at both ends - which is the largest single piece of work in the
proposal and the one most likely to be underestimated.

**THE FORMULA CARS ARE THREE AND THE PROPOSAL SAYS ONE.** `FORMULA_BODIES` is VECTOR, APEX and COMET.
"A single formula car that is just a novelty" leaves two of them unaccounted for. Are they cut, kept
as rivals only, or is the prize one of the three with the others still to be found?

**AND A BODY KEY IS PERSISTED, SO RECLASSIFYING IS A MIGRATION.** [[RLG-197]] records it: the chosen
car is written into the save by key, and the unlock CLASS is what gates it. Moving PICKUP and CAB out
of production means a save that unlocked production and is sitting in a PICKUP has to keep its car.
`RENAMED` is the table that already does this kind of repair, and a migration is never removed later.

## THE QUESTIONS THIS HAS TO ANSWER BEFORE ANY OF IT IS BUILT

    1. TWO CLASSES OR ONE for the non-racing vehicles? The owner offered both and preferred
       neither. Three groups (production / low-end traffic / utility) or two (production /
       everything else)?
    2. WHAT IS THE THIRD PRODUCTION CAR? It has to be designed and drawn.
    3. WHAT HAPPENS TO THE OTHER TWO FORMULA CARS?
    4. DOES THE PLAYER CHOOSE the class a formula car enters, and where does that control live?
    5. WHAT DOES A SUPER GOLD PAY once formula is the top of the ladder and the iridescent
       paints - currently paid by a FORMULA gold - have no tournament to be won in?
    6. AND DOES THE POLICE LADDER MOVE WITH IT? The CRUISER is paid by a sports gold and the
       SUPERCRUISER by a super gold, both with pursuit on. A production tier underneath them
       changes what those golds are worth and where the police cars sit.

**THE SAFEST ORDER, IF IT IS TAKEN.** The fleet resort first, because it is the part that everything
else reads - `BODY_CLASS`, `RACE_BANNED`, `classOf`, `racerBodies`, the garage list, the silhouettes
and the captions all key off it. The ladder second. The formula novelty last, because it is the only
part that needs new interface. And the third production car is its own piece of work and should not
be inside any of those three.

## AND ONE QUESTION THE DECISION CREATES

**A FORMULA CAR IN A PRODUCTION RACE IS NOT A CONTEST, AND ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR IS WHY.**
The owner's standing rule is that every car runs the same physics and a rival is competitive
because it DRIVES well. A formula car against a field of saloons is therefore an easy win by
the car alone, and nothing in the engine will close that gap for it. Three readings are
possible and they are not the same product: the novelty IS the walkover and that is the joke;
or a formula car pays nothing when it enters a lower class; or the rubber band, which is the
ONE named exception to the physics rule, is what holds the field to it. The owner has to say
which.

**THE SAFEST ORDER IS UNCHANGED.** The fleet resort first, because everything reads it. The
ladder second. The formula novelty last, because it alone needs interface. The new production
cars are their own work and sit outside all three.

in their own words: *"1) one class merged unlocked at 50 min Test drive w Timed. 2) saloon, coupe,

## Q1 IS ANSWERED - ONE MERGED CLASS

The low-end traffic cars and the utility vehicles become **one class**, not two. So the fleet
sorts into three groups rather than four:

    production   the LOW END RACE cars - SALOON, COUPE, and one new car
    (merged)     everything else that is not a racer - CAB, PICKUP, VAN, SEMI,
                 AMBULANCE - unlocked on ONE trigger
    racing       sports, super, and formula as they already stand

**AND IT COLLAPSES TWO UNLOCK FLAGS INTO ONE.** `production` and `utility` are separate keys in
the save today, granted at 50 and 25 miles by two branches in `step`, and `carLocked`, `openBy`,
`SECRET_UNLOCK`, `isNovelty` and `UNLOCK_HOW` all read them as a pair. One class means one key.
The pair is already special-cased in four places by an `unlocked('traffic')` fall-through written
for the older hundred-mile rule, which is the migration seam this should use rather than a new one.

## Q2 IS ANSWERED IN SHAPE AND NOT IN DETAIL - SALOON, COUPE, AND ONE NEW CAR

So it is ONE new body, not several: the plural in the owner's earlier sentence meant the class, not
the count. **WHAT THAT CAR IS HAS NOT BEEN DECIDED** - no name, no shape, no place between the
SALOON's 0.56 vmax and the COUPE's 0.60. It needs a record, a rig, paint, and sprites at both ends
that agree under `car-ends-test`. It stays its own piece of work and sits outside the resort.

## Q4 IS ANSWERED - A BUTTON IN THE GARAGE THAT CYCLES THE CLASS

The control the formula novelty needs is **a new garage button that cycles the class the car will
enter**. It is the same shape as the MODE button that already sits there, which is the reason it
is cheap: `modeLabel` is one function answering one question so that the label and the cycle
cannot disagree, and this follows it.

**IT LANDS ON THE SURFACE [[RLG-210]] IS ABOUT TO REBUILD.** That ruling makes the garage card
larger, centred and floor-anchored, and it already defers a FLIP button to after the menu refactor.
A second new button on the same card is a reason to design the two together rather than to add one
now and move it later. The order in the queue does not change; the collision is recorded so that
whoever takes RLG-210 knows a third control is coming.

## Q5 IS NOT ANSWERED, AND IT GREW

*"Not sure. We will need to evaluate a bunch of new rewards."* So this is no longer only "what does
a super gold pay". The whole reward schedule is reopened by the ladder: there is a new tournament
at the bottom that pays nothing yet, a formula gold that no longer exists, and the iridescent
paints with no event to be won in. **This is its own conversation and it blocks nothing in the
resort** - the resort is classes and gates, and the rewards are what the gates pay.

## Q6 IS NOT ANSWERED - THE POLICE LADDER STAYS OPEN

The CRUISER is paid by a sports gold and the SUPERCRUISER by a super gold. Both still exist under
the new ladder, so nothing breaks by leaving this alone; what is unsettled is whether a production
gold should pay a police car too, and there is no third force car for it to pay.

---

## AND THE QUESTION THE DECISION CREATED IS ANSWERED OUTRIGHT

*"Yeah formula is endgame. It's meant to be broken."*

**THE WALKOVER IS THE NOVELTY.** A formula car entering a production race wins by the car alone,
and that is the intent rather than a balance fault to be corrected. This closes it in the strongest
of the three possible directions and it has two consequences worth writing down.

**NOTHING IS TO BE ADDED TO HOLD THE FIELD TO IT.** The rubber band is the one named exception to
one-physics-for-every-car, and it must NOT be reached for here. A later session finding that a
formula car laps a production field must not read that as a defect.

**AND IT PUTS WEIGHT ON WHAT A FORMULA ENTRY PAYS**, which is inside Q5. A broken car is a joy
once and a way to farm every reward in the game forever, and those are different things. The owner
has not ruled on it and it must not be assumed either way.

## WHAT IS LEFT BEFORE THE RESORT CAN START

    OPEN, and it is ONE question - MINUTES or MILES? The answer above says
    "50 min Test drive w Timed". Every existing trigger of this kind is
    DISTANCE - 25 miles utility, 50 miles production, and the older hundred-
    mile rule before them - and the proposal recorded on 2026-09-11 said
    "driving fifty miles in test drive". Fifty MINUTES is a new quantity that
    nothing tracks, and it is roughly two and a half times as far at the speed
    the interstate is actually driven. It also has to say whether it
    ACCUMULATES across runs, because `dist` is per-run today and the run clock
    ends a run at zero.

    NOT BLOCKING - the new production car (Q2 detail), the reward schedule
    (Q5), and the police ladder (Q6).
