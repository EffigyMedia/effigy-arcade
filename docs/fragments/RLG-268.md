### RLG-268 — Out of time or busted offers retry or quit, and a quit tournament is saved per class
- **RULED 2026-09-16T00:41:06-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "When you run out of time or get busted, the options should be retry from that race or quit the tournament. And if you quit the tournament, the state of the tournament is saved for that class of vehicle. So that means if you quit in the middle of a sports tournament, whenever you have a sports car selected in the garage and tournament, you see the last saved state of that tournament and it continues. If you would have switched to a supercar, the tournament would either show a new state or a saved state of another super tournament that you quit out of without completing. Same goes for a production class. Quitting a single race is not persistent."

THREE PARTS.
1. THE END SCREEN OFFERS TWO THINGS after a run ends on the clock or on a bust: RETRY THAT RACE, or QUIT THE TOURNAMENT.
2. QUITTING SAVES THE TOURNAMENT, and the save is PER VEHICLE CLASS. Picking a sports car later shows that sports tournament where it was left; a supercar shows the super tournament, saved or new; production likewise. The class is the key.
3. A SINGLE RACE IS NOT SAVED. Quitting one keeps nothing.

WHAT EXISTS TODAY (code read, 0.14.43): `tourState` reports `on`, `round` and `pts`, and `TOUR_PTS` scores a round; the tournament is per-run state and nothing writes it to the save. `showEnd(reason)` draws one screen with no retry. Every persisted key is `effigyarcade.<scope>.<what>.v1` and per-class storage would sit there.

NOT ASKED YET: whether a saved tournament keeps the car it was started in as well as the class, and what RETRY costs - a fresh clock, the same round, and whether the points already won in that round survive.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T03:19:33-04:00** — THE TWO OPEN QUESTIONS ARE ANSWERED, AND THEY OPENED TWO MORE WHICH ARE ALSO ANSWERED (owner, 2026-09-16).

THE CLASS IS THE ONLY KEY, and a saved tournament does NOT remember the car. Pick any sports car and the saved sports tournament continues in the car you just chose. The owner's reason for preferring it: it is the version that can be held in your head, and it lets a player switch cars mid-tournament when one is not working out. The alternatives - putting you back in the saved car, or keying the save on the car itself - were both offered and both declined.

RETRIES ARE LIMITED, THREE FOR THE WHOLE TOURNAMENT, spent wherever they are needed across the four rounds. Unlimited retry was offered first and declined. So a player can lose one round badly and recover, and cannot lose three. The count belongs on the end screen.

AND RUNNING OUT IS THE REAL LOSS CONDITION. Out of retries ends the tournament, the saved state for that class is CLEARED, and picking that class again starts a fresh one. The end screen at that point offers only the way out - there is nothing left to retry.

WHAT THAT SETTLES ABOUT THE SHAPE. The end screen after a run ends on the clock or on a bust offers RETRY THAT RACE and QUIT THE TOURNAMENT while retries remain, and QUIT alone when they do not. Retry is a fresh clock on the same round with the standings untouched; the failed round scores nobody.

AND THE DEFECT THIS SITS ON TOP OF, found by a code read of 0.14.49: `wreck()` and the OUT OF TIME branch have NO `tourOn` CHECK AT ALL. Both call `showEnd(reason)` directly, and `showEnd` has no tournament awareness of any kind. So today, being busted or running out of time DURING a tournament drops the player onto the generic end card - RUN IT AGAIN, CHANGE CAR, MAIN MENU - while `tourRound` and `tourPts` are left standing in memory, and RUN IT AGAIN calls `start()` which re-runs the current round without saying that is what it is doing. The tournament is neither ended nor acknowledged. That is the hole this ruling fills rather than a separate defect.

WHERE THE STATE LIVES TODAY, so the save has something to write: `tourRound`, `tourPts` and `tourField` are closure-local and die with the page; `tourReset()` rebuilds them; `tourScore(place)` is the only writer of points; `tourRound++` at the finish is the only advance; `TOUR_MILES` has four entries, so a tournament is four rounds. `classOf(optBody)` answers production, sports, super or formula, and formula has no league. Every persisted key is `effigyarcade.<scope>.<what>.v1`, and the per-class tournament save sits there.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T03:36:08-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.50, ALL THREE PARTS.

THE DEFECT UNDERNEATH IT WAS LARGER THAN THE FEATURE. `wreck()` and the OUT OF TIME branch both call `showEnd` directly and `showEnd` had no tournament awareness of any kind, so losing a round drew the ORDINARY end card - RUN IT AGAIN, CHANGE CAR, MAIN MENU - while `tourRound` and `tourPts` sat untouched in memory behind it, and RUN IT AGAIN called `start()` and re-ran the current round without saying that is what it was doing. The tournament was neither ended nor acknowledged. The falsifier reproduces exactly that and the harness reports `card offers again,garage,menu`.

THE BRANCH IS IN `showEnd` AND NOT AT THE TWO CALL SITES, which is [[RLG-232]]'s shape: naming every exit is what this codebase has paid for three times, so a third way to end a run added later is covered by construction.

RETRIES. `TOUR_RETRIES` is 3 for the whole tournament, reset by `tourReset`. The attempt is spent on the PRESS and not when the run ends - a player who quits after failing has not used one, and charging them would make the tournament they saved worse than the one they were in a moment earlier. `start()` re-runs the same round on its own because `tourRound` was never advanced, and `tourScore` only ever runs at the finish line, so the failed round scores nobody and the standings a retry races against are the ones carried in. Measured: retries 3 to 2 on the press, round 0 to 0, points 0 to 0.

THE SAVE IS ONE KEY WITH A ROW PER CLASS - `interstate-tour` - holding round, points, retries and the field's point totals. It is a `-suffix` slot, so `ownedBy` reaches it and `Arcade.save.clear` erases it exactly like `interstate-opts`; it is registered in `smoke-test`'s `saves()` list, with a note saying it rides the same code path as `-opts` and so is a registration rather than an independent proof. Only the field's POINTS are stored, not the rival objects: a rival is rebuilt from its index by `tourReset`, and persisting the objects would persist a shape that function owns.

`tourClass` IS THE PIECE THAT WAS NOT OBVIOUS. The tournament in memory is a set of bare module variables and nothing about them says which league they came from, so without it a supercar picked mid-way through a sports ladder would carry that round, those points and that field straight into the super tournament.

AND THE SWAP IS HUNG OFF ONE EVENT. `enforceModeRules` runs on every garage redraw - every paint swatch, every arrow - so `tourSwap` returns immediately unless the class under the player CHANGED. That guard is load-bearing rather than an optimisation: firing every time would overwrite a part-driven ladder with the copy on disk sixty presses running. It runs even when the tournament is off, deliberately: a player who changes class with the mode on SINGLE RACE has still moved class, and their ladder must be parked then.

THE MODE BUTTON LOADS RATHER THAN RESETS. `raceTour = true` called `tourReset()`, which was right while a tournament was per-run state and is the one thing that must not happen now - the owner's rule is that selecting a class with a quit tournament in it CONTINUES it, and the MODE button is exactly where a player asks for that.

A SPENT TOURNAMENT CLEARS ITS ROW. `enforceModeRules`'s `tourDone` retirement now clears the save as well as the memory; left behind, the next car of that class would load a completed ladder off disk and the retirement would have been undone by a save it did not know about.

OUT OF ATTEMPTS CLEARS IT TOO, and the clear happens when the card OPENS rather than on the way out, because every button on that screen leads out of it and the outcome must not depend on which one is pressed.

RETIRE IS NOW "QUIT & SAVE" on both screens (owner, 2026-09-16). The old label was true when quitting threw the ladder away and is a lie now that it keeps it - this is the case where a label has to move with the behaviour, because a player who reads RETIRE and means it would avoid the one button that does what they want.

`tools/tour-save-test.py` IS THE PROOF AND IT DRIVES THE REAL BUTTONS: a car picked with the garage arrows, MODE pressed until the label reads TOURNAMENT, the clock run out, and the card read off the screen. `API.tourState` is read for numbers and never for navigation ([[RLG-065]]). `--selftest` drops the tournament flag on the frame the run ends - which IS the old build, because the flag is the only thing `showEnd` branches on - and the file reports it on four checks.

TWO HARNESS MISTAKES WORTH RECORDING. It first picked VECTOR as the supercar: `BODY_CLASS` puts VECTOR, APEX and COMET in `formula`, and a formula car has NO LEAGUE at all ([[RLG-213]]) - the one class that can never hold a tournament. And its walk back to the garage assumed one card; the doors differ on every screen, so it reads what is offered instead.

tour-exit-test, league-test, class-test, race-test and smoke-test all pass on this build.

WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE: whether three attempts is the right number, and whether losing the last one lands as a real ending or as an abrupt one. Both are the owner's on a device, and the count is a single constant.

STILL OPEN AND NOT PART OF THIS: [[RLG-274]], the grid order between rounds, which the owner raised while this was being built.
