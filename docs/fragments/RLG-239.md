### RLG-239 — The crate splits into three pickups: a jerry can for time, a NOS bottle, and a red toolbox
- **RULED 2026-09-13T13:34:14-04:00** — **REQUESTED 2026-09-13, NOT STARTED.** Owner: *"I'd like to separate the pickup crates into the 3 constituent pickups: Fuel Jerry Can for fuel (time), a NOS bottle for NOS, and a red toolbox for repairs. We can omit the type of pickup based what the node requires. This may mean we need more intelligent spawning and a faster interval."*

## THIS REVERSES [[RLG-125]], AND THE NEW REQUEST ALREADY ANSWERS PART OF WHY IT WAS DROPPED

On 2026-08-31 the owner proposed three crates (repair, NOS, time) and dropped them for one crate that gives everything the car can use. RLG-125 recorded four objections:

| RLG-125 objection | What the 2026-09-13 request says |
|---|---|
| At one crate every 20 to 34 seconds there is never a choice to make. | Answered in direction: "more intelligent spawning and a faster interval". The interval is not set. |
| The three are not symmetric, and time is worthless when the clock is off. | Partly answered: "omit the type based on what the node requires". What "requires" means is not set. |
| A car that never sees one type meets fewer pickups, unless the share is redistributed. | Not answered. |
| At 35,000 units a crate is a smudge, so the type must read by colour. | Answered: three different objects - a jerry can, a bottle, a RED toolbox. |

RLG-125 also noted that a dedicated NOS pickup gives a full-bottle car nothing. Omitting by need answers that only if need is read when the pickup spawns and is still true when it is reached.

## OPEN, AND OWNER-ONLY

1. What "what the node requires" means: the car's current need (damage, bottle level, clock running), the car's capability (no bottle, no NOS bottles ever), or the mode.
2. The spawn interval, and whether pickups come in groups so a choice exists.
3. What each pays. Today one crate pays 10 seconds when the clock runs, repairs 25 per cent, and adds 25 per cent NOS.
4. Whether an omitted type's share goes to the others.
5. Whether the jerry can is the refill for the fuel gauge the owner asked for the same day.

The owner now calls time FUEL. The fuel gauge design (the same day's request) should be drawn with the jerry can in mind.
