### RLG-259 — A police car directly in front slowly forces the car behind to a stop, for the player and racers alike
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:14:41-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, the full answer this fragment comes from: "I want to get rid of the radio only patrols and speed traps can add to your engaged cruisers, they all obey the same slot allotment. It still seems like police cruisers, and possibly also interceptors do not collide with each other. They need to have their own navigation behavior so that they avoid collisions with traffic and each each other intelligently. We are also missing the proper functionality for them to slow you down if a cruiser or interceptor is directly in front of you. It should force your car slowly to a stop and it's up to the player or other racers NPC's cause they should all function the same to quickly swing to the side to get out from behind their slow down triggering zone so you can speed up and go around them. Another thing I noticed was that the roadblocks disappeared in the mirror as you passed them, which tells me they are despond too quickly or stop being rendered or whatever."

THIS FRAGMENT IS THE FOURTH AND FIFTH SENTENCES. The car behind a cruiser or Interceptor is forced slowly to a stop while it is in that car's slow-down zone. The player and racer NPCs work the same way, and each escapes by swinging sideways out of the zone. RLG-247 built a related rule: a cruiser lined up in front brakes and busts a slow player. The owner says the proper function is still missing.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:19:31-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, added while the switch was being removed: "The forced slow down triggering zone on a cruiser or interceptor is the same implementation that should be used during the intercept game when the player is playing as police, except obviously the roles are reversed."

SO THERE IS ONE ZONE, OWNED BY A POLICE CAR, NOT BY WHO IS CHASED. In Hot Pursuit a cruiser or Interceptor ahead of the player or a racer forces that car to slow. In INTERCEPT the player drives the police car, and the zone in front of the player forces the racer behind it to slow. RLG-203 and the pursuit-stop rule (RLG-247) already describe INTERCEPT as the inverse of Hot Pursuit; this ruling makes that one code path.
