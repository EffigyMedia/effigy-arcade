### RLG-259 — A police car directly in front slowly forces the car behind to a stop, for the player and racers alike
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:14:41-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, the full answer this fragment comes from: "I want to get rid of the radio only patrols and speed traps can add to your engaged cruisers, they all obey the same slot allotment. It still seems like police cruisers, and possibly also interceptors do not collide with each other. They need to have their own navigation behavior so that they avoid collisions with traffic and each each other intelligently. We are also missing the proper functionality for them to slow you down if a cruiser or interceptor is directly in front of you. It should force your car slowly to a stop and it's up to the player or other racers NPC's cause they should all function the same to quickly swing to the side to get out from behind their slow down triggering zone so you can speed up and go around them. Another thing I noticed was that the roadblocks disappeared in the mirror as you passed them, which tells me they are despond too quickly or stop being rendered or whatever."

THIS FRAGMENT IS THE FOURTH AND FIFTH SENTENCES. The car behind a cruiser or Interceptor is forced slowly to a stop while it is in that car's slow-down zone. The player and racer NPCs work the same way, and each escapes by swinging sideways out of the zone. RLG-247 built a related rule: a cruiser lined up in front brakes and busts a slow player. The owner says the proper function is still missing.
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:19:31-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, added while the switch was being removed: "The forced slow down triggering zone on a cruiser or interceptor is the same implementation that should be used during the intercept game when the player is playing as police, except obviously the roles are reversed."

SO THERE IS ONE ZONE, OWNED BY A POLICE CAR, NOT BY WHO IS CHASED. In Hot Pursuit a cruiser or Interceptor ahead of the player or a racer forces that car to slow. In INTERCEPT the player drives the police car, and the zone in front of the player forces the racer behind it to slow. RLG-203 and the pursuit-stop rule (RLG-247) already describe INTERCEPT as the inverse of Hot Pursuit; this ruling makes that one code path.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T00:26:33-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.43. `stopBlockerAhead(z, x, self)` returns the police car in front of a given car: any live, non-trap cruiser within PURSUIT_STOP.near and PURSUIT_STOP.wide, and - when the player is the police car - the player. `stopZoneCap(prev, cur, blocked, dt)` decays a cap from the speed the car had at PURSUIT_STOP.decel while it is blocked and drops it the moment it is not. The player reads it after the throttle, the brake, NOS and `spdHold`; a rival reads it after `fieldHold`. Both are two lines because it is one rule.

IT BEATS A HELD SPEED ON PURPOSE. A forced stop a pinned throttle can out-run is not a forced stop, and a harness pinning a speed stands in for a player holding the throttle down.

WHAT IT REPLACES: nothing. RLG-247 made the cruiser in front BRAKE and RLG-203 made a rival lift for the player on shift; neither takes hold of the car behind, which is what the owner asked for. Both still run.

THE ESCAPE IS THE STEERING RACE. A chasing cruiser steers at the player at 1.15 a second, so getting PURSUIT_STOP.wide across before it follows is the owner's "quickly swing to the side". Measured in the harness by holding the cruiser in its lane; in play it follows.

PROOF. tools/stop-zone-test.py, four arms, three consecutive clean runs. LINED: a cruiser 1,500 ahead in the line, the player pinned at 120mph, falls to 0. ASIDE: the same with the player held wide + 0.2 across, never below 120. RELEASE: lined up, then moved aside at two seconds, back to 120. INTERCEPT: a rival held 1,500 behind the player's police car falls to 0, and one held aside does not.

FOUR THINGS THE ARMS HAD TO LEARN, each of them a measurement.
1. THE LOW POINT, NOT THE LAST READING, for the player and the rival alike: a car dragged to a stop in a cruiser's line is BUSTED (RLG-247) and a rival held there is STOPPED (RLG-203), and both release the zone - after which a pinned throttle returns the car to the pin within a second.
2. THE CRUISER IS RE-PLACED EVERY FRAME. Mutating one placed cruiser drifted: a cruiser that gives up becomes a parked trap, a trap is not a blocker, and the arm went on moving a trap around and read "no drag" on a build that drags.
3. THE WINGMEN ARE CLEARED IN THE RIVAL ARMS. On shift the player has friendly cruisers, they are police cars, and a rival behind one is dragged by this very zone - correctly, and not what that arm is measuring.
4. THE NEGATIVE RIVAL ARM RUNS FIRST AND LASTS TWO SECONDS, inside STOP_HOLD, because a rival stopped by the arm before it leaves the race and disturbs the shift for whatever runs next.
