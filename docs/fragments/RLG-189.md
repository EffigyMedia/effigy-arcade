### RLG-189 — The badge sits on the face rather than under the windscreen
- **RULED 2026-09-09T16:27:48-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"I'd like you to lower the badge on all three supercar fronts. For the crest,
I'd like it to be directly in between the headlights. The other two I'd like to be down towards the
center of the painted hood."*

**IT WAS TUCKED UNDER THE WINDSCREEN, AND THE RECORD SAYS WHY.** `topY + 0.038` was chosen after an
earlier attempt put the badge at `topY + 0.155`, where it landed on the lamp line of every face and
sat inside CREST's light bar. Clearing the lamps upward solved that and left the mark hard against
the glass with the whole bonnet empty below it - which is the space the owner has been filling all
afternoon.

**THE TWO ANSWERS DIFFER BECAUSE THE TWO FACES DO, and that is the useful part.** CREST wears a single
band right across its nose, so the only place a badge can be *between* the headlights is the break at
the middle of it: the band's own centre line, `by2 + bh2*0.5`. STALLION and MATADOR carry a lamp at
each side with paint between them, so their badge drops onto that paint at `topY + 0.090`. One
constant for all three would have had to be wrong for one of them, which is the same shape of answer
the wing colours and the tail lamps already take.

**NOT VERIFIED ON A DEVICE.** Whether 0.090 is far enough down for the two front-engined faces is a
judgement about a picture, and the owner has the device.
