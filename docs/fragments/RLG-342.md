### RLG-342 — The practice clock setting reads CHECKPOINTS rather than TIMED
- **RULED 2026-09-24T12:21:28-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-24: "Instead of the setting saying timed, I'd rather it say checkpoints."

THE LABEL ONLY. `timedRun`, `data-act="timed"`, the saved key and `API.setTimed` all keep their names. Renaming a STORED key is how a setting silently goes back to its default for every player who had already set it, and six harnesses read that name - race-test alone asserts it three times. What the owner asked to change is what the row says, and that is what changed.

AND THE NAME IS THE BETTER ONE ON ITS MERITS. What the switch turns on is the gantries and the seconds they pay; naming it after the thing you drive through says that, where naming it after the clock said only that a number was counting.
