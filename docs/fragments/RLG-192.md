### RLG-192 — The CREST's spoiler wears the car's own paint
- **RULED 2026-09-09T19:03:56-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"let's make its spoiler colored like the matador's."*

**A CREST IN ANY PAINT WORE A BLACK WING.** MATADOR's aerofoil is `o.lo` with a white lift along its
top edge and stanchions in `o.body` - the car's own colour. CREST's ducktail blade was a fixed
'#1a1d22' with '#15171b' uprights, so a colour the player chose in the garage stopped at the deck and
the largest single feature on the back of the car ignored it.

**THE SHAPE IS WHAT SEPARATES THE TWO CARS, NOT THE PAINT.** MATADOR wears an aerofoil on stanchions
that stand up from the deck; CREST wears a swan-neck blade hanging from uprights above a ducktail.
Those are different wings and they stay different wings. What was doing the separating by accident
was that one of them was painted and the other was not, which is not a design decision - it is what a
hardcoded colour looks like after the rest of the car learned to take the player's.

Same three values as the high wing now, on the ducktail's own geometry. Both ends read `carWing`, so
the face and the tail cannot disagree about it ([[RLG-184]]).
