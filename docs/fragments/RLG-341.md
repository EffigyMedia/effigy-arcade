### RLG-341 — Headlight glare on the road is drawn over other vehicles
- **RULED 2026-09-24T12:09:08-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-24, from the device: "Other vehicles, headlight glare on the road can be seen through them."

READ AS A DRAW ORDER FAULT. The wash a car's headlights lay on the tarmac is painted OVER the vehicles rather than under them, so a car standing in another car's beam has the glare drawn across it and reads as transparent. That is the same shape as the two reports already answered this month - a car drawn through scenery, and a car drawn through the checkpoint signs - and both were order rather than geometry.

WHERE TO LOOK: the `beams` and `wash` layers are separate from `sprites` in the layer list, so the order between them is explicit and readable. `paintBucket` sorts far to near INSIDE one sprite bucket, which orders cars against each other; a wash drawn after the whole sprite pass is a different pass entirely and no per-bucket sort can help it.

AND CHECK WHETHER IT IS THE PLAYER'S OWN BEAM OR ANOTHER CAR'S. `API.headsLit` and the beam layer report what is lit; the two cases have different fixes, because the player's wash is drawn once at a known place and an NPC's travels with the car that casts it.

NOT DIAGNOSED. NOT STARTED. It arrived while two other reports were in flight and is recorded here so it is not lost.
