### RLG-040 — Traffic merges only a fraction of a lane, and does not finish the move
- **RULED 2026-08-28T17:33:05-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "when traffic merges, they don't go fully to a new lane. It's like a fraction
of a lane. We need to make sure they fully merge a new lane, and that when we make the roadway
larger that persists."

**The same fault is measured from the rivals' side in [[RLG-033]] part 1, evidence [[EVD-007]]:**
rivals spend about 60% of their time off a lane centre with the lateral term on, against 0% with it
removed. They are not overtaking and they are not in a lane either.

**The shape of it.** A lane change is written as a per-frame lateral RATE that lasts exactly as long
as something is in front of the car. When the pressure goes, the car is pulled back toward the
centre of the lane index it started in - and nothing ever updates that index. So a car leans out far
enough to unblock its own scan, stops leaning, and returns to where it began. There is no target
lane and no notion of the move being finished.

**What is wanted:** a lane change has a TARGET lane, the car commits to it, and it is finished -
either completed or abandoned as a decision, not left half done by a decaying pressure.

**The second half of the ruling is a constraint on the fix, not a separate job.** [[RLG-024]] widens
the road, and the owner requires that full merging still holds afterwards. That rules out any fix
expressed in absolute lateral distance or in a hard-coded four-lane table: it has to be expressed in
LANES. `LANE_X` and `LANES` already exist and the fix must go through them.

Traffic and rivals are two code paths and this is one idea. They may be two units; they may not be
two designs.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T22:13:08-04:00** — **BUILT in v0.9.10, and the fault was not where the code read as if it would be.** The prediction
written before the instrument ran was that deliberate merges were the fractional ones, because the
decision aimed at `c.x + 0.50` clamped to `0.86` - from the outermost lane that is a fifth of a lane
onto the verge, and not a lane at all. That path exists and is fixed, but it is not what the owner
saw: `tools/merge-test.py` measured deliberate merges at a median of 1.00 lanes with 96-100% of them
ending on a lane centre, before anything was changed.

**What moves a fraction of a lane is GIVING WAY.** `keepLaneOpen` leans the car furthest ahead
toward the verge to hold a corridor open, and it set `c.yielding` - which nothing anywhere ever
cleared. So a car that once made room was slowed for the rest of its life, could never merge again
because the merge decision tests `!c.yielding`, and was left standing wherever the lean had stopped,
which is between two lanes. It was 42-46% of every lateral move on the road.

**Measured, two independent invocations each side.** The discriminating figure reads position over
time and no engine state at all, so no change inside the merge logic can make it report success by
moving a flag:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| at rest and between two lanes, share of at-rest samples | **7.8%** | **1.3%** |
| off a lane centre, share of all samples | 9-15% | 3% |
| merges the engine decided on, per 60s run | 41-50 | 56-64 |

**The check was watched failing before it was believed.** The change was stashed, the harness run
against the engine as it was, and the parked-between-lanes line reported 7.8% and FAILED. The first
version of that check did something worse than fail wrongly - it ABSTAINED. It asked where a car
went after it stopped giving way, and on the defective engine there is no afterwards, so it reported
"0 samples" and said nothing. A check that goes quiet on the defect is the vacuous kind this project
keeps finding, and it was rewritten rather than kept.

**What was changed:**

- **A yield ends.** `yielding` is a timer that `keepLaneOpen` renews while that window still needs
  the room. When it runs out the car commits to the lane it is nearest and drives fully into it. The
  lean ITSELF is untouched, because the corridor guarantee depends on it and it is deliberately
  allowed onto road that no lane covers ([[RLG-037]]).
- **And it gets its pace back.** `cruiseFloor` was written on rogues at the top of every frame and
  read by NOTHING - the vestige of this same fix, never wired up. It is now set for every car and is
  the ceiling a car recovers to, so nobody carries a permanent penalty for having been polite.
- **A merge targets a LANE, one at a time**, from the lane the car is in rather than from wherever
  it is standing. There is no half lane past the outermost one to aim at any more.
- **Every lateral number is in `LANE_W`** - the merge rate, the arrival threshold, the idle drift
  bound, the spawn jitter, the verge the yield leans onto. `LANE_W` moved up beside `LANE_X` so
  traffic and rivals share one definition. That is the ruling's second half: widen the road under
  [[RLG-024]] and full merging is still full merging, with nothing to remember.
- **The idle drift does not run during a move.** It was being added on top of the merge every frame.
- The horn's scatter keeps its own lane index until it ARRIVES. Writing the new one at the moment of
  asking was how a car that never finished could claim a lane it was not in.

**THE PLAYER IS SLOWER ON THE INTERSTATE, and it is this change that did it.** `drive-test`'s peak
speed over eight runs: 126-182mph, mean 156, two of them under the 150 assertion. Four runs on the
engine as it was: 150-188, mean 175, none under. Cars that gave way used to park on the shoulder at
48mph forever, which left a permanent hole in the traffic; they rejoin now, so the lanes are
genuinely fuller. That is the change working rather than a defect, but it makes [[RLG-056]] worse and
eight runs against four is not a settled number. **The threshold was not moved.** Recorded for
revisiting, by the owner's decision of 2026-08-28.

**Not changed, and deliberately:** the lean itself, its `WARN` band and its urgency curve. The
corridor read 0.409 against a 0.34 limit after the change, in one run. [[RLG-037]] says the guarantee
already fails about one run in four under horn pressure, and nothing here was tuned toward it.
