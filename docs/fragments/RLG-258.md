### RLG-258 — Police cars navigate round traffic and each other, and collide with each other
- **RULED 2026-09-15T16:14:40-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, the full answer this fragment comes from: "I want to get rid of the radio only patrols and speed traps can add to your engaged cruisers, they all obey the same slot allotment. It still seems like police cruisers, and possibly also interceptors do not collide with each other. They need to have their own navigation behavior so that they avoid collisions with traffic and each each other intelligently. We are also missing the proper functionality for them to slow you down if a cruiser or interceptor is directly in front of you. It should force your car slowly to a stop and it's up to the player or other racers NPC's cause they should all function the same to quickly swing to the side to get out from behind their slow down triggering zone so you can speed up and go around them. Another thing I noticed was that the roadblocks disappeared in the mirror as you passed them, which tells me they are despond too quickly or stop being rendered or whatever."

THIS FRAGMENT IS THE SECOND AND THIRD SENTENCES: cruisers, and possibly Interceptors, do not collide with each other, and they need their own navigation that avoids traffic and each other. RLG-253 recorded the same report earlier: chasing cruisers stack on the player and pin the player in place.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T01:10:07-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.14.46, both halves.
1. THEY COLLIDE. The cruiser update tested against the traffic, the roadblocks and the player and against another police car by nothing at all. It now pairs each car with the ones before it in the array: 30 damage each - against the traffic's 45, because two police cars trading paint at similar speeds is a shunt - and both lose a fifth of their speed. `hurtCop` gives each the invulnerability window, so one contact is not counted every frame. A parked trap is scenery here as everywhere (RLG-173).
2. THEY DRIVE AROUND EACH OTHER. Both navigation loops read `traffic` only: the LIFT that caps a cruiser's speed to whatever is directly in front, and the SWERVE that picks a line around it. Both now see other police cars. The swerve between two police cars is weighted 1.1 against the traffic's 1.9, deliberately: two cruisers that shove each other aside as hard as they avoid a lorry cannot hold the box around the player (RLG-158), and the box is the manoeuvre the owner asked for.

PROOF. tools/cop-cop-test.py. THROUGH: two cruisers held in one place for two seconds take 120 damage each; on 2770bf08 they take nothing, which is the check that tells the builds apart. TRAP: a cruiser overlapping a parked trap takes nothing. AVOID: two cruisers left to drive themselves overlap on 0 of 276 frames - AN INVARIANT RATHER THAN A DISCRIMINATOR, and the file says so: the old build also separates in that staging, so the arm is kept to catch a future change that makes them merge, not to prove this one. An earlier version of that arm asked whether the car behind ended up slower or across, which is true of a cruiser doing anything at all, and passed on the engine with no avoidance in it.
