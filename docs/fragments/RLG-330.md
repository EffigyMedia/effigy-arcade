### RLG-330 — The valley range is cut off by a hard mask and should reach the ground
- **RULED 2026-09-23T20:47:55-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-23, from the device: "The mountains on the valley look good, except for the hard cut-off mask. Let them complete their face down to the ground."

SO THE SHAPE IS RIGHT AND THE BOTTOM EDGE IS WRONG. The peaks across the valley are drawn by `rangePeak` under [[RLG-297]], and something is clipping them at a line rather than letting the face run down to where it meets the ground. A straight horizontal edge under a mountain is the tell - it reads as a mask, which is exactly what the owner called it.

NOT STARTED. The first job is to find what sets that bottom edge: a clip rectangle, a fixed foot height, or a fill that stops early.
- **RULED 2026-09-24T00:02:38-04:00** — CLARIFIED BY THE OWNER, 2026-09-24: "I think we were talking about different cliff bases. I meant the one over the edge going down into the void not the one that goes up from the roadway."

SO THIS FRAGMENT WAS ABOUT THE DOWNWARD SIDE ALL ALONG. The "hard cut-off mask" is at the foot of the fall over the edge - where the cliff face meets the valley floor - not at the foot of the face that rises from the roadside. The upward face was never the subject and was fine until [[RLG-331]]'s first cut dimmed it.

WHICH MEANS [[RLG-329]] HAS ALREADY MOVED THIS. Before it there was no face over the edge at all, so its "base" was wherever the hole stopped. There is a face now, hazed down its own fall and meeting the floor. WHETHER A HARD EDGE REMAINS AT THAT MEETING IS A QUESTION FOR THE DEVICE, and nothing should be changed here until the owner has looked at the new picture - the thing being judged is different from the thing that was reported.
