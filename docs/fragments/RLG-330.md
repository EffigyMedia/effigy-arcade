### RLG-330 — The valley range is cut off by a hard mask and should reach the ground
- **RULED 2026-09-23T20:47:55-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-23, from the device: "The mountains on the valley look good, except for the hard cut-off mask. Let them complete their face down to the ground."

SO THE SHAPE IS RIGHT AND THE BOTTOM EDGE IS WRONG. The peaks across the valley are drawn by `rangePeak` under [[RLG-297]], and something is clipping them at a line rather than letting the face run down to where it meets the ground. A straight horizontal edge under a mountain is the tell - it reads as a mask, which is exactly what the owner called it.

NOT STARTED. The first job is to find what sets that bottom edge: a clip rectangle, a fixed foot height, or a fill that stops early.
