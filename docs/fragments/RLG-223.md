### RLG-223 — A locked car's garage shows nothing but the way out of it, and the menus want an overhaul

**TRACKED, NOT STARTED.** Owner, 2026-09-12: *"If you don't have a car unlocked we don't wanna show
any options in the garage other than changing car. That being said I would like to overhaul the
menus, but we can do that together in a bit with hiding buttons and what not."*

---

## THESE ARE TWO ITEMS AND ONLY ONE OF THEM IS READY

**1. THE LOCKED-CAR RULE IS DECIDED.** Standing on a car you do not own, the garage offers one thing:
moving to another car. Paint, mode, entry class, flip, ENTER — none of them mean anything about a car
you cannot drive, and offering them invites a tap that can only be refused.

**2. THE MENU OVERHAUL IS NOT, AND THE OWNER SAID SO EXPLICITLY.** *"We can do that together in a
bit."* **Do not start it.** It is a design conversation about which buttons exist and when they are
hidden, and the owner has asked to be in it.

## WHY THE FIRST ONE FITS WHAT IS ALREADY RULED

[[RLG-115]] settled the shape of this once: a control that cannot act is **greyed rather than
removed** where the greying teaches a rule the player meets elsewhere — the MODE button is that case.
The owner's note here is the other half of the same idea, and it is not a contradiction: **a control
that is meaningless rather than merely unavailable should not be on the screen at all.**

Whoever builds this should put that distinction to the owner if it is not obvious per control, rather
than guessing which of the two treatments each one gets. The one thing that is certain is the car
picker stays.

## AND THE OWNER CUTS EXPLANATORY TEXT

Recorded on 2026-09-12 and worth repeating here because a hiding pass is exactly where it gets
violated: **ship the control and no caption.** A garage that hides its options must not grow a line
telling the player why they are hidden. The absence is the message; the card already says the car is
locked.

## WHAT A LOCKED CAR'S GARAGE HAS NOW

The card as before (the silhouette, `???`, LOCKED and how to unlock it), the two arrows, and BACK. Nothing else. Removed on a locked car: the paint swatches, GEARBOX, STRIPES, WORK VEHICLES, the formula ENTER class, MODE and its notes, TIME, TIMED, HOT PURSUIT, a fork's own buttons (Motorsport's QUALIFY), and the greyed LOCKED drive button. BACK stays because it leaves the garage rather than setting anything in it.

- `showGarage` returns early for a locked car, and hands `openVeil` only `prev`, `next` and `back`. A stale veil or a hardware press cannot reach a setting whose button is not drawn. `drive` still checks `carLocked` for the same reason.
- **WORK VEHICLES is removed too.** It is a filter on which cars the arrows reach, so it could be read as part of changing car. I removed it to follow the owner's words, "no options other than changing car". The owner can put it back.
- This does not contradict [[RLG-115]]. MODE is greyed on a van because the van is drivable and the greying teaches a rule. A car you cannot drive has no rule to teach, so its controls are absent. No caption says why.

## THE ARROWS DO NOT MOVE UNDER THE THUMB

The first build centred a locked garage like every menu, and it is shorter, so the arrows sat about 100 px lower than on an owned car. I sent the owner a capture and asked. **Owner chose: keep the arrows still.**

- Each driving cabinet pins the garage to the top: `#veil:has(.gbox){align-content:start}`.
- An owned garage records where its arrows are (`garagePickTop`). A locked card gets a `.gspace` spacer sized to bring its arrows to that line. It is measured, because the owned card's height depends on its note, stats and swatches.
- The line is always known: `enforceCarRules` never opens the garage on a locked car, so a locked car is reached only by an arrow from an owned one.
- The arrows share their row (`.gbox.pick`) instead of standing as two 46 px squares.
