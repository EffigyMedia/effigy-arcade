### RLG-210 — The garage card shows one end of a car, large, centred and floor-anchored
- **RULED 2026-09-11T02:20:09-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED.** Owner, 2026-09-11: *"I've decided I only want the front of a vehicle shown
in the garage. That way it can be larger and centered. We should anchor all the vehicles at the
bottom instead of the top too. And try to have a single box fit them all (except we grow that box for
unorthodox size vehicles - which are mainly in the utility class)."* And, a moment later: *"Maybe
after we refactor the menu design, there could be a flip button that shows either end of a car one at
a time."*

**FOUR DECISIONS AND ONE SUGGESTION**, and they are separable:

    1. ONE END ONLY - the FRONT - rather than both ends on the card.
    2. LARGER AND CENTRED, which is what the freed space buys.
    3. ANCHORED AT THE BOTTOM rather than the top, so the cars sit on a common floor.
    4. ONE BOX FOR ALL OF THEM, grown only for the unorthodox sizes - mainly utility.
    5. AND LATER, A FLIP BUTTON that shows either end one at a time. Explicitly placed AFTER the
       menu refactor, and phrased as "maybe" - it is a suggestion and not part of 1 to 4.

**WHY THE BOTTOM ANCHOR IS THE LOAD-BEARING ONE.** A row of vehicles anchored at the TOP hangs them
from a ceiling, so a tall van and a low supercar share a roofline and nothing shares a ground. Cars
on a road have a common floor and no common roof, so a top anchor is the one choice that makes every
card look wrong in the same way. It is also what makes a single box possible at all: heights differ
far more than widths across this fleet, and a floor absorbs that difference where a ceiling
advertises it.

**AND THE EXCEPTION IS NAMED RATHER THAN DISCOVERED.** "Unorthodox size vehicles - which are mainly
in the utility class" is the SEMI, the VAN and the AMBULANCE. `BODY_CLASS` already names that class,
so the rule can ask the record rather than measuring a sprite - and this project's standing rule is
that the record declares what a vehicle is. **But "mainly" is the owner's own hedge**, so a
class-only test is probably wrong: the PICKUP is production and is a tall vehicle, and the FORMULA
cars are extreme in the other direction. Measure the fleet before choosing between a class test and
a size test.

**WHAT THIS TOUCHES THAT IS NOT OBVIOUS.**

    `drawGarageCar` is the painter, and the card is also drawn in the fleet sheet tool - a
    change to the framing that skips `tools/fleet-sheet.py` leaves the two disagreeing.

    THE FRONT SPRITES ARE THE LESS COMPLETE HALF. RLG-053's lamp declaration is finished for
    every REAR sprite in the game - 84 lamps across 26 sprites - and the FRONT sprites and the
    rear-view are explicitly NOT converted. Showing the front on the card makes the unconverted
    half the one the player looks at every time they open the garage.

    `interceptor-test.py` and `class-test.py` both read the card - its height, its note and its
    label - so the framing change has to be made with them, not before them.

**THE FLIP BUTTON IS ALSO THE ANSWER TO A COST.** Showing one end hides the other, and the rear is
where every lamp, every stripe and the number are. The flip restores that without giving the space
back, which is why it belongs in this ruling rather than in one of its own - but the owner put it
after the refactor and it should stay there.
- **RULED 2026-09-12T09:35:41-04:00** — **BUILT, v0.13.86 - ALL FIVE PARTS, INCLUDING THE FLIP.** The button was deferred in the ruling
above and the owner brought it forward on 2026-09-12: *"Can we put a little button in the corner of
the view pane that toggles between front and rear view - I just want front to be the default and the
default is restored every time the garage opens."*

## WHAT SHIPPED

    ONE END         `garageEnd` decides, and it starts at 'front'. A car with no front
                    sprite still shows its back rather than an empty card.
    LARGER          `GARAGE_WIDE` is 300 where `GARAGE_HALF` was 150, so a car is drawn
                    at roughly twice the width. Measured: a MUSCLE takes 224 pixels
                    against a 126 ceiling under the two-end layout.
    CENTRED         one `put` at x=150 rather than two at 75 and 225.
    ON THE FLOOR    unchanged - `FLOOR = TOP + drawnH` already did this and RLG-182 is
                    what made it true. Now asserted rather than assumed.
    ONE BOX         unchanged - `garageCardHeight` already reserves two tiers keyed on
                    `isBigBody`, which is the ruling's "grown only for the odd sizes".
    THE FLIP        a chip in the top right of the view pane.

**THE FIT IS STILL TAKEN ACROSS BOTH ENDS, WHICH IS THE ONE THING THAT LOOKS WRONG AND IS NOT.** If
the scale followed whichever end is on screen, flipping the card would RESIZE the car - and a car
that changes size when you look at its other end is precisely what [[RLG-182]] spent a ruling
fixing. Both ends are measured, one is drawn.

**THE BUTTON SAYS WHERE IT WILL TAKE YOU, NOT WHERE YOU ARE** - it reads REAR while the front is
showing. A toggle labelled with its current state is the oldest ambiguity in an interface and there
is no room in a card corner for both. **It is on the LOCKED card too**, because a silhouette has two
ends and the shape is the whole invitation.

**AND THE TOUCH TARGET IS BIGGER THAN THE BUTTON LOOKS.** "A little button" is smaller than the 44
pixels a thumb needs, so the visible chip is small and an `::after` lays a 44-pixel square over its
centre. Padding the chip itself to 44 was built first and photographed: it reads as a panel rather
than as a corner control.

## THE RESET IS ON ENTRY, NOT ON EVERY REDRAW, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE RULE

`showGarage` is what EVERY control in that screen calls to redraw itself - the paint swatches, the
gearbox, the mode, the clock. A reset written at the top of it flips the card back to the front the
moment you touch a paint swatch while looking at the rear. It sits inside the `state !== 'garage'`
branch instead, which is already the only question in that function that means "the garage opened",
because it is what tears down a finished run. **It is also not persisted**: a flip is a look at the
car in front of you rather than a preference, so it stays out of the save.

## THE PROOF, AND ITS FIRST VERSION PASSED WITH THE DEFECT PRESENT

`tools/garage-card-test.py` reads the painted pixels rather than the code. The one-end check first
asked whether the middle four pixels of the card were painted, on the reasoning that two cars side
by side leave a gap down the centre. **It passed with both ends drawn.** Widening the fit to the
whole card had made each half-drawn car wide enough to overlap the middle, so the check no longer
separated the two layouts - and it was only found by putting the old layout back and watching.

It measures against the engine's own fit now: `carEnds` gives each end's ink box and drawn height,
so the width ONE car should occupy is arithmetic, and two cars side by side span that plus the 150
between their centres. **Watched failing** with the two-end layout restored - 299 painted against
the 224 one end should take - and again with the reset moved to the top of `showGarage`, where
"changing the paint leaves the rear showing" failed with the button reading REAR.

## WHAT A HARNESS CANNOT JUDGE

Whether the car now reads as too large in the card, whether the chip is legible at arm's length on a
phone, and whether the front is the right default for a player who wants to see the paint they just
chose - the rear is where every stripe and lamp live. **And the front sprites are the less complete
half**: RLG-053's lamp declaration is finished for every REAR sprite in the game and explicitly not
for the fronts, so the half the player now looks at first is the one that has had less work.
