- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-10T23:47:57-04:00** - INTERCEPT is playable. A rival can be stopped, the field can be cleared, the race finishing loses the shift, and the player never works alone.

THE STOP IS A MANOEUVRE, NOT DAMAGE, which is the owner's own distinction. A rival under a fifth of top speed, held there for two and a half seconds, with the PLAYER ahead of it and in its line, is out: it pulls to the verge and comes to rest. The condition asks about the player and no other car, because the owner put the emphasis on YOU. Four tunables with committed defaults, and the ruling left their values open - they are a first answer and a judgement on a device.

AND THE MODE'S CORE VERB WAS MISSING FROM THE ENGINE. `ahead` caps a rival's speed to whatever is directly in front of it and scanned traffic, the rivals and the police - not the player. `laneView` twenty lines below carries a note about exactly that blindness, and the fix had reached the LANE CHOICE and not the SPEED. It is gated on the shift and DELIBERATELY not fixed generally: ungating it changes how every rival behaves in every race in both driving games, no ruling asks for it, and no harness can judge it. Tracked as RLG-209 for the owner.

THE WINGMEN ARE A FLOOR AND A CAP WILL NOT DO IT. Raising `dispatchCap` put no cars on the road at all: the radio reinforces a pursuit in progress and on shift nothing is ever on the player, by design. The cap said two and the road sent none. The floor is its own rule now, fills to strength, and is checked every three to six seconds.

Both endings go through the finish path rather than the crash path, because `wreck()` returns early while the run clock has time left. The loss is a RIVAL reaching the line; the player reaching it does nothing.

THE HARNESS STAGES THE CONDITION AND MEASURES THE RULE, and says so. The manoeuvre cannot be flown by an autopilot, so nothing here shows a player CAN bring a rival to that speed - only the device answers that. Every positive has a negative beside it and the hold is sampled half way through.
