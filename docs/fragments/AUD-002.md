### AUD-002 — Sweep: what the mirror is offered, and how long each thing lives behind the player
- **RULED 2026-09-15T23:30:51-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15, after the roadblock fix: "Before we move onto ruling 259 can you do a sweep for all things showing in the mirror and make sure they behave correctly."

TWO QUESTIONS PER KIND: is the mirror OFFERED it, and does it LIVE long enough behind the player to be drawn? The glass draws to `MIRROR_BACK`, 34,000 units, so anything culled sooner is offered a thing that is no longer there. That is the fault RLG-260 fixed for roadblocks and RLG-133 and RLG-108 fixed for boards and crates.

FOUR THINGS CAME OUT OF IT, all in 0.14.41.
1. THE FINISH LINE HAD NO `kind` ON ITS MIRROR ITEM, so `viewKinds.glass` could never record it and the sweep could not tell a finish line missing from the glass from one the drive never reached. It is drawn from its own flag, so nothing about the picture changes.
2. THE CULLS NOW READ `MIRROR_BACK`. Traffic and the two police sites had 34000 written out, which is the same number today and is exactly how the roadblock's 2,000 and the board's 8,000 drifted from it before.
3. THE SWEEP IS DETERMINISTIC. mirror-test parked nothing: it drove and hoped. A board is two miles out, a crate has to fall behind while it watches, and police behind the camera are chance - so it reported "missing from the glass" for things that were never behind the car. It now parks a board, a crate and a cruiser behind, as it already forced a roadblock, and adds a finish-line arm through `API.parkFinish`. Six kinds are handed to both views on two consecutive runs.
4. `signs` IS DEAD and is left alone for now. Nothing in the file pushes to it; it is declared, filtered every frame at 4,000 and reset on a new run. Both views carry a branch for it. The 4,000 cull is inside `MIRROR_BACK` and would be the same fault as the roadblock's if anything ever filled the array, so whoever revives road signs must move that line. Tracked as RLG-264.

WHAT THIS SWEEP CANNOT SAY. Two kinds were reasoned about rather than measured, because staging them costs a race and a bridge crossing: rivals 'g' and bridge towers 'w'. Both are pushed to the mirror with the same bound as the rest, rivals are deliberately never culled (RLG-029) and towers are generated from the crossing's own position rather than kept in an array, so neither can be culled early - but no harness drove them. It also says nothing about whether any of them LOOK right in the glass, which is the owner's verdict on the device.
