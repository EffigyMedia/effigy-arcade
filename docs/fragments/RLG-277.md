### RLG-277 — A car you have hit is still solid, and contact is one event rather than many
- **RULED 2026-09-16T10:08:40-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "When you collide with a car you are then allowed to just drive through them. We can't allow that to happen but I also don't want it to machine gun fire collisions."

TWO REQUIREMENTS AND THEY PULL AGAINST EACH OTHER, which is why this is a ruling rather than a bug fix. A car must never become a thing you can pass through - it is solid, and a solid car that is briefly not solid is worse than one that was never solid, because the player learns to exploit it. AND a car you are resting against must not fire a collision every frame, which is the machine-gun the owner is naming: the hit sound, the damage tick and the shunt all repeating at frame rate.

THE MECHANISM TO LOOK AT FIRST is whatever grace period the engine gives after a hit. If a collision sets an invulnerability window and that window turns the car INTANGIBLE rather than merely SILENT, then both halves of the report are the same defect seen from two ends: the window is what stops the machine gun, and the window is what lets you drive through.

THE SHAPE OF THE FIX, if that is what it is: the grace must suppress the RESPONSE and never the SOLIDITY. A car in contact still pushes - the two bodies stay apart - while the damage, the sound and the shunt fire once and then hold off. Contact that is sustained is one event, not many; contact that is broken and remade is a new event.

NOT MEASURED YET AND NOT STARTED. See [[RLG-058]] for the per-car colliders and [[RLG-171]] for the traffic-damage guard, both of which touch this code.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T10:24:32-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.56. Solidity and response are separated, and the two halves of the owner's report were one line.

THE DEFECT, STATED EXACTLY. Every collision test in the engine read `if(iframe <= 0 && ...overlapping...)` - traffic at one site, rivals at another, cruisers at a third. `iframe` is the mercy window that stops one hit repeating every frame, and because it gated the OVERLAP TEST ITSELF, the other car was not merely silent for those 0.9 seconds, it was not there. The window that prevents the machine gun IS the window that lets you drive through, seen from the other end. That is why the owner could report both things about one mechanism.

THE FIX IS TWO FUNCTIONS WHERE THERE WAS ONE TEST. `contactHold(o, dt)` runs whenever two bodies overlap, mercy window or not, and does nothing but keep them apart - no damage, no sound, no sparks. `contactFires(o)` decides whether the CRASH happens, and it fires once when contact begins and then holds off for as long as that contact lasts.

ALONG THE ROAD THE LEVER IS SPEED, NOT POSITION. `pos` is the world's own clock and dragging it back to resolve an overlap would rubber-band the entire road, so a car in front caps your speed at its own. That was already half-written at the bottom of `impactWith`, for rear-end hits only, with a comment saying exactly what it was for: "you cannot drive through the car you just hit from behind".

ACROSS THE ROAD IT RESOLVES AT A RATE RATHER THAN INSTANTLY. `CONTACT.rate` is 1.30 road half-widths a second, split by mass so the lighter body gives way more. A full resolve on one frame throws the player a lane sideways, which reads as a hit rather than as a body.

CONTACT BROKEN AND REMADE IS A NEW EVENT, and the latch lives on the OBJECT rather than on a timer. Two cars touching you at once are two events; one car leaned on for eight seconds is one. A timer is what would bring the machine gun back. The latch clears with HYSTERESIS - `CONTACT.clear` is 0.22 of the overlap width - because a body sitting exactly on the boundary jitters in and out of it at frame rate.

A HIT THE WINDOW SILENCES IS DELAYED, NOT LOST. Without care the latch would be set while the hush was on, and a second car touched half a second after the first would then cost nothing at all, ever. `contactFires` clears the latch instead, so the crash fires on the frame the hush ends if the two bodies are still touching. Once. `iframe` keeps its real job - the count-in, where nothing may hit a car nobody is driving yet, and the seconds after a roadblock - but it is no longer a per-car tangibility switch.

MEASURED IN TWO RUNS, BECAUSE ONE CONTACT CANNOT SHOW BOTH THINGS. A car struck head-on is shoved aside within a few frames, which is correct - so "did the player end up past it" is NOT the test for driving through. `tools/contact-test.py` runs (a) a stationary car dead ahead driven into at speed, and (b) a car alongside with the wheel held into it for two seconds, the car's speed matched to the player's so there is something to lean on.

  run one   132 frames nose-to-tail, closest approach 0.24 of the overlap width, speed 6893 to 0, one hit
  run two   32 frames of contact, THREE crash responses

NOT VACUOUS, WATCHED FAILING WITH EACH HALF PUT BACK SEPARATELY. With the old `iframe` gate restored the player's centre sat at 0.00 of the overlap width for 12 frames - the same point as the car, which is the owner's report reproduced as a number - and run one reported 24 hits instead of 1. With solidity kept but the latch and window removed, run two reported 14 responses instead of 3. Note that the speed check PASSED with the pass-through defect present, at 2569 against 6893: it is the same-point check that catches it, and that is why both are there.

A COUNT OF CALLS WOULD HAVE PROVED NOTHING. A crash response is counted as a STEP IN DAMAGE, which is what the player feels, rather than as a call to a counter written for this check.

AND THE AUTOPILOT STOPPED HURTING ITSELF. drive-test's Interstate worst damage fell from 23.99 per cent to 3.83 on the same driver: it was grinding through cars it could not be stopped by. That is a side effect of the fix rather than a target of it, and it is the clearest sign the old behaviour was reachable in ordinary driving.

ONE DIAGNOSTIC MOVED AND IT IS MINE. `collide-test` derives the player's collision half-width from where the two cars come to rest after a staged collision, and it now reads 0.2774 against a drawn 0.2300 where it read 0.2298 before. The separation resolves at a rate now, so the resting position differs. THAT NUMBER IS ALREADY PRINTED RATHER THAN ASSERTED, by its own note, because its arithmetic multiplies any shift in one edge by about seven and it is bimodal between runs. The check that IS asserted - the edge against the half-width sum the engine declares - still passes. It is worth a look if the resting gap ever looks wide on a device.

drive-test 36/37 with the known environmental settle line, smoke-test 7/7, contact-test green. collide-test's five failures and impact-test's wedge are PRE-EXISTING, confirmed by running both at HEAD with this work stashed.

STILL THE OWNER'S CALL, ON A DEVICE: whether the shove feels right, whether being held against a car reads as contact or as a wall, and whether ONE hit is the right cost for a long grind - a sustained scrape currently costs one hit and no continuing penalty, which is the least this could do and may be too little.
