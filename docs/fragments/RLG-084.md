### RLG-084 — A radar detector that warns of a speed trap ahead
- **RULED 2026-08-30T17:43:04-04:00** — REQUESTED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-08-30: "Make a record for adding a radar detector unit/police
speed trap detector as another upgrade."

WHAT IT WOULD BE, and the engine already has everything it needs to know. A speed trap is a cruiser
parked on the verge with its engine off, holding `trap` and `armed`, and it fires when anything passes
it above 80mph within 7,000 units. A detector is therefore a READ of a thing that already exists: the
distance to the nearest armed trap ahead. Nothing has to be spawned, tagged or remembered for it.

THE INTERESTING DESIGN QUESTION IS NOT THE WARNING, IT IS THE RANGE. Too short and it is a jump
scare; too long and it removes the tension the trap exists to create. The trap's own window is 7,000
units, and the road draws to 30,000 - so a detector that reaches the whole drawn road makes traps
free, and one that reaches 8,000 gives about a second and a half at 170mph. Somewhere between is a
choice about how hard the game is, which is the owner's.

AND IT IS AN UPGRADE, WHICH IS THE PART THAT DOES NOT EXIST YET. [[RLG-081]] records the same
blocker for forced induction: there is no system for upgrading a car. So this is two pieces of work -
the detector, which is small, and the thing that lets a player own one, which is not. Until then it
would have to be a permanent fitting or a garage toggle, and neither is what the owner asked for.

WHAT IT SHOULD SOUND AND LOOK LIKE IS OPEN. The obvious shapes are a rising tick like a real
detector, an icon that lights, or a bar that fills as you close on the trap - the last of which reads
without sound, which matters on a phone that may be muted. This project has no HUD element that
counts DOWN to anything yet, so whatever is chosen is a new idiom rather than a variation.

ONE THING TO AVOID, from the pursuit work of the same day: do not let the detector tell you about
traps you have already tripped, or about the parked cruisers that are no longer armed. `armed` is
already on the record and the check is one condition - but a detector that beeps at a spent trap
teaches the player to ignore it, which is the same failure as a check that cries wolf.
