### RLG-241 — Road rage: a driver the player angers tries to run them down
- **RULED 2026-09-13T14:12:57-04:00** — **REQUESTED 2026-09-13, NOT STARTED.** Owner: *"We should have a road rage state built around the evidence/personality system where if you piss off another driver they may try to run you down and hit you."*

## WHAT IT BUILDS ON

- **The four personalities** ([[RLG-206]]): COMMUTER 0, SPEEDER 1, OUTLAW 2, RACER 3, an ordinal scale of lawlessness that two police tests read as one. Each carries an obedience value.
- **The standing rule that the car is capable and the driver decides** ([[RLG-042]], [[RLG-052]], [[RLG-054]]). Road rage is a driver's decision, so it must be a state of the driver and never a branch that names a vehicle class.
- **The police already choose a target by an event** (v0.13.41): a cruiser switches to the player only when the player passes it closely and faster. An angered driver choosing to chase is the same shape.

## OPEN, AND OWNER-ONLY

1. What angers a driver: a near miss, a collision, the horn, being cut off, being boxed, tailgating, or a mix.
2. Whether the chance of rage follows the personality scale, and whether a COMMUTER can rage at all.
3. What "run you down" means in play: ram from behind, side-swipe, block, or chase; and how damage is dealt to both cars.
4. How it ends: distance, time, a crash, the police, or the driver cooling off.
5. Whether a raging driver draws the police, and whether the player's defence against one counts toward heat.
6. Whether it happens in races, or only on the open road.

"The evidence system" in the owner's words is taken to be the personality and obedience state above; confirm that before building.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T22:49:46-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.70. Owner answered the four gating questions on 2026-09-16.

WHO RAGES follows the personality scale and a COMMUTER never does. `RAGE_ODDS` is indexed by `mind`, the same shape as `OBEY` and the indicator habit beside it. Measured through the ENGINE'S OWN ROLL rather than read back off the table - 400 provoked drivers of each mind gave 0, 74, 225 and 152 against a table of 0, 0.18, 0.55, 0.40.

WHAT ANGERS THEM: being hit, and the player's horn. The owner took those two and left the near miss and the cut-off alone, and the reason matters - both happen constantly in ordinary driving, and a rage that fires when you did not mean anything by it teaches nothing. The collision hangs on [[RLG-277]]'s contact EVENT, so one rub is one provocation. The horn is rolled on the frame a driver REFUSES to move, which is the moment the horn has actually annoyed somebody; a driver who obliges has nothing to be angry about. IT IS GATED ON `horning`, the player's own horn, because `scatter` is the single path for every siren too - without it a passing ambulance would enrage the traffic it was asking to move over.

WHAT THEY DO IS THE OWNER'S OWN ANSWER and it was more than any option offered: "what they try to do would depend on their position from you and maybe they should try to get in front of you and brake check you first but if they're not faster than you, they quickly give up on that and do one of the others based position instead." So there are four moves - CUT to get past, BLOCK to brake-check from in front, SWIPE alongside, RAM from behind - re-picked from position every `RAGE_LOOK`, and CUT is abandoned within `RAGE_GIVEUP` by a driver whose car cannot actually out-run the player.

HOW IT ENDS: a fuse re-armed while the car is still on you, and a distance at which it gives up regardless - the police's own shape, so a player who has learnt one has learnt the other.

IT IS A STATE OF THE DRIVER AND NEVER A CLASS, which is the standing rule three separate rulings state. Nothing here names a vehicle.

AND AN ANGRY DRIVER STILL DRIVES THE CAR IT HAS. ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR is not suspended by temper: `TYPE_VMAX` caps every move. The first build left that out and every rager passed the player whatever it was driving, so a van told to ram simply overtook and brake-checked instead.

TWO BUILD FAULTS WORTH KEEPING. (1) `rageStep` advanced `c.z` itself, and every car's `z` is already integrated in the COLLISION loop - the note beside a wrecked car there says so in as many words, "it advances HERE like everything else so that it cannot be moved twice in one frame". A rager closed at double its speed, every staged position came out as BLOCK, and nothing could be outrun. (2) The check asserted what a rager "settles on" after five seconds, which is the wrong thing to assert: an encounter MOVES. A truck alongside a faster player cannot stay alongside and correctly becomes a RAM; a sedan that brake-checks pulls the player level and correctly becomes a SWIPE. Both were the code being right and the check being written against a snapshot. What is ruled is that the move follows the POSITION, so that is asserted now - every sampled frame against the offset on that frame.

AND THE HARNESS'S OWN "SLOWER" CAR WAS NOT SLOWER. It set a speed the rager overrides by design, so a 'slow' sedan passed exactly as a fast one did. The vehicle is what makes it slow: a TRUCK tops out at 0.34 of MAX against a player at 0.45.

NOT VACUOUS, watched failing on both claims: a COMMUTER given odds raged 198 of 400, and a move that ignored position was caught as RAM at a `dz` of +1,237.

TWO THINGS DEFAULTED RATHER THAN RULED, and the owner was not asked: a rager draws no police and defending yourself adds no heat, because coupling a stranger's temper to the heat economy would make it the player's criminal record. And it is open-road only, through `roadFurniture`.

rage-test, traffic-test, obedience-test and smoke-test pass.

WHAT NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE: whether being hunted is frightening or merely annoying, whether a brake-check reads as malice or as traffic being bad, and whether the odds put one on you too often. `RAGE_ODDS`, `RAGE_SECS`, `RAGE_LOST` and `RAGE_PUSH` are each one number.
