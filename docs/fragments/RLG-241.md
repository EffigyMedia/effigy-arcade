### RLG-241 — Road rage: a driver the player angers tries to run them down
- **RULED 2026-09-13T14:12:57-04:00** — **REQUESTED 2026-09-13, NOT STARTED.** Owner: *"We should have a road rage state built around the evidence/personality system where if you piss off another driver they may try to run you down and hit you."*

## WHAT IT BUILDS ON

- **The four personalities** ([[RLG-206]]): COMMUTER 0, SPEEDER 1, OUTLAW 2, RACER 3, an ordinal scale of lawlessness that two police tests read as one. Each carries an obedience value.
- **The standing rule that the car is capable and the driver decides** ([[RLG-042]], [[RLG-052]], [[RLG-054]]). Road rage is a driver's decision, so it must be a state of the driver and never a branch that names a vehicle class.
- **The police already choose a target by an event** (v0.13.41): a cruiser switches to the player only when the player passes it closely and faster. An angered driver choosing to chase is the same shape.

## OPEN, AND OWNER-ONLY

1. What angers a driver: a near miss, a collision, the horn, being cut off, being boxed, tailgating, or a mix.
2. Whether the chance of rage follows the personality scale, and whether a COMMUTER can rage at all.
3. What "run you down" means in play: ram from behind, side-swipe, block, or chase; and how damage is dealt to both cars.
4. How it ends: distance, time, a crash, the police, or the driver cooling off.
5. Whether a raging driver draws the police, and whether the player's defence against one counts toward heat.
6. Whether it happens in races, or only on the open road.

"The evidence system" in the owner's words is taken to be the personality and obedience state above; confirm that before building.
