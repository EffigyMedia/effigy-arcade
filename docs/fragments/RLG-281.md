### RLG-281 — The road to the horizon is the wrong colour for its place
- **RULED 2026-09-16T12:54:47-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "the simulated roadway that goes off into the horizon, needs to match the color of the road per biome."

WHAT THIS IS. [[RLG-101]] carries the tarmac past where the road pass stops painting, on its own coarse walk to the vanishing point - `FAR_ROAD_STEPS` is 30 - so the road reaches the horizon instead of ending in a band under the skyline. That far ribbon is painted with its own colour, and the near road's colour is built by `tarmacTone`, which takes the segment's light/dark parity, the distance fade and whether it is a deck. The two do not agree, and a place that tints its tarmac shows the join.

THE FAR SEA ALREADY SOLVED THE SAME PROBLEM and its record is the place to start. `SEA_HAZE` was MEASURED rather than chosen - six values read from the same band at the same hour, because the road is generated per load and the day is always turning, so two runs cannot be compared. The winning value sat at a clean minimum of the step at the join with the curve rising on both sides of it. A colour match at a join is exactly that kind of measurement.

AND farroad-test ALREADY PRINTS THE NUMBER, without asserting on it: "the tone step reads 33.5 here and has swung 34, 84 and 39 across runs of one build - the join is the owner's to judge." So the instrument exists, the fault is already visible to it, and the reason it was left unasserted is that nobody had ruled on what the step should be. The owner has now.

NOT STARTED.
