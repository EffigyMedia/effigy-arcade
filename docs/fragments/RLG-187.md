### RLG-187 — The CREST's face glass follows the dome, as its rear screen does
- **RULED 2026-09-09T16:21:19-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"the rear window on the crest follows the curve of the cabin top whereas in
the front there is a giant void between the glass and the roof edge. We should fill that void with
glass."*

**THE FACE WAS BUILDING ITS SCREEN AGAINST A ROOFLINE THIS CAR HAS NOT GOT.** CREST is the one body
with `dome:true` - a single arc from one shoulder to the other rather than a shouldered greenhouse -
and its tail has always drawn a wide shallow pane set into that arc. The face built a pane against
`roofT` and `rr2`, which describe the shouldered roof every OTHER supercar has. While the two ends
each drew their own greenhouse the mismatch was hidden; [[RLG-184]] made both trace one, and the
pane was then plainly inside an arc it knew nothing about.

**THE FIX IS THE TAIL'S OWN PANE, LIFTED OUT WHOLE.** `carDomeGlassPath` is the declaration and both
painters trace it, so the screen springs from the line the dome springs from and reaches the roof
rail at both ends. The face gets the tail's reflection across the top of it as well. A car with a
shouldered roof is untouched and keeps the pane it had.

**AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME THIS SHAPE OF FAULT HAS APPEARED IN TWO DAYS** - a part described twice,
where the second description was written against an assumption that had stopped being true. The
answer each time has been the same: delete one of the two and let both ends trace what is left.
