### RLG-135 — The mirror rain checks are unguarded - their vacuity controls are red
- **RULED 2026-08-31T21:30:18-04:00** — FOUND WHILE BUILDING [[RLG-133]] AND NOT TOUCHED THERE. Three checks in tools/mirror-rain-test.py fail:

    the frozen dry scene holds still between samples
    with no particles the check goes red, so it measures them and nothing else
    and the snow check goes red with the particles off as well

THE BASELINE WAS RUN RATHER THAN THE FAILURE ASSUMED TO BE OLD. The commit before RLG-133 fails the same three, with the same wording, so this is not a regression from that unit.

WHAT MAKES IT WORSE THAN AN ORDINARY RED CHECK. Two of the three ARE the vacuity guards - they exist to turn the particles off and prove the rain checks go red without them. While they fail, the rain and snow checks above them are passing with nothing standing behind them, and this project has already been bitten four times by a guard that passed with the defect present. The mirror's precipitation, shipped in 0.10.17 and never judged on a device, is currently unproven rather than proven.

THE FIRST QUESTION IS WHETHER THE CONTROL OR THE SUBJECT IS BROKEN. 'The frozen dry scene holds still between samples' is a claim about the harness's own ability to freeze the world, and it is listed first for a reason: if the scene will not hold still, the other two cannot mean anything and there is nothing wrong with the rain at all. Read that one before either of the others.

AND THE THREAD ALREADY CARRIES A WARNING ABOUT A NEIGHBOURING HARNESS. weather-test is recorded as flaky on both arms and unusable for telling a regression from noise. Whether these two facts share a cause has not been looked at.

NOT STARTED.
