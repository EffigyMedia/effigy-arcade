### RLG-273 — Re-evaluate the collider: cars can still be driven through
- **RULED 2026-09-16T02:21:37-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED, AND THE OWNER PUT IT AT THE END OF THE QUEUE. Owner, 2026-09-16: "Add another task to the end of the queue that we need to reevaluate and fix the collider because I still feel like I can slip through vehicles like touching them."

"STILL" IS THE WORD THAT MATTERS. [[RLG-058]] made the colliders true to the sprite and this report comes after it, so the earlier work did not close the complaint. Read what RLG-058 actually changed before assuming this is the same defect.

WHAT IT MIGHT BE, AND THEY ARE DIFFERENT REPAIRS. The collider may be too narrow, so the cars overlap in the picture without touching in the model. It may be the LENGTH: a billboard has no depth, so a car's z extent is a number somebody chose rather than a thing the sprite can be measured against, and a fast player can pass through a car between two frames. Or the hit may be registering and reading as nothing, in which case it is the impact response rather than the collider.

THE PLAYER IS THE ONE TO LOOK AT FIRST. RLG-058's note records that traffic and rivals were already true to the sprite while the PLAYER was drawn at 0.265 and collided at 0.26 with one number for all six cars.

MEASURE, DO NOT TUNE. `API.colliderProbe` and `API.probeImpact` exist. A harness can drive a car at a parked one at a known speed and a known lateral offset and say whether a hit was registered, at what offset it stops being registered, and how that compares with where the two sprites overlap on the screen. The screen overlap is the ground truth the owner is reporting against, because what they see is the pictures touching.
