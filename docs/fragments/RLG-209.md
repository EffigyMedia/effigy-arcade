### RLG-209 — Should a rival lift for the player in front of it in an ordinary race
- **RULED 2026-09-10T23:47:31-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED. IT IS A QUESTION FOR THE OWNER AND IT WAS FOUND RATHER THAN ASKED FOR.**

**A RIVAL CANNOT SEE THE PLAYER AS A CAR IN FRONT OF IT.** `ahead` inside `stepRacers` caps a rival's
target speed to whatever is directly ahead of it in its lane. It scans the traffic, the other rivals
and the police. It does not scan the player. So a rival closing on the player's car does not lift for
it: it swerves around, or it arrives.

**THE SAME OMISSION IS ALREADY WRITTEN DOWN TWENTY LINES BELOW, AND WAS FIXED THERE.** `laneView`
carries this note: *"THE PLAYER IS A CAR. Rivals scanned traffic, each other and the police and were
blind to the one car the player is sitting in - the same omission that let them drive straight
through you before they were made solid. A rival that merges into the player's lane because it cannot
see the player is that bug again, wearing a lane change."* That reasoning applies word for word to
`ahead`. It reached the LANE CHOICE and it did not reach the SPEED.

**WHY IT SURFACED NOW.** [[RLG-203]]'s INTERCEPT is built on the player getting in front of a rival
and slowing it down, and that is impossible while the rival will not lift. It is fixed there and
**gated on the shift**: `playerIsPolice()` is the condition, so nothing about an ordinary race
changed in that unit.

**WHAT THE OWNER HAS TO DECIDE, AND IT IS A FEEL QUESTION.** Ungating it changes how every rival
behaves in every race in BOTH driving games. A rival that catches you in your lane would ease off
behind you where today it presses and eventually hits you. That could read as better AI - cars that
queue rather than ram - or it could read as the field going soft, because being pressed from behind
is part of what makes a lead feel thin.

**THREE THINGS THAT MAKE IT A JUDGEMENT RATHER THAN A DEFECT.**

    IT WOULD MAKE RACES EASIER, and by an unmeasured amount. A rival that lifts is a rival that
    does not force you to defend.

    RLG-019 MADE RIVALS COLLIDE WITH THE PLAYER ON PURPOSE, so being hit from behind is a
    designed consequence and not an accident. Lifting would remove most of the occasions for it.

    NO HARNESS CAN JUDGE IT. This is rendering-and-feel territory - the owner's verdict on a
    device is the only instrument, and it should be judged against a build where the two arms
    can be compared.

**IF IT IS TAKEN, IT IS FOUR LINES AND THE SEAM IS ALREADY OPEN** - the clause exists in `ahead`
today with `playerIsPolice()` in front of it, so the change is removing a condition rather than
writing a behaviour.
