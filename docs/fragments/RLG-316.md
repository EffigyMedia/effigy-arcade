### RLG-316 — The valley range clips through the near mountain and draws over the road on a twisting, climbing road
- **RULED 2026-09-21T19:22:29-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21, from the device: 'Wow the mountain scenery looks terrible in the valley because as the road twists and turns they do unnatural things like clipping through the near side mountain. And the mountain bugs out and renders through the road depending on the road angle and height.'

'The mountain scenery in the valley' is the RANGE drawn across the drop (RLG-297), the same object [[RLG-309]] was about. Two faults are described and they are one mechanism.

WHY IT CLIPS THROUGH THE NEAR MOUNTAIN - FIRST READING, FROM THE CODE, NOT YET MEASURED. `drawValleyRange` is painted ONCE, on the first slice at or nearer than `RANGE_AT` of the draw, straight after that slice's floor. Everything NEARER is painted afterwards and lands in front of it, which is the design. But everything FARTHER was painted BEFORE it - including the far part of the wall on the other side of the road. On a straight road that wall is on its own side of the vanishing point and the range's clip keeps them apart. On a bend the far wall swings across the vanishing point into the range's half of the frame, and the range, painted later, covers it. That is a mountain clipping through the one in front of it.

WHY IT RENDERS THROUGH THE ROAD - SAME CAUSE. The range is clipped to a RECTANGLE, one side of `vx` between its crest and its feet. The road's far slices beyond `RANGE_AT` are painted before it too, so on a crest or a turn the rectangle lands over far road, and the range is drawn across it. A rectangle is not the valley.

THE FIX THE MECHANISM POINTS AT: the range may only appear where the VALLEY FLOOR is visible - clipped to the floor the drop pass painted, not to a rectangle. The floor is already built per slice as a polygon out to the edge of the screen; the union of the floor polygons farther than the range is exactly 'the valley you can see across'.

AND I MUST CHECK WHETHER v0.14.115 MADE THIS WORSE. RLG-309 changed the range's corner term from the horizon's SMOOTHED swing, borrowed, to the road's RAW turn at the range's own distance. That is geometrically truer and it tracks a twisting road more closely, which may make the range swing across the near mountain more often. The occlusion fault exists either way; the change may have raised how often it is seen.

Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-22T10:27:11-04:00** — BUILT in 0.14.124 (UNT-417), with RLG-309's parallax in the same change, because the reading above was right that they are one mechanism - and the mechanism was the model, not only the clip. The range was one tiled picture moved by three screen-space offsets (lane, bend at one distance, drift with travel) and painted once, at one depth, in a rectangle. No point of it moved like a point in the world, and nothing nearer could stand in front of it except by the order it happened to be painted in.

IT IS A ROW OF PEAKS NOW. Every RANGE_STEP (10) segments of a place that declares range, a peak stands RANGE_OUT (16) road widths out on the drop side, RANGE_H (20) camera heights tall, on the valley floor, each drawing half the place's skyline art from a hashed offset. rangePeak() is one painter for both views. The road walk draws each peak at its own slice, straight after that slice's floor, including slices hidden behind a crest - so the projection moves it and nearer ground, the near mountain and the road paint over it. drawValleyRange, its three offsets, RANGE_SCALE, RANGE_AT and RANGE_SWING are gone.

THE GLASS: inside its 34,000-unit reach a peak 16 road widths out is mostly outside the pane, so the glass also draws the peaks from MIRROR_BACK out to the windscreen's own reach before its walk, easing out over the last quarter. Peaks inside the reach are drawn solid so there is no hole at the hand-over.

TUNING, MEASURED ON SCREEN AND NOT FINAL. With peaks in the world the standoff decides where the range can appear at all. At 30 no peak was on screen inside the draw - the first screenshots judged right at 30 were the SKYLINE, which uses the same art, and a frame with the range switched off told them apart. At 12 and 40 camera heights the drop side was a wall of snow at the rail. 16 and 20 stand a row of peaks in the valley beyond the rail, receding along it. It is the owner's call on the device, and API.rangeModel sets step, h, out, sink and alpha live.

PROVED by tools/range-peak-test.py: driven straight and bent both ways at speed, every peak drawn in either view stands exactly RANGE_OUT road widths from the road's centre at its own distance (worst error 0.0000), from 22 segments in the windscreen and 34 in the glass. The falsify arm adds a screen-space drift and fails exactly the two placement checks. wall-plane-test now follows one peak: parked it holds to 0.00 px, driving it slides 5.2 px. tools/range-drift-proof.py is retired: it proved the rate of the offset model this removes. NOT MEASURED: pixel occlusion on a particular crest; it follows from the draw order.
