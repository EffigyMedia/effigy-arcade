### RLG-254 — A speed trap never engages; it calls cruisers in behind, and traps thin out so heat can cool
- **RULED 2026-09-15T02:15:05-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-15, on speed traps: "speed traps shouldn't engage you anyways all they should do is call for spawning new cruisers behind you so in that sense speed traps are just set dressing. We might also want them to be less frequent so we have the opportunity to cool down the heat. It might need to be dynamic."

WHAT TO BUILD:
1. A trap never becomes a chasing cruiser for the player. Passing one over the limit raises heat and calls cruisers in from behind (the radio spawner, `spawnCop`), and the trap stays parked. This reverses the part of RLG-247 (0.14.26) that turned a passed trap into a chaser.
2. Traps are laid less often, and the rate depends on the pursuit, so that heat can cool. Today `nextCopT = max(2, rnd(5,10) - heat*0.6)` with 4 to 8 traps held on the road (TRAPS_BASE, TRAPS_MAX): measured at 11 laid in one minute. The dynamic rule is not set by the owner: propose one that thins traps while the player is cooling, keep it tunable, and record it as reversible.

DECIDE WITH THE OWNER BEFORE BUILDING: whether the NPC branch (a trap that pulls over a speeding traffic car, RLG-046) stays, and what now makes the Interceptor eligible, since `supersEarned` is set by passing a trap at 170mph and that pass still happens.
