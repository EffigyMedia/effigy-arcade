### RLG-280 — The mirror does not carry the road's boundaries
- **RULED 2026-09-16T12:54:47-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "all the new boundaries items you just added, are invisible in the mirror."

IT IS AN OMISSION IN TODAY'S WORK AND IT IS MINE. [[RLG-265]] drew the guard rail, the city's concrete barrier and the cliff drop in `drawRoad`, which paints the WINDSCREEN. The mirror is a second road pass with its own walk - coarser steps, its own height, its own scale - and it has to be told about anything new beside the road. The bridge's ironwork already is: `drawTruss` is called twice, once at the windscreen's pass and once at the mirror's, with `H_M` instead of `H`. Nothing added today got its second call.

SO THE MIRROR IS NOT A COPY OF THE SCREEN, and that is the thing to remember rather than the specific fix. Every roadside object this engine draws has to be drawn in two places or it does not exist behind you - and the mirror walks in 900-unit steps where the road ahead walks in 200s, which is why `postIn` asks the WORLD rather than a segment index. A rail spaced by index would tick at a different rate in the glass.

WHAT HAS TO BE ADDED: `drawRail` on the hazard side and both sides of a city, and the drop's ground cut and lit lip. `railX()` and `hazardSide()` are already global and take no arguments, so neither needs new plumbing.

AND A CHECK HAS TO COVER BOTH VIEWS, because `hazard-test` proved all of this against the windscreen alone and passed. bridge-test is the pattern - it asserts the ironwork in the glass separately from the ironwork on the screen, for exactly this reason.

NOT STARTED.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T13:14:51-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.63. The rail, the city's barrier and the cliff drop are all in the glass now.

THE SAME PAINTER, NOT A SECOND ONE. `drawRail` takes the view's vertical scale as an argument, exactly as `drawTruss` does, so the mirror gets the same beam, the same lit lip, the same posts and the same barrier from one definition. The ironwork has been drawn this way since [[RLG-112]] and the note beside it says why: a second copy is the thing the mirror pass exists to forbid.

AND THE POSTS TICK AT THE SAME PITCH IN BOTH VIEWS because `postIn` asks the WORLD position rather than a segment index. The mirror walks in 900-unit steps and the road ahead in 200s, so an index cycle would have put the rail on a different spacing in the glass - which is the drift the mirror's own note warns about and the reason `drawRail` was written that way on the first day.

THE DROP IS THE WATER'S SHAPE, one block below it in the same loop: a fill from a line out to the edge of the pane, carried to the bottom, walked far to near so each nearer slice paints over the last. Without it the glass showed a guard rail standing on open ground, which is the exact picture [[RLG-278]] was raised to remove from the windscreen.

THE CHECK COULD NOT HAVE CAUGHT THIS AND NOW CAN. `hazard-test`'s frame diff read the LOWER HALF of the screen only - the mirror band was deliberately excluded, on the reasoning that a change up there is the glass agreeing rather than a second rail. That reasoning is sound and it is also exactly what let the omission through: the check proved the windscreen and said nothing about the glass, and it passed all day. It now measures the two bands separately and asserts both. bridge-test is the precedent - it asserts its ironwork in the glass separately, for this reason.

MEASURED: MOUNTAIN 258 pixels in the mirror against 4,776 on the road; CITY 96 against 1,171; FARMLAND 3 and 16, which is noise. NOT VACUOUS, watched failing with the mirror's own calls removed - which is precisely what shipped this morning: 4 pixels in the glass for both MOUNTAIN and CITY, while the windscreen was unchanged at 3,776 and 1,087.

THE BAR IS 30 AND IT IS MEASURED: the noise floor is 3 to 4 pixels and a drawn boundary is 96 to 258, so it sits in an empty band of more than three to one on the near side.

hazard-test, mirror-test, bridge-test and smoke-test pass.

THE LESSON, STATED FOR THE NEXT THING ADDED BESIDE THE ROAD: the mirror is not a copy of the screen. Every roadside object has to be drawn in two places or it does not exist behind you, and any check written for one of them will pass while the other is empty.
