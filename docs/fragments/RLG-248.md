### RLG-248 — A dispatched Interceptor is never seen
- **RULED 2026-09-14T21:09:58-04:00** — **TRACKED, NOT STARTED.** Owner, 2026-09-14: *"Also when an interceptor is dispatched I never see it."*

A pursuit dispatches an Interceptor (the `SUPERCRUISER` body, `superc` on a cruiser), and the owner never sees one on the road.

**Candidates, none measured:** it spawns and is culled before it is drawn; it spawns ahead and out of range; it never closes on the player; or a dispatch is announced and no car is created. The mirror and the forward view draw it from different sprites (`SP.superCopFront` and `SP.superCop`), so check both. [[RLG-175]] (Interceptors earned by rivals) and the interceptor-test harness are the places to start.

It is likely to share a cause with [[RLG-247]], because both are about where pursuit cars spawn.
