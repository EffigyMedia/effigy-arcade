### UNT-209 - The road runs to the horizon as one body of tarmac

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-08T05:42:33-04:00** - The tarmac is carried to the vanishing point as one body of road, walked in the same steps the drawn road walks in, closing to a point at the horizon. The markings are deliberately not carried - past that distance one is under a pixel wide. Two faults were found by measurement: an even stride put every point inside a quarter-pixel of the horizon so the band was a straight cone, and the band had to move to the road's side of the haze or the join was a tone step of 46. The tone step the ruling asked for is printed rather than asserted, with the four sampling schemes that failed written down: the quantity is real and this instrument cannot hold it still, so the join needs the owner on a device.
