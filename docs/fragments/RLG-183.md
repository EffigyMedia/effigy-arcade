### RLG-183 — Show a little of the tyres under each car in the garage

TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-09, in the same breath as [[RLG-182]]: *"we need to show a
little bit of the tires on the bottom."*

**THE HEIGHT HALF OF THAT REPORT IS BUILT AND THIS HALF IS NOT**, because they turned out to be
different kinds of problem. The heights were a layout fault - one scale for two drawings, and each
end placed by its top. This one is not a layout fault at all.

**THE SILHOUETTE ANSWERED IT DIRECTLY.** A locked car is painted as a flat fill through
`source-atop`, so the grey shape IS the sprite's own ink. **Its bottom edge is flat across the
whole width, on both ends of every car.** Nothing is being cropped, hidden or misplaced: there is
no wheel in the garage sprite below the body line to show.

**SO THIS NEEDS THE PAINTER, NOT THE CARD.** The garage draws `SP.player` and `SP.playerFront` as
finished images, and the wheels a car has on the road are not part of them. Whatever is done has to
put wheels into the picture - either into those sprites, or drawn by the garage under them.

**WHAT MUST NOT BE DONE, and it is the obvious wrong first move:** lowering the floor line or
padding the ink box to leave room. That moves a car with no wheels further up the card and leaves
a gap under it. The bottom of the body is currently the bottom of the picture, and the picture has
to gain something before the layout should change.

**AND IT TOUCHES THE FLEET, NOT A CAR.** Seventeen garage bodies, two ends each. The declaration
machinery from [[RLG-053]] is the precedent for adding a part to every vehicle from one definition
rather than by hand - that ruling converted 84 lamps across 26 sprites - and the same warning
applies here: a wheel drawn from guessed coordinates over a sprite that already knows its own
geometry is the mistake found three times in two days ([[RLG-177]], [[RLG-179]]).

**ONE QUESTION FOR THE OWNER before it is built:** on the road the car is a billboard seen from
directly behind or in front, so the wheels that would show are the two outer edges of the tyres
under the body. Is "a little bit of the tires" that - two slivers at the corners - or a raised body
showing the whole lower half of each wheel? The two look very different and only one of them
matches the sprites' own perspective.
