### RLG-156 — One-mile drag races on the Interstate, forced manual, one on one
- **RULED 2026-09-05T19:59:43-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-05: "I have another idea that I'd like to add to the I suppose
interstate version: 1 mile drag race races. These are forced manual transmission 1v1 1 mile races
without turning two cars just going straight - we will need the NPC AI to not shift absolutely
perfectly all the time for this mode. Just track this we don't need to build it right this second."

WHAT IT IS, in the owner's terms: a mode on INTERSTATE. One mile. One on one. A straight road with no
turning. The gearbox is FORCED TO MANUAL whatever the player's setting says. And the rival must NOT
shift perfectly every time.

WHY IT SUITS THIS GAME. A drag race is the launch and the shifts and nothing else, and both already
exist here as real systems rather than as approximations. `LAUNCH` is a modelled standing start with
its own shove and bog; the manual gate is a physical H-pattern the thumb drags through, with per-car
gear counts and ratios that RLG-069 already proved are the car's own. A mode that removes steering
and scenery leaves exactly the two mechanics this engine models most carefully.

THE ONE THING THAT IS NOT GROUNDWORK, AND IT IS THE OWNER'S OWN REQUIREMENT: **RIVALS HAVE NO
GEARBOX.** Searched, and there is no `r.gear` anywhere - a rival is driven by speed and acceleration
curves, not by simulated gearchanges. So "the NPC AI should not shift absolutely perfectly" cannot be
built by degrading something that exists. A rival needs a gearbox FIRST, and only then can it shift
imperfectly. That is the real cost of this feature and it should be priced before anything else is
designed.

AND A SHIFT ERROR IS A DRIVER TRAIT, NEVER A VEHICLE ONE. This project has ruled it three times
(RLG-042, RLG-052, RLG-054) and restated it in the pickup correction: the car is CAPABLE and the
driver DECIDES; a class describes the vehicle, not who is holding the wheel. So the imperfection
belongs on the RIVAL, beside the personalities RLG-054 asks for, and must not be a number on the
BODY. Two cars of the same model, one driven well and one badly, is the whole point of a drag race.

A STRAIGHT ROAD IS NEW. There is no straight-road override in the generator. `isStraight(z)` exists
but it only ASKS whether a stretch happens to be straight enough for a roadblock; nothing forces one.
A mile of guaranteed straight, flat road is a generator mode rather than a setting.

FOUR THINGS ARE THE OWNER'S CALL AND NONE SHOULD BE GUESSED:

  (i) WHERE THE MODE LIVES. MODE is a three-state cycle in the garage - TEST DRIVE, SINGLE RACE,
      TOURNAMENT. A fourth state on that cycle, or a separate control, are different answers and the
      cycle is already long enough that a fourth press may be one too many.

  (ii) WHETHER RLG-115 APPLIES. A drag race is a race, so on the rule as built a production or
      utility car could not enter one. But a quarter-mile in a pickup is a real thing people do, and
      the ruling's own reasoning was about a saloon on a starting grid against three supercars -
      which a heads-up drag race is not. This is the first case where that rule may want an
      exception, and it should be asked rather than inferred.

  (iii) WHAT LOSING COSTS AND WINNING PAYS. The tournament ladder is a class ladder; a drag mode
      sits outside it or joins it, and those are different games.

  (iv) HOW MUCH THE RIVAL MISSES BY. "Not absolutely perfectly all the time" is a feel, and the range
      between a missed shift being rare and it being the whole mode is wide. It should be set from
      play, and the lever should be a tunable with a committed default rather than an edited
      constant.

WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME. A drag race is decided by the driver, so the rival must not be given a
better car or a rubber band - ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR is the owner's standing ruling and the rubber
band is its single named exception, granted for a circuit rather than for a straight line. A rival
that wins a drag race by being faster than its own stats would be the exact fault RLG-033 exists to
prevent.

AND THE HARNESS WOULD NEED A NEW DRIVER. `drive-test` steers with a keyboard and holds the throttle
down; a drag race needs neither steering nor a full-throttle autopilot, but it does need someone to
work the gate. It is also the first mode where the RIGHT answer is a fixed distance rather than a
lap or a timer, so nothing existing measures it.

INTERSTATE ONLY, as the owner said. Motorsport is a circuit and a straight mile is not a circuit; the
`CFG` seam is where the difference belongs, and `CFG.circuitOnly` is the flag that already separates
them.
