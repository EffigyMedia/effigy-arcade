### RLG-156 — One-mile drag races on the Interstate, forced manual, one on one
- **RULED 2026-09-05T19:59:43-04:00** — TRACKED, NOT STARTED. Owner, 2026-09-05: "I have another idea that I'd like to add to the I suppose
interstate version: 1 mile drag race races. These are forced manual transmission 1v1 1 mile races
without turning two cars just going straight - we will need the NPC AI to not shift absolutely
perfectly all the time for this mode. Just track this we don't need to build it right this second."

WHAT IT IS, in the owner's terms: a mode on INTERSTATE. One mile. One on one. A straight road with no
turning. The gearbox is FORCED TO MANUAL whatever the player's setting says. And the rival must NOT
shift perfectly every time.

WHY IT SUITS THIS GAME. A drag race is the launch and the shifts and nothing else, and both already
exist here as real systems rather than as approximations. `LAUNCH` is a modelled standing start with
its own shove and bog; the manual gate is a physical H-pattern the thumb drags through, with per-car
gear counts and ratios that RLG-069 already proved are the car's own. A mode that removes steering
and scenery leaves exactly the two mechanics this engine models most carefully.

THE ONE THING THAT IS NOT GROUNDWORK, AND IT IS THE OWNER'S OWN REQUIREMENT: **RIVALS HAVE NO
GEARBOX.** Searched, and there is no `r.gear` anywhere - a rival is driven by speed and acceleration
curves, not by simulated gearchanges. So "the NPC AI should not shift absolutely perfectly" cannot be
built by degrading something that exists. A rival needs a gearbox FIRST, and only then can it shift
imperfectly. That is the real cost of this feature and it should be priced before anything else is
designed.

AND A SHIFT ERROR IS A DRIVER TRAIT, NEVER A VEHICLE ONE. This project has ruled it three times
(RLG-042, RLG-052, RLG-054) and restated it in the pickup correction: the car is CAPABLE and the
driver DECIDES; a class describes the vehicle, not who is holding the wheel. So the imperfection
belongs on the RIVAL, beside the personalities RLG-054 asks for, and must not be a number on the
BODY. Two cars of the same model, one driven well and one badly, is the whole point of a drag race.

A STRAIGHT ROAD IS NEW. There is no straight-road override in the generator. `isStraight(z)` exists
but it only ASKS whether a stretch happens to be straight enough for a roadblock; nothing forces one.
A mile of guaranteed straight, flat road is a generator mode rather than a setting.

FOUR THINGS ARE THE OWNER'S CALL AND NONE SHOULD BE GUESSED:

  (i) WHERE THE MODE LIVES. MODE is a three-state cycle in the garage - TEST DRIVE, SINGLE RACE,
      TOURNAMENT. A fourth state on that cycle, or a separate control, are different answers and the
      cycle is already long enough that a fourth press may be one too many.

  (ii) WHETHER RLG-115 APPLIES. A drag race is a race, so on the rule as built a production or
      utility car could not enter one. But a quarter-mile in a pickup is a real thing people do, and
      the ruling's own reasoning was about a saloon on a starting grid against three supercars -
      which a heads-up drag race is not. This is the first case where that rule may want an
      exception, and it should be asked rather than inferred.

  (iii) WHAT LOSING COSTS AND WINNING PAYS. The tournament ladder is a class ladder; a drag mode
      sits outside it or joins it, and those are different games.

  (iv) HOW MUCH THE RIVAL MISSES BY. "Not absolutely perfectly all the time" is a feel, and the range
      between a missed shift being rare and it being the whole mode is wide. It should be set from
      play, and the lever should be a tunable with a committed default rather than an edited
      constant.

WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME. A drag race is decided by the driver, so the rival must not be given a
better car or a rubber band - ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR is the owner's standing ruling and the rubber
band is its single named exception, granted for a circuit rather than for a straight line. A rival
that wins a drag race by being faster than its own stats would be the exact fault RLG-033 exists to
prevent.

AND THE HARNESS WOULD NEED A NEW DRIVER. `drive-test` steers with a keyboard and holds the throttle
down; a drag race needs neither steering nor a full-throttle autopilot, but it does need someone to
work the gate. It is also the first mode where the RIGHT answer is a fixed distance rather than a
lap or a timer, so nothing existing measures it.

INTERSTATE ONLY, as the owner said. Motorsport is a circuit and a straight mile is not a circuit; the
`CFG` seam is where the difference belongs, and `CFG.circuitOnly` is the flag that already separates
them.
- **RULED 2026-09-06T03:36:33-04:00** — CORRECTION, 2026-09-06, and the owner was right. This fragment says "RIVALS HAVE NO GEARBOX" and
that a rival "is driven by speed and acceleration curves, not by simulated gearchanges". THAT IS
FALSE. It was inferred from one weak search - `grep` for `r.gear` finding nothing - and a missing
variable name is not evidence of a missing system. The owner challenged it on the standing ruling
that NPCs run the same mechanics as the player, and reading the code proves them correct.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE. A rival runs the SAME engine and the SAME transmission as the player, per car:

  - `aiAccel` is `1000 * aiGearFactor(v, key) * pullOf(key) * dt`. The player's line is
    `1000 * gearFactor() * pull * dt`. The same expression and the same constant.
  - `aiGearFactor` reads `gearTableFor(key)` - that car's own ratio table, the same one the player's
    shifter uses - and applies `G.ratio / 2.0` exactly as the player's `gearFactor` does.
  - It computes rpm against `redlineFor(key)`, that car's own redline, and puts it through
    `torqueAt(rpm, rl)` - THE SAME TORQUE CURVE, the same function object, not a copy.
  - `vmaxOf(key)`, `pullOf(key)` and `brakeOf(key)` are all per-body and shared.

The comment above `aiAccel` says so outright, and records that this was already fixed once: the AI
used to use `2850 * aiGearFactor` with no `pull` at all, and RLG-038 corrected it to the player's
line because "the owner's ruling is one physics for every car". So the shared model is not an
accident - it is a ruling that was already enforced.

THE REAL DIFFERENCE IS ONE THING AND IT IS MUCH SMALLER. The player's gear is STATE: `let gear = 1`,
and `gearFactor()` reads `gearTable()[gear-1]`, so the player can be in the wrong gear, can be late,
can miss one. The rival's gear is DERIVED FROM SPEED every frame: `aiGearFactor` computes
`r = v / vmaxOf(key)` and picks whichever band contains it. A rival is therefore always in exactly
the right gear, instantaneously, with no shift event at all - no clutch interruption, no torque drop,
nothing that takes time.

SO THE GAP IS NOT "NO GEARBOX". IT IS "NO SHIFT EVENT". The rival has the whole gearbox and the whole
engine; what it has not got is a HELD gear that changes at a moment. That is precisely and only what
[[RLG-156]]'s drag mode needs, because an imperfect shift has to have a shift to be imperfect about.

WHAT THAT CHANGES ABOUT THE COSTING IN THIS FRAGMENT. It is smaller than stated, and better shaped.
Give a rival a held `gear` plus a shift moment - reading the ratio table it ALREADY reads - and the
existing torque curve does the rest: a late shift sits on the limiter and makes 0.06 of torque, an
early one lugs at 0.30, and both fall out of `torqueAt` without a special case. The imperfection is
then a DRIVER trait on the rival beside RLG-054's personalities, exactly as this fragment already
requires, and it costs a variable and a timer rather than a physics model.

AND THE LESSON IS THE ONE THIS PROJECT KEEPS RELEARNING. A search that cannot return a positive
result is not evidence of a negative one - CLAUDE.md section 5 says exactly that, and this is the
second time in two days that reading beat grepping: the ambulance's missing bar was also diagnosed
wrongly from a search before the early `return` was found by reading. The claim was also stated to
the owner as fact in a handover, which is how it got challenged rather than shipped.

- **RULED 2026-09-19T18:04:17-04:00** — BUILT, v0.14.95. DRAG RACE is the last stop on every car's MODE control in Interstate (TEST DRIVE, SINGLE RACE, TOURNAMENT, DRAG RACE for a racing car; TEST DRIVE, INTERCEPT, INTERCEPT TOURNAMENT, DRAG RACE for a police car). Motorsport does not offer it: dragOffered() is !CFG.circuitOnly, the seam that already keeps traffic off a circuit. A car with no race of its own would get TEST DRIVE and DRAG RACE; none is in the garage today, because the utility class is barred (RLG-249).

HOW. It is mode 'race' with a flag, dragOn, as INTERCEPT is - a new value of mode would have switched off every race gate at once. optDrag is the player's pick, kept apart from raceMode and duty; a police car in a drag race is not on shift (playerIsPolice reads dragOn). The run: a standing start (rollStartMph is 0 in a drag race, though Interstate otherwise starts rolling at 50); the gearbox forced MANUAL for the race, with the player's own setting kept in dragKeepBox and put back in the garage, and the pause menu's gearbox changing the kept setting, not the race's; one straight, flat segment of each road list, finite (the renderer integrates the span every quarter second), laid after freshWorld; the player in the lane left of centre; no traffic seed, no waves, no traffic from behind, no ambulance, no deer, no police, traps or roadblocks, no clock, and a dry road.

THE RIVAL. The same body in a random paint from what that body may wear, never the player's; level with the player's car (pos + PLAYER_Z), in the next lane, holding it. It chases its body's own top speed - no rubber band and no field ceiling, because ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR has the band as its only exception. Its sprite is painted on demand through carSprites, because the field's cache covers the racing classes only and a missing entry drew the PLAYER'S sprite. THE DRIVER is drawn per race from DRAG_DRIVERS - SHARP 20%, STEADY 60%, SLOPPY 20% - each with a point in the band where it changes up, a chance of a late change that sits on the limiter, a shift time and a launch; torqueAt makes a late change cost time with no special case. A drag rival does not change back DOWN: an early change left it below the next gear's band and the field's downshift rule then hunted, six changes in a four-gear car.

THE END. The mile is timed from GO (dragT). A rival still short of the line when the player crosses is timed by what it had left at its speed, and the card marks that time with ~. The card shows YOU WIN or YOU LOSE, both times, the best in this car and the top speed, with RACE AGAIN, CHANGE CAR and MAIN MENU. The best is kept per body in interstate-drag (the <id>-suffix rule; registered in smoke-test's saves() list), and the drag finish skips the best-distance write that labels the launcher's cabinet card.

PROVEN by tools/drag-test.py (EVD-1107), new: the MODE walk on a production car, a supercar and a police car; from rest; MANUAL though the setting is AUTO; straight and flat for the mile; no traffic or police all the way, no clock, dry; one rival, same car, another paint, level and in the next lane; the race ends at the mile on its own card with both times; the best is saved for the HATCH and the launcher label is untouched; the gearbox comes back; and THE DRIVER DECIDES - the same HATCH ran 24.49 s with a SHARP driver and 25.13 s with a SLOPPY one. The harness drives the player in the page, changing up at the top of each band through API.putGear, which places the knob through placeKnob. NOT VACUOUS: with MANUAL not forced and the road not straightened, the manual, straight-road and lane checks failed. smoke-test, locked-garage-test pass (EVD-1108, EVD-1110); shift-tour-test's police-cycle check was written for three stops and now asserts three stops, then DRAG RACE, then home (EVD-1112); drive-test fails only the known flaky nitrous settle line (EVD-1109). How a drag race feels, and whether the rival's misses read as a driver, is the owner's call on the device.
