### RLG-097 — The pop on GO is still there
- **RULED 2026-08-31T08:35:31-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "There's still a pop on GO. Maybe the scenery dressing is reloading? I don't know
something is shifting. I can't put my finger on it."

THIS IS THE FOURTH REPORT OF A POP AT GO AND THE THIRD MECHANISM WILL NOT BE THE LAST ONE GUESSED.
Two have already been found and fixed, and both were real: 0.10.11 and 0.10.16 rolled the biome and
then the weather and the cloud AT the first frame after GO, because a counter left at zero means a
roll is due and the count-in returned from the frame update before the world ran. 0.10.12 fixed the
horizon snapping at GO. Every one of those was confirmed gone on the device. Something else is
shifting.

THE OWNER'S GUESS IS A LEAD AND IT IS THE FIRST PLACE TO LOOK, NOT THE ONLY ONE. "The scenery
dressing is reloading" names a concrete suspect: the roadside scenery, the skyline and the sprite
caches are all built lazily and keyed on something, and anything keyed on a value the reset changes
will rebuild on the first frame of a run. RLG-094 has just shown what that looks like from the
inside - the skyline was rebuilt whenever an hour bucket moved, and nobody had noticed because the
rebuild is invisible until the thing rebuilt comes out different.

AND THE DIAGNOSIS IS IN THE DETAIL, NOT IN AN INSTRUMENT. RLG-041 is the standing lesson: six fixes
over eleven versions, five of them real mechanisms and none of them the one the owner was seeing, and
every step forward came from the owner's own words rather than from a number. "I can't put my finger
on it" is the honest report of somebody who has looked; it is not a licence to guess. ASK FOR THE
DETAIL FIRST - does it move up, sideways, or change size; is it the road, the scenery, the sky or the
car; does it happen on RETRY as well as on a fresh DRIVE; does it happen at every time of day.

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN AND SHOULD NOT BE RE-DERIVED. `API.restart()` runs the same path RETRY and
DRIVE both take, `API.worldState()` returns exactly the fields `freshWorld` owns, and
`tools/retry-test.py` and `tools/start-test.py` already assert that nothing accumulated survives a
reset and that a run starts at what it rolled. Whatever is shifting is either not in `worldState`, or
is being rebuilt rather than reset - and those are two different searches.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T15:35:28-04:00** — MECHANISM FOUND AND FIXED, 2026-08-31, as [[RLG-121]]. The fourth report carried the detail that named it: the owner's own car JUMPS TO A DIFFERENT PLACE and EVERYTHING SHIFTS AT ONCE, identically on RETRY and on a fresh run. That is not a pop-in, which is what the first three reports were diagnosed as and fixed for - it is the whole picture moving sideways by 8.5 per cent of the screen width in one frame, because `viewShift` is assigned only inside `step()` and `step()` returned while the count was up. Measured at 0 during the count and -40.8 pixels after it on a 480-pixel viewport. THE LESSON HELD AGAIN: three real mechanisms were found by instrument and none was what the owner was seeing; this one came from four words of theirs. NOT CLOSED FROM HERE - only the device can say the pop is gone.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T23:44:20-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-08-31: "RLG-097 has been fixed. Let's move both of these to the end and just do a quick
analysis to verify these facts."

VERIFIED AS FAR AS A HARNESS CAN, AND THAT LIMIT IS THE POINT. This project has [[RLG-041]]'s lesson
written down: five real mechanisms were found and fixed for that defect and NONE was the one the
owner was seeing. So the check does not claim the pop is gone - it cannot see a pop. It asks the
narrower question a harness can settle: does anything the picture is drawn through still STEP across
the count-in boundary.

MEASURED, every frame across the count and into driving:

    viewShift   no step at all
    horizon     0.0000
    camX        0.0043 largest single-frame step
    playerX     0.0043
    pushK       0.2850, at 82 ms
    bendT       0.2417, at 116 ms

`viewShift` IS THE ONE [[RLG-121]] NAMED and it is the one that matters: on a touch layout it goes
from 0 to minus 8.5 per cent of the screen width, which at 480 wide is 40.8 pixels - the entire
forward view moving sideways in one frame. It no longer moves at all.

THE TWO THAT DO STEP ARE NOT THE POP, and saying so is more useful than reporting them as clean.
`pushK` is the cornering force and `bendT` the bend easing; both step in the FIRST HUNDRED
MILLISECONDS of the count, which is them building from zero as the road ahead is read, and both are
chased rather than assigned. They are not at GO, and they are the behaviour RLG-121 deliberately kept
- the force arrives at GO already built rather than starting from zero in front of the player.

SO THE FIX IS CONSISTENT WITH THE OWNER'S REPORT AND IS NOT PROOF OF IT. The owner has driven it and
says it is fixed; that is the evidence that counts, and this only says nothing contradicts it.
