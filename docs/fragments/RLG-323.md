### RLG-323 — The racers use every stripe version, every random colour and every underglow
- **RULED 2026-09-21T22:39:35-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-21: 'And I want to make sure all the stripe versions, random colors, and underglows are being used by the racers.' (The message said 'undergoes'; underglow is the only reading that fits a car's livery.)

A VERIFICATION FIRST, then a fix if it finds a gap. The question is whether the racer generator can reach every stripe variant, every paint in the random pool and every underglow colour the garage offers - or whether some of them exist only for the player. It is the fleet rule seen from the livery side: a rival is not a lesser car, and it should not be a plainer one either.

THE PROOF IS A TALLY, not a reading of the source: spawn many grids and count which stripe, paint and underglow each racer got, against the full list each table declares. Anything that is declared and never drawn is the finding. Not started.
- **RULED 2026-09-22T11:36:45-04:00** — BUILT in 0.14.126 (UNT-419). THE TALLY CAME FIRST AND FOUND THE GAP: a rival's livery was one boolean, r.striped, painted as the plain pair from a prebuilt cache. Four of the five patterns, every stripe colour, two-tone and the underglow existed only on the player's car. Body paint was already the whole table, iridescent included, except the player's own colour.

NOW each rival on a body that takes a livery rolls one at the grid (racerLivery): a pattern from all five when striped (RACER_STRIPES 0.35, unchanged), its own stripe colour at RACER_STRIPE_COL 0.5, a second tone at RACER_TWOTONE 0.25, an underglow at RACER_GLOW 0.25 - every colour from the garage's own tables. carSprites, the garage's painter, draws the rear and the front; paintGlow, split out of drawPlayer, draws the glow under the car in both views, at the car's own fade. A rival with no stripes and no second tone keeps the prebuilt sprite; the rest are painted in buildField, before the count-in, into a cache emptied at every grid.

PROVED by tools/livery-grid-test.py over 120 grids (1,320 rivals): all five patterns, all twelve base colours as stripes and as tones, all seven glow colours, every paint but the player's, and a grid raced with every chance at one draws rivals in their livery sprite over a glow. The falsify arm puts back the plain pair and fails exactly the pattern check. REVIEWED before commit (RVW for UNT-419): sound with findings - an unbounded cache, a first-frame build stall, a glow ignoring its car's fade, a null-accepting setter, and weak harness checks - all fixed; race-test then caught a null dereference the stall fix introduced in the mirror, fixed and re-run. The chances are tunables (API.racerLivery); how a rival looks in each livery is the owner's call on the device.
