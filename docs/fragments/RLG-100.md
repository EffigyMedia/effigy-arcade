### RLG-100 — The checkpoint budget is not fair across the classes
- **RULED 2026-08-31T08:35:32-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "with the sports car class, I am barely able to make it from checkpoint to
checkpoint going absolute full speed the whole time. We may need to reevaluate either the distances
between checkpoints, the time rewarded when you cross one, or both. We may need to adjust these per
vehicle class to make it always fair."

THE REPORT IS A MEASUREMENT WAITING TO BE TAKEN, AND IT MUST BE TAKEN BEFORE A NUMBER MOVES. "Barely
able at absolute full speed" is a statement about the margin between the time a leg gives you and the
time the fastest possible drive of that leg costs. That margin is computable for every class from
things the engine already knows - the distance between boards, the time awarded, and the car's own
top speed - and it should be TABULATED for all six cars before anything is tuned. RLG-055's lesson is
exactly this: the fleet's vmax column turned out true while three comments around it were false, and
nobody could have predicted which way that would fall.

THE OWNER NAMED TWO LEVERS AND THERE IS A THIRD. Distance between boards, and time awarded. The third
is the one that makes the margin uneven in the first place: a slow car spends longer on a leg, so it
needs MORE time, not the same - which is why a single pair of numbers cannot be fair to six cars with
different top speeds. That is what "adjust these per vehicle class" is reaching for.

AND PER-CLASS NUMBERS ARE A COLLISION WITH A STANDING RULING, WHICH HAS TO BE FACED RATHER THAN
SIDESTEPPED. ONE PHYSICS FOR EVERY CAR (owner, 2026-08-28) with the rubber band as the only named
exception, and "never write a branch that names a vehicle class". A checkpoint budget is NOT physics -
it is the rules of the event, the way a rally's target time is - so a per-class allowance does not
break that rule. But the distinction should be written down before it is built, because the next
session reading a `if(class === ...)` in the checkpoint code will not be able to tell it from the
thing that is forbidden. THE BETTER SHAPE IS A FORMULA, NOT A TABLE: time awarded derived from the
leg's length and the car's own top speed, so a seventh car is fair for nothing and no branch names
anybody.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN IS A NUMBER MOVED UNTIL THE SPORTS CAR FEELS RIGHT. That leaves the other five
unmeasured, and the owner's own words say the goal is "always fair" - which is a property of the
whole fleet.

THE INSTRUMENT DOES NOT EXIST YET. `stat-test.py` measures acceleration and top speed only, and
`race-test.py` is the circuit. Nothing measures a leg of the Interstate against the clock it gives
you. That harness is the first piece of work here, and it is also what would tell the owner whether
this is a distance problem, a time problem, or both.

pick up solved that issue."

THE AWARD IS REAL AND THE CONCLUSION DOES NOT FOLLOW. Both halves matter and they point different
ways, so this is recorded as a MITIGATION rather than as a closure.

THE TEN SECONDS WORK. `crate-test` centres the car, parks a crate in its path and reads the clock
either side, for every case including a car with nothing else to gain: a ROADSTER at full health goes
59.3 to 65.7, a LORRY 65.7 to 72.2, and a damaged lorry is paid in health AND seconds because each
award is taken in turn. That is not in doubt.

BUT THE CLOCK WAS MEASURED AND THE CRATES DID NOT SAVE IT. Three cars driven at full throttle for
eighty seconds each, on the real road with traffic:

    TUNER     clock 60.0 -> 22.6, lowest  7.9 over 4.18 miles, 0 crates taken, reached 134 of 146 mph
    ROADSTER  clock 60.0 -> 22.6, lowest 12.1 over 4.56 miles, 0 crates taken, reached 135 of 153 mph
    MUSCLE    clock 60.0 ->  2.6, lowest  2.6 over 3.98 miles, 0 crates taken, reached 134 of 160 mph

ZERO CRATES IN ALL THREE RUNS, AND THAT IS THE FINDING. Crates are parked ON THE SHOULDER - taking
one means leaving the road - so a driver holding a straight line never collects one. Every second in
those runs came from checkpoints alone, and a car still came within 2.6 seconds of running the clock
out. An earlier pass of the same measurement had the TUNER at 2.6 as well.

SO THE MARGIN IS UNCHANGED FOR A PLAYER WHO DOES NOT DETOUR. What the crate award did was give a
player who DOES detour a way to buy the margin back - which is a real answer, and it is almost
certainly why the owner stopped feeling the problem, because a person playing collects crates and an
autopilot does not.

THE ARITHMETIC UNDERNEATH, which says why the game is marginal by construction. A checkpoint is two
miles and pays twenty seconds. Two miles in twenty seconds is 360mph, and nothing on the board does a
third of that - so EVERY car runs a deficit on every leg and the run is a countdown that opening
credit and pickups extend. That is a design, not a defect, and it is worth stating plainly because
"the sports car barely makes checkpoint to checkpoint" reads like a bug and is the intended shape.

WHAT IS LEFT OF RLG-100, and it is smaller than it was. The question is no longer "can a car reach
the next checkpoint" but "is the deficit the same shape for all six cars, and is the MUSCLE's harsher
than it should be". It reached 134 of its declared 160mph in eighty seconds of real traffic, which is
the widest gap of the three and is the thing to look at first if this is picked up.

MOVED TO THE END OF THE QUEUE AT THE OWNER'S DIRECTION. Not closed: the evidence says mitigated for a
collecting player and unchanged otherwise.

AND ONE THING THIS DID NOT MEASURE. Every run above is the autopilot at full throttle on a road with
traffic, so the cars did not reach their declared top speeds - 134 against 146, 135 against 153, 134
against 160. That is the real driving condition rather than a fault in the reading, but a clean-road
measurement would give a different and more flattering set of numbers.

RLG-235'S INTENT IS THE TEST: "a timed run is designed to END, and distance is the score. It must punish bad driving, and a good driver can keep it going." At full throttle, nobody can keep it going now. NO NUMBER WAS MOVED. That decision is the owner's, and it is put to them.
