### RLG-161 — A roadblock is drawn in the mirror

Owner, 2026-09-07: "a police roadblock is invisible in the mirror."

The mirror redraws the world behind the car and has its own pass over what to include.
Roadblocks are absent from it, so one you have just threaded disappears the moment it is
behind you.
