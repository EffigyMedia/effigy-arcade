### RLG-267 — The busted bar completes and nothing happens
- **RULED 2026-09-16T00:20:32-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "When the busted bar completes, the race is over and you are busted. Right now that doesn't work."

WHAT THE CODE SAYS (0.14.42, before the stop zone lands). `bustT` counts up while `boxed` is true and `wreck('BUSTED')` fires at three seconds; `boxed` is set either by a cruiser within 2,600 of a nearly stopped player, or by one within PURSUIT_STOP.near in the player's line while the player is under PURSUIT_STOP.spd (RLG-247). bust-test passes, and pursuit-stop-test's LINED arm ends in BUSTED - so the rule fires in a staged scene and not in the owner's play.

CANDIDATES, NONE MEASURED YET: the bar the owner watches may not be `bustT` at all; `held`, `optEasy` or `playerIsPolice` may be gating it in the mode being played; the count decays at 1.6x while the condition flickers, so a bar that LOOKS full may never reach three; or the wreck path may be reached and not end the run in a race. Ask which mode it was, and whether the bar fills and empties or fills and sticks.
- **RULED 2026-09-16T00:44:50-04:00** — FOUND AND BUILT IN 0.14.44. `wreck(reason)` has one path for a clock still running: two seconds, a fresh car in the middle of the road, carry on. BUSTED went down it, so the bar filled, the crash penalty was paid, the cruisers were still alongside a car that was now stopped, and the bar filled again - which is the loop the owner watched. `wreck` now sends BUSTED to the end-of-run path whatever the clock says. It is the inverse of the shift, where a rival the player holds in front of them is stopped and out (RLG-203).

PROOF. tools/bust-ends-test.py, in Hot Pursuit with sixty seconds on the clock: the run ends at 3.7 s showing BUSTED with 57 s still to run, and it happens ONCE. The control drives damage to the limit into a parked lorry: that still costs seconds rather than the run. Against 772b2d9c the first three checks fail - the same BUSTED is recorded at 3 s and the run carries on.

TWO HARNESSES HAD TO LEARN THE NEW RULE, and both were measuring the old one:
1. pursuit-stop-test measured the cruiser's braking against the player's LIVE speed. RLG-259's zone now drags the player down too, so it was comparing two falling numbers and read 9.0. It reads the pinned speed now.
2. stop-zone-test ran its later arms on a run the first arm had just ENDED, so every reading was zero. It restarts the run between arms, and waits for the car to reach the pin before it samples - a restarted run comes out of the countdown at a rolling start.

bust, stop-zone, pursuit-stop and smoke pass.
