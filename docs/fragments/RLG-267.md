### RLG-267 — The busted bar completes and nothing happens
- **RULED 2026-09-16T00:20:32-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-16: "When the busted bar completes, the race is over and you are busted. Right now that doesn't work."

WHAT THE CODE SAYS (0.14.42, before the stop zone lands). `bustT` counts up while `boxed` is true and `wreck('BUSTED')` fires at three seconds; `boxed` is set either by a cruiser within 2,600 of a nearly stopped player, or by one within PURSUIT_STOP.near in the player's line while the player is under PURSUIT_STOP.spd (RLG-247). bust-test passes, and pursuit-stop-test's LINED arm ends in BUSTED - so the rule fires in a staged scene and not in the owner's play.

CANDIDATES, NONE MEASURED YET: the bar the owner watches may not be `bustT` at all; `held`, `optEasy` or `playerIsPolice` may be gating it in the mode being played; the count decays at 1.6x while the condition flickers, so a bar that LOOKS full may never reach three; or the wreck path may be reached and not end the run in a race. Ask which mode it was, and whether the bar fills and empties or fills and sticks.
