### RLG-237 — A tunnel's wall is solid: the car and the camera stay inside the bore
- **RULED 2026-09-13T13:30:37-04:00** — **REPORTED 2026-09-13, from the device.** Owner: *"The tunnel walls can be clipped through by the
camera."* The screenshot showed a car on the right shoulder inside a bore, a hard vertical edge
where the right wall stopped, and the world outside the tunnel beyond it.

**RULED THE SAME DAY.** Asked to choose between a solid wall and a wider bore, the owner chose the
solid wall: the car scrapes to a stop against it, as at the road edge, and the crates on the tunnel
shoulder stay reachable.

**BUILT, v0.14.12. NOT SIGNED** ([[RLG-116]]).

## THE FIRST DIAGNOSIS WAS WRONG, AND THE OWNER WAS TOLD IT

Before asking the question above, I told the owner the camera could leave the bore: the wall stands
at 1.248 road half-widths, the car's limit is 1.18, and the camera may sit 0.10 further out. That
arithmetic is true, but **it is not what the owner saw.** The first version of the harness measured
the camera held hard against the wall on the old limit, and the camera stayed 118.8 pixels inside.
The check could not fail, so it was not evidence.

## THERE WERE TWO FAULTS

**1. THE TUBE BEGAN AT THE CAR, NOT AT THE CAMERA. This is the one in the photograph.**
`boreMouth` returned `pos + PLAYER_Z + 40`, which is the car's depth. The comment above it said "the
camera once it is behind you". So no wall was drawn over the PLAYER_Z of road between the eye and
the car. With the car in the middle of the road, the edge of that gap projects off the screen. With
the car near a wall it lands on the screen, and the outside world shows past it. That is the
vertical edge in the owner's screenshot.

It now begins `BORE.near` (60 units) ahead of the camera. `proj` refuses anything 30 units or
closer. The walls are painted before the cars, so the car is still drawn over them.

**2. THE CAR'S SIDE PASSED THROUGH THE WALL.** [[RLG-105]] moved the wall out to 1.248 so it was
"clear of the 1.18 clamp", and compared the wall with the car's centre only. The car is about 0.24
wide. Measured on the old limit, the car's side sat 9.2 to 9.8 pixels through the wall. This is what
the owner's choice of a solid wall fixes.

`edgeX()` is the car's limit. Outside a bore it is 1.18, unchanged. Inside one it is the wall less
half the car and `BORE.skin` (0.02), and never more than 1.18. The wall position is
`1 + SCENE_UNIT*BORE.out/ROAD`, the same sum `roadsideAt` draws the wall with, so a wider car or a
wider road keeps its gap. The steering clamp and the edge scrape both read it. The scrape keeps its
old offsets, 0.03 and 0.05 inside the edge, so the bore's wall scrapes exactly as the road's edge
does. A bore begins for the car at its mouth, and the camera follows it in.

A separate camera limit was written first and then removed. The car's limit already keeps the
camera inside, because the camera is never more than 0.10 from the car and half the car is more than
that. No falsifier could make that code fail, so it was not kept.

## THE SHADING KEEPS ITS ANCHOR

The vault gradient and the wall gradient were laid between two points of the NEAREST section.
Moving that section to 60 units from the eye would put both points thousands of pixels off the
screen and paint the visible wall one colour. The gradients take the first section at the car's
depth, which is where the nearest section stood before, so the colours are unchanged.

## THE PROOF

`tools/tunnel-wall-test.py`, new. It drives into a bore with the run clock off, holds the car hard
against the right wall and then the left, and reads `API.boreClearance`, which measures the picture
in pixels: the wall at the nearest section the last frame drew, and the car's sides against
`borePointAt`, the function the walls are painted from.

    ok    on the open road the car still reaches the old edge   furthest out 1.150
    ok    right the car is held hard over inside the bore   furthest out 1.080, 40 samples
    ok    right no open world shows past the wall near the camera   the wall edge sits -1010 px inside the glass at worst, nearest section 60 units out
    ok    right the car never passes through the wall   closest to the wall 27.0 px
    (and the same three on the left)

The open-world check counts only frames with the CAMERA past the mouth. The car passes the mouth
PLAYER_Z before the camera, and in between the tube rightly begins at the mouth. The first run
counted those frames and failed on them, which was a harness fault.
