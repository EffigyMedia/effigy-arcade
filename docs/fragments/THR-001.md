- THE DEBUG SWITCHES LIST A CAR THEY WILL NOT LET YOU DRIVE (RLG-214, found 2026-09-12). `openBy` widens for `dbgTraffic`, `dbgRacers` and `dbgPolice` and decides what the garage LISTS; `carLocked` never consults them and decides what can be DRIVEN.
