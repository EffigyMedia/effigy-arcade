### RLG-345 — A race reports 1ST when you came last, because the board card comes first
- **RULED 2026-09-29T21:06:19-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-29, from the device: "no matter what place I get in the single race and possibly even the tournament races it shows I get first place."

THE PLACE IS COMPUTED CORRECTLY AND THE PLAYER IS READING A DIFFERENT SCREEN. Reproduced: a SINGLE RACE entered from the garage, the car held at 18 per cent of top speed until all ELEVEN rivals were up the road, then the line brought under the car with `API.parkFinish`. `gridSlot().place` reads **12** at the finish, which is right. The three screens that follow, in order:

| | what it says |
|---|---|
| 1 | `SINGLE RACE` - **1ST ON THE BOARD** - `HATCH · 15.450 S`, with the initials to tap in |
| 2 | the leaderboard itself: `1 AAA HATCH 15.450 S` |
| 3 | **`FINISHED 12TH`**, with the run's figures |

THE FIRST SCREEN IS THE LEADERBOARD'S AND ITS ORDINAL IS A TIME RANK. `boardOffer` heads the initials card with `(at + 1) + ordinal(at + 1) + 'ON THE BOARD'`, where `at` is what `boardWouldRank` says the RUN'S TIME is worth. On a short or empty board almost any time lands first, so a player who came last is shown a large `1ST` the instant they cross the line, and their actual finishing position arrives two taps later.

IT IS THE ORDER, AND THE ORDER IS DELIBERATE. `showInitials` says so at the point it hands over: "the board first, so a player sees where they landed, and BACK from it is the run's own end card rather than the board menu". That reasoning is about the BOARD making sense on its own, and it was written without the case where the two ordinals disagree.

NOT FIXED, BECAUSE THE REPAIR IS A CHOICE ABOUT THE PRODUCT AND NOT A MECHANICAL ONE. Either the run's own end card comes FIRST and the board offer follows it - which means `showEnd`'s three buttons stop being terminal and have to route through the board - or the board card stops leading with a bare ordinal. The second is cheaper and the first is what the owner's sentence actually asks for. THE OWNER DECIDES, and [[RLG-296]]'s standing rule applies to whatever is chosen: ship the control and no caption, because a line explaining that one ordinal is a time and the other is a position is exactly the explanatory text they cut.

AND THE TOURNAMENT PATH IS THE SAME SHAPE, so whatever is decided lands in both: `boardOffer('tour', ...)` runs before `showTrophy` on the last round and before `showRound(place)` on every other one.
