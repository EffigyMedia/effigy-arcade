### RLG-345 — A race reports 1ST when you came last, because the board card comes first
- **RULED 2026-09-29T21:06:19-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-29, from the device: "no matter what place I get in the single race and possibly even the tournament races it shows I get first place."

THE PLACE IS COMPUTED CORRECTLY AND THE PLAYER IS READING A DIFFERENT SCREEN. Reproduced: a SINGLE RACE entered from the garage, the car held at 18 per cent of top speed until all ELEVEN rivals were up the road, then the line brought under the car with `API.parkFinish`. `gridSlot().place` reads **12** at the finish, which is right. The three screens that follow, in order:

| | what it says |
|---|---|
| 1 | `SINGLE RACE` - **1ST ON THE BOARD** - `HATCH · 15.450 S`, with the initials to tap in |
| 2 | the leaderboard itself: `1 AAA HATCH 15.450 S` |
| 3 | **`FINISHED 12TH`**, with the run's figures |

THE FIRST SCREEN IS THE LEADERBOARD'S AND ITS ORDINAL IS A TIME RANK. `boardOffer` heads the initials card with `(at + 1) + ordinal(at + 1) + 'ON THE BOARD'`, where `at` is what `boardWouldRank` says the RUN'S TIME is worth. On a short or empty board almost any time lands first, so a player who came last is shown a large `1ST` the instant they cross the line, and their actual finishing position arrives two taps later.

IT IS THE ORDER, AND THE ORDER IS DELIBERATE. `showInitials` says so at the point it hands over: "the board first, so a player sees where they landed, and BACK from it is the run's own end card rather than the board menu". That reasoning is about the BOARD making sense on its own, and it was written without the case where the two ordinals disagree.

NOT FIXED, BECAUSE THE REPAIR IS A CHOICE ABOUT THE PRODUCT AND NOT A MECHANICAL ONE. Either the run's own end card comes FIRST and the board offer follows it - which means `showEnd`'s three buttons stop being terminal and have to route through the board - or the board card stops leading with a bare ordinal. The second is cheaper and the first is what the owner's sentence actually asks for. THE OWNER DECIDES, and [[RLG-296]]'s standing rule applies to whatever is chosen: ship the control and no caption, because a line explaining that one ordinal is a time and the other is a position is exactly the explanatory text they cut.

AND THE TOURNAMENT PATH IS THE SAME SHAPE, so whatever is decided lands in both: `boardOffer('tour', ...)` runs before `showTrophy` on the last round and before `showRound(place)` on every other one.
- **RULED 2026-09-29T21:18:56-04:00** — OWNER-DECIDED 2026-09-29, from three options: the result comes FIRST and the board follows it. Built at 0.14.156 for the SINGLE RACE.

HOW. A finish no longer calls `boardOffer`. It writes the entry to `boardPending` and shows the run's own end card, and `boardThen` wraps each of that card's three buttons: whichever way the player leaves, the board is offered first and the button's own action runs when they are done with it. Nothing about the board changes - the initials, the ten places and the BACK that returns to the run's card all behave exactly as before. The order of the two screens is the whole change.

PROVED BY PUTTING THE DEFECT BACK. `tools/race-result-test.py` enters a SINGLE RACE from the garage, holds the car at 18 per cent of top speed until the whole field is up the road - a check that finishes FIRST cannot tell the two ordinals apart, which is the entire question - brings the line under the car and taps through the screens. With the fix: `FINISHED 12TH`, then the board, then the board's own screen, then the menu. With the old two lines restored, FOUR of its five checks fail and the first card reads `1ST ON THE BOARD`. It also asserts the card against the LIVE place, so a future break in the place itself cannot pass behind a politely-worded card.

THE LADDER KEEPS THE OLD ORDER AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. The trophy was given the same treatment and it breaks the way out of a tournament: `tour-exit-test` leaves by MAIN MENU and lands on the board instead of the title, because the board now sits between them. It passed at HEAD and failed with the change, which is what says the change caused it rather than the harness being flaky. The owner reported the race and said only "possibly" of the tournament, so the confirmed half ships and this one waits for their word and for that harness to learn the new flow. The trophy has the same confusion - `1ST ON THE BOARD` over a bronze - and it is still open.
- **RULED 2026-09-29T21:26:42-04:00** — THE LADDER DOES IT TOO, AND THE OWNER SAID SO ON BEING TOLD WHAT THE ORDINAL WAS (2026-09-29): "if that placement is about the leaderboard, then it definitely is doing it in the tournament as well not possibly. There needs to be a race results screen specifically. Then we could handle the leaderboard once that screen is exited." The "possibly" in the first report is retired and the hedge in this fragment with it.

BOTH HALVES SHIP AT 0.14.156. The trophy is the ladder's result screen, so it comes first and the board is spent on the way out of it - by NEW TOURNAMENT or by MAIN MENU. SEE YOUR NEW CAR deliberately does NOT spend it: it is not a way out, the trophy is still underneath, and the player comes back to it.

THE ORDER IS NOW ASSERTED IN BOTH PLACES, and in the ladder's case the assertion had to be built because the harness could not see the fault. `tour-exit-test`'s screen detector TAPS THROUGH a board when it meets one - it has done since RLG-322 - so it reached the trophy either way and could not tell which came first. It records the order now and fails if a board stood in front of a result. Watched failing with the old two lines restored: "the board was reached 1 time(s) before a result screen". A harness that walks past the fault it is standing in is worth naming; this one did, for the whole of this report's life.

AND THE HARNESS LEARNED THE NEW WAY OUT rather than the change being trimmed to suit it. Leaving a trophy by MAIN MENU now passes through the initials and the board before the title, so `tour-exit-test` taps through them. That is the flow the owner asked for; the earlier decision to hold the ladder back was made only because this harness failed, and it was the wrong reading of a failure - the test was asserting the old order, not protecting anything.
