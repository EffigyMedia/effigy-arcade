### RLG-185 — One outline is not one drawing: a formula car's tail keeps its own design
- **RULED 2026-09-09T16:01:46-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09, on seeing 0.13.52:** *"the formula cars have been completely fucked up. The rear
has the same design as the front and it shouldn't be true. The original was pretty good."*

**THE RULING WAS NEVER THAT THE TWO ENDS ARE THE SAME PICTURE.** [[RLG-184]] is that they are the same
OBJECT - "it's looking at the same vehicle straight on from the front and straight on from the back" -
so the OUTLINE is shared and the details inside it are not. Running one function at both ends
satisfied the letter of that and threw away the point of it. A formula car's tail is a diffuser
between two slicks under a deep wing; its face is a nose cone, wishbones and a front wing. Those are
two pictures of one car, and collapsing them lost a drawing the owner liked for nothing the ruling
asked for.

**WHAT WAS WRONG WITH THE REASONING, and it is worth naming because it will recur.** Every other
vehicle in the fleet reached agreement by DELETING one of two drawings of the same part - a second
shadow, a second set of wheels, a second body path. That was right there, because the two drawings
were of the same thing. The formula car's two ends are drawings of DIFFERENT things that happen to
occupy one outline, and the same move applied to them deleted content rather than duplication. The
test is not "are these two blocks of code similar" but "are these two blocks drawing the same part of
the vehicle".

**THE SHAPE THE FIX TAKES.** `formulaShell` declares where the tyres are, how big they are, where the
wing sits, and what the bodywork at the bottom occupies - in the TAIL's numbers, because the tail is
the drawing that was kept. `formulaTyres`, `formulaWing` and `formulaTubPath` draw the parts both ends
genuinely share. Then `paintFormulaTail` paints the diffuser, the rain light and the badge into it,
and `paintFormulaFace` paints a nose cone, the wishbones and a front wing into the same outline.
Measured with `tools/car-ends-test.py`: all 24 columns agree exactly, on all three cars.

**AND THE BOTTOM OF THE CAR IS TWO SHAPES, DECLARED TOGETHER.** A diffuser is narrow and a front wing
is wide, so neither alone could be the shared bottom edge. The declaration carries both - a deep box
in the middle and a shallow plane right across - and each end paints its own thing into each: the
diffuser and the floor edge at the tail, the splitter and the wing at the face.

**NOT VERIFIED ON A DEVICE.** The tail is the drawing that shipped before 0.13.52 and is restored
whole. The face is new work on the tail's proportions - larger tyres and a wider wing than the face
carried before - and whether it reads as a formula car's face is the owner's call.
