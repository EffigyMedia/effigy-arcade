### RLG-266 — The reward splits into three pickups: repair, NOS and fuel
- **RULED 2026-09-15T23:47:46-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-15: "I'd also like to split the rewards into their three pick ups, which means we need to design the look of the three pick ups. Remember if a pick up is not needed for a mode, we just omit it from being spawned."

WHAT ONE CRATE PAYS TODAY (road.js near 19855): 25 of damage repaired, 25 of NOS, and CRATE_SECS on the clock when `clockRuns()`. One box pays all three, and the line that announces it reads "+n HEALTH +n NOS +n SECONDS" (RLG-126). RLG-107 already ruled that a reward paid in a currency the mode does not use is an invisible reward, which is why the seconds are gated on the clock; splitting the box carries that rule into the SPAWNER instead - a pickup a mode has no use for is never laid.

THE THREE LOOKS, the owner's own after a proposal: a RED TOOLBOX with a silver handle and a silver latch for the repair; a BLUE NOS BOTTLE lying on its side with a metal collar and a valve, as proposed; and a RED JERRY CAN for the third, because "time is represented by fuel".

TWO SILHOUETTES ARE RED, which is deliberate and is the owner's choice - the toolbox and the can are different shapes and that is what has to carry at distance on a phone.

NOT SETTLED YET: which pickup each mode spawns; whether the amounts stay at 25, 25 and CRATE_SECS; and whether fuel pays the clock, the fuel gauge, or both.
