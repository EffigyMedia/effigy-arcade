### RLG-046 — The police engage any car breaking the limit, not only the player
- **RULED 2026-08-28T17:34:15-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "police will react to and engage any car they catch breaking the speed limit,
not just the player."

The owner marked it "a note for later, if it isn't already recorded". **It was not recorded.** The
nearest thing in the store is [[RLG-030]], the police gap-list, which is open and is about what the
police system does not do yet; this is a specific item for it and is filed separately so it is not
lost inside a list.

**What exists today.** The police already interact with rivals: `stepRacers` gives a rival and a
cruiser a chance to put each other out (road.js, the `if(!optEasy)` block), and rivals scan cruisers
as obstacles. So cars other than the player are not invisible to the police. **What does not exist
is a cruiser choosing to pursue one of them for speeding.** Heat, pursuit and the chase state are
the player's alone.

**This ruling depends on [[RLG-045]] and cannot be built before it.** "Breaking the speed limit"
needs a speed limit that exists in the world model, and [[RLG-045]] is what introduces one - 70-80mph,
kept by about 90% of drivers. Until that lands there is nothing for a cruiser to measure a car
against, and a police system built on a number invented here would then disagree with the one
RLG-045 establishes.

**It is also the point of RLG-045's speeders.** A driver who decides to speed is only interesting if
something can happen to them, and this is the something.
