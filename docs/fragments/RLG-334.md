### RLG-334 — The camera clips into the up face when the car runs close to it
- **RULED 2026-09-24T00:05:40-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-24, from the device: "we need to prevent the camera from clipping through the mountain face that goes up, which happens in certain vehicles when you drive too close to it."

REPRODUCED IN A HARNESS AND IT IS EASY TO SEE. Hold a MOUNTAIN, force the road to bend TOWARD the hazard side with `API.holdCurve`, and drive: the car is carried to the wall's side of the road and the wall fills half the frame with the car inside it. The shot is `bend-toward.png` in that session's scratch.

"IN CERTAIN VEHICLES" IS THE CLUE AND IT POINTS AT WIDTH. The wall's foot stands at a fixed offset from the road edge - `wallFootX` off `railX()` - while how far the car may travel sideways is the road's own half-width. A wider body reaches closer to that foot at the same lane position, so the same road clips for one car and not another. The camera rides the car, so when the body reaches the foot the eye is already inside the face.

TWO WAYS TO ANSWER IT AND THEY ARE NOT EQUAL. Clamping the car's lateral travel stops the clipping but CHANGES THE DRIVING, and differently per car, which is the kind of quiet handling change that wants the owner's say. Holding the wall's foot outboard of the widest body leaves the driving alone and moves the scenery instead. The second looks right and it is unmeasured.

NOT STARTED. The first job is to find the real numbers: the widest body's half-width against `railX()` and the wall foot, so the answer is a measured clearance rather than a nudged constant.
