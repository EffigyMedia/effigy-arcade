### RLG-182 — A car's front and back are not the same height in the garage, and no tyres show

Owner, 2026-09-09, on seeing a locked car's silhouette: *"That silhouette raises a concern that I
hadn't caught before. The height of the vehicles aren't the same. Every vehicle needs to have the
same height, and we need to show a little bit of the tires on the bottom. Run this check against
every vehicle."* And, correcting a misreading before it could be made: *"When I say every vehicle
needs to be the same height, I mean its front relative to its back, not different vehicles
relative to each other."*

**THE SILHOUETTE IS WHAT MADE IT VISIBLE.** The fault is not new — it has been in the garage since
the card grew two ends — but a car painted in its own colours hides it, and a flat grey shape
against a dark card does not. That is worth recording on its own: [[RLG-180]] was built for
discoverability and its first effect was to expose a rendering fault nobody had caught.

## The measurement, against every vehicle, as asked

`tools/car-ends-test.py` walks all seventeen garage bodies. **Ten agreed exactly and seven did
not, and every one of the seven is a winged car:**

| car | back | front | out by |
|---|---|---|---|
| STALLION | 69.0 | 86.0 | **17.0** |
| MATADOR | 61.9 | 76.1 | **14.2** |
| APEX / COMET / VECTOR | 61.9 | 71.2 | **9.3** |
| CREST | 79.4 | 83.8 | 4.4 |
| LORRY | 134.4 | 137.2 | 2.8 |

AMBULANCE, CAB, COUPE, CRUISER, MUSCLE, PICKUP, ROADSTER, SALOON, TUNER and VAN were all 0.0 —
which is why this could sit unnoticed: **the three cars a fresh save can drive are three of the
ten that agree.**

## Why

`garageFit` measured the ink of both ends and picked ONE scale from the larger of them. Two
drawings with different amounts of ink then came out at two different heights. And `drawGarageCar`
placed each end's content **TOP** on the ceiling line, so the two started together and finished
apart — the rear of a STALLION stopped seventeen pixels above its own front.

**THE CODE ALREADY CLAIMED OTHERWISE, IN WRITING.** The comment in `drawGarageCar`, written for
[[RLG-087]], says: *"Both are then drawn at ONE scale... with their content bottoms on the same
line. A car is the same size from both ends and stands on the same floor, which is the whole of
what the owner asked for."* The scale half was true. **The floor half was never what the code
did**, and the comment kept it looking correct to anybody who read it instead of measuring it.
That is the third time in two days that a comment stating a fact about the drawing has been found
to be out of date — see [[RLG-177]] and [[RLG-179]].

## What was built

Each end now gets **its own scale**, chosen so both reach the same drawn height, and the height is
still the one the larger end would have taken — so nothing grows and neither end leaves its half of
the card. And the **bottom** is what is placed, so they stand on one line. Re-measured: all
seventeen at 0.0.

## The tyres are NOT done, and here is what is known

**The silhouette answers the question directly: there is no wheel in the sprite below the body.**
The grey shape is the sprite's own ink, and its bottom edge is flat across the whole width on both
ends of every car. So this is not a cropping or an alignment problem that the layout can fix —
**nothing is being cut off, because there is nothing there.**

Showing "a little bit of the tires" therefore needs the garage view to draw wheels that the body
sprite does not carry. That is a painter change across the fleet rather than a layout one, it
touches the same declaration machinery [[RLG-053]] set up, and it should be its own unit. **Do not
attempt it by lowering the floor or extending the box** — that would move a car that has no wheels
down the card and leave a gap.
