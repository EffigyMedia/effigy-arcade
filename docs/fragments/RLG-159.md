### RLG-159 — Traffic gets out of the way of the police, so the avoidance is two-sided

Owner, 2026-09-07: "Seems like traffic doesn't avoid crashing into the police either,
so it's two-sided."

The civilian traffic AI reads other traffic and the player when it decides to slow or to
change lane. It does not read the `cops` array at all, so a cruiser is invisible to every
car on the road - which is why a pursuit ploughs a furrow through the traffic rather than
parting it.

Pairs with RLG-158: one is the police noticing the traffic, this is the traffic noticing
the police.
