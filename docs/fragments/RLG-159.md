### RLG-159 — Traffic gets out of the way of the police, so the avoidance is two-sided

Owner, 2026-09-07: "Seems like traffic doesn't avoid crashing into the police either,
so it's two-sided."

The civilian traffic AI reads other traffic and the player when it decides to slow or to
change lane. It does not read the `cops` array at all, so a cruiser is invisible to every
car on the road - which is why a pursuit ploughs a furrow through the traffic rather than
parting it.

Pairs with RLG-158: one is the police noticing the traffic, this is the traffic noticing
the police.
- **RULED 2026-09-08T03:04:28-04:00** — BUILT 2026-09-08, v0.13.31. Four sweeps now read `cops` as well as `traffic`: the one that
asks whether a lane is clear to move into, the one that asks whether moving there would close the
road, the one that asks how fast a lane is, and the one that stops a car driving into the back of
the thing in front of it. Each already read the PLAYER as a named special case; the police are a
third list of cars in the same lanes and are read for the same reason.

A PARKED CRUISER IS A THIRD LIST, NOT A THIRD BEHAVIOUR. Nothing about how civilians drive changed
- the same margins, the same urgency, the same corridor guarantee. What changed is what they can
see. That is why the diff is a helper closure in each sweep rather than new logic: an obstacle is
an obstacle.

THE PROOF IS A CONTROLLED APPROACH, and two statistics were tried first and measured nothing:

  - INTERPENETRATION, counted as frames in which a traffic car sat inside a cruiser: 2.3 readings
    per hundred samples before the change and 3.2 after. The collision code resolves an overlap
    within a frame or two, so the state barely exists to be sampled.
  - CRUISERS PUT OUT BY TRAFFIC, which is [[RLG-158]]'s own sentence as a number. ZERO over ninety
    seconds of pursuit, and zero again across five conditions from one star at half speed to five
    stars at nine tenths - two to six damage events in forty-five seconds and never the three hits
    a cruiser needs to go down. THE OWNER'S "constantly crashing into traffic and destroying
    themselves" DOES NOT REPRODUCE IN A HARNESS. That is a fact about the harness or about how a
    real player drives, and it is NOT evidence that it does not happen on the device.

What works is one cruiser parked in one lane and one traffic car put behind it in the same lane,
closing, with the road swept of everything else. The car moved 0.78 of a lane to go round it and
never occupied the same road; on the engine before the change it moved 0.00 and drove straight on.

ONE OF THE TWO ASSERTIONS IS THE PROOF AND THE OTHER IS ONLY A GUARD. Run against the old engine
the REACTION check goes red and the OVERLAP check stays green, because at a 120ms sample the few
frames of interpenetration are stepped over. A green overlap line is not evidence of anything, and
the harness says so where a later session will read it.

THE CORRIDOR GUARANTEE WAS CHECKED ON BOTH ARMS and the failure is [[RLG-037]]'s known flake rather
than this change: three runs on the new engine gave 0.234, 0.352 and 0.389 against a 0.34 limit,
and three on the old gave 0.384, 0.209 and 0.369. One failure each, overlapping ranges. A single
run of that harness may not be quoted, and this is why.
