### RLG-085 — Every vehicle throws its own headlight beam
- **RULED 2026-08-30T18:59:11-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-30, immediately after the player's beam was rebuilt: would it be too much if all the other vehicles also had headlight beams.

NOT TOO MUCH, WITH A CAP AND A MEASUREMENT. The player's beam costs seven polygon fills a frame - three shells for each of two lamps, plus the pool. Giving every vehicle the same treatment is what would be too much; giving each one a single shell, no pool and a shorter throw is roughly one fill each, and the count is then a question of how many vehicles are allowed to have one at once rather than of how many are on the road.

ONLY THE ONES DRIVING AWAY FROM THE CAMERA. In the forward view every vehicle is seen from behind, so its lamps point down the road and its beam lies on tarmac the player can see. That is the whole of the effect and it is worth having: traffic ahead of you lighting the road it is driving on is one of the things that makes a night road read as a night road. An oncoming vehicle points its lamps AT the lens, which is a different feature - glare rather than a pool of light - and it belongs with the rear-view, where the oncoming cars actually are.

AND IT MUST BE MEASURED IN A FOREST AT NIGHT BEFORE IT LANDS. FOREST is already the heaviest biome and this machine cannot say by how much: fps-test reads it at 48 to 60 across samples on one unchanged build, against 60 to 60 for desert and tundra, so the spread is as large as the effect. A per-vehicle beam is exactly the kind of change that turns a 48 into something the owner feels on the device. The levers if it costs too much are the cap on how many vehicles get one and the length of the throw, in that order.

THE SAME RULE THE PLAYER'S BEAM TAKES: it is off in daylight by the clock rather than by the weather, because a beam is the light you can see lying on the road and you cannot see that at noon however wet it is.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T19:31:28-04:00** — BUILT, 2026-08-30. One cone per vehicle, capped at twelve, ahead of the camera only, fading out with distance so the far ones thin rather than switching off at the cap and popping. A wreck has no lamps left and a speed trap parked on the verge is waiting rather than driving, so neither throws anything. The lamps sit at the FRONT of the car, half its length ahead of the point it is drawn at, because a truck's beam starting at its tail runs out from under its own trailer.

ONE PAINTER NOW DRAWS EVERY BEAM IN THE GAME. The player's three shells, the pool at its bumper and a delivery van's single cone are the same function at different sizes. That was worth doing for its own sake: the beam has already been rebuilt once for riding crests and following the bend, and a second copy of that walk would have been a second place for it to be wrong.

THE COST IS BELOW WHAT THIS MACHINE CAN MEASURE, AND THAT IS NOT THE SAME AS FREE. fps-test was given a --phase argument first, because it started at the dusk default and let the clock run, so a two-second sample straddled the lights coming on - not a thing to average over when the change being measured is a light. Pinned at midnight, before and after: CITY 48.8-56.4 against 54.4-60.4, FOREST 46.4-53.2 against 52.4-59.6, SWAMP 50.8-59.2 against 50.0-55.2. Every range overlaps, which by the harness's own rule means no change was measured. FOREST reading FASTER afterwards is the clearest sign that what is being seen is noise.

SO THE INSTRUMENT WAS CHECKED AGAINST A COST IT SHOULD HAVE SEEN. With the cap raised to 48 - four times what ships - FOREST read 53.6-58.4, CITY 55.2-60.0 and SWAMP 54.4-59.6, which is still the same overlapping band. That is the honest result: this desktop cannot resolve the cost of these beams even at four times the shipped number, so no claim is made that they are cheap on a phone. THE CAP IS A BOUND ON A DEVICE NOBODY HAS MEASURED. Twelve was chosen to cover the traffic actually in view, and the levers if the device disagrees are this cap and then the reach, in that order.

AND THE OWNER JUDGES IT. What a harness cannot say is whether a road full of traffic beams reads as a night road or as a mess, and whether the light from a car two hundred metres ahead is worth the fill it costs.
