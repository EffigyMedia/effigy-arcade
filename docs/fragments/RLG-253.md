### RLG-253 — Engaged police cruisers still appear ahead of the player in Hot Pursuit
- **RULED 2026-09-15T02:15:04-04:00** — TRACKED, IN PROGRESS, NOT FIXED. Owner, 2026-09-15, playing Hot Pursuit after 0.14.26 was pushed: "There is still cruisers actively being spawned in front of me at a very frequent rate." Then: "I quite literally see cop cars spawning in up ahead and they come back at me presumably by driving slower, but it's constant." Then: "Yes, I see them up ahead with their lights engaged, and they are tracking my X position on the road. They are absolutely engaged on me."

The owner has asked for this several times (RLG-157, RLG-247). RLG-247's trap fix did not remove what they see.

MEASURED, AND NOT YET REPRODUCED. On 0.14.27, 60 seconds of Hot Pursuit at heat 3, player speed held and lanes changed every 5 seconds, a 100 ms sampler of every car in `cops`: no moving (non-trap) cruiser was ever 8,000 units or more ahead. That held at 60-95 % of top speed (27 cruisers) and at 25-50 % (11 cruisers). In the same minute the road put 11 parked traps and 7 dormant patrol cars ahead. The owner ruled both out: they are engaged, lit, and follow the player's X. Scratch probe: far_ahead.py (not kept in the repository).

THE HARNESS DIFFERS FROM PLAY IN WAYS THAT MATTER, and the next session tests these first:
1. CRASHES. `wreck()` stops the player for WRECK_SECS (2 s) while chasing cruisers keep moving at chase speed, so they can pass far ahead and then slow toward the player (`want = spd + clamp((wantDz - dz)*2.2, -2600, 3400)`), engaged and steering to playerX. Real play crashes often; the harness never crashed. Test with a staged crash (API.setDmg near the limit plus a traffic hit, or a direct call to the wreck path) and cruisers behind.
2. STOPPING AND BRAKING HARD, and NOS, which the harness never did.
3. Race modes with pursuit on (SINGLE RACE / TOURNAMENT) and a timed run, which may place things differently.
4. The owner's phone build. The owner did not say what the BUILD row reads; ask if the cause stays hidden.

WHEN FOUND: the rule is that a police car engaged on the player is never ahead of the player unless it passed the player on the road (the RLG-247 front station). A car that got ahead while the player was stopped or wrecked must not come back from ahead. The proof must reproduce the owner's picture first (an engaged, lit cruiser first drawn ahead), then show it gone, with the old commit as the falsifier.

MEASURED ON 0.14.27 BEFORE THIS RULING (scratch probes first_seen.py and passing.py, not kept):
1. BIRTH AND FIRST DRAW. 90 s of Hot Pursuit at heat 3 with speed changes, stops and lane changes. No moving cruiser was born ahead or first drawn ahead. Patrols engaged at dz -256 to +85. Radio and Interceptor cars were born at about -4,000 to -4,800 and first drawn at about -430. Parked traps were born 34,000 to 50,000 ahead and first drawn at about 29,000, eighteen of them in 90 s.
2. NO RENDERING FAULT OF THE KIND THE ENTRY ABOVE NAMED. The forward pass buckets by `floor(z/SEG) - floor(pos/SEG)` with no wrap, and `drawSprite` drops anything less than 430 units ahead of the camera. A car behind the camera is never drawn ahead.
3. A BLIND BAND BESIDE THE PLAYER. IT IS REAL, AND IT IS NOT THE CAUSE. The mirror shows only `k.z < pos`, which is PLAYER_Z (about 860) behind the player's car. The forward view draws a car from 430 behind the player, and the player's own car covers it until it is roughly 500 ahead. Screenshot: a cruiser 416 behind is in neither view. Chasing cruisers went from that band to visible ahead 4 times in 90 s, and 3 of the 4 were in the mirror less than 2 s before. That is not "constant".
4. EVERY PARKED TRAP FLASHES ITS BAR. `drawCopLights` runs for every 'k' item and does not test `trap` or `armed`, so a trap parked on the verge far ahead is drawn lit. With 18 traps in 90 s, a parked trap is the most frequent lit police car ahead of the player.

DECIDED: a parked, armed trap is drawn dark in the forward view and in the mirror. Its bar lights when it engages. The rogue cruiser report stays open until the owner tests the dark traps on the device.

RECORDED, NOT BUILT: when the rogue cruiser is solved, a trap that engages goes back to pulling out and chasing the player. Today a trap engages only after the player has passed it (RLG-247).

THAT IDENTIFIED THE CAR. Dark with engagement OFF is the parked-trap state of 0.14.28. So the rogue cruisers were speed traps, and the entries above that set traps aside on the owner's word that the cars were "engaged" were wrong: the bar was lit on every trap until 0.14.28, which is what made a trap look engaged.

CAUSE, FOUND AND MEASURED. The last steering line of the cruiser update, `k.x += clamp(aim - k.x, ...)`, had no `parked` guard, and `aim` is the player's X. A parked trap kept its speed of zero (RLG-173) and slid off the verge into the road, then held the player's lane while standing still. Measured on 071dea1f: a trap spawned at x -1.16 reached the centre line, and moved 2.04 to 2.16 across the road in six seconds of lane changes. The earlier probes logged traps and discounted them, and no harness read a trap's X.

BUILT IN 0.14.31. The line is skipped for a parked trap. An engaged trap still steers.

PROOF. tools/trap-post-test.py holds a spawned trap 6,000 units ahead while the player changes lanes every 1.5 s. It asserts that the trap stays at least 1.1 from the centre and moves less than 0.02, and, as a control, that an engaged trap does steer. It passes on 0.14.31 and fails both trap checks against 071dea1f. trap-dark, trap-switch, roadblock, heat, bust and smoke pass. pursuit-stop-test failed its LINED arm once and passed on a rerun, and it passes on 071dea1f.
