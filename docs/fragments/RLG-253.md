### RLG-253 — Engaged police cruisers still appear ahead of the player in Hot Pursuit
- **RULED 2026-09-15T02:15:04-04:00** — TRACKED, IN PROGRESS, NOT FIXED. Owner, 2026-09-15, playing Hot Pursuit after 0.14.26 was pushed: "There is still cruisers actively being spawned in front of me at a very frequent rate." Then: "I quite literally see cop cars spawning in up ahead and they come back at me presumably by driving slower, but it's constant." Then: "Yes, I see them up ahead with their lights engaged, and they are tracking my X position on the road. They are absolutely engaged on me."

The owner has asked for this several times (RLG-157, RLG-247). RLG-247's trap fix did not remove what they see.

MEASURED, AND NOT YET REPRODUCED. On 0.14.27, 60 seconds of Hot Pursuit at heat 3, player speed held and lanes changed every 5 seconds, a 100 ms sampler of every car in `cops`: no moving (non-trap) cruiser was ever 8,000 units or more ahead. That held at 60-95 % of top speed (27 cruisers) and at 25-50 % (11 cruisers). In the same minute the road put 11 parked traps and 7 dormant patrol cars ahead. The owner ruled both out: they are engaged, lit, and follow the player's X. Scratch probe: far_ahead.py (not kept in the repository).

THE HARNESS DIFFERS FROM PLAY IN WAYS THAT MATTER, and the next session tests these first:
1. CRASHES. `wreck()` stops the player for WRECK_SECS (2 s) while chasing cruisers keep moving at chase speed, so they can pass far ahead and then slow toward the player (`want = spd + clamp((wantDz - dz)*2.2, -2600, 3400)`), engaged and steering to playerX. Real play crashes often; the harness never crashed. Test with a staged crash (API.setDmg near the limit plus a traffic hit, or a direct call to the wreck path) and cruisers behind.
2. STOPPING AND BRAKING HARD, and NOS, which the harness never did.
3. Race modes with pursuit on (SINGLE RACE / TOURNAMENT) and a timed run, which may place things differently.
4. The owner's phone build. The owner did not say what the BUILD row reads; ask if the cause stays hidden.

WHEN FOUND: the rule is that a police car engaged on the player is never ahead of the player unless it passed the player on the road (the RLG-247 front station). A car that got ahead while the player was stopped or wrecked must not come back from ahead. The proof must reproduce the owner's picture first (an engaged, lit cruiser first drawn ahead), then show it gone, with the old commit as the falsifier.
