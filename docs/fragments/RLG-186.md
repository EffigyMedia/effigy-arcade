### RLG-186 — The CREST gets a face: a big grille with detail in it, and bumper detail
- **RULED 2026-09-09T16:07:41-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-09-09:** *"Since there is so much unused space on the front of the crest, I'd like to
fill much of that in with a big grill. Let's put some interest in detail inside that grill and maybe
show bumper detail."*

**THE SPACE WAS REAL AND IT WAS MOST OF THE CAR.** CREST's face carried a light bar across the top, a
mouth 0.06 of the sprite deep under it, and then a quarter of the car's height of bare paint down to
a single flat band at the bottom. The reason it looked like that is in the code's own comment - "a
rear-engined car has almost no nose, and it should look it" - which is true of a real one and is not
an argument for an empty rectangle on a card the player is looking AT rather than following.

**WHAT IS THERE NOW.** A mouth running from just under the light bar to just above the bumper, in a
bright surround; a lattice inside it rather than a row of bars, because at the size a car draws on a
phone a row of bars reads as a stripe and only crossing them reads as a grille; a blade across the
middle in the car's own paint, with a shadow under it so it stands off the mesh; a brake duct
outboard of each end of the mouth, with two strakes in it; and a bumper that is a lip with a bright
top edge and a vent let into each end rather than one flat band.

**EVERY LINE OF IT IS INSIDE THE BODY PATH.** [[RLG-184]] made the outline the thing both painters
share, so a detail added at one end can now break a car that was in agreement. This one cannot: it is
painted onto bodywork the tail already draws. `tools/car-ends-test.py` was run after it and all
seventeen bodies still match, which is the check that would have caught it.

**NOT VERIFIED ON A DEVICE.** Whether the grille is the right size, whether the blade reads on a
white car, and whether the ducts are legible at road distance are all the owner's call.
