### RLG-127 — The bottle lets the engine overspeed by a tenth, so it is worth opening at the top of the last gear
- **RULED 2026-08-31T19:06:46-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "let's allow NOS room to exceed final gear redline limit by 10%, whatever speed that earns you. That way NOS is still useful at max tach." And then, on being told a note in the file argued the opposite: "yes, the old rule says not to arbitrarily increase speed, but I want to justify it by allowing the engine to turn a little faster than it's mechanically supposed to."

THE SECOND SENTENCE IS THE WHOLE DESIGN AND IT IS BETTER THAN THE FIRST IMPLEMENTATION OF IT. The first attempt raised the SPEED ceiling by a tenth directly, which is a number granted beside the physics. What was built instead raises the LIMITER by a tenth, and the speed follows: a gear's speed ceiling is its rev limiter expressed as a speed, so lifting the limiter lifts that ceiling by exactly the same tenth and the extra speed is EARNED through the gearing the car already has. One constant, `NOS_REV`, and everything downstream inherits it.

IT REVERSES A NOTE THAT WAS WRONG TWICE OVER. The comment that used to sit in this expression read: "I had it raising top speed, which is wrong: more oxygen means more power in the gear you are in, so you climb the rev bands faster and reach the SAME ceiling sooner. What sets top speed is aero drag, and a bottle does nothing about that."

THE PHYSICS IN IT IS WRONG. Top speed is where power BALANCES drag, not where drag acts alone, so more power does move it. And the GAME reasoning was wrong in a way the owner felt directly: in the final gear `gearCap` IS `carTop`, so a bottle aimed at `carTop` bought nothing at all in the one place a player most wants it - flat out, needle on the limiter, nothing left to shift into. A resource that does nothing in the situation you are saving it for is a dead button.

IT IS NOT FREE SPEED, and the mechanism that makes it temporary was already there. `overRun` sheds hard above the car's own top end and is gated on `!nosOn`, so the tenth is a thing you HOLD rather than a thing you reach and keep. The moment the bottle shuts, the limiter is back and the speed is scrubbed off.

THE HARD CEILING HAD TO MOVE WITH IT. `spd` is clamped to `FLEET_TOP * 1.10`, and that flat 1.10 was exactly the bottle's new ceiling on the fastest car in the fleet - so the COMET would have been clamped at precisely the speed the bottle is supposed to earn it. It is derived from `NOS_REV` now, plus a frame of overshoot, so the two cannot disagree.

PROVED IN THE SAME PLACE AS THE INVARIANT IT IS AN EXCEPTION TO. `tools/drive-test.py` already asserts the revs never pass the limiter - a check that has been earned three times over, because the limiter has been broken three times. The exception is measured beside it deliberately: a limiter with an exception nobody tests is a limiter with a hole. Measured on both machines: 100.0 per cent of redline with the bottle shut, 110.0 per cent with it open, 110.0 per cent of the car's declared top end for the speed, and back to 100.0 the moment it is released. Setting `NOS_REV` to 1.00 fails both halves.

AND THE MEASUREMENT WAS WRONG FOUR TIMES BEFORE IT WAS RIGHT, which is the same lesson this project keeps paying for. Letting the car COAST after forcing it fast read 52 per cent of top end, because no throttle was held and it fell away from the ceiling rather than onto it. Starting BELOW and asking it to climb read 0.4 per cent: 0.94 to 1.10 of top end is a 17 per cent gain in the one place acceleration is weakest, where the torque curve gives 6 per cent at the limiter. Leaving the TRAFFIC ON read 35 per cent, because a car driven at 1.3 times its own top speed through civilian traffic hits something - the samples showed speed dropping 18,613 to 4,137 between two frames, which is a collision and not a ceiling. And the bottle would not stay held under a synthetic button press on one machine while staying held on the other.

A CEILING IS MEASURED FROM ABOVE, ON AN EMPTY ROAD, WITH THE THROTTLE HELD. That is the shape, and it is the same shape [[RLG-055]]'s five faults arrived at: hold every controlled variable every frame, and take the reading where the thing you do not care about does not exist.

THE BUTTON PATH IS STILL COVERED. The real control is pressed and the check asserts the bottle opened; the measurement itself is held through `API.holdNos`, which refuses on a car with no bottle so it cannot fake the one thing [[RLG-107]] is about.

NOT VERIFIED FROM HERE: whether a tenth is the right amount, and whether the needle sitting past the red on the dial reads as deliberate or as a fault. Both are the owner's on the device, and the tenth is one config row.
- **RULED 2026-09-09T22:27:33-04:00** — **THE CHECK THAT PROVED THE BUTTON WORKED WAS READING ITS OWN SETTER, 2026-09-09.** The line
`the bottle can be held open for the check` read `nosOn` back after `holdNos(true)` had already set
it. It could not have failed. And the message written under it for the failure case - *"the nitrous
button did not take, so nothing below is about the bottle"* - is a `detail`, which this harness
prints on a PASS as well as on a failure. So the run said the button had not taken, on a line marked
`ok`, and four assertions below it went on reporting numbers.

**THAT IS WHY ONE OF THEM MOVED ON ITS OWN.** `so the car settles a tenth above its own top end`
failed on one run at 113.4% and passed on the next with no code change.

**THREE THINGS WERE WRONG AND THEY ARE SEPARATE.**

- **The button was never tested.** It is pressed FIRST now, with nothing holding the bottle open, and
  what `nosOn` says then is about the button. `holdNos` comes after, for the measurement, which is
  what it was added for.

- **A PRECONDITION THAT FAILS MUST NOT SCORE THE CHECKS BELOW IT.** `Result` has a third state.
  BLOCKED is not a pass, it counts against the run exactly as a failure does, and it names what
  stopped the check instead of quoting a number about a car that was doing something else. The run's
  closing line says how many checks could not be run, because a suite that quietly measured nothing
  looks identical to one that measured everything.

- **THE CEILING IS ONLY A CEILING WHILE THE ENGINE IS ON THE LIMITER.** A gear's speed ceiling IS its
  rev limiter expressed as a speed, so a car below the limiter is not at its ceiling and its speed
  says nothing about one. The old loop took the lowest reading over twenty-six waits of 90ms whatever
  the engine was doing. On the CIRCUIT that is never a ceiling - the road bends and the car scrubs
  speed into the corner - and the ungated version read **58.8% of top end and called it settled**. A
  sample now counts only while the revs are at 98% of the limiter or better, the fall is measured
  against `simTime` rather than wall time ([[RLG-055]]), and it stops when the number stops moving.
  If there was never enough straight road, the check reports that it could not be measured.

**MEASURED AFTER THE CHANGE:** 110.0% on Interstate and 108.0 to 110.0% on Raceway, over three runs
of both machines, 37 of 37 checks passing each time.

**`tools/nos-falsify.py` IS THE PROOF AND IT HAS TWO ARMS**, because one defect cannot demonstrate
both halves. Unwire the button and only the button line fails, while the three measurements below it
still run - which is correct, since they are taken through `holdNos` on purpose. Make `holdNos`
refuse and the button line still PASSES while those three come out BLOCKED. The second arm caught an
error in the proof's own expectation before it caught anything in the harness.
